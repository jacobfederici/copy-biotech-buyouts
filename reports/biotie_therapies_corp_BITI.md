## Summary

Acorda Therapeutics acquired Biotie Therapies Oyj via a voluntary public tender offer for all outstanding shares, ADSs, and equity instruments for a total equity value of approximately $363 million in cash. The acquisition was driven by Acorda's interest in Biotie's late-stage neurology pipeline, specifically tozadenant for Parkinson's disease.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2015 following an unsolicited approach by Acorda CEO Ron Cohen to Biotie CEO Timo Veromaa. Acorda initially proposed $25.50 per ADS in November 2015. Biotie's Board, advised by Guggenheim Securities, Davis Polk, and Hannes Snellman, formed a Transaction Committee (Bill Burns and Don Bailey) to oversee the process. Biotie countered at $31.00 per ADS in December, but Acorda only moved to $26.25. During a market check, Guggenheim contacted six potential alternative buyers; all declined to pursue a transaction. In January 2016, Acorda adjusted the per-ADS price to $25.60 (EUR 23.5680) to ensure the total cash outlay for all equity interests, including complex warrant liabilities calculated via Black-Scholes, did not exceed a $363 million ceiling. The Board approved the final terms on January 19, 2016, after Guggenheim rendered a fairness opinion.

## Rationale

The Biotie Board recommended the deal because the cash consideration provided immediate liquidity at a significant premium (95% over the last trading day). They noted the high risks and costs associated with independent Phase 3 development and commercialization of tozadenant, including the need for significant dilutive capital raises. Acorda sought the deal to expand its neurology pipeline with a late-stage asset (tozadenant) that fits its existing commercial infrastructure.
