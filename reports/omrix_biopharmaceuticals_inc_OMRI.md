## Summary

Omrix Biopharmaceuticals, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Johnson & Johnson and its subsidiary Binder Merger Sub, Inc., under which Johnson & Johnson will acquire all outstanding shares of Omrix for $25.00 per share in cash. The transaction is structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger, aimed at integrating Omrix's biosurgical and hemostasis products into Johnson & Johnson's Ethicon franchise.

## Timeline

- 2008-06-13: CEO Robert Taub informs the Board of an approach by an Investor Group; Special Committee of independent directors is formed.
- 2008-06-17: Special Committee selects UBS as financial advisor and Skadden as legal advisor.
- 2008-06-25: Investor Group submits a non-binding proposal of $21.00 to $25.00 per share.
- 2008-07-09: Special Committee authorizes UBS to contact Johnson & Johnson (Parent) to gauge interest.
- 2008-08-01: Omrix and Parent execute a Non-Disclosure Agreement; Parent begins due diligence.

- 2008-09-08: Parent increases its non-binding proposal to $29.00 per share.
- 2008-10-22: Parent reduces its proposal back to $25.00 per share, citing diligence findings regarding capital expansion overcapacity and IVIG business risks.
- 2008-10-26: Special Committee and Board initially reject the $25.00 offer; CEO Robert Taub recommends rejection.
- 2008-11-13: Special Committee reverses decision and votes unanimously to accept $25.00 offer after CEO Taub changes his recommendation due to market volatility and business risks.
- 2008-11-23: Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement and the $25.00 per share consideration.
- 2008-11-24: Joint press release issued announcing the transaction.
- 2008-11-25: Purchaser commences the tender offer.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in June 2008 following an unsolicited approach by an 'Investor Group'. The Omrix Special Committee, led by Larry Ellberger, proactively contacted Johnson & Johnson (Parent) due to their existing commercial relationship. Parent initially offered $25.00 in August, raised it to $29.00 in September, but lowered it back to $25.00 in October after conducting deep diligence on Omrix's IVIG business and capital expansion plans. The Board initially rejected the lower price on October 26. However, by mid-November, CEO Robert Taub and the Special Committee reconsidered, citing deteriorating global financial market conditions and specific operational risks. Final negotiations involved Parent requiring amended employment and retention agreements for CEO Robert Taub and COO Nissim Mashiach as a condition of the deal. UBS provided a fairness opinion on November 23, 2008, supporting the $25.00 price.

## Rationale

The Board recommended the deal because it provided immediate cash liquidity at a significant premium (46.4% over the one-month average) during a period of extreme market volatility. They also considered the substantial capital expenditures and risks required for Omrix to remain independent, including clinical trial delays and the risks associated with the IVIG business.

    *   **Reported Details:** Reports indicated that BTG, then Israel's leading biotech company, was in the final stages of acquiring Omrix to expand its product line. Omrix was a private company at the time of this rumor.

*   **August 18, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Globes*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unnamed U.S. Hedge Fund; Ethicon Inc. (Johnson & Johnson subsidiary)

    *   **Reported Details:** Sources reported that Omrix received a buyout offer from a U.S. hedge fund to take the company private at $25 per share. The report also revealed that Omrix had previously turned down an acquisition offer from its partner, Ethicon (Johnson & Johnson), a few months prior because the offer was deemed too low. CEO Robert Taub was reportedly using the hedge fund offer to pressure Ethicon into making a superior bid.

*   **August 19, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *FierceBiotech*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unnamed New York Hedge Fund
    *   **Rumored Price:** $25.00 per share (13–14% premium)
    *   **Reported Details:** Following the *Globes* report, industry outlets noted a significant jump in Omrix's share price based on the speculation of a $25 per share offer from a New York-based hedge fund.

The presence of the J&J team triggered a massive spike in trading volume and share price on the Friday before the official Monday announcement.

(Note: While *Globes* reported a rumored $27 price on this date, the official deal announced the next day was $25 per share).
