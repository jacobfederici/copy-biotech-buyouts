## Summary

Depomed, Inc. was the target of an unsolicited hostile exchange offer by Horizon Pharma plc. Horizon initially proposed to acquire Depomed for $29.25 per share in stock in May 2015, later increasing the offer to $33.00 per share and eventually setting a fixed exchange ratio of 0.95 Horizon shares per Depomed share. The Depomed Board of Directors consistently rejected these proposals, citing substantial undervaluation and concerns over Horizon's business model. Following a preliminary injunction granted by a California court in November 2015 regarding Horizon's misuse of confidential information, and subsequent litigation, the parties mutually agreed to settle and terminate the takeover attempt in April 2016.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began with Horizon expressing interest in Depomed's product CAMBIA in early 2015, which Depomed CEO James Schoeneck declined. On May 27, 2015, Horizon CEO Timothy Walbert proposed an all-stock acquisition at $29.25 per share. Depomed's board, advised by Morgan Stanley and Baker Botts, rejected this as opportunistic. Horizon went public with the offer on July 7, 2015. Following this, Horizon's financial advisors (Citi and Jefferies) suggested a $3.00 increase to $32.25, and later $33.00. Depomed adopted a Rights Agreement on July 12 to defend against the bid. On August 13, Horizon set a fixed exchange ratio of 0.95. Depomed filed suit on August 3, 2015, alleging Horizon used confidential data from a previous NUCYNTA due diligence process with Janssen. The legal battle culminated in a November 2015 injunction against Horizon, leading to the eventual settlement in April 2016.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition bids, and takeover speculation for Depomed, Inc. (now Assertio Holdings, Inc.) prior to any confirmed deal or major corporate restructuring.

*   **May 27, 2015**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Horizon Pharma (Public Disclosure)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Horizon Pharma plc
    *   **Rumored Price:** $29.25 per share (all-stock)
    *   **Details:** Horizon Pharma privately submitted an unsolicited proposal to acquire Depomed. This remained private until July 2015.

*   **July 7, 2015**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reuters / PR Newswire
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Horizon Pharma plc
    *   **Rumored Price:** $29.25 per share (approx. $3 billion total)
    *   **Details:** Horizon Pharma went public with its hostile bid after Depomed’s board refused to engage in discussions. The offer represented a 42% premium over Depomed’s closing price on July 6.

*   **July 21, 2015**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** BioPharma Dive / StreetInsider
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Horizon Pharma plc
    *   **Rumored Price:** $33.00 per share
    *   **Details:** Horizon increased its all-stock offer to $33.00 per share. Depomed’s board subsequently rejected this bid as "inadequate" and adopted a "poison pill" shareholder rights plan to block the takeover.

*   **August 7, 2015**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** FirstWord Pharma / The Irish Times
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Horizon Pharma plc
    *   **Rumored Price:** $33.00 per share (with potential 25% cash component)
    *   **Details:** Reports indicated Horizon was prepared to include a cash component of up to 25% in its $33.00 per share offer to entice shareholders, though Depomed continued to resist.

*   **September 8, 2015**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Associated Press / Yahoo Finance
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Horizon Pharma plc
    *   **Rumored Price:** $33.00 per share (Exchange ratio of 0.95 Horizon shares per Depomed share)
    *   **Details:** Horizon officially launched a hostile exchange offer directly to Depomed shareholders, bypassing the board.

*   **April 7, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reuters / Proactive Investors
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unnamed Strategic Buyers (Speculated via Activist)
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified
    *   **Details:** Activist investor Starboard Value LP disclosed a 9.8% stake and sent a letter to Depomed’s board urging them to explore a sale of the company, citing "egregious" governance and significant interest from potential acquirers that the board had allegedly stymied.

*   **September 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** New York Post / Reuters
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** KKR & Co., Daiichi Sankyo, and other unnamed pharmaceutical companies.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Speculated premium; market cap was approx. $1.5 billion at the time.
    *   **Details:** Rumors circulated that Depomed had hired Morgan Stanley to run a formal sale process following continued pressure from Starboard Value. Reports suggested that private equity firms and international drugmakers were reviewing the company's books.

*   **March 2017**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** BioPharma Dive / Bloomberg
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unnamed Strategic Buyers
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified
    *   **Details:** Speculation regarding a sale intensified as Depomed reached a settlement with Starboard Value to replace its CEO and several board members. Analysts reported that the leadership change was a precursor to making the company more attractive for an eventual acquisition.
