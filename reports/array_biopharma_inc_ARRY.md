## Summary

Array BioPharma Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Pfizer Inc. for $48.00 per share in cash, representing a total equity value of approximately $10.7 billion. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, aimed at strengthening Pfizer's oncology portfolio with Array's approved BRAF/MEK inhibitor combination and its broader pipeline of targeted cancer therapies.

- 2018-10-08: Party A approaches Array CEO Ron Squarer regarding a potential merger; no economic terms proposed.

- 2019-03-21: Array and Pfizer execute a confidential disclosure agreement (CDA).

- 2019-06-03: Pfizer increases its non-binding proposal to $48.00 per share in cash.

- 2019-06-17: Public announcement of the merger agreement.
- 2019-06-28: Pfizer commences the tender offer.
- 2019-07-29: Tender offer expires with approximately 77% of shares tendered.
- 2019-07-30: Merger completed; Array becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Pfizer.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest following Array's positive BEACON trial data in May 2019. Pfizer, which had been in informal contact since late 2018, submitted an initial bid of $44.00 per share on May 29, 2019. Array's Executive Committee, advised by Centerview Partners and Skadden Arps, determined this undervalued the company's prospects. On June 3, 2019, Pfizer's Douglas Giordano communicated an increased offer of $48.00 per share, conditioned on exclusivity and a June 17 announcement. During this period, Array also engaged with 'Party B', but that party indicated it could not meet the required premium or timeline. Diligence proceeded through early June, including site visits to Boulder. Final negotiations focused on the termination fee (set at $400 million) and employee retention/severance programs. The Executive Committee approved the deal on June 14, 2019, after receiving a fairness opinion from Centerview.

## Rationale

The Array Board recommended the deal based on the significant cash premium (63% over the last closing price), the immediate liquidity provided to stockholders, and the risks associated with remaining a standalone company, including commercialization challenges and the need for significant future funding. Pfizer sought to acquire Array to bolster its oncology franchise with the BRAFTOVI/MEKTOVI combination and a robust targeted therapy pipeline.

## Evidence

- At a meeting of the Executive Committee held on June 14, 2019, the Executive Committee unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions are fair to, and in the best interest of, Array and its stockholders (0001193125-19-185111).
- On July 30, 2019, Pfizer completed its acquisition of Array by consummating the Merger without a meeting of stockholders of Array in accordance with Section 251(h) (0001193125-19-206255).

## Decision Notes

The deal value was calculated using the per-share price of $48.00 and the 223,128,985 shares outstanding as of June 27, 2019. The transaction successfully closed via a tender offer and subsequent merger on July 30, 2019.
