## Summary

Amgen Inc. acquired Micromet, Inc. for $11.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer followed by a short-form merger. The transaction focused on Micromet's proprietary BiTE (bispecific T-cell engager) antibody platform and its lead clinical candidate, blinatumomab, for the treatment of cancer.

## Timeline

- 2011-01-10: Dr. Christian Itin (Micromet CEO) and Dr. Roger Perlmutter (Amgen EVP) meet to discuss collaboration; Amgen expresses interest in blinatumomab.
- 2011-04-05: Amgen introduces the idea of a strategic transaction during collaboration negotiations.
- 2011-07-11: Micromet and Amgen enter into a solid tumor BiTE collaboration and license agreement.
- 2011-07-18: Amgen CEO Kevin Sharer submits a non-binding proposal to acquire Micromet for $9.00 per share in cash.
- 2011-07-26: Micromet Board rejects the $9.00 offer as inadequate.
- 2011-08-18: Parties enter into a confidentiality agreement with a standstill; Amgen conducts limited due diligence.
- 2011-09-01: Amgen reiterates $9.00 per share offer; Micromet Board reaffirms rejection on September 8.
- 2011-10-28: Amgen proposes $9.00 cash plus a $3.00 CVR based on blinatumomab milestones.
- 2011-10-30: Micromet Board rejects the CVR proposal.
- 2011-12-21: Amgen proposes an all-cash offer of $10.75 per share.
- 2012-01-02: Micromet Board counters at $11.00 per share and authorizes outreach to other parties.
- 2012-01-05: Amgen verbally agrees to the $11.00 per share price subject to diligence and documentation.
- 2012-01-23: Micromet Board requests $11.50 per share due to stock price increase; Amgen refuses.
- 2012-01-25: Micromet Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement at $11.00 per share.
- 2012-02-02: Purchaser commences the tender offer.
- 2012-03-01: Initial tender offer period expires with ~83.95% of shares tendered.
- 2012-03-06: Subsequent offering period expires; total shares tendered reach ~88.34%.
- 2012-03-07: Purchaser exercises Top-Up Option to reach 90% ownership and completes short-form merger.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2011, initially focused on a technology collaboration. Amgen first proposed an acquisition at $9.00 per share in July 2011. Micromet's Board, advised by Goldman Sachs and Cooley LLP, rejected multiple $9.00 offers (including one with a $3.00 CVR) as inadequate. In late 2011, Micromet initiated a formal partnering process for blinatumomab, contacting 21 parties. Simultaneously, Goldman Sachs contacted 7 large pharmaceutical companies regarding a potential acquisition; three (Companies B, C, and D) conducted diligence but none submitted proposals. Amgen increased its offer to $10.75 in December 2011, which Micromet countered at $11.00. After a final attempt by Micromet to raise the price to $11.50 in January 2012 was rejected by Amgen, the Board accepted the $11.00 offer. Regulatory clearance was obtained via HSR Act filings in February 2012.

The Micromet Board determined the cash offer provided immediate liquidity and a significant premium (34% over the last trading day and 85% over the six-month average). The Board considered the risks of remaining standalone, including the need to raise $150 million in dilutive capital by 2014 and the inherent clinical/regulatory risks of drug development. Amgen sought to acquire the proprietary BiTE technology and the lead asset blinatumomab to strengthen its oncology pipeline.

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a short-form merger following a tender offer and the exercise of a Top-Up Option. Shares outstanding (92,375,454) are as of January 25, 2012, per the 14D-9. The $1.16 billion total funding requirement mentioned in the 8-K includes the cost of acquiring all shares, options, and warrants.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors and acquisition speculation for Micromet, Inc. (MITI) prior to the official announcement of its acquisition by Amgen on January 26, 2012.

*   **July 2011**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Various (Industry Analysis)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Amgen
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specified
    *   **Details:** Following a major licensing and discovery collaboration agreement between Amgen and Micromet in July 2011, industry analysts began speculating that Micromet was a natural "bolt-on" acquisition target for Amgen. The deal, which involved Micromet's BiTE technology, was seen as a precursor to a full takeover.

*   **Late 2011 – Early January 2012**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Market Speculation / Trading Blogs
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Amgen, Sanofi, or Roche
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Speculation of a premium in the 30%–50% range.
    *   **Details:** Increased trading volume and stock price appreciation led to heightened chatter among biotech investors and on platforms like StockTwits and Seeking Alpha. Speculation focused on Micromet's lead drug candidate, blinatumomab, and the likelihood that a larger partner (specifically Amgen or Sanofi) would move to acquire the company before Phase II data was fully released.

*   **January 25, 2012 (The day before the announcement)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Bloomberg / Market Rumors
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Amgen
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Noted as a "significant premium" over the current trading price ($8.28).
    *   **Details:** Rumors intensified in the final 24 hours before the deal, with reports circulating that Amgen was in advanced talks to purchase the company to bolster its oncology pipeline. Micromet's stock rose significantly in anticipation of a formal announcement.

**Confirmed Deal Announcement:** On January 26, 2012, Amgen officially announced it would acquire Micromet, Inc. for $11.00 per share in cash, totaling approximately $1.16 billion.
