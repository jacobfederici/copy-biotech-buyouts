## Summary

Ultragenyx Pharmaceutical Inc. acquired Dimension Therapeutics, Inc. through a cash tender offer for $6.00 per share, totaling approximately $152.3 million. The acquisition was completed on November 7, 2017, following the expiration of the tender offer, and Dimension Therapeutics became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Ultragenyx.

## Interaction History

The Board of Dimension Therapeutics, Inc. (the 'Company') regularly reviewed strategic opportunities. In December 2016 and January 2017, Party A, a publicly traded biopharmaceutical company, expressed interest in a stock-for-stock business combination with Dimension's CEO, Dr. Annalisa Jenkins. A mutual confidentiality agreement was signed on January 20, 2017. On January 30, 2017, following disappointing DTX101 trial results, the Board concluded Party A's low-premium stock offer was not in the best interest of stockholders and decided to seek a financial advisor to explore strategic alternatives, including a cash sale. Dimension's stock price dropped significantly after the DTX101 announcement on January 31, 2017.

In February 2017, Dimension engaged a Financial Advisor. On February 22, 2017, the Board, with the Financial Advisor, discussed strategic options and decided to privately solicit interest in a cash acquisition from 14 potential acquirers. An ad hoc Strategic Committee was formed to oversee this process. By April 12, 2017, all contacted companies declined interest, citing insufficient value in Dimension's pipeline, and the sale process was terminated. The Board again dismissed Party A's March 15, 2017, stock-for-stock proposal (30% premium to diluted equity value) as not compelling.

Between April 23 and April 28, 2017, Dimension received unsolicited indications of interest from Party B and Party C for all-stock business combinations. On May 11, 2017, REGENXBIO Inc.'s CEO contacted Dr. Jenkins, expressing interest in any strategic process. On May 18, 2017, the Board decided to engage MTS Health Partners L.P. ('MTS') as its financial advisor to evaluate these expressions of interest and other strategic alternatives. On May 25, 2017, REGENXBIO submitted a non-binding proposal for a stock-for-stock acquisition at $1.85 per share (a 50% premium to Dimension's closing price on May 24, 2017), requesting 30-day exclusivity. The Board, with MTS, reviewed various strategic alternatives, including different types of business combinations, and approved contacting five publicly traded companies (Party B, Party C, REGENXBIO, Party D, Party E) and one privately held company. The Board again decided not to contact Party A.

From May 26 to July 6, 2017, MTS contacted these companies, and an additional unsolicited company (Party F). All six entered mutual confidentiality agreements with reciprocal standstill provisions. Most participated in diligence. By July 6, 2017, Dimension received non-binding indications of interest from Party B ($1.41/share implied), Party C ($1.40/share implied + $15M CVR), Party D ($1.46/share implied), and REGENXBIO ($1.85/share implied, 6.3% pro forma equity). Party F and Party E declined interest. On July 12, 2017, the Board decided to drop Party B due to its lower offer and directed MTS to encourage Party C, Party D, and REGENXBIO to increase their offers, setting an August 1, 2017, deadline for second-round bids. A draft merger agreement was provided to these parties on July 17, 2017. By July 28, 2017, Party C and Party D withdrew from the process.

On July 31, 2017, Party B submitted an unsolicited revised proposal at an implied $2.57 per share. On August 1, 2017, REGENXBIO submitted a revised proposal at $1.85 per share, with a revised merger agreement increasing the termination fee to 4%. On August 7, 2017, REGENXBIO further revised its offer to $2.10 per share. On August 9, 2017, the Board, with MTS, discussed the revised proposals from Party B ($2.52/share implied) and REGENXBIO ($2.10/share implied). The Board instructed MTS to request best and final offers by August 14, 2017. Dr. Jenkins also discussed potential equity financing with Stockholder 1.

On August 14, 2017, Party B offered an implied $2.27 per share, and REGENXBIO offered an implied $2.36 per share. On August 15, 2017, the Board favored REGENXBIO's offer and instructed MTS to inform Party B its offer was insufficient. The Board also directed Goodwin to finalize the merger agreement with REGENXBIO, including equity award assumption and FDA actions in the material adverse effect definition. Discussions with Stockholder 1 and Stockholder 2 regarding equity financing continued, with Stockholder 2 conditioning support for a business combination on Dimension contacting additional companies. On August 17, 2017, Party B presented an unsolicited revised proposal at an implied $3.09 per share. The Board, considering Stockholder 2's demand, directed MTS to contact Party A and inform REGENXBIO of the higher unsolicited proposal and stockholder demands. Party A entered a new confidentiality agreement on August 18, 2017, and began diligence.

On August 21, 2017, Stockholder 1 and Stockholder 2 reiterated their request for Dimension to contact additional companies. The Board directed management to contact 12 additional companies, all of whom declined interest. On August 22, 2017, REGENXBIO presented a revised non-binding proposal at an implied $3.25 per share, with a deadline of 5:00 P.M. on August 23, 2017. On August 23, 2017, Party A presented a preliminary non-binding proposal at $3.20 per share upfront plus a $15 million CVR (potentially an additional $0.57 per share), but without a marked merger agreement. Stockholder 1 and Stockholder 2 indicated they would likely support a business combination. The Board instructed management to seek a higher price from REGENXBIO and for Dr. Gemayel to contact REGENXBIO's chairman about Party A's proposal. REGENXBIO's August 22 proposal expired. Party A then indicated it needed more time for diligence and had stopped work on the potential transaction.

On August 24, 2017, the Strategic Committee directed Dr. Gemayel to ask REGENXBIO to reinstate its August 22 proposal and to confirm stockholder support for a REGENXBIO transaction at $3.25 per share. Later that day, REGENXBIO reinstated its proposal at an implied $3.25 per share (based on August 22 closing prices), with an extended deadline. The Board, after considering MTS Securities' oral fairness opinion, unanimously approved the REGENXBIO Merger Agreement, which was then executed. The implied value of the REGENXBIO offer was approximately $3.41 per share based on August 24, 2017, closing prices. The transaction was announced on August 25, 2017.

On September 18, 2017, Ultragenyx Pharmaceutical Inc. made an unsolicited, non-binding proposal to acquire Dimension for $5.50 per share in cash via tender offer ('Ultragenyx September 18 Proposal'). The Board determined this could reasonably lead to a 'Superior Proposal' under the REGENXBIO Merger Agreement. Dimension entered a confidentiality agreement with Ultragenyx and began discussions. On September 29, 2017, Ultragenyx submitted a binding offer for $6.00 per share in cash ('Ultragenyx September 29 Offer'), including a final, signed merger agreement and an offer to pay the $2.85 million termination fee to REGENXBIO. The implied value of the REGENXBIO stock consideration was approximately $5.18 per share as of September 29, 2017. On October 1, 2017, the Board directed management to notify REGENXBIO of its intention to withdraw its recommendation and enter into an agreement with Ultragenyx. REGENXBIO waived its matching rights. On October 2, 2017, the Board unanimously approved the Merger Agreement with Ultragenyx, and MTS Securities rendered an oral fairness opinion. Dimension terminated the REGENXBIO Merger Agreement and executed the Merger Agreement with Ultragenyx. The transaction was announced on October 3, 2017. The tender offer commenced on October 10, 2017, and expired on November 6, 2017. On November 7, 2017, the merger was completed, and Dimension became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Ultragenyx.

## Rationale

The Board's rationale for recommending the offer included the certainty, immediate value, and liquidity provided by the all-cash offer price of $6.00 per share, which represented a significant premium (400% to the closing price on August 24, 2017, and 225% over the 30-day volume weighted average prior to October 2, 2017). The Board considered the long-term risks to Dimension's business, including operational execution risk, evolving industry dynamics, high costs and risks associated with developing, testing, manufacturing, and commercializing clinical-stage products, and the need for significant additional capital that would be dilutive to existing stockholders. The Board believed the value offered was more favorable than remaining an independent public company, especially given the outcome of the DTX101 trial and the reprioritization of Dimension's programs, which indicated a significant capital raise would be required to extend financial resources past mid-2018. The Board also noted the robust arm's-length negotiation process involving 27 different parties and the absence of competing proposals after the REGENXBIO announcement. The tender offer structure was seen as providing a relatively short timeframe for closing, reducing uncertainty and disruption. The business reputation and financial resources of Ultragenyx also supported the conclusion that the transaction could be completed quickly and orderly.

No pre-deal rumors or speculative reports regarding a buyout of Dimension Therapeutics, Inc. (DMTX) were identified in major financial or biotech news outlets prior to the first confirmed deal announcement.

The acquisition process for Dimension Therapeutics was characterized by a public bidding war that began with a definitive agreement, rather than a period of leaked rumors or market speculation. The timeline of the public acquisition events is as follows:

*   **August 25, 2017:** **REGENXBIO Inc.** announced a definitive agreement to acquire Dimension Therapeutics in an all-stock transaction valued at approximately **$3.41 per share** (approx. $86 million). This was the first public disclosure of any acquisition interest.
*   **September 18, 2017:** **Ultragenyx Pharmaceutical Inc.** made an unsolicited, non-binding proposal to acquire Dimension for **$5.50 per share** in cash (approx. $138 million). This was reported by outlets such as *FierceBiotech*, *TheStreet*, and *Endpoints News* as a "surprise" counter-offer.
*   **October 3, 2017:** Dimension Therapeutics officially terminated its agreement with REGENXBIO and entered into a definitive merger agreement with **Ultragenyx** at an increased price of **$6.00 per share** in cash (approx. $151 million).
