## Summary

Amgen Inc. acquired Onyx Pharmaceuticals for $125.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $10.6 billion. The transaction followed a competitive strategic review process initiated after an unsolicited $120.00 per share proposal from Amgen in June 2013. The merger was completed via Section 251(h) of the DGCL, making Onyx a wholly-owned subsidiary of Amgen.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began with an unsolicited $120.00/share bid from Amgen CEO Robert Bradway to Onyx CEO Dr. Anthony Coles in June 2013. Onyx, advised by Centerview Partners and Goodwin Procter, rejected this and contacted 12 other potential strategic acquirors. Six parties entered confidentiality agreements. Strategic Acquiror E initially proposed $125-$130 but withdrew after the DMC recommended the FOCUS study continue to completion. Strategic Acquiror D proposed a complex structured deal ($35 cash + equity) which the Board viewed as having high execution risk and uncertain value. Amgen raised its bid to $130.00 in late July but then demanded unblinded interim data from the FOCUS study. Onyx refused on ethical, regulatory, and scientific grounds. Consequently, Amgen reduced its offer to $121.00 on August 18. Onyx countered with $126.00 plus CVRs. Amgen rejected CVRs but raised the cash price to $125.00 as its 'best and final' offer. The Onyx Board accepted this on August 24, 2013, noting the lack of other all-cash bidders and the certainty of value.

## Rationale

The Onyx Board recommended the deal because the $125.00 price represented a 44% premium to the unaffected share price on June 28, 2013, and provided immediate liquidity and certainty of value. The Board concluded that Amgen's offer was the highest reasonably obtainable after an exhaustive strategic review process where other bidders either withdrew or offered less certain, structured consideration. Amgen sought the acquisition to bolster its oncology portfolio with Kyprolis and leverage Onyx's expertise in proteasome inhibition.

## Evidence

- At a meeting held on August 24, 2013, the Board unanimously: (i) determined that the Merger Agreement, the Offer, the Merger and the other transactions contemplated by the Merger Agreement, are advisable and in the best interests of Onyx and Onyx’s stockholders... (iii) resolved to recommend that Onyx’s stockholders accept the Offer and tender their Shares (0001193125-13-354854).
- Purchaser accepted for payment all such Shares validly tendered and not withdrawn pursuant to the Offer on or prior to the Expiration Date... As a result of the acceptance of such Shares, a change in control of the Company has occurred (0001193125-13-387156).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. Shares outstanding (73,430,031) were sourced from the SC 14D9 as of August 23, 2013. The $10.6B deal value includes the assumption of debt and the cash-out of equity awards and convertible notes. CVRs were discussed during negotiations but ultimately rejected by Amgen.

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, and reported takeover interest for Onyx Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (ONXX) prior to the confirmed deal announcement with Amgen on August 25, 2013.

*   **January 2012**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** ISI Group (Industry Survey)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Not specified (General target)
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not reported
    *   **Details:** Onyx was named the "top biotechnology takeover target for 2012" in an industry survey of investors conducted by ISI Group.

*   **April 25, 2012**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reuters / BioSpace
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Bayer AG
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not reported (Stock rose 9% to a 52-week high of $47.33)
    *   **Details:** Speculation intensified that longtime partner Bayer was "zeroing in on a large acquisition" that could happen within days. Analysts pointed to a recent legal settlement between the two companies as a precursor to a deal.

*   **June 28, 2013**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Financial Post
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Amgen
    *   **Rumored Price:** $120.00 per share
    *   **Details:** Reported that Amgen had made an unsolicited $10 billion bid for Onyx. This report preceded Onyx’s official confirmation of the offer on June 30.

*   **June 30, 2013**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Onyx Pharmaceuticals (Official Press Release) / TheStreet
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Amgen (Confirmed offer), plus "other third parties"
    *   **Rumored Price:** $120.00 per share (Amgen offer)
    *   **Details:** Onyx officially confirmed it rejected Amgen’s $120/share bid as "significantly undervaluing" the company and announced it had authorized financial advisors to contact other potential acquirers who had expressed interest.

*   **July 1–2, 2013**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reuters / Fox Business / FirstWord Pharma
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Pfizer, Novartis, Bayer, Roche, and Celgene
    *   **Rumored Price:** Analysts suggested a deal could reach $140–$150 per share
    *   **Details:** Following the rejection of Amgen, reports surfaced that Pfizer and Novartis were among "several large pharmaceutical companies" showing preliminary interest in bidding for Onyx.

*   **July 16, 2013**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** FirstWord Pharma / Bloomberg
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Amgen, Pfizer, Bristol-Myers Squibb, and Gilead Sciences
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not reported
    *   **Details:** Sources "with knowledge of the matter" stated that approximately five companies, including Pfizer and Amgen, remained interested, while Bristol-Myers Squibb and Gilead had expressed interest but were undecided on making formal offers.

*   **July 18–19, 2013**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reuters / FierceBiotech
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Pfizer (Withdrawing), AstraZeneca, Bristol-Myers Squibb, Gilead
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not reported
    *   **Details:** Reuters reported that Pfizer had decided not to submit a bid for Onyx. The report also noted that AstraZeneca, Bristol-Myers Squibb, and Gilead were likely "turned off" by the high valuation.

*   **August 8, 2013**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reuters / Bloomberg
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Amgen
    *   **Rumored Price:** $130.00 per share
    *   **Details:** Reports circulated that Amgen had sweetened its bid to $130 per share to drive out other bidders and that a deal could be reached within days.

*   **August 12–15, 2013**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Various (Bloomberg/Reuters)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Amgen
    *   **Rumored Price:** $130.00 per share
    *   **Details:** Speculation grew that the deal was stalled due to Amgen’s demand to see data from Onyx’s "FOCUS" clinical trial for Kyprolis.

*   **August 24, 2013**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** The New York Times
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Amgen
    *   **Rumored Price:** $125.00 per share
    *   **Details:** Reported that Amgen was nearing a deal to acquire Onyx for approximately $125 per share, slightly lower than the previously rumored $130 bid, following the completion of due diligence.
