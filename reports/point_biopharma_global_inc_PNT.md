## Summary

POINT Biopharma Global Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Eli Lilly and Company for $12.50 per share in cash, representing an approximately 87% premium to the closing price prior to the announcement. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, aimed at expanding Lilly's oncology portfolio into the radiopharmaceutical space.

## Timeline

- 2023-06-15: POINT and Lilly enter into a confidentiality agreement.
- 2023-08-24: Lilly delivers a non-binding written indication of interest to acquire POINT for $12.00 per share.
- 2023-08-25: POINT Board meets to discuss Lilly's proposal and directs Centerview to contact other potential parties (Company A and Company B).
- 2023-09-13: Company B submits a proposal for $13.00 upfront plus $7.00 in CVRs, contingent on SPLASH trial results.

- 2023-10-13: Lilly commences the tender offer.
- 2023-12-22: Tender offer expires with approximately 67.97% of shares tendered.
- 2023-12-27: Merger completed; POINT becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of Lilly.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest in August 2023 when Jacob Van Naarden (Loxo@Lilly) delivered a $12.00 per share proposal to Joe McCann (POINT CEO). The POINT Board, advised by Centerview Partners and Skadden Arps, engaged in a market check, contacting Company A (who declined) and Company B. Company B submitted several highly contingent proposals involving CVRs and stock consideration, which the POINT Board viewed as having insufficient upfront value and excessive complexity regarding the SPLASH trial read-out conditions. On September 19, Lilly increased its offer to $12.50. POINT attempted to counter at $13.00 on September 29, but Lilly indicated $12.50 was their limit. The Board ultimately favored Lilly's all-cash certainty over Company B's contingent mixed-consideration offers. Regulatory clearance involved HSR Act filings and a specific NRC Condition regarding the transfer of control of radioactive materials licenses, which was satisfied prior to closing.

## Rationale

The POINT Board determined the transaction was superior to continuing as a standalone company due to the significant cash premium, the risks associated with the upcoming SPLASH trial data readout, and the substantial capital required for commercial scale-up. Lilly sought the acquisition to establish a leading platform in radiopharmaceuticals, acquiring POINT's specialized manufacturing facility in Indianapolis and its late-stage pipeline.

## Decision Notes

The deal value is approximately $1.4 billion on a fully diluted basis. Shares outstanding (105,831,891) were sourced from the 14D-9 filing as of October 9, 2023. The transaction included a specific closing condition for NRC approval due to the nature of radiopharmaceutical manufacturing.
