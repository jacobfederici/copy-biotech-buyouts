## Summary

Provention Bio, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Sanofi S.A. on March 12, 2023, under which Sanofi's subsidiary, Zest Acquisition Sub, Inc., launched a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares of Provention for $25.00 per share in cash. The transaction, valued at approximately $2.9 billion on an equity basis, was completed on April 27, 2023, following the successful tender of approximately 73% of outstanding shares and a subsequent merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL.

- 2022-02-11: Parties execute a Confidentiality Agreement to facilitate due diligence on teplizumab.

- 2023-04-27: Merger completed; Provention becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of Sanofi.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2021 focused on a partnership for teplizumab. In October 2022, the parties signed a Co-Promotion Agreement giving Sanofi a Right of First Negotiation (ROFN). In early 2023, Sanofi's interest shifted toward a full acquisition. On February 14, 2023, Sanofi's Loic Gonnet and Olivier Charmeil proposed $21.00 per share. Provention's CEO Ashleigh Palmer and Board Chair Wayne Pisano, advised by Centerview and BofA Securities, countered at $25.00 on February 20. Sanofi accepted the $25.00 price on February 22, subject to exclusivity through March 13. During the exclusivity period, legal counsel (Ropes & Gray for Provention; Weil for Sanofi) negotiated the merger agreement, specifically focusing on the termination fee (settled at $100M) and a reverse termination fee for antitrust failure (settled at $158M). The Provention Board reviewed management forecasts and fairness opinions before unanimously approving the deal on March 12, 2023.

## Rationale

The Provention Board determined the $25.00 cash price provided immediate, certain value at a substantial premium (273% over the unaffected price). They considered the significant execution risks of remaining independent, including the capital-intensive nature of a commercial launch for TZIELD, the risk of unfavorable results from the ongoing PROTECT Phase 3 study, and the need for future dilutive equity raises. Sanofi sought to bolster its General Medicines and immunology portfolio through the acquisition of a first-in-class T1D therapy.

## Evidence

- The Company Board, by unanimous vote at a meeting on March 12, 2023, (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions, including the Offer and the Merger, are advisable and fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company and its stockholders (0001193125-23-078544).
- On April 27, 2023, following consummation of the Offer, Purchaser merged with and into the Company (the 'Merger'), with the Company as the surviving corporation (0001493152-23-013783).

## Decision Notes

The deal value of $2.9B is the stated equity value in the filings. Shares outstanding (94,705,874) are as of March 17, 2023, per the 14D-9. The transaction utilized Section 251(h) of the DGCL to bypass a shareholder vote.
