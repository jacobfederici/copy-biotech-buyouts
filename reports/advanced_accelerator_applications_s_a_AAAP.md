## Summary

Advanced Accelerator Applications S.A. (AAA) entered into a Memorandum of Understanding with Novartis AG for a cash tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares and ADSs for $41.00 per share and $82.00 per ADS, representing a total equity value of approximately $3.9 billion. The deal focused on AAA's lead radiotherapeutic product, Lutathera, and its radiopharmaceutical pipeline, providing Novartis with a specialized oncology platform.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began with an unsolicited outreach by Pascal Touchon (Novartis) to Stefano Buono (AAA) in July 2017. Novartis initially proposed $62.50 per ADS, which the AAA Board, advised by Jefferies and Davis Polk, rejected as insufficient. Following a management presentation in Geneva and site visits to facilities in Italy, the Netherlands, and New Jersey, Novartis raised its offer to $75.00 in September and eventually to a 'best and final' $82.00 per ADS in October. AAA's Transaction Committee, including Claudio Costamagna and François Nader, pushed for higher value, initially countering at $82.50. The parties negotiated deal protections, including a $115 million termination fee and an 80% minimum tender condition. One other party, 'Party A', made an unsolicited inquiry following media leaks but was deemed by AAA management to lack the financial capacity and strategic focus to compete.

## Rationale

The AAA Board recommended the deal because the $82.00 per ADS price provided a significant premium (123.3% over the 90-day VWAP) and immediate liquidity. They noted the risks of remaining independent, including the costs of building a commercial infrastructure for Lutathera. Novartis sought to acquire AAA to integrate its radiopharmaceutical platform and international commercial infrastructure to support the launch of Lutathera and other pipeline candidates.
