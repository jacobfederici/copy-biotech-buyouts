## Summary

Gilead Sciences, Inc. acquired Forty Seven, Inc. for $95.50 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $4.9 billion. The transaction focused on Forty Seven's lead clinical candidate, magrolimab, a first-in-class antibody targeting CD47 for the treatment of several cancers, including myelodysplastic syndrome and acute myeloid leukemia.

## Timeline

- 2018-06-22: Forty Seven enters into a mutual confidentiality agreement with Party X.
- 2019-12-09: Forty Seven announces updated clinical data for magrolimab in MDS and AML, leading to a 111% stock price increase.
- 2020-01-07: Forty Seven and Gilead enter into a mutual confidentiality agreement.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in summer 2018 as partnership discussions with Gilead, Party X, Party Y, and Party Z. Following positive magrolimab data in Dec 2019, Gilead pivoted to acquisition interest on Jan 31, 2020. Gilead's initial bid of $57.50 (Feb 10) was countered by Forty Seven's board, leading to a series of increases to $64.50 (Feb 23), $66.00 (Feb 25), and $77.00 (Feb 28). A competitive dynamic emerged when Party X bid $79.00 (mixed consideration) on Feb 27. Forty Seven's board, led by CEO Mark McCamish and advised by Centerview Partners, pushed for 'best and final' offers by March 1. Gilead responded with a significant jump to $95.50 cash. Party X requested more time (until March 3) and did not provide a specific final value. The board determined the risk of losing Gilead's certain cash offer outweighed waiting for Party X's uncertain proposal and approved the Gilead transaction on March 1, 2020.

## Rationale

The Forty Seven board determined that the $95.50 cash price provided immediate and certain value that outweighed the risks of remaining standalone, including clinical development, regulatory approval, and the need for significant future capital raises. Gilead sought to strengthen its immuno-oncology portfolio by acquiring magrolimab, which showed high activity in MDS and AML patients.

## Evidence

- At a meeting of the Company Board held on March 1, 2020, the Company Board unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions, including the Offer and the Merger, are fair to, and in the best interest of, the Company and its stockholders (0001193125-20-068151).
- On April 7, 2020, following consummation of the Offer, Purchaser merged with and into the Company (0001104659-20-043980).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under DGCL 251(h). Shares outstanding (48,151,481) were sourced from the SC 14D9 as of Feb 29, 2020. The $4.9B deal value includes the impact of outstanding options and equity awards.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors and acquisition speculation regarding Forty Seven, Inc. (FTSV) prior to the official deal announcement on March 2, 2020.

*   **February 27, 2020**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Bloomberg News
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Gilead Sciences, Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specified in the initial report, though the report noted Forty Seven had a market value of approximately $2.3 billion at the time.
    *   **Details:** Bloomberg reported that Gilead Sciences had made a takeover approach to Forty Seven. The report stated that Gilead was exploring several options, including a potential partnership or a full acquisition. It also noted that Forty Seven had received interest from other "potential suitors." Following this report, Forty Seven's stock price surged approximately 20–30% in after-hours trading.

*   **February 28, 2020**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Various (MarketWatch, Investor's Business Daily, The Street, NASDAQ)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Gilead Sciences, Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Speculation by some investors and analysts suggested a potential valuation of approximately **$3 billion**.
    *   **Details:** Following the Bloomberg report, multiple financial outlets covered the "rumored Gilead takeover offer." Reports highlighted that the interest was likely driven by Forty Seven's promising clinical data for its lead drug, magrolimab, presented in late 2019. Analysts noted that Gilead was under pressure to expand its oncology pipeline following its previous acquisition of Kite Pharma.

*   **March 1, 2020 (Eve of Announcement)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** The Wall Street Journal / Bloomberg (Updates)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Gilead Sciences, Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Reports indicated a deal was imminent at a significant premium to the current trading price.
    *   **Details:** Reports circulated that Gilead was in "advanced talks" to acquire Forty Seven and that a deal could be announced as early as Monday, March 2. The speculation shifted from a "potential approach" to a "near-certain deal."

**Confirmed Deal Announcement:** On **March 2, 2020**, Gilead Sciences and Forty Seven, Inc. officially announced a definitive agreement for Gilead to acquire Forty Seven for **$95.50 per share** in cash, valuing the company at approximately **$4.9 billion**.
