## Summary

USANA Health Sciences, Inc. is the subject of a cash tender offer by its majority shareholder, Gull Holdings, Ltd., to acquire all outstanding shares not already owned by the offer participants for $26.00 per share. The offer is led by Dr. Myron W. Wentz, the company's founder, Chairman, and CEO, who already controls approximately 67.9% of the company's common stock. The transaction is structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger to take the company private.

## Timeline

- 2007-06-01: Dr. Myron Wentz informs the Board he is exploring potential going-private transactions and requests confidential information.
- 2008-05-12: Dr. Wentz delivers a formal proposal to the Board to acquire all public shares for $26.00 per share in cash.
- 2008-05-13: Offer Participants issue a press release announcing the intent to conduct a tender offer.
- 2008-05-16: USANA Board appoints a Special Committee of independent directors (Robert Anciaux, Jerry McClain, and Ronald Poelman) to evaluate the offer.

- 2008-05-20: USANA issues a press release announcing the formation of the Special Committee.
- 2008-06-02: Unity Acquisition Corp. formally commences the tender offer and files Schedule TO.
- 2008-06-02: Special Committee engages McColl Partners, LLC as financial advisor.
- 2008-06-10: Special Committee meets with advisors to review preliminary financial analysis and interviews CFO Gilbert Fuller regarding projections.
- 2008-06-12: Class action lawsuit (Earp vs. Wentz et al.) is served to the company alleging breach of fiduciary duty.
- 2008-06-13: Special Committee files Schedule 14D-9 stating it is unable to take a position at this time and advises shareholders to defer action.

## Interaction History

The transaction was initiated by Dr. Myron Wentz, the company's founder and majority owner, who expressed interest in a going-private deal in June 2007. On May 12, 2008, Gull Holdings, Ltd. submitted a formal letter proposing a $26.00 per share cash offer, representing a 24.8% premium over the previous day's close. The Board formed a Special Committee of independent directors (Anciaux, McClain, Poelman) to evaluate the proposal. The Special Committee interviewed four investment banks before selecting McColl Partners on June 2, 2008. During June 2008, the Special Committee conducted due diligence, reviewed financial projections with CFO Gilbert Fuller, and evaluated the terms of the offer. As of June 13, 2008, the Special Committee had not reached a conclusion on the fairness of the price and requested shareholders take no action while they continued their evaluation.
