## Summary

Alexion Pharmaceuticals acquired Portola Pharmaceuticals for $18.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $1.41 billion. The acquisition was designed to diversify Alexion's portfolio by adding Andexxa, the first and only approved Factor Xa inhibitor reversal agent, which fits Alexion's expertise in critical care and rare diseases.

## Timeline

- 2019-08-27: Portola and Alexion enter into a commercial confidentiality agreement regarding a potential license of assets.
- 2019-12-13: Alexion CFO Aradhana Sarin sends a letter to Portola CEO Scott Garland expressing interest in a potential acquisition.
- 2020-01-09: Portola preannounces Q4 2019 Andexxa revenues significantly below analyst expectations; stock price drops from $24.74 to $14.76.
- 2020-02-27: Alexion makes a non-binding proposal to acquire Portola for $18.00 per share in cash.
- 2020-03-30: Alexion revises proposal to $16.00 cash plus a $4.00 CVR for full Andexxa approval, labeling it 'best and final'.
- 2020-04-01: Alexion returns to an all-cash offer of $18.00 per share in exchange for exclusivity.
- 2020-04-04: Portola and Alexion enter into a new Confidentiality Agreement with a standstill and exclusivity through May 6, 2020.
- 2020-05-04: Portola Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement and Centerview Partners renders a fairness opinion.
- 2020-05-05: Transaction is publicly announced.
- 2020-05-27: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2020-07-01: Tender offer expires with 79.7% of shares tendered.
- 2020-07-02: Merger completed via Section 251(h) of the DGCL.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2019 following failed licensing talks for Ondexxya (EU name for Andexxa). On December 13, 2019, Alexion CFO Aradhana Sarin expressed interest in an acquisition. Following a significant drop in Portola's stock price in January 2020 due to poor earnings, Alexion CEO Ludwig Hantson proposed $18.00 per share on February 27, 2020. Portola's board initially viewed this as inadequate and briefly contacted one other party on March 3, 2020, who declined interest. As the COVID-19 pandemic intensified, Alexion lowered its offer on March 30 to $16.00 plus a $4.00 CVR. Portola CEO Scott Garland pushed back, preferring all-cash certainty. Alexion restored the $18.00 all-cash bid on April 1, 2020, contingent on exclusivity. Due diligence proceeded through April, and the deal was finalized on May 4, 2020. Regulatory clearances were obtained from the FTC/DOJ (HSR) and German FCO by May 20, 2020, and Austrian authorities by June 12, 2020.

## Rationale

The Portola Board determined the deal was superior to a standalone strategy given the risks of commercializing Andexxa during the COVID-19 pandemic, which reduced hospital visits and utilization. The board also noted Portola's near-term need for highly dilutive capital raises. Alexion sought to leverage its existing commercial infrastructure in hematology and critical care to accelerate Andexxa's adoption.

## Evidence

- At a meeting held on May 4, 2020, after careful consideration, the Portola Board, among other things, unanimously: determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions, including the Offer and the Merger, are advisable, fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company and the Company's stockholders (0001193125-20-152745).
- On July 2, 2020, following consummation of the Offer, Purchaser merged with and into Portola (the 'Merger'), with Portola surviving as a direct, wholly owned subsidiary of Alexion (0001104659-20-080238).
