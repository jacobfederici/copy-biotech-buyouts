## Summary

Deciphera Pharmaceuticals, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by ONO Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd. for $25.60 per share in cash, representing an equity value of approximately $2.4 billion. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The deal provides ONO with Deciphera's commercialized product QINLOCK and its clinical-stage pipeline, including vimseltinib and DCC-3116, leveraging Deciphera's switch-control kinase inhibitor platform.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began following a long-standing business development relationship between Deciphera and ONO. On February 13, 2024, ONO's Chairman Gyo Sagara met with Deciphera CEO Steven Hoerter to propose an acquisition at $22.50 per share. The Deciphera Board, advised by J.P. Morgan and Goodwin Procter, rejected this as inadequate. ONO raised its bid to $25.00 on March 1, 2024. Deciphera subsequently conducted a targeted market check, contacting four other parties (Parties A, B, C, and D). Only Party A engaged, eventually offering $19.00 per share on April 12, which was significantly lower than ONO's bid and contingent on debt financing. On March 28, ONO raised its bid to $25.50, characterizing it as final. Deciphera pushed for a formal bidding process, leading to a final round where ONO initially held at $25.50 on April 26. Deciphera counter-offered at $26.00, and ONO responded on April 27 with a 'last, best and final' offer of $25.60. The Board approved this final price on April 28, 2024, noting the 75% premium over the unaffected closing price. Regulatory clearance was obtained via HSR filing on May 6, 2024, with the waiting period expiring prior to the offer completion.

## Rationale

The Deciphera Board determined the deal was in the best interests of stockholders due to the significant cash premium (75% over the unaffected price), the certainty of value compared to the risks of standalone commercialization, and the need for substantial future capital to fund its pipeline. ONO sought the acquisition to expand its oncology portfolio and leverage Deciphera's proprietary kinase inhibitor platform and R&D capabilities in the US and Europe.
