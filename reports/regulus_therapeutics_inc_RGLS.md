## Summary

Novartis AG acquired Regulus Therapeutics Inc. for $7.00 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) of $7.00 per share payable upon FDA approval of farabursen (RGLS 8429) for the treatment of autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease (ADPKD) by December 31, 2034. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL.

## Interaction History

Following positive clinical data in mid-2024, Regulus engaged Evercore to conduct a broad outreach to 22 potential counterparties. Initial interest focused on licensing and co-development from Parties A, B, C, and D. In March 2025, Party A shifted to an acquisition proposal of $2.50 per share. Novartis entered the process in April 2025 with an initial bid of $3.00 cash plus a $1.50 CVR. A competitive bidding war ensued between Party A and Novartis throughout April. Novartis aggressively increased its bid from $3.00 to $6.00, then $6.50, and finally a 'best and final' offer of $7.00 cash plus a $7.00 CVR on April 28. Party A's final verbal indication was $5.50 cash plus $8.00 in CVRs, but it did not submit this in writing, sticking to its $5.00/$5.00 proposal. The Regulus Board, advised by Evercore and Latham & Watkins, determined the Novartis offer provided the highest certain value and superior CVR terms (regulatory approval vs. sales milestones).

## Rationale

The Regulus Board recommended the deal because it provided the highest value reasonably obtainable, representing a 108% premium to the unaffected stock price. The Board cited the risks of remaining independent, including the need for highly dilutive future financing (~$750M) to fund Phase 3 trials and commercialization. Novartis's reputation and resources were viewed as increasing the likelihood of achieving the CVR milestone.
