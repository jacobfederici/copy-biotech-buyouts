## Summary

BioDelivery Sciences International Inc., a Raleigh-based specialty pharmaceutical company with commercial and clinical stage pain and neurology products, was acquired by Collegium Pharmaceuticals Inc. in an all-cash tender offer and merger valued at $5.60 per share. The tender offer commenced February 18, 2022 and the merger was completed March 22, 2022. The transaction followed a competitive process in which Collegium incrementally increased its offer from an initial $4.60 per share on January 12 to the final $5.60 per share agreed on February 4, 2022. Two other potential acquirers (identified as Party A and Party C in the filings) also expressed interest but did not match Collegium's final offer. The Board of BioDelivery Sciences determined the transaction was in the best interest of shareholders, citing both the robust negotiation process and Collegium's financial resources and ability to close quickly.

## Interaction History

The transaction process was initiated by Collegium on December 22, 2021 when Joseph Ciaffoni, President and CEO of Collegium, called Jeff Bailey, CEO of BioDelivery, to explore a potential strategic transaction. Following preliminary discussions, the parties executed a mutual confidentiality agreement on December 29, 2021. Initial legal due diligence discussions between the companies' management and IP counsel occurred on January 5, 2022. On January 6, Ciaffoni called Bailey to indicate that Collegium was prepared to submit a formal proposal, though no specific terms were discussed at that time.

Throughout early and mid-January 2022, the BioDelivery Board actively pursued a competitive process with the assistance of its financial advisor Moelis and legal counsel Goodwin Procter. The Board directed outreach to multiple potential acquirers identified as Party A, Party B, and Party C. On January 12, Collegium submitted its initial written non-binding proposal at $4.60 per share, which the BioDelivery Board and Transaction Committee rejected as insufficient after reviewing Moelis' analysis on January 12 and January 25 board meetings. The Board authorized Moelis to indicate the Company would only negotiate exclusively if offered at least $5.60 per share and if Collegium provided clarity on material adverse effect carve-outs, financing, and acceptable exclusivity terms.

During the competitive period from January 12 to early February, the Board was informed of interest from multiple parties. Party A submitted a written proposal on January 31 at $5.00 per share all-cash, followed by a second written proposal on February 4 at $5.25 per share ($2.05 cash / $3.20 stock). Party C indicated ongoing interest in an all-stock merger of equals structure but did not submit a formal proposal. Party B explicitly declined to pursue a transaction on February 3, stating it would reconsider in one to two months.

On February 4, after Collegium addressed the Board's conditions regarding Material Adverse Effect definition and financing certainty, Collegium submitted its revised proposal at $5.60 per share all-cash. The Board authorized management to enter into exclusive negotiations with Collegium that day. Between February 4 and February 14, the Company and Collegium (with their respective counsel and financial advisors) negotiated the definitive Merger Agreement, with particular focus on the definition of Material Adverse Effect to include protections for regulatory and clinical events affecting BioDelivery's products, the reverse termination fee structure, and the HSR extension period. Representatives of Goodwin (BioDelivery) and Troutman Pepper Hamilton Sanders LLP (Collegium) exchanged multiple drafts and held telephonic discussions to resolve the remaining issues.

On February 13, the Board held two meetings. At the first meeting, representatives of Goodwin and Moelis summarized the remaining open issues in the merger agreement. At a second meeting later that day, Goodwin reviewed the Board's fiduciary duties in the context of a sale transaction, and Moelis rendered its fairness opinion concluding that the $5.60 per share consideration was fair from a financial point of view to BioDelivery shareholders. The Board unanimously approved the Merger Agreement at the conclusion of the second meeting on February 13, and the parties executed the agreement in the early morning hours of February 14, 2022.

Before the stock market open on February 14, BioDelivery and Collegium each issued a press release announcing the Merger Agreement and forthcoming tender offer. The tender offer commenced on February 18, 2022 and was scheduled to expire at midnight Eastern Time on March 18, 2022 (20 business days from commencement). The offer was oversubscribed and the tender was completed, with Purchaser announcing acceptance for payment on March 21, 2022. Following the consummation of the tender offer and satisfaction of remaining conditions, the merger was completed on March 22, 2022, with BioDelivery becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Collegium.

## Pipeline

BioDelivery Sciences operated a portfolio of specialty pharmaceutical products and programs focused on pain management and neurology. As of the Merger Agreement date (February 14, 2022), the Company's pipeline consisted of the following:

| Asset / program | Modality / type | Target / mechanism | Disease / indication | Stage | Rights / partner | Deal relevance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BELBUCA | Buccal film (opioid) | mu-opioid receptor agonist | Chronic pain / opioid | Approved / commercial | Owned | Core commercial product with market exclusivity vs Alvogen ANDA filer through 2032 (patent litigation favorable ruling); strong revenue contributor |
| BUNAVAIL | Buccal film (opioid/naloxone combination) | mu-opioid receptor agonist / naloxone antagonist | Opioid use disorder | Approved / commercial | Owned; previously licensed to Collegium 2016-2017, reacquired December 2017 | Commercial product; previously partnered with Collegium |
| ONSOLIS (FENTANYL TRANSMUCOSAL SYSTEM) | Oral transmucosal film (opioid) | mu-opioid receptor agonist | Breakthrough cancer pain | Approved / commercial | Licensed from specialty pharma partner; previously licensed to Collegium 2016-2017, reacquired December 2017 | Commercial product; previously partnered with Collegium; patent infringement litigation history vs MonoSol Rx |
| ELYXYB | Oral transmucosal spray (NSAID) | Selective COX-2 inhibitor | Acute migraine | Clinical / Phase 3 (late-stage, upcoming launch in 2022) | Owned | Key upcoming launch; Company and Collegium discussed ELYXYB commercialization plans in late January diligence meetings; Moelis performed separate risk-adjusted valuation of ELYXYB |
| Not disclosed | Not disclosed | Not disclosed | Hemophilia A indication | Clinical / early-stage | Not disclosed | Mentioned in Board rationale as therapeutic area attracting buyer interest |

The Company described itself as a commercial-stage specialty pharmaceutical company dedicated to patients living with chronic conditions. The Board and advisors' materials emphasized the significant competitive risks and costs associated with commercializing pain products in the opioid space, noting the number of competitors in the market. The key strategic attraction of the transaction to Collegium appeared to be the combination of BELBUCA (with secured market exclusivity through 2032), ONSOLIS, and BUNAVAIL with Collegium's existing opioid franchise, as well as the anticipated launch of ELYXYB for acute migraine. The Board considered that BioDelivery would require additional capital to complete clinical development for certain product candidates and potentially commercialize them, and that future fundraising could be highly dilutive to stockholders.

## Rationale

The Board considered multiple factors in determining that the all-cash transaction at $5.60 per share was in the best interest of BioDelivery Sciences and its shareholders:

**Strategic Fit and Buyer Capability**: The Board considered Collegium's business reputation, the substantial financial resources of Collegium and Purchaser, and their capability to complete the transaction relatively quickly and in an orderly manner. The Board noted that Collegium and BioDelivery had prior business relationships through the licensing and reacquisition of ONSOLIS and BUNAVAIL, and that Collegium understood the Company's pipeline and products.

**Operating and Financial Challenges**: The Board reviewed the Company's operating and financial performance and prospects, including the significant competitive risks and considerable costs associated with successfully commercializing and expanding the Company's pain products in a crowded opioid market. The Board considered that the Company may require additional capital to complete remaining clinical development and commercialization of product candidates (such as ELYXYB), and that future fundraising could be highly dilutive to existing shareholders or available only on unfavorable terms.

**Robust Negotiation Process**: The Board viewed the transaction terms as the result of robust arm's-length negotiations. Collegium's initial offer of $4.60 per share on January 12, 2022 was negotiated upward to $5.60 per share by February 4, 2022—a 55% increase. The Board engaged multiple potential bidders (Party A, Party B, and Party C) and instructed Moelis to solicit competing interest; Party A submitted a mixed-consideration proposal at $5.25 per share, but no other bidder matched Collegium's terms. The exclusivity period was time-limited (ten days from February 4, with extension to February 14 if financing commitment received), and the Company retained the ability to consider unsolicited superior proposals subject to matching rights for Collegium.

**Certainty of Consideration and Timing**: The all-cash consideration provided certainty of value and immediate liquidity for shareholders, allowing them to realize value while avoiding ongoing regulatory, commercialization, and business execution risks. The tender offer structure and Section 251(h) merger mechanics supported anticipated completion in a relatively short timeframe (completed March 22, 2022, just over a month after announcement).

**Merger Agreement Protections**: The Board noted that the Merger Agreement included customary protections for the Company's shareholders, including: (1) exclusions of certain regulatory and clinical events from the Material Adverse Effect definition, protecting the Company from unforeseen product or regulatory issues; (2) limited conditions to closing; (3) a reverse termination fee payable by Collegium ($18.1M, per SEC disclosures) in certain circumstances, supporting deal certainty; (4) the Company's ability to terminate and accept a superior proposal subject to a matching right; and (5) a reasonable termination fee of $18.1M payable by the Company in specified circumstances, which the Board believed would not likely preclude competing bids.

**Fairness Opinion**: The Board received a fairness opinion from its financial advisor, Moelis & Company LLC, dated February 13, 2022, concluding that the $5.60 per share consideration was fair from a financial point of view to BioDelivery Sciences shareholders (other than excluded shareholders).

**Countervailing Risks**: The Board acknowledged several risks: shareholders would not participate in future value from a standalone strategy; the announcement could disrupt business relationships, employee retention, and partner relations; the Company was bound by exclusivity and termination fee obligations; if the transaction failed, the Company would have incurred substantial costs and distraction; operating restrictions under the Merger Agreement could limit business opportunities; and the consideration would be taxable to shareholders.
