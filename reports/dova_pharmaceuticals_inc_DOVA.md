## Summary

Dova Pharmaceuticals was acquired by Swedish Orphan Biovitrum AB (Sobi) via a tender offer for $27.50 per share in cash plus a $1.50 contingent value right. The transaction focused on Dova's lead product Doptelet (avatrombopag), particularly its potential in Chemotherapy-Induced Thrombocytopenia (CIT). The deal was completed on November 12, 2019, following a tender of approximately 91.2% of outstanding shares.

## Timeline

- 2018-01-15: Dova and Sobi enter a mutual commercial confidentiality agreement regarding Doptelet licensing in Europe.
- 2019-06-26: FDA approves Doptelet for the treatment of chronic immune thrombocytopenia (ITP).
- 2019-08-14: Sobi CEO Guido Oelkers meets with Dova CEO David Zaccardelli and expresses interest in an acquisition.
- 2019-08-15: Sobi submits a non-binding proposal to acquire Dova for $23.00 per share in cash.
- 2019-08-19: Dova and Sobi enter a transaction-specific confidentiality agreement with a standstill provision.
- 2019-08-25: Dova distributes a process letter to Sobi and other interested parties (Party C and Party D) requesting indications of interest by September 30.
- 2019-09-26: Sobi verbally increases its offer to $27.50 per share in cash plus a $1.50 CVR.
- 2019-09-29: Dova Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement and CVR Agreement.
- 2019-09-30: Merger Agreement is executed and publicly announced.
- 2019-10-11: Purchaser commences the tender offer.
- 2019-11-08: Tender offer expires at 11:59 p.m. ET.
- 2019-11-12: Merger completed; Dova becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Sobi.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2018 as licensing discussions for Doptelet in Europe. Following FDA approval of Doptelet for ITP in June 2019, Dova's board authorized a broader strategic review. Sobi expressed acquisition interest on August 14, 2019, initially offering $23.00 per share. Dova's financial advisors, Jefferies and Evercore, contacted 28 potential parties. Five parties (Sobi, Party A, Party B, Party C, and Party D) showed varying levels of interest. Party C expressed interest but only at a low premium; Party D and Party E eventually declined to proceed. On September 26, 2019, Sobi's CEO Guido Oelkers met with Dova Chairman Paul Manning and Sean Stalfort, increasing the offer to $27.50 plus a $1.50 CVR. The Dova board determined this was the best obtainable offer. Support agreements were secured from Manning and Stalfort, representing approximately 53% of outstanding shares.

## Rationale

The Dova board determined the offer provided compelling value and immediate liquidity compared to the risks of remaining standalone, including the need for dilutive future financing and the execution risks of commercializing Doptelet for ITP and developing it for CIT. Sobi sought to expand its hematology portfolio and leverage its global infrastructure to commercialize Doptelet.

## Evidence

- At a meeting held on September 29, 2019, after careful consideration, the Dova Board, among other things, unanimously: determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions, including the Offer and the Merger are advisable and fair to, and in the best interest of, Dova and its stockholders (SC 14D9).
- On November 12, 2019, Swedish Orphan Biovitrum AB (publ)... completed the previously announced acquisition of Dova Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (8-K, 2019-11-12).

## Decision Notes

The CVR is tied to a single milestone: FDA approval of avatrombopag for CIT. Shares outstanding (28,841,998) taken from the SC 14D9 'Securities' section as of October 7, 2019. The $868M deal value cited in the 8-K includes the payoff of Dova's debt and equity award settlements.
