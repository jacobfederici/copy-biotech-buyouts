## Summary

bluebird bio, Inc. was acquired by Beacon Parent Holdings, L.P., an entity backed by The Carlyle Group and SK Capital Partners, via a cash tender offer. Stockholders were initially offered $3.00 per share plus a $6.84 CVR, which was later amended to include an alternative $5.00 all-cash election. The transaction was driven by bluebird's critical liquidity position and risk of bankruptcy, ultimately providing a path to sustain its gene therapy portfolio including ZYNTEGLO, SKYSONA, and LYFGENIA.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2024 as bluebird bio faced a severe cash runway crisis. PJC (David Meek) initially proposed a $100M deal, which evolved into a per-share offer. Parallel outreach to over 100 financing sources failed to yield a lead investor. Party A proposed a bankruptcy-contingent asset purchase that would leave stockholders with zero recovery. PJC's offer was significantly reduced in December 2024 after diligence revealed higher-than-expected deferred payables. The deal was nearly derailed in early February 2025 as Carlyle sought internal approvals, but was saved when SK Capital joined as a co-investor. Following the initial merger announcement, an unsolicited bid from Ayrmid Ltd prompted an amendment to the deal terms on May 13, 2025, introducing a $5.00 all-cash election to compete with the new proposal.

## Rationale

The bluebird Board determined the transaction was the best available alternative to bankruptcy or liquidation. Management's liquidation analysis suggested that a wind-down would result in $0 recovery for stockholders after satisfying the Hercules Capital debt. The deal provided immediate liquidity and a potential upside via the CVR, while securing the necessary consent from the company's secured lender to maintain existing debt facilities.

## Evidence

- At a meeting of the Company Board held on February 20, 2025, the Company Board, among other things, unanimously: (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereby... are advisable, fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company and its stockholders (0001193125-25-031354).

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition proposals, and takeover speculation for bluebird bio, Inc. (BLUE) that occurred before the finalization of its acquisition.

*   **November 2024**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Endpoints News* (reported retrospectively via SEC filings on March 7, 2025)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** PJ Carlin & Co. (an entity backed by Carlyle and SK Capital)
    *   **Rumored Price:** $100 million upfront in cash, plus $60 million in potential future payments.
    *   **Details:** The company received an initial private offer to be acquired as its financial situation began to deteriorate following a failed private offering in September.

*   **December 2024**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Endpoints News* (reported retrospectively via SEC filings on March 7, 2025)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Undisclosed "Party A"
    *   **Rumored Price:** Up to $25 million in cash.
    *   **Details:** An undisclosed party expressed interest in acquiring the company’s assets and assuming certain liabilities, but only on the condition that bluebird bio first filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy.

*   **December 27, 2024**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Endpoints News* (reported retrospectively via SEC filings on March 7, 2025)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** PJ Carlin & Co. (Carlyle and SK Capital)
    *   **Rumored Price:** $3.00 per share upfront, plus a $6.00 per share Contingent Value Right (CVR).
    *   **Details:** Following due diligence on bluebird’s 2024 financial projections and deferred accounts payable, the Carlyle-backed entity drastically reduced its initial November offer.

*   **February 21, 2025 (Confirmed Deal Announcement)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Business Wire*, *Reuters*, *Fierce Pharma*
    *   **Acquirer:** Carlyle Group and SK Capital
    *   **Price:** $3.00 per share in cash plus a CVR of $6.84 per share (tied to $600 million in net sales by end of 2027).
    *   **Details:** Bluebird bio officially announced it had entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired and taken private.

*   **March 28, 2025**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Fierce Pharma*, *The Pharma Letter*, *BioSpace*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Ayrmid, Ltd.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $4.50 per share upfront plus a $6.84 per share CVR.
    *   **Details:** Bluebird bio confirmed receipt of an unsolicited, non-binding written proposal from Ayrmid. The offer represented a 50% premium over the upfront cash component of the existing Carlyle/SK Capital deal.

*   **April 16, 2025**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Investing.com*, *FirstWord Pharma*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Ayrmid, Ltd.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $4.50 per share upfront.
    *   **Details:** Reports emerged that Ayrmid failed to deliver a binding offer or secure necessary financing despite an extension of the due diligence period. Bluebird’s board subsequently reaffirmed its recommendation for the original Carlyle/SK Capital transaction.

*   **May 14, 2025**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Fierce Pharma*
    *   **Acquirer:** Carlyle and SK Capital (Revised Offer)
    *   **Price:** $5.00 per share in cash (as an alternative to the $3.00 + CVR structure).
    *   **Details:** Due to low shareholder tender rates, the acquirers amended the merger agreement to offer a higher all-cash upfront option to incentivize stockholders to approve the deal.
