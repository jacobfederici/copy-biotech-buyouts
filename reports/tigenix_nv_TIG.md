## Summary

Takeda Pharmaceutical Company Limited entered into an Offer and Support Agreement to acquire TiGenix NV via a voluntary public tender offer for all outstanding ordinary shares, ADSs, and warrants. The offer price of €1.78 per share in cash represents a significant premium over the historical trading price, aimed at integrating TiGenix's lead asset, Cx601, into Takeda's global gastroenterology portfolio following its European marketing authorization.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in June 2017 when Takeda's Marcello Agosti approached TiGenix CEO Eduardo Bravo. Initial discussions focused on expanding a 2016 licensing deal for Cx601. Takeda moved from a collaboration interest to an acquisition interest in August 2017 with an initial bid of €1.25/share. The TiGenix Board, advised by Cowen and legal counsel (Davis Polk, Osborne Clarke, Equipo Economico), rejected multiple offers (€1.25, €1.60, €1.70) citing undervaluation of the pipeline and synergies. Takeda eventually raised the offer to €1.78/share in November 2017. Negotiations were briefly paused in December 2017 as Takeda sought clarity on the Mesoblast licensing deal and orphan drug status. Following the execution of the Mesoblast deal and a positive CHMP opinion on December 15, 2017, Takeda reaffirmed its €1.78 bid. The Board approved the deal on January 4, 2018, after determining that the cash offer provided immediate liquidity and certainty compared to the risks of standalone commercialization and capital raises.

## Rationale

The TiGenix Board recommended the deal because the €1.78 cash price provided a significant premium (78.2% over the Jan 3, 2018 closing price) and immediate liquidity. They noted the high costs and risks associated with standalone commercialization of Cx601 in the US and the potential for significant dilution if the company were to raise the necessary capital independently. Takeda sought the acquisition to consolidate global rights to Cx601 and leverage its existing gastroenterology infrastructure.
