## Summary

Acceleron Pharma Inc. was acquired by Merck Sharp & Dohme Corp. for $180.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $11.5 billion. The transaction focused on Acceleron's pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) candidate, sotatercept, and its commercialized hematology drug, Reblozyl. Despite opposition from several large shareholders including Avoro Capital and Holocene Advisors who argued the price undervalued the company's Phase 3 prospects, the tender offer successfully concluded with approximately 63.3% of shares tendered, leading to a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in July 2021 when Merck (represented by Sunil Patel and Dr. Dean Li) approached Acceleron CEO Habib Dable. Merck's initial $160/share bid was rejected as insufficient. Acceleron formed a 'Project Phoenix Committee' (Dr. Nader, Messrs. Dable, Hite, and Kearney) to oversee the process. Merck increased its bid to $175 in August. Acceleron conducted a market check, contacting Party A (a strategic partner) and Party C. Party A declined an acquisition, instead proposing a royalty forgiveness swap involving Reblozyl and sotatercept, which Acceleron's board rejected as value-destructive. Merck eventually raised its offer to $180 on September 13, labeling it 'best and final.' Following press leaks on September 24, Party B expressed interest but withdrew after failing to meet price and antitrust commitment requirements. Acceleron's board requested a further price increase on September 28, which Merck CEO Robert Davis declined. The board approved the $180 deal on September 29, 2021.

## Rationale

The Acceleron Board determined that the $180 cash price provided immediate and certain value that was superior to the risk-adjusted standalone value. Key risks cited included the inherent uncertainty of Phase 3 clinical trials (STELLAR), the challenges of global commercialization for a company with limited sales experience, and the potential for drug pricing legislation to impact future revenues. Merck sought to bolster its cardiovascular and pulmonary pipeline with a potential multi-billion dollar blockbuster (sotatercept) and a steady royalty stream from Reblozyl.

## Evidence

- The Company Board, by unanimous vote at a meeting on September 29, 2021, (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions are fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company and its stockholders... (v) resolved to recommend that the Company’s stockholders accept the Offer (0001104659-21-125391).
- On November 19, 2021, Purchaser accepted for purchase all Shares validly tendered... representing approximately 63.3% of the outstanding Shares (0001104659-21-142257).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a tender offer and subsequent merger under DGCL 251(h). Shares outstanding (61,147,922) were sourced from the SC 14D9 filed Oct 12, 2021. The deal value of $11.5B is the stated total equity value in the filings. No CVRs were included.

    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** **Merck & Co.**
    *   **Rumored Price:** Approximately $180 per share (Total value of ~$11 billion).

*   **September 27–28, 2021**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Various (Fierce Pharma, RBC Capital Markets, Raymond James)
