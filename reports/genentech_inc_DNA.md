## Summary

Genentech, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Roche Holding Ltd following a multi-month unsolicited bid process. Initially, Roche proposed an $89.00 per share acquisition in July 2008, which the Genentech Special Committee rejected as inadequate. After Roche launched a unilateral tender offer at $86.50 per share in February 2009, further negotiations led to a friendly agreement at $95.00 per share in cash. The transaction consolidates Roche's ownership of Genentech, ensuring long-term access to Genentech's robust oncology pipeline and commercial rights beyond the 2015 expiration of their existing affiliation agreement.

## Interaction History

The transaction began in late 2007 when Roche Chairman Dr. Franz Humer approached Genentech CEO Dr. Art Levinson regarding enhanced anti-dilution rights. After Genentech's independent directors resisted these changes without compensation, Roche proposed a full acquisition at $89.00 per share on July 20, 2008. A Special Committee (Drs. Sanders, Boyer, and Ms. Reed) was formed and hired Goldman Sachs and Latham & Watkins. Throughout late 2008, the parties clashed over valuation; Genentech management produced a '2008 Financial Plan' showing higher value than the '2007 Long Range Plan' Roche relied upon. On December 12, 2008, the Special Committee countered at $112.00 per share. Roche dismissed this and, citing deteriorating market conditions, launched a hostile tender offer at $86.50 on February 9, 2009. Genentech's committee recommended rejection on February 23, 2009, citing 'unparalleled research success' and 'robust pipeline.' Following the release of positive clinical data and further private negotiations, Roche increased its offer to $95.00 on March 12, 2009, which the Special Committee approved.

## Rationale

Roche sought full ownership to simplify its corporate structure, realize annual pre-tax cost synergies of $750M-$850M, and ensure perpetual access to Genentech's drug pipeline after the 2015 expiration of existing commercial rights. The Genentech Special Committee eventually recommended the $95.00 offer as it represented a significant improvement over the hostile $86.50 bid and provided immediate liquidity to stockholders at a fair value relative to the company's long-term standalone prospects.

## Evidence

## Decision Notes

The deal_value is computed as $95.00 per share multiplied by the 1,053,413,655 shares outstanding as of Feb 6, 2009. Note that Roche already owned approximately 55.8% of the company; the total equity value reflects the entire company valuation, while Roche's financing requirement of ~$42.1B reflected the cost to acquire the minority interest and options.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors and acquisition speculation regarding Genentech Inc. (DNA) prior to the confirmed deal announcement on March 12, 2009.

*   **May 1995**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *FundingUniverse* (Historical Archive)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Roche Holding Ltd.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $60.00 per share
    *   **Reported Details:** Speculation regarding a full takeover emerged as Roche’s original 1990 option to buy the remainder of Genentech was set to expire on June 30, 1995. Instead of a buyout, the two companies reached an agreement to extend the option to 1999.

*   **Early 1999**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Various financial news outlets (e.g., *FundingUniverse*)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Roche Holding Ltd.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $82.50 per share
    *   **Reported Details:** Speculation intensified as the extended 1995 option deadline approached. Reports indicated Roche was likely to exercise its right to buy the remaining 33% of the company it did not own. (Roche did exercise this option in June 1999, but subsequently sold shares back to the public, maintaining Genentech as a listed entity).

*   **June 20, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *The Wall Street Journal* (referenced in later corporate retrospectives)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Roche Holding Ltd.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified (Stock was trading at approximately $74.76)
    *   **Reported Details:** Market activity and internal corporate relations noted a significant uptick in speculation that Roche was preparing a bid to acquire the 44% of Genentech it did not already own.

*   **July 20, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *The Wall Street Journal*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Roche Holding Ltd.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Cash offer (Specific price of $89.00 leaked shortly before the official letter)
    *   **Reported Details:** A reporter from *The Wall Street Journal* contacted Genentech’s corporate relations department for comment on a detailed story stating that Roche was moving to buy the company for cash. The report contained specific details that caught Genentech executives off-guard, occurring just hours before the formal offer was delivered to CEO Arthur Levinson.

*   **July 21, 2008 (Pre-Market/Early Morning)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Bloomberg*, *Reuters*, *The New York Times*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Roche Holding Ltd.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $89.00 per share
    *   **Reported Details:** Widespread reporting of a "proposal" by Roche to acquire the outstanding minority stake for $43.7 billion. While this was a formal proposal, it remained "speculative" in terms of a final deal, as it was an unsolicited bid that had not yet been accepted or negotiated by Genentech’s board.

*   **August 13–14, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *The New York Times*, *ETF Trends*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Roche Holding Ltd.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $100.00 – $125.00 per share (Speculated "fair" price)
    *   **Reported Details:** Following Genentech's formal rejection of the $89.00 bid, rumors circulated among analysts and investors that Roche would be forced to raise its offer significantly. Investors like OrbiMed Advisors publicly speculated that a price of $125.00 would be required to reach a deal.

*   **January 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *FierceBiotech*, *24/7 Wall St.*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Roche Holding Ltd.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Below $89.00 per share
    *   **Reported Details:** Speculation shifted toward a "hostile" turn in the deal. Rumors suggested that due to the worsening global financial crisis, Roche might lower its bid rather than raise it. This was confirmed on January 30, 2009, when Roche launched a hostile tender offer at $86.50.
