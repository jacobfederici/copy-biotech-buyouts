## Summary

Blueprint Medicines Corporation entered into a definitive agreement with Sanofi on June 2, 2025, for Sanofi to acquire the company via a tender offer for $129.00 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $6.00 per share. The transaction, valued at approximately $9.1 billion in upfront cash, was completed on July 17, 2025, following the successful tender of approximately 70.90% of outstanding shares and a subsequent short-form merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in May 2024 with a confidentiality agreement focused on BLU-808. In November 2024, Blueprint CEO Kathryn Haviland and R&D President Fouad Namouni met with Sanofi to discuss a broader acquisition. Sanofi proposed $124.00/share on Jan 17, 2025, which the Board rejected as inadequate. Sanofi revised this on Jan 24 to $129.00 plus a CVR (milestones for BLU-808 and elenestinib). Blueprint counter-offered $135.00 plus a $5.00 CVR on Jan 29. Sanofi walked away on Feb 5. On May 15, Sanofi CEO Paul Hudson contacted Haviland with a 'final' offer of $129.00 plus a $6.00 CVR (milestones for BLU-808 only). Blueprint conducted a market check with four other parties (Parties A, B, C, and D); Party A showed brief interest but declined, while others declined immediately. Blueprint entered exclusivity with Sanofi on May 18. Final negotiations focused on CVR milestone definitions and employee retention (rollover of 50% of 2025 equity awards). The Board approved the deal on May 31, 2025, following fairness opinions from Centerview and Jefferies.

## Rationale

The Blueprint Board determined the offer provided immediate and certain cash value at a significant premium (27% over the last closing price) while allowing shareholders to retain upside via the CVR. They considered the risks of remaining standalone, including dependence on a single commercial product (AYVAKIT), clinical development risks for elenestinib and BLU-808, and the need for future capital raises. Sanofi sought to expand its immunology and inflammation pipeline through Blueprint's mast cell expertise.

## Evidence

- On July 17, 2025, following consummation of the Offer, Purchaser merged with and into the Company (the 'Merger'), with the Company as the surviving corporation (the 'Surviving Corporation'). (8-K, 2025-07-18)
- The Company Board unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereby... are advisable and fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company and the holders of the Shares. (SC 14D9, 2025-06-17)

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a tender offer and 251(h) merger. CVR milestones are: $2.00 for dosing the 5th patient in a Phase 2b or Phase 3 study of BLU-808 by Dec 31, 2028; and $4.00 for FDA approval of BLU-808 by June 30, 2032. Shares outstanding (64,733,629) taken from the SC 14D9 as of June 12, 2025.
