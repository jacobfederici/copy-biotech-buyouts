## Summary

Revance Therapeutics, Inc., a Nashville-based commercial-stage aesthetics company with two primary products — DAXXIFY® (daxibotulinumtoxinA-lanm injection, a long-duration botulinum toxin) and the RHA® Collection of dermal fillers (distributed under an exclusive agreement with Teoxane SA) — entered into an original merger agreement with Crown Laboratories, Inc. on August 11, 2024, at $6.66 per share. Commencement of the tender offer was repeatedly deferred through October and November 2024 after Teoxane SA issued a breach notice and threatened to terminate the distribution agreement, leading Crown to demand renegotiation of terms. Following months of counter-proposals, on December 7, 2024, Revance and Crown executed an Amended and Restated Agreement and Plan of Merger at $3.10 per share. The offer price was subsequently increased to $3.65 per share via a second amendment dated January 17, 2025. The tender offer expired on February 4, 2025, with approximately 82% of outstanding shares tendered. On February 6, 2025, Crown completed the merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. Revance was delisted from Nasdaq and became a wholly owned subsidiary of Crown.

## Interaction History

**Early Engagement with Hildred/Crown (November 2023 – February 2024)**

On November 13, 2023, a representative of Crown and Hildred Capital Management contacted Mr. Foley to request a meeting. On November 27, 2023, Mr. Foley participated in an initial discussion with a Hildred representative following an industry conference in New York. During the meeting, general discussions covered Revance's industry and business and Hildred's portfolio company Crown, with Hildred expressing interest in a potential strategic transaction or investment in Revance.

On January 29, 2024, Hildred executed the Hildred NDA, which included customary terms and a limited standstill with "don't ask don't waive" provisions. The standstill, among other things, restricted Hildred from soliciting proxies but permitted direct private solicitations to the Revance Board after making the same solicitation to management.

On February 7, 2024, the Revance Board held a meeting attended by Centerview Partners LLC, which had a long-standing relationship with Revance and was selected as financial advisor due to that relationship and its biopharmaceutical transaction expertise. The board discussed strategic opportunities with Party A, Party B, and Hildred, and authorized management to continue discussions. Centerview attended Revance Board meetings from time to time, reviewed the strategic landscape, and advised the board. On February 25, 2024, the Revance Board directed management to share February Projections (2024–2026 profit and loss) with Hildred and Crown.

**Parallel Party Outreach (February – June 2024)**

Between February and June 2024, Revance engaged with multiple parties. Party A (NDA April 2023), Party B (NDA February 2024), Party C (withdrew April 2024), Party D and Party E (NDA May 6, 2024), Party F (discussions ended April 8, 2024 on competing products basis), Party G and Party O (NDAs June 25, 2024), Party H (not interested), Party I (not interested), Party J (NDA May 2, 2024; withdrew June 28, 2024), Party K (NDA May 3, 2024; discontinued after May 21 meeting), Party L (NDA June 20, 2024), Party M and Party N (both declined June 24, 2024). By August 11, 2024, Crown was the only party to submit a proposal in connection with a strategic transaction.

**Crown Price Negotiation (April – August 2024)**

On April 3, 2024, Crown submitted the April Proposal at $9.25/share. The Revance Board discussed the proposal on April 7, 2024, and authorized Centerview to inform PJT Partners LP (Crown's financial advisor) that Revance would grant Crown further diligence access.

On May 9, 2024, Revance released Q1 2024 earnings below Wall Street consensus. The closing price on May 10, 2024, was $3.32, down 23% from the prior day, attributed in the filing to "an increasingly competitive neurotoxin landscape as well as softening global filler market dynamics."

On May 31, 2024, Crown indicated it planned to submit a revised proposal at $6.25/share. Crown submitted the June 3 Proposal ($6.25/share, 120% premium to May 31 close of ~$2.84) on June 3, 2024, noting that its due diligence had resulted in reductions in assumed operating performance and commercial synergies and increases in certain business risks. On June 7, 2024, Mr. Foley informed Hildred that the board viewed the June 3 Proposal as insufficient.

On June 21, 2024, Crown submitted the June 21 Proposal at $6.50/share (126% premium to June 20 close of ~$2.88), representing a fully diluted equity value of $719M based on Crown's modeling, and described it as Crown's "final" offer. On June 25, 2024, the Revance Board authorized management to communicate that Revance was willing to proceed at the $719M fully diluted equity value but requested a CVR tied to revenue. Crown rejected the CVR.

Between July 4 and August 10, 2024, Skadden and Kirkland exchanged revised drafts of the Original Merger Agreement, negotiating termination fees, debt financing covenants, and treatment of non-executive equity awards. The August Projections (2024–2040, risk-adjusted) were formally approved by the Revance Board on August 11, 2024. Centerview confirmed to PJT that the final price per share based on updated capitalization information and the $719M fully diluted equity value was $6.66. On August 11, 2024, the Revance Board unanimously approved and Revance and Crown executed the Original Merger Agreement. Centerview rendered its fairness opinion to the board on August 11, 2024. The transaction was announced on August 12, 2024.

**Teoxane Dispute and Renegotiation (August – November 2024)**

On August 16, 2024, Revance received the Teoxane Notice from Teoxane SA, alleging material breaches of the exclusive distribution agreement governing RHA® Collection dermal fillers, specifically concerning buffer stock levels and promotional requirements. Revance disputed the allegations. Between August 20 and October 24, 2024, Revance negotiated with Teoxane regarding modified terms, communicated regularly with Crown, and considered Crown's input. The tender offer commencement was deferred repeatedly: August 27, September 19, October 3, October 18, and October 25, 2024.

The Revance Board held ten meetings between September 6 and October 17, 2024, to discuss Teoxane negotiations and merger implications. On October 17, 2024, Crown conveyed that its ability to proceed with the merger depended on resolution of Teoxane's claims and Crown's approval of any material amendments. On October 23, 2024, Teoxane threatened to terminate the distribution agreement if no amendment were reached by October 25. The Revance Board unanimously agreed on October 23 that entering the Sixth Amendment and ANZ Distribution Agreement was in the best interests of stockholders.

On October 24, 2024, Revance notified Crown of the proposed Sixth Amendment and ANZ Distribution Agreement terms and sought Crown's approval. Crown withheld approval, stating that the Sixth Amendment increased minimum payments beyond projected sales, created termination risk, and imposed more burdensome operating terms, and that the ANZ Distribution Agreement provided Teoxane with unfavorable-to-Revance terms for the Australian market. Revance nonetheless entered into the Sixth Amendment, ANZ Distribution Agreement, and Settlement and Release Agreement with Teoxane on October 24, 2024.

On October 30, 2024, representatives of Crown informed Revance that Crown was unwilling to proceed under the Original Merger Agreement and submitted the October 30 Proposal: $2.25/share cash plus $0.50/share litigation holdback plus CVR up to $3.25/share tied to Teoxane distribution agreement performance. The Revance Board determined on October 30 that the $2.25 cash amount was inadequate, the litigation holdback was inappropriate, and the CVR was substantially uncertain. Mr. Foley was authorized to respond.

After Mr. Foley spoke with a Crown representative on October 31, Revance submitted the November 4 Proposal ($5.50/share cash plus CVR up to $2.00/share). Crown rejected it the same day. On November 6, 2024, Mr. Schilke presented a preliminary revised stand-alone outlook to the Revance Board reflecting debt obligations, revised distribution agreement, and commercial headwinds. On November 7, 2024, Revance filed a 10-Q with a going concern disclosure: management identified "substantial doubt about Revance's ability to continue as a going concern" and noted that Revance may be required to "refinance its debt, conduct additional offerings, restructure operations, sell assets or reduce operating expenses."

On November 8, 2024, Crown submitted the November 8 Proposal ($3.00/share, no CVR); Crown removed the CVR citing complexity. On November 10, Revance Board reviewed and approved the Updated Outlook (2025–2027). On November 13, Revance Board reviewed November Projections (2025–2040), reflecting lower revenue growth for DAXXIFY® and RHA® products, reduced operating expenses, and adjusted Teoxane distribution agreement assumptions. Centerview presented preliminary valuation analysis. Revance submitted the November 14 Proposal ($3.00/share plus CVR up to $1.50/share). Crown responded on November 15 with the November 15 Proposal ($3.10/share, no CVR, uncapped excess settlement fee).

On November 17, Revance established through its advisors that full claim waiver and no expense reimbursement were non-negotiable. Revance submitted the November 18 Proposal ($3.25/share, no CVR, mutual release). Crown responded November 19 with $3.10/share, no CVR, no expense reimbursement, and a full release of claims including all Teoxane-related matters.

**A&R Merger Agreement Negotiation (November 21 – December 7, 2024)**

On November 21, 2024, Skadden sent the initial draft of the A&R Merger Agreement to Kirkland. Between November 21 and December 7, 2024, parties negotiated the release of claims, interim covenants, and timing. On November 24, Party B Overture was received (interest in $250M investment for majority stake); Revance could not engage under the Original Merger Agreement.

On November 25, the U.S. District Court for the District of Delaware issued the Allergan Summary Judgment in Revance's favor, excluding lost profits from the patent litigation with Allergan Pharmaceuticals Ireland. Revance's outside patent litigation with Allergan remained ongoing with a jury trial scheduled for December 2024.

On December 3, 2024, the December 3 8-K disclosed that on November 22, 2024, China's NMPA approved the Fosun Pharma subsidiary's biologics license application for DaxibotulinumtoxinA for cervical dystonia in adult patients.

On December 4, 2024, the Revance Board discussed the stand-alone alternative versus entering the A&R Merger Agreement, weighing the Allergan Summary Judgment, the China CD Approval, the Party B Overture, and potential capital raising against going-concern risks and debt maturities. The board authorized management to conduct additional analysis and to request additional time from Crown.

On December 7, 2024, the Revance Board unanimously approved the A&R Merger Agreement at $3.10/share. Centerview rendered an oral opinion, subsequently confirmed in writing, that the $3.10 merger consideration was fair from a financial point of view to Revance's stockholders. The A&R Merger Agreement included a mutual release of claims: Crown released claims arising from the Original Merger Agreement and the Teoxane relationship; Revance released claims arising from the Original Merger Agreement. The termination fee payable by Revance to Crown was $13,373,000.

**Post-Signing and Price Amendment (December 2024 – February 2025)**

On December 9, 2024, Party P contacted Revance via text message (Party P Overture) expressing interest in a potential counteroffer. Revance did not engage. On December 11, Crown and Revance executed a technical amendment clarifying the Expiration Time calculation. On December 12, Crown commenced the tender offer.

On January 17, 2025, the parties executed the Second Amendment to the A&R Merger Agreement, increasing the offer price from $3.10 to $3.65 per share. On January 21, Crown announced extension of the Expiration Time to February 4, 2025. The offer expired February 4, 2025, with 86,197,893 shares (approximately 82% of outstanding shares) validly tendered. On February 6, 2025, Merger Sub accepted shares and the merger was effected under DGCL Section 251(h). All incumbent directors resigned at the effective time. Jeffery A. Bedard became sole director and Crown officers replaced Revance management.

## Rationale

The SC 14D9 sets out the Revance Board's stated reasons for recommending the A&R Merger Agreement. The board weighed the certainty of $3.10/share (subsequently amended to $3.65/share) in cash against the risks of continuing as a stand-alone company. Key stated factors included:

**Financial position:** The filing states that as of the November 7, 2024 10-Q, management identified "substantial doubt about Revance's ability to continue as a going concern." The board cited the size of Revance's outstanding debt, nearing maturities under the Athyrium Note Purchase Agreement and the 2027 Convertible Notes, and the need to potentially refinance debt, conduct additional equity offerings, restructure operations, or reduce operating expenses.

**Commercial headwinds:** The board cited an increasingly competitive neurotoxin and hyaluronic acid filler landscape, slower-than-anticipated DAXXIFY® commercialization in both cervical dystonia and glabellar lines indications, relatively flat US Hyaluronic Acid Filler Market growth, and pricing pressure from aesthetic account consolidation.

**Distribution Agreement risk:** The Distribution Agreement with Teoxane SA (granting exclusive US rights to the RHA® Collection) was set to expire in 2029, with renewal described as uncertain. The October 24, 2024 Sixth Amendment resolved the Teoxane breach dispute but established new minimum purchase commitments through 2029, which Crown viewed as increasing minimum payments beyond projected sales levels.

**Market process:** Revance engaged with over a dozen third parties across strategics and financial sponsors. As of August 11, 2024, Crown was the only party to have submitted a proposal. The board considered the low likelihood that other counterparties would engage on a comparable timeline and under superior terms, while noting that the fiduciary out provisions in the A&R Merger Agreement permitted response to an unsolicited Superior Proposal.

**Potentially positive developments considered:** The board evaluated the November 25, 2024 Allergan Summary Judgment (excluding lost profits from the patent litigation) and the November 22, 2024 China NMPA cervical dystonia approval for Fosun Pharma, and the November 24, 2024 Party B Overture ($250M majority investment interest). The board noted these developments in the context of its December 7, 2024 deliberations but proceeded with the A&R Merger Agreement.

**Centerview's fairness opinion:** Centerview rendered opinions on both August 11, 2024 (Original Merger Agreement at $6.66/share) and December 7, 2024 (A&R Merger Agreement at $3.10/share) that the respective merger considerations were fair, from a financial point of view, to Revance stockholders.
