## Summary

H. Lundbeck A/S acquired Chelsea Therapeutics International, Ltd. for $6.44 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $1.50 based on sales milestones for Northera. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL.

## Timeline

- 2012-07-01: Chelsea initiates corporate restructuring and begins exploring strategic alternatives following an FDA Complete Response Letter for Northera.

- 2014-01-14: FDA Advisory Committee (CRDAC) recommends approval of Northera in a 16-to-1 vote.

- 2014-04-30: Lundbeck agrees to include a third CVR milestone for 2017 sales.

- 2014-05-23: Lundbeck commences the tender offer.
- 2014-06-20: Tender offer expires at midnight.
- 2014-06-23: Merger completed; Chelsea becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Lundbeck.

## Interaction History

Following a 2012 FDA CRL for Northera, Chelsea's board, led by interim CEO Joseph Oliveto, engaged Torreya Capital to run a broad sale process. Over 80 companies were contacted. Lundbeck entered the process in April 2013. After Northera received FDA approval in February 2014, the process intensified. Lundbeck initially bid $6.44 cash on March 31, 2014. Chelsea's board, advised by Deutsche Bank and Torreya, pushed for higher value. Lundbeck introduced CVRs on April 11, 2014, citing concerns over the value of Chelsea's Net Operating Losses (NOLs). Chelsea directors Dr. Michael Weiser and William Rueckert met Lundbeck's CEO Ulf Wiinberg in London on April 23 to negotiate the CVR structure, eventually securing a third milestone for 2017 sales. Other potential bidders (Companies A through K) dropped out due to valuation concerns or a preference for equity-heavy consideration, which Chelsea's board rejected in favor of Lundbeck's cash-certain offer.

## Rationale

The Chelsea board determined the deal was superior to a standalone strategy which would require raising over $30 million in dilutive capital to fund commercialization. Lundbeck's established U.S. neurology sales force and infrastructure were viewed as better positioned to maximize Northera's commercial potential and the likelihood of CVR payouts. Lundbeck sought the acquisition to strengthen its U.S. rare disorder franchise.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors and acquisition speculation for Chelsea Therapeutics International, Ltd. (CHTP) reported prior to the official deal announcement on May 8, 2014.

*   **Mid-March 2014**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Fierce Pharma* (referencing rumors that emerged during this period)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Not specified (general "buyout" speculation)
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported
    *   **Details:** Rumors first emerged that Chelsea could be a buyout target following the FDA approval of its lead drug, Northera, in February 2014. However, these rumors were characterized by analysts at the time as "highly speculative" due to concerns over the drug's "bumpy" launch and a black-box safety warning on its label.

*   **March 19, 2014**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Money Morning* (referencing historical trading activity and speculation)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Not specified
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Speculation linked to a high trading price of **$6.26 per share**
    *   **Details:** Market speculation intensified as the stock reached a multi-month high of $6.26. Investors and analysts began debating whether the company would seek a partner to commercialize Northera or opt for a full sale, despite management's public stance that they could "go it alone."

*   **Late March – Early May 2014**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *The Deal* / *Fierce Biotech*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Not specified (general "Wall Street back-and-forth")
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported
    *   **Details:** Reports later confirmed there were "months of speculation" and "Wall Street back-and-forth" regarding a potential partner or buyer. This period was marked by volatility in the share price as investors weighed the likelihood of a strategic transaction against the risks of an independent drug launch.

**Note on Confirmed Deal:** On May 8, 2014, Chelsea Therapeutics officially announced it would be acquired by H. Lundbeck A/S for $6.44 per share in cash plus Contingent Value Rights (CVRs) worth up to $1.50, totaling a potential $7.94 per share. Prior to this date, no specific reporting outlet had publicly identified Lundbeck as the definitive buyer or leaked the exact $6.44 cash price.
