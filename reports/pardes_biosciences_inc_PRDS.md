## Summary

Pardes Biosciences was acquired by MediPacific, Inc., an affiliate of Foresite Capital, via a tender offer for $2.13 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR). The transaction followed Pardes' decision to suspend development of its lead COVID-19 antiviral candidate, pomotrelvir, after it failed to meet its primary endpoint in a Phase 2 clinical trial.

## Interaction History

Following the failure of pomotrelvir in Phase 2, Pardes engaged Leerink Partners to conduct a broad strategic review. The Special Committee evaluated 133 potential reverse merger candidates and several financial buyers. Nine strategic parties were contacted, and eight submitted indications of interest. Strategic Party A, B, and C were shortlisted, but eventually withdrew or failed to meet the committee's requirements for concurrent financing and cash returns. Simultaneously, the committee negotiated with Financial Buyer A and Foresite (a 27% shareholder). Foresite, led by Dr. James Tananbaum (who recused himself from Pardes board votes), initially offered $1.93 per share. After a competitive bidding process where Financial Buyer A offered net cash minus $12.5 million, Foresite improved its bid to net cash minus $12 million and extended the CVR period to five years. The Special Committee favored Foresite due to its familiarity with the company, which promised a faster, more certain closing to preserve cash. The final cash price was adjusted upward to $2.13 per share based on the final Closing Net Cash determination.

## Rationale

The Pardes Board and Special Committee determined the deal was superior to a statutory liquidation or a reverse merger. A liquidation would have incurred ongoing public company costs and required significant cash holdbacks for contingent liabilities. The cash-out merger provided immediate liquidity at a premium to the market price and a higher percentage of cash return than comparable biotech 'shell' transactions, while the CVR preserved potential upside for the failed clinical assets.

## Evidence

- On August 31, 2023, as a result of its acceptance of, and payment for, the shares of Common Stock tendered in the Offer, Merger Sub acquired a sufficient number of shares of Common Stock to complete the merger... without a vote of the stockholders (0001140361-23-041917).
- The Special Committee unanimously determined that the terms of the Offer, the Merger and the other Transactions are fair to, and in the best interests of, Pardes and the Unaffiliated Stockholders (0001193125-23-196577).
