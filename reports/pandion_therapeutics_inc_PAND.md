## Summary

Pandion Therapeutics, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Merck Sharp & Dohme Corp. for $60.00 per share in cash, representing a total equity value of approximately $1.85 billion. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, aimed at expanding Merck's immunology pipeline with Pandion's TALON platform and lead candidate PT-101.

## Timeline

- 2018-04-09: Pandion and Merck enter into a Confidentiality Agreement.
- 2020-01-31: Merck submits a non-binding term sheet for the IL-2 program for $65M upfront plus milestones; Pandion rejects it as insufficient.
- 2020-07-16: Pandion prices its initial public offering at $18.00 per share.
- 2020-10-12: Merck proposes a 50-50 partnership and a 19.9% equity stake; Pandion rejects the value.
- 2021-01-04: Pandion announces positive Phase 1a data for PT-101.
- 2021-02-07: Merck submits a non-binding proposal to acquire Pandion for $40.00 per share in cash.
- 2021-02-09: Merck increases offer to $50.00 per share; Pandion counter-proposes $60.00 per share. Merck accepts and the parties enter into an exclusivity agreement.
- 2021-02-24: Pandion Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement at $60.00 per share.
- 2021-02-25: Public announcement of the transaction.
- 2021-03-04: Merck commences the tender offer.
- 2021-03-31: Tender offer expires with 88.6% of shares tendered.
- 2021-04-01: Merck completes the acquisition via merger.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2020 with Merck (Parent) proposing an asset acquisition of the IL-2 program, which Pandion's management, including CEO Rahul Kakkar and SVP Vikas Goyal, rejected. Following Pandion's IPO and positive Phase 1a data in January 2021, Merck returned with a whole-company acquisition offer of $40.00 per share on February 7, 2021. Pandion's board, advised by Centerview Partners and Skadden Arps, pushed for a higher valuation. On February 9, 2021, Merck raised its bid to $50.00 contingent on exclusivity. Pandion counter-offered $60.00, which Merck accepted. During the exclusivity period, Merck conducted confirmatory diligence, including site audits of contract manufacturing vendors. Pandion also contacted 'Party A' (a global biopharmaceutical company) to solicit interest, but Party A could not commit to a competitive timeline. The Pandion Board approved the final $60.00 offer on February 24, 2021, after receiving a fairness opinion from Centerview.

## Rationale

The Pandion Board recommended the deal due to the significant cash premium (134% over the last trading day), the certainty of value compared to the risks of independent clinical development and commercialization, and the need for substantial future capital raises. Merck sought to acquire Pandion to integrate the TALON platform and PT-101 into its broader immunology portfolio.

## Evidence

- At a meeting held on February 24, 2021, the Pandion Board unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereby, including the Offer and the Merger, are fair to, and in the best interest of, Pandion and its stockholders (0001193125-21-068343).
- On April 1, 2021, Merck completed its acquisition of Pandion by consummating the Merger without a meeting of stockholders of Pandion in accordance with Section 251(h) (0001193125-21-103344).

## Decision Notes

The deal value is approximately $1.85 billion on a fully diluted basis. Shares outstanding (29,515,583) were sourced from the SC 14D9 as of March 1, 2021. The transaction included no CVRs. The merger was completed under DGCL 251(h) following a successful tender offer where 88.6% of shares were tendered.
