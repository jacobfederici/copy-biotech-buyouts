## Summary

On April 4, 2011, sanofi-aventis, through its wholly-owned subsidiary GC Merger Corp., completed a successful exchange offer for a majority of Genzyme Corporation's outstanding shares at $74.00 in cash plus one Contingent Value Right (CVR) per share. This followed an initial unsolicited tender offer launched by Sanofi on October 4, 2010, at $69.00 per share, which the Genzyme Board initially rejected as undervaluing the company's pipeline and recovery from manufacturing setbacks. After negotiations and the signing of a Merger Agreement on February 16, 2011, the tender offer was amended to reflect the agreed-upon consideration. The acquisition was finalized on April 8, 2011, via a short-form merger, making Genzyme a wholly-owned subsidiary of sanofi-aventis.

## Interaction History

Prior to Genzyme's 2010 annual meeting, Sanofi CEO Christopher Viehbacher called Genzyme CEO Henri Termeer to express support during Genzyme's proxy contest and indicated interest in a strategic transaction. Termeer responded that Genzyme could potentially be open to a strategic partnership but asked to follow up after the proxy contest. Following market rumors in July 2010, Genzyme's Strategic Planning Committee met and concluded it was not an appropriate time for a transaction due to the company's depressed share price resulting from manufacturing difficulties.

On July 29, 2010, Sanofi sent a non-binding proposal to acquire Genzyme for $69.00 per share. The Genzyme Board reviewed the proposal with its financial advisors (Credit Suisse and Goldman Sachs) and unanimously rejected it on August 11, 2010, stating the offer undervalued the company's recovery efforts and new product pipeline, particularly alemtuzumab. Sanofi reiterated its $69.00 proposal publicly on August 29, 2010.

On September 20, 2010, Viehbacher and Termeer met. Viehbacher proposed a price range of $69.00 to $80.00 per share, while Termeer declined to agree to any range and emphasized Genzyme's intrinsic value. On October 4, 2010, Sanofi launched a formal tender offer at $69.00 per share. The Genzyme Board unanimously recommended shareholders reject the offer on October 7, 2010. Subsequently, the parties negotiated and entered into a Merger Agreement on February 16, 2011, amending the offer to $74.00 in cash plus one CVR per share, which successfully closed on April 8, 2011.

## Rationale

Sanofi stated in its initial proposal that the acquisition would provide a new sustainable growth platform, leveraging Sanofi's global footprint and manufacturing expertise to address Genzyme's manufacturing issues and establish Genzyme as Sanofi's global center for excellence in rare diseases. Genzyme's Board eventually accepted the amended $74.00 per share plus CVR offer, which better reflected the value of Genzyme's pipeline, particularly alemtuzumab, and its recovery from manufacturing setbacks.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, and unsolicited approaches for Genzyme Corp (GENZ) prior to the confirmed definitive agreement with Sanofi-Aventis on February 16, 2011.

*   **July 2, 2010**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Market Rumors / SEC Filings (later confirmed in Schedule 14D-9)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi-Aventis
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified (general interest)
    *   **Details:** Market rumors surfaced indicating that Sanofi-Aventis was interested in a "potential large acquisition in the United States." Genzyme management contacted financial advisors (Goldman Sachs and Credit Suisse) on July 4 to discuss these specific rumors.

*   **July 23, 2010**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Bloomberg News / Wall Street Journal
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi-Aventis
    *   **Rumored Price:** $18.7 billion (approx. $69–$70 per share)
    *   **Details:** Reports emerged that Sanofi-Aventis had made an informal approach to acquire Genzyme. The news caused Genzyme’s stock to rise more than 15% in a single day.

*   **July 26, 2010**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Wall Street Journal / PharmaTimes
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi-Aventis, GlaxoSmithKline (GSK), and Amgen
    *   **Rumored Price:** $20 billion (approx. $75–$80 per share)
    *   **Details:** Sources claimed Genzyme was seeking a bid closer to $20 billion to reflect turnaround potential. The report also noted that GlaxoSmithKline had made a "very casual approach" and speculated that Amgen might also be interested.

*   **July 29, 2010**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Private Letter (later disclosed publicly)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi-Aventis
    *   **Rumored Price:** $69.00 per share
    *   **Details:** Sanofi-Aventis CEO Chris Viehbacher sent a private, non-binding proposal to Genzyme CEO Henri Termeer to acquire the company for $69 per share in cash.

*   **August 29, 2010**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Sanofi-Aventis (Public Disclosure of Unsolicited Bid)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi-Aventis
    *   **Rumored Price:** $69.00 per share ($18.5 billion total)
    *   **Details:** After Genzyme management refused to engage, Sanofi publicly disclosed its $69-per-share offer. This moved the situation from "rumor" to an active, unsolicited pursuit, though no deal had been reached.

*   **September 20, 2010**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Financial Times / Various
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi-Aventis
    *   **Rumored Price:** $80.00 per share (Target Price)
    *   **Details:** Reports circulated regarding an "unproductive" meeting between the CEOs of Sanofi and Genzyme. Genzyme’s CEO Henri Termeer was quoted stating that a "fairer value" would be closer to $80 per share, the price level before the company's 2009 manufacturing crisis.

*   **October 4, 2010**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Sanofi-Aventis (Hostile Tender Offer Launch)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi-Aventis
    *   **Rumored Price:** $69.00 per share
    *   **Details:** Sanofi officially launched a hostile tender offer directly to Genzyme shareholders at the unchanged price of $69 per share.

*   **January 2011**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Bloomberg / Reuters
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi-Aventis
    *   **Rumored Price:** $70–$74 per share plus a "Contingent Value Right" (CVR)
    *   **Details:** Speculation intensified that the two companies were finally negotiating a "value bridge" using a CVR. Reports suggested Sanofi was willing to raise the cash portion of the bid if Genzyme opened its books for due diligence, which occurred on January 31, 2011.
