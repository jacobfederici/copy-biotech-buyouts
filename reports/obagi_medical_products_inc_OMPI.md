## Summary

Obagi Medical Products, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Valeant Pharmaceuticals International on March 19, 2013, initially at $19.75 per share. Following an amendment on April 3, 2013, the offer price was increased to $24.00 per share in cash. The transaction was structured as a two-step tender offer followed by a short-form merger, which was completed on April 25, 2013, after Valeant acquired approximately 84.12% of the outstanding shares and exercised a top-up option.

## Interaction History

The transaction followed a multi-year strategic review process. In 2009, Obagi engaged in discussions with three potential buyers, including Valeant, but talks were postponed due to intellectual property claims by founder Dr. Zein Obagi. In late 2012, Obagi received an unsolicited bid from Bidder C at $16.50, prompting the board to engage Morgan Stanley. Throughout early 2013, Morgan Stanley contacted 12 potential buyers. Valeant re-entered the process in February 2013, initially offering $16.75. Negotiations between Obagi Chairman Albert J. Fitzgibbons III and Valeant CEO J. Michael Pearson led to successive price increases to $18.75, $19.25, and finally $19.75. The board approved the $19.75 deal on March 19, 2013. Following the commencement of the offer, the parties amended the agreement on April 3, 2013, to increase the price to $24.00 per share. Regulatory clearances were obtained under the HSR Act and Ukrainian competition laws.

## Rationale

The Obagi board determined that the cash offer provided immediate and certain value to stockholders, avoiding the execution risks of a stand-alone strategy involving expansion into Japan and e-commerce. Valeant sought to leverage its scale and existing dermatology portfolio (Medicis) to realize synergies with Obagi's physician-dispensed skin care leadership.

## Evidence

- On April 25, 2013, Valeant announced the completion of the Offer... Purchaser merged with and into Obagi, with Obagi surviving the merger and continuing as a wholly-owned subsidiary of VPI (0001193125-13-177406).

## Decision Notes

The deal value was calculated using the final amended offer price of $24.00 per share multiplied by the 17,434,138 shares outstanding as of March 19, 2013. The 8-K from April 26, 2013, confirms the total cash required for shares and equity awards was approximately $439.5 million.
