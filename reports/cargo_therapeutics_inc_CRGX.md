## Summary

CARGO Therapeutics entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Concentra Biosciences, an affiliate of Tang Capital Partners, for $4.379 per share in cash plus a contingent value right. The deal followed CARGO's decision to discontinue its lead Phase 2 study and reduce its workforce by 90%. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, closing after approximately 71.48% of shares were tendered.

## Interaction History

Following the failure of the FIRCE-1 study in January 2025, CARGO and TD Cowen contacted 16 strategic buyers and later 141 potential reverse merger counterparties. By April 2025, the board pivoted to prioritize financial buyers due to market conditions. Key bidders included Party A, Party B, Party I, and Concentra (Parent). Negotiations with Party B and Parent were most intensive, focusing on 'net cash' definitions and lease assignment liabilities. Parent initially proposed a 6% cash retention, which was negotiated down to 2% and finally a fixed $4.4 million retention. Party B's proposal involved a 4% retention. On July 3, 2025, the Board chose Parent's offer due to higher per-share value and closing certainty. Party I submitted a 'highly interested' letter on July 4, but it lacked firm financing. The deal was finalized on July 7, 2025.
