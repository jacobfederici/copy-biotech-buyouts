## Summary

TherapeuticsMD entered into a merger agreement with Athene Parent, Inc. (an affiliate of EW Healthcare Partners) on May 27, 2022, for a cash tender offer at $10.00 per share. The transaction was driven by the company's deteriorating liquidity and a pending debt maturity with Sixth Street Specialty Lending. However, the offer expired on July 12, 2022, because the minimum tender condition (a majority of outstanding shares) was not met. Consequently, TherapeuticsMD terminated the merger agreement on July 13, 2022, and subsequently pursued an asset divestiture strategy with Mayne Pharma.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2022 managed by Greenhill & Co. as the company faced a liquidity crisis and potential bankruptcy. EW Healthcare Partners emerged as the sole definitive bidder after a process involving 83 potential strategic acquirers. Initial proposals in February 2022 were as high as $18.00 per share but were contingent on manufacturing due diligence for ANNOVERA. Throughout April and May, EW lowered its offer multiple times, citing ANNOVERA manufacturing failure rates, high payor rejection rates, and the company's significant cash burn. A proposal involving CVRs was abandoned because the company could not register them in time to meet the June 1 maturity of its Sixth Street debt. On May 19, EW submitted its 'best and final' offer of $10.00 per share. Despite the Board's attempt to negotiate higher following an FDA sNDA approval on May 20, EW refused to increase the price. The Board approved the $10.00 offer on May 27 as the only alternative to a Chapter 11 filing.

## Rationale

The Board recommended the deal primarily due to the company's critical financial condition. TherapeuticsMD was in default of its minimum cash covenants with Sixth Street Specialty Lending and faced a debt maturity on June 1, 2022 (later extended to July 13). The Board concluded that without the merger, the company would likely be forced to file for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection, which would likely result in zero value for stockholders.

## Evidence

- On July 13, 2022, the Company delivered a written termination notice to Parent and Merger Sub terminating the Merger Agreement pursuant to the terms thereof, effective immediately. (0001193125-22-193376)
- The Board reviewed, together with representatives of Greenhill and G2, the lack of potential alternatives to the Transactions, including the lack of funding for the execution of senior management’s standalone business plan, which threatened the Company’s potential to remain an independent public company. (SC 14D9)

## Decision Notes

The deal value is computed based on 8,846,756 shares outstanding as of June 2, 2022, multiplied by the $10.00 offer price. The status is marked as terminated because the tender offer failed to meet the minimum condition and the agreement was formally cancelled on July 13, 2022.
