## Summary

MorphoSys AG entered into a Business Combination Agreement with Novartis AG and its subsidiary Novartis BidCo AG, under which Novartis launched a voluntary public takeover offer for all outstanding MorphoSys shares at €68.00 per share in cash. The transaction was primarily driven by Novartis's interest in MorphoSys's late-stage oncology pipeline, specifically pelabresib. Following the successful completion of the initial tender offer, the parties entered into a Delisting Agreement on June 20, 2024, to facilitate the removal of MorphoSys shares from the Frankfurt Stock Exchange and the delisting of ADSs from Nasdaq.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2023, with the parties entering a confidentiality agreement on November 27, 2023. Throughout December 2023 and January 2024, Novartis conducted due diligence on MorphoSys's clinical programs, particularly pelabresib. On February 5, 2024, the boards approved the Business Combination Agreement providing for a cash tender offer at €68.00 per share. The offer was formally launched on April 11, 2024. Following the successful tender of a majority of shares, the parties negotiated a Delisting Agreement, signed on June 20, 2024, which outlined the process for withdrawing MorphoSys shares from trading on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange and terminating the ADS program on Nasdaq. The Management Board, led by CEO Jean-Paul Kress and CFO Lucinda Crabtree, and the Supervisory Board reviewed the offer with financial advice from Centerview Partners LLC.

## Rationale

The MorphoSys boards determined that the offer provided immediate and certain cash value at a significant premium to the historical share price. For Novartis, the acquisition secures full rights to pelabresib, a potential first-in-class treatment for myelofibrosis, strengthening its oncology portfolio.

    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reuters

*   **February 5, 2024 (Mid-Morning)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reuters / Bloomberg / Investing.com
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Incyte Corporation (as a rival bidder)

Reports suggested a potential "bidding war" between the two companies, both of which had existing partnerships with MorphoSys (Incyte on the drug Monjuvi and Novartis on preclinical programs).

*   **February 5, 2024 (Afternoon - Pre-Announcement)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Various (Market Speculation)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Novartis
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Speculation intensified as MorphoSys shares surged to approximately $16.00 (ADS) and €57.40 on the German exchange.
    *   **Details:** Market reports noted that Novartis appeared to be "leading the race" and had potentially outmaneuvered Incyte. Speculation shifted toward a "bolt-on" deal structure under $5 billion, consistent with recent public comments from Novartis CEO Vas Narasimhan.

**Note on the Official Announcement:**
The official deal was confirmed later on **February 5, 2024**, after the market close. Novartis announced a voluntary public takeover offer at **€68.00 per share** (approximately $2.9 billion total), representing an 89% premium to the "unaffected" closing price on January 25, 2024 (the date MorphoSys later identified as the last day before rumors began to impact the stock price). Simultaneously, it was announced that Incyte would acquire full global rights to the drug Monjuvi for $25 million.
