## Summary

Icosavax, Inc. was acquired by AstraZeneca for $15.00 per share in cash plus a contingent value right of up to $5.00 per share. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, aimed at bolstering AstraZeneca's vaccine portfolio with Icosavax's virus-like particle (VLP) platform and lead respiratory vaccine candidate IVX-A12.

## Interaction History

Following Phase 1 data in May 2023, Icosavax management, led by CEO Adam Simpson, engaged in outreach to 13 biopharmaceutical companies. AstraZeneca emerged as the primary suitor. On September 29, 2023, AstraZeneca proposed $12.00 upfront. The Icosavax Board, advised by Centerview Partners and Latham & Watkins, rejected this as inadequate. AstraZeneca subsequently raised its offer to $14.00 (Oct 23) and $14.50 (Nov 2). During this period, Centerview contacted Party A, Party B, and Party C, all of whom declined interest. On November 8, AstraZeneca delivered a 'best and final' offer of $15.00 upfront plus a $5.00 CVR. Diligence continued through November, focusing on the upcoming Phase 2 data for IVX-A12. On December 5, Icosavax shared the Phase 2 results with AstraZeneca, which confirmed they were acceptable. Final negotiations focused on CVR milestone definitions and the termination fee (settled at 3.25% of equity value). The Board approved the deal on December 11, 2023.

## Rationale

The Icosavax Board determined the deal was superior to remaining standalone, citing the significant capital (several times the company's market cap) required to fund Phase 3 trials for IVX-A12 and the risks of competition from larger peers. AstraZeneca sought to acquire Icosavax to gain access to its VLP technology platform and lead respiratory vaccine candidate, viewing it as a strategic fit for their Vaccines and Immune Therapies division.

## Evidence

- At a meeting of the Company Board held on December 11, 2023, the Company Board, among other things, unanimously: (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereby... are advisable, fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company (0001193125-23-293333)
- Parent completed the acquisition of the Company on February 19, 2024, by causing Merger Sub to merge with and into the Company (0001104659-24-025251)

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a tender offer. CVR milestones: $4.00 for FDA approval of a combination vaccine (RSV + hMPV or PIV3) by the 6th anniversary; $1.00 for $200M cumulative net sales in EU/UK/Canada by the 7th anniversary. Shares outstanding (50,802,709) sourced from the SC 14D9 as of Dec 22, 2023.
