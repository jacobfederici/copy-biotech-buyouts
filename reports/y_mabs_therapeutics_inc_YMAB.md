## Summary

Y-mAbs Therapeutics, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with SERB Pharmaceuticals (via Perseus BidCo US, Inc.) to be acquired for $8.60 per share in cash. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, valuing the company at approximately $413 million on an aggregate basis. The deal followed a five-month strategic review process where the company explored asset sales of its lead product Danyelza and financing for its RIT platform before ultimately deciding on a whole-company sale.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2025 following unsolicited interest from SERB and Party A. Initially, both parties focused on acquiring the Danyelza asset. Party A proposed $205M upfront on Feb 14, while SERB proposed $300M upfront on March 13. The Y-mAbs Board, advised by Centerview Partners and Cooley LLP, determined these undervalued the asset and launched a broader strategic review, contacting 30 potential parties. By May, most parties had declined, leaving SERB as the primary bidder. SERB's May 19 proposal remained focused on an asset purchase. The Board pushed for a whole-company sale to avoid value leakage and the complexity of running RIT as a standalone public entity. SERB pivoted to a whole-company offer on June 8 at $7.80/share plus a contingent right (DAP). Y-mAbs countered at $9.00 cash. SERB raised to $8.55 on June 25 but demanded amendments to RIT-related commercial contracts. After Y-mAbs demonstrated these were unnecessary, SERB maintained $8.55 on July 10. Final negotiations in early August led to SERB's 'best and final' offer of $8.60 per share on August 3, which the Board accepted on August 4.
