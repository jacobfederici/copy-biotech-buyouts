## Summary

DURECT Corporation was acquired by Bausch Health Americas, Inc. via a tender offer for $1.75 per share in cash plus a non-tradeable contingent value right (CVR) representing up to $350 million in potential milestone payments. The transaction was structured as a two-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, following a multi-year strategic review process by DURECT to address liquidity concerns and funding requirements for its lead asset, larsucosterol.

## Timeline

- 2025-06-30: Parent and DURECT reach preliminary agreement on $60M upfront terms.
- 2025-07-18: Parent reduces upfront offer to $56M for equity holders ($1.75/share) due to warrant-related costs.

- 2025-09-11: Merger completed; DURECT becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Bausch Health Americas.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2024 after DURECT's AHFIRM trial failed to meet primary endpoints in late 2023. Parent (Bausch Health) initially proposed a licensing deal in May 2024, which DURECT rejected in favor of an acquisition. Multiple rounds of bidding occurred throughout 2024, with upfront offers ranging from $40M to $75M. Parent walked away twice (June 2024 and December 2024) due to regulatory concerns and internal priorities. In early 2025, Parent re-engaged but lowered the upfront cash to $25M, citing higher Phase 3 costs. DURECT countered at $40M. By June 2025, as DURECT faced a 'going concern' warning and explored dilutive financing, Parent returned with a $56M equity value offer. Final negotiations in July 2025 focused on the CVR term (settled at 10 years post-launch or 2045) and the treatment of warrants. Parent's final offer of $1.75 per share was accepted on July 28, 2025.
