## Summary

Endo Pharmaceuticals Holdings Inc. acquired Penwest Pharmaceuticals Co. for $5.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer followed by a second-step merger. The transaction resolved a dispute regarding Endo's development of a non-Penwest tamper-resistant formulation of oxymorphone and aligned with Penwest's board-mandated strategy to return capital to shareholders following a successful proxy contest by Tang Capital and Perceptive Life Sciences.

## Timeline

- 2010-07-06: New Penwest Board adopts operating priorities to wind down operations and return capital to shareholders.
- 2010-07-16: Endo informs Penwest it filed an NDA for a tamper-resistant oxymorphone product (TRF Product) not using Penwest technology.
- 2010-07-23: Endo CFO Alan Levin inquires if Penwest would be interested in an acquisition at $4.00 per share.
- 2010-07-27: Penwest counters at $6.50 per share.
- 2010-07-28: Endo raises offer to $4.25; Penwest counters at $5.00 plus a spin-off of certain assets; Endo offers $5.00 for the whole company.
- 2010-08-09: Penwest Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement at $5.00 per share.
- 2010-08-20: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2010-09-17: Tender offer expires with approximately 82.8% of shares tendered.
- 2010-09-20: Purchaser accepts shares for payment; change in control occurs.
- 2010-11-04: Special meeting of shareholders held to approve the second-step merger.

## Interaction History

Negotiations were triggered by a potential breach of exclusivity in the Strategic Alliance Agreement after Endo developed a non-Penwest tamper-resistant formulation of Opana ER. On July 23, 2010, Endo's Alan Levin proposed a $4.00 per share cash acquisition to Penwest CEO Jennifer Good. Penwest's board, advised by BofA Merrill Lynch and WilmerHale, countered at $6.50 on July 27. On July 28, Endo raised the offer to $4.25. Penwest then proposed $5.00 plus a spin-off of drug delivery and A0001 assets. Endo countered with two options: $4.50 with a spin-off or $4.75 for the whole company. Later that evening, Endo agreed to $5.00 per share for the entire company. Due diligence occurred from July 30 to August 9. The board approved the deal on August 9, 2010, after receiving a fairness opinion from BofA Merrill Lynch.

## Rationale

The Penwest board determined the offer provided certainty of value and was the quickest means to return capital to shareholders, consistent with the strategy adopted in July 2010. It also resolved the legal risks and royalty uncertainties associated with Endo's development of a competing tamper-resistant product. Endo sought to gain full control over the Opana ER franchise and eliminate royalty obligations.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The acquisition of Penwest Pharmaceuticals Co. (PPCO) by Endo Pharmaceuticals was announced on **August 9, 2010**. Prior to this definitive agreement, the company was the subject of intense shareholder activism and public speculation regarding a potential sale or liquidation, primarily driven by a proxy battle with Tang Capital Partners.

The following is a chronological timeline of rumors and speculation appearing before the deal announcement:

*   **December 11, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Seeking Alpha / The Conference Board
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unspecified (Strategic Buyer)
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported
    *   **Details:** Analysts and activist reports began identifying Penwest as a prime target for a "forced" sale or liquidation in 2009. Reports noted that activist investors were likely to target the company to "maximize value from royalties" (specifically the Opana ER royalty stream) rather than continuing as a standalone drug development entity.

*   **March 31, 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** SEC Filings / Press Releases (Tang Capital Partners)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unspecified (Liquidation/Sale)
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported
    *   **Details:** Tang Capital Partners, LP and Perceptive Life Sciences Master Fund Ltd. launched a formal proxy contest. They publicly advocated for the company to "wind down" its money-losing drug development operations and explore a sale of the company or its royalty assets to return cash to shareholders. This created widespread market speculation that a takeover or asset sale was the only viable path forward.

*   **June 10, 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Greenbackd / Value Investing Blogs
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unspecified
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Speculation centered on "Net Cash Value" (NCAV)
    *   **Details:** Following the election of Tang Capital’s nominees to the board, investment blogs and retail investor forums reported that a sale or liquidation was "imminent." Speculation focused on whether a strategic partner like Endo Pharmaceuticals (its Opana ER partner) would step in to buy the company to capture the full royalty stream.

*   **May 20, 2010**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reuters / Bloomberg (Market Speculation)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Endo Pharmaceuticals
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported
    *   **Details:** Market chatter increased regarding Endo Pharmaceuticals as the most logical buyer. Reports highlighted that Penwest’s board was now controlled by activists (Tang Capital) whose explicit goal was to monetize the company's assets. Analysts noted that Endo buying Penwest would be "immediately accretive" by eliminating royalty payments Endo owed to Penwest.

*   **August 6, 2010 (The Friday before the Monday announcement)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Market Watch / Various Trading Desks
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Endo Pharmaceuticals
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Significant premium over the then-trading price of ~$4.20
    *   **Details:** Heavy trading volume and a 4.8% spike in the stock price occurred on the last trading day before the announcement. Traders cited "renewed takeover speculation" involving Endo Pharmaceuticals, noting that the long-standing partnership and the activists' presence on the board made a deal announcement likely in the near term.
