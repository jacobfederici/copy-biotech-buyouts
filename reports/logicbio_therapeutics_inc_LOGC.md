## Summary

LogicBio Therapeutics, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Alexion Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (a subsidiary of AstraZeneca) for $2.07 per share in cash. The transaction was structured as a two-step tender offer under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, representing a significant premium over the company's trading price following a period of financial distress and a clinical hold on its lead program. The deal closed on November 16, 2022, after approximately 81.76% of shares were tendered.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2021 as collaboration talks but shifted to acquisition interest in early 2022. LogicBio faced significant headwinds, including an FDA clinical hold on its lead asset LB-001 and a Nasdaq delisting notice. Party A made an initial bid of $1.56/share in April 2022, which the board rejected as insufficient. Alexion (Parent), led by CEO Marc Dunoyer, engaged in extensive due diligence throughout Summer 2022. Parent initially offered $1.60/share on July 18, 2022, citing the importance of retaining LogicBio's R&D team. LogicBio's CEO Frederic Chereau pushed for higher value, leading to an increased bid of $1.72 on August 8 and finally $2.07 on August 25. A key point of contention was Parent's insistence on 'Key Employee Conditions,' requiring three specific executives (Chereau, Nacht, and Hebben) to sign new offer letters and remain employed through closing. Despite board concerns over the non-customary nature of these conditions, they were accepted to secure the $2.07 price, which represented a 660% premium over the then-current trading price.

## Rationale

The LogicBio board recommended the deal due to the company's deteriorating cash position, the high risks associated with clinical-stage biotechnology development, and the lack of viable alternative financing that would not be highly dilutive. The $2.07 price represented a 660% premium over the closing price prior to approval. For Alexion/AstraZeneca, the acquisition was driven by the desire to acquire LogicBio's R&D team and its proprietary GeneRide and sAAVy platforms to accelerate their genomic medicine pipeline.

## Evidence

- The Board of Directors unanimously supports the Offer, the Merger and the other Transactions and recommends that the Company’s stockholders accept the Offer and tender their Shares to Purchaser pursuant to the Offer. (SC 14D9)
- On November 16, 2022, pursuant to the terms of the Merger Agreement and in accordance with Section 251(h) of the DGCL, Purchaser was merged with and into the Company (8-K, 2022-11-16)
