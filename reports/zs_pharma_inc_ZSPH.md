## Summary

AstraZeneca, through its subsidiary Zeneca Inc., acquired ZS Pharma for $90.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $2.7 billion. The acquisition focuses on ZS Pharma's lead asset, ZS-9, a potential treatment for hyperkalemia that was under FDA review at the time of the deal. The transaction was completed via a merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL following the successful tender of approximately 86.6% of outstanding shares.

## Interaction History

Negotiations were characterized by a competitive three-party process involving Party A, Party B, and AstraZeneca. Party A initiated the process with an $85.00 bid in August 2015. The Board, advised by Goldman Sachs and Simpson Thacher, conducted a market check of eight potential bidders. AstraZeneca and Party B emerged as the primary contenders. A critical point of negotiation was the 'Regulatory Exception' in the Material Adverse Effect definition, which required the buyer to assume the risk of FDA actions regarding ZS-9. AstraZeneca initially bid $91.00, then $93.00, but dropped to $83.00 plus a $12.00 CVR on October 29 citing IP concerns. The Board rejected the CVR as the sales milestones were deemed unrealistic. Party B offered $90.00 but insisted on waiting for the FDA mid-cycle review results or excluding regulatory risks from the deal protections. On November 5, AstraZeneca CEO Pascal Soriot and ZS Pharma CEO Robert Alexander negotiated a final all-cash price of $90.00. AstraZeneca's willingness to accept the Regulatory Exception and provide an all-cash certain value without waiting for the FDA review led the Board to approve their offer over Party B's conditional proposal.

## Rationale

The ZS Pharma Board recommended the deal because the $90.00 cash price provided immediate liquidity and a significant premium (37.7% over the unaffected price) while shifting the substantial risks of regulatory approval, clinical outcomes, and commercial launch of ZS-9 to AstraZeneca. AstraZeneca sought the acquisition to strengthen its cardiovascular and metabolic disease portfolio with a potential best-in-class treatment for hyperkalemia.

## Decision Notes

The deal was accepted as a completed acquisition. The $90.00 price was the final negotiated all-cash offer. A CVR was proposed by AstraZeneca during negotiations ($12.00 based on $1.1B net sales) but was rejected by the ZS Pharma board.
