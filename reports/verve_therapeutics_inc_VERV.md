## Summary

Eli Lilly and Company acquired Verve Therapeutics for $10.50 per share in cash plus a contingent value right of up to $3.00 per share. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, aimed at integrating Verve's in vivo gene editing cardiovascular programs into Lilly's portfolio.

## Timeline

- 2024-11-16: Lilly representative meets with Verve CEO Dr. Sekar Kathiresan to express interest in a potential acquisition.
- 2025-01-13: Verve and Lilly enter into a confidentiality agreement.
- 2025-04-01: Lilly submits a non-binding proposal of $10.00 per share plus a $3.00 CVR.
- 2025-05-14: Lilly submits a revised proposal of $10.00 per share plus a CVR of up to $5.00 with multiple milestones.
- 2025-05-20: Verve Board directs financial advisors to counter with $10.50 per share plus a $3.00 CVR.
- 2025-05-23: Lilly accepts the counterproposal subject to exclusivity.
- 2025-05-24: Verve and Lilly enter into an exclusivity agreement through June 15, 2025.
- 2025-06-16: Verve Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement.
- 2025-06-17: Public announcement of the transaction.
- 2025-06-25: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2025-07-23: Tender offer expires with 55.7% of shares tendered.
- 2025-07-25: Merger completed; Verve becomes an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of Lilly.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2024 following existing collaborations (LPA Collaboration Agreement). Lilly's initial April 2025 proposal of $10.00 + $3.00 CVR was rejected by the Verve Board as insufficient. During May 2025, Verve's advisors (Centerview and Guggenheim) contacted six other potential buyers (Parties A-F); only Party A entered a confidentiality agreement but ultimately declined to bid. Lilly's May 14 proposal offered $10.00 cash plus a $5.00 CVR tied to clinical, regulatory, and sales milestones. Verve countered on May 20 with $10.50 cash and a simplified $3.00 CVR tied only to the Phase 3 dosing milestone, which Lilly accepted. Final negotiations focused on the CVR 'Milestone Offset' for patent licenses, capped at $0.50.
