## Summary

United Therapeutics Corporation acquired Miromatrix Medical Inc. via a two-step merger process involving a tender offer for $3.25 per share in cash plus a contingent value right of $1.75 per share. The transaction aimed to integrate Miromatrix's bioengineered organ technology into United Therapeutics' existing organ manufacturing pipeline.

## Timeline

- 2023-03-10: Miromatrix completes a $10 million equity financing but determines proceeds are insufficient to fund strategic plans.
- 2023-05-15: Board approves commencement of a broad strategic partnering outreach process.
- 2023-07-17: United Therapeutics expresses interest in an acquisition rather than a partnership.
- 2023-07-21: United Therapeutics submits initial non-binding indication of interest at $2.75 per share.
- 2023-08-29: United Therapeutics revises offer to $3.25 cash plus $1.25 CVR.
- 2023-09-07: United Therapeutics submits 'best and final' offer of $3.25 cash plus $1.75 CVR.
- 2023-10-29: Merger Agreement executed by Miromatrix, United Therapeutics, and Morpheus Subsidiary Inc.
- 2023-11-13: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2023-12-11: Tender offer expires with ~83.43% of shares tendered.
- 2023-12-13: Merger completed; Miromatrix becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of United Therapeutics.

## Interaction History

Following a liquidity crunch in early 2023, Miromatrix management (CEO Jeffrey Ross and CFO James Douglas) contacted over 50 potential partners. United Therapeutics (represented by Dr. Elizabeth Eades) emerged as an acquirer in July 2023. Initial bids started at $2.75 cash. Miromatrix countered at $5.00. Negotiations shifted to a CVR structure to bridge valuation gaps regarding the 'mirokidney' clinical timeline. A 'Party A' proposed a partnership but declined to make an acquisition offer. United Therapeutics eventually raised its offer to $3.25 cash plus a $1.75 CVR tied to the first human implantation of mirokidney. Piper Sandler provided a fairness opinion on October 29, 2023, leading to board approval.
