## Summary

Kezar Life Sciences was acquired by Aurinia Pharmaceuticals via a cash tender offer for $6.955 per share plus a contingent value right. The deal followed a strategic review process initiated after clinical setbacks for Kezar's lead candidate, zetomipzomib, and an unsolicited bid from Concentra Biosciences. The transaction was structured under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, requiring no shareholder vote, and closed on May 11, 2026.

## Interaction History

Following safety setbacks in late 2024, Kezar faced an unsolicited bid from Kevin Tang's Concentra Biosciences. The Board rejected the initial $1.10/share bid as opportunistic. In October 2025, after failing to align with the FDA on a registrational path for zetomipzomib in AIH, Kezar hired TD Cowen to run a broad strategic process, contacting 77 parties. Negotiations narrowed to 'Party E' and Concentra. On March 23, 2026, Kevin Tang (now CEO of Aurinia) proposed that Aurinia, rather than Concentra, acquire Kezar to leverage Aurinia's clinical infrastructure. Aurinia offered a $1.5M premium over a $50M net cash floor plus a complex CVR. Kezar's Board approved the Aurinia deal on March 29, 2026, noting it provided superior risk-adjusted value compared to a standalone liquidation.

## Rationale

The Kezar Board determined the deal was superior to a standalone liquidation. Rationale included the certainty of immediate cash, the premium over net cash, and the ability for shareholders to retain upside via CVRs while avoiding the costs and contingent liability holdbacks associated with a multi-year dissolution process. Aurinia sought to acquire Kezar's cash and pipeline assets to potentially resume development of zetomipzomib.

## Evidence

- On May 11, 2026, Merger Sub merged with and into the Company... the separate corporate existence of Merger Sub ceased and the Company continued as the surviving corporation (0001140361-26-020407).
- The Company Board unanimously: (i) determined that the Offer, the Merger and the other Transactions are fair to, and in the best interests of the Company and the holders of the Shares (SC 14D9).

The deal was accepted as a completed acquisition. CVR milestones include $5M for NDA submission and $12.5M for NDA approval. Shares outstanding (7,387,701) sourced from the SC 14D9 'Securities' section as of April 10, 2026.

    *   **Reporting Outlet:** SEC Filing (Schedule 13D/A) / Press Release
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Concentra Biosciences, LLC (an affiliate of Tang Capital Partners, LP)
    *   **Rumored Price:** $1.10 per share in cash
    *   **Details:** Concentra Biosciences submitted an unsolicited non-binding proposal to acquire 100% of Kezar Life Sciences for $1.10 per share plus a contingent value right (CVR) representing 80% of the net proceeds from any out-license or sale of Kezar’s programs. Kezar’s board subsequently rejected the offer on October 17, 2024, stating it "substantially undervalued" the company and implemented a "poison pill" stockholder rights plan to prevent a hostile takeover.

*   **October 17, 2024**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Official Press Release / Business Wire
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unspecified (Strategic Alternatives Process)
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not reported
    *   **Details:** Following the rejection of the Concentra bid, Kezar officially announced it had initiated a process to explore "strategic alternatives," which typically signals to the market that the company is actively seeking a buyer, merger partner, or other major transaction.

*   **March 12, 2026**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Business Wire / Company Press Release
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Enodia Therapeutics
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not reported (Asset Purchase)
    *   **Details:** While not a full company buyout rumor, it was reported that Enodia Therapeutics acquired Kezar’s Sec61-based discovery and development program assets. This fueled speculation regarding the "sum-of-the-parts" value of Kezar as it continued its strategic review.

    *   **Details:** Following the appointment of Kevin Tang (who had previously attempted to buy Kezar via Concentra) as CEO of Aurinia Pharmaceuticals in late March 2026, market speculation intensified that Aurinia would become the primary suitor for Kezar to consolidate Tang’s interests and Kezar’s autoimmune pipeline.

*   **March 30, 2026**
