## Summary

GlaxoSmithKline plc, through its subsidiary SmithKline Beecham Corporation, acquired Praecis Pharmaceuticals Incorporated for $5.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer followed by a short-form merger. The transaction was valued at approximately $54.8 million and focused on acquiring Praecis's drug discovery technology, DirectSelect, and its oncology pipeline after the target faced significant liquidity constraints and a strategic restructuring.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2005 focused on a technology collaboration for DirectSelect. In June 2006, Praecis faced a cash crunch and engaged Canaccord Adams to run a broad sale process, contacting over 100 parties. Seven parties were invited to bid. A 'Party A' proposed a stock-for-stock deal, while GSK initially only wanted to buy the DirectSelect asset for $10 million. Praecis CEO Kevin McLaughlin and Canaccord's Greg Benning pushed GSK to consider a whole-company acquisition. GSK's Moncef Slaoui and WWBD representatives eventually proposed $60 million in November 2006. After due diligence, GSK lowered the price to $5.00/share ($54.8M). Party A made a final 'best and final' stock offer of $47 million on Dec 20, 2006. The Praecis Board, led by Chairman Malcolm Gefter and Lead Director Henry McCance, determined the GSK cash offer was superior in value and certainty.

## Rationale

The Praecis Board recommended the deal because the company's cash position would only support operations through late 2007. The $5.00 price represented a 150% premium over the pre-announcement closing price. The Board also cited the failure to secure acceptable financing and the superiority of GSK's cash offer over Party A's lower-valued stock proposal.

## Decision Notes

The deal value of $54.8 million is the stated aggregate equity value in the background of the transaction section of the 14D-9. Shares outstanding (10,744,643) were sourced from the 14D-9 Item 1(b) as of January 8, 2007. The acquisition was completed via a short-form merger following a top-up option exercise.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

No pre-deal rumors exist for Praecis Pharmaceuticals Incorporated (PRCS).

The acquisition of Praecis Pharmaceuticals by GlaxoSmithKline (GSK) for $54.8 million ($5.00 per share) was announced on December 20, 2006. Prior to this announcement, there were no documented reports of a potential buyout, specific acquisition rumors, or takeover speculation in major financial news outlets or pharmaceutical trade publications. 

While the companies had an existing relationship—having entered into a two-year pilot study and option agreement regarding Praecis' DirectSelect technology on April 10, 2006—this was reported as a standard research collaboration rather than a precursor to an acquisition. Following the deal's announcement, Praecis CEO Kevin McLaughlin noted that the transaction "culminates a process initiated and directed by our Board," indicating that the sale process had been conducted privately without leaking to the press.
