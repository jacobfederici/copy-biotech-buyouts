## Summary

Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc. acquired Patheon N.V., a leading global contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO), for $35.00 per share in cash. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a subsequent offering period and a potential post-offer reorganization to ensure 100% ownership.

## Timeline

- 2016-12-02: Financial advisor of Party A contacts Patheon CEO James Mullen expressing interest in an acquisition.
- 2017-01-04: Party A submits unsolicited non-binding proposal at $34.00 to $37.00 per share.
- 2017-01-17: Patheon Board rejects Party A's proposal to focus on standalone business plan.
- 2017-02-28: Thermo Fisher CEO Marc Casper meets Patheon CEO James Mullen and expresses interest in an acquisition.

- 2017-05-09: Thermo Fisher submits revised proposal at $34.00 per share with committed financing.
- 2017-05-12: Party A informs Patheon it will not submit a final proposal; Thermo Fisher increases offer to $35.00 per share.
- 2017-05-14: Patheon Board unanimously approves the Purchase Agreement and recommends the offer.
- 2017-05-15: Execution of Purchase Agreement and public announcement.
- 2017-05-31: Commencement of the Tender Offer.

- 2017-08-28: Tender offer expires at 5:00 p.m. New York City time.
- 2017-08-29: Offer Closing: Purchaser accepts 95.3% of outstanding shares for payment.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2016 with an unsolicited approach from Party A. In early 2017, Thermo Fisher CEO Marc Casper initiated discussions with Patheon CEO James Mullen. Patheon formed a Transaction Committee (Mullen, Agroskin, Eykerman, Cogut) to manage competing interests from Thermo Fisher and Party A. Thermo Fisher initially offered $33.00, while Party A indicated a range up to $38.00. However, Party A failed to provide a definitive actionable proposal or arrange a CEO-level meeting. Thermo Fisher leveraged its ability to move quickly before its scheduled investor day on May 17, 2017. After Patheon pushed for $35.50, Thermo Fisher made a final offer of $35.00 contingent on resolving deal protection terms. The Board ultimately accepted $35.00 after Party A withdrew from the process on May 12, 2017. Regulatory clearances were obtained from the FTC (HSR Act), European Commission, and authorities in Canada and Brazil.

## Rationale

The Patheon Board determined the offer provided immediate liquidity and certainty of value at a significant premium (35% over the last closing price). Strategically, the board noted that joining Thermo Fisher would provide Patheon with better access to Asia-Pacific markets, cross-selling opportunities through Thermo Fisher's deep biopharma relationships, and the stability of a world leader in science services.

## Evidence

- On August 29, 2017, Purchaser accepted for payment all Shares validly tendered and not properly withdrawn prior to the Expiration Time (such time, the 'Offer Closing') (8-K, 2017-09-05).
- The Patheon Board unanimously recommends that Patheon’s shareholders accept the Offer and tender their Shares in the Offer (SC 14D9).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed following a successful tender offer where 95.3% of shares were tendered. Shares outstanding were sourced from the SC 14D9 filing dated May 31, 2017. The deal value of $7.2B includes the assumption of approximately $2B in debt.
