## Summary

Anacor Pharmaceuticals, Inc. was acquired by Pfizer Inc. via a cash tender offer for $99.25 per share, representing an aggregate transaction value of approximately $5.2 billion. The deal focused on Anacor's lead asset, crisaborole, a non-steroidal topical treatment for atopic dermatitis, and was completed following a competitive bidding process involving multiple pharmaceutical companies.

## Interaction History

Following positive Phase 3 data for crisaborole in July 2015, Anacor's Board, led by CEO Paul Berns and Lead Director William Rieflin, engaged Citi to explore strategic options. Initial negotiations in late 2015 with 'Party A' and 'Party B' stalled after Party A lowered its valuation due to market volatility. In April 2016, Pfizer entered the process, requesting diligence. Simultaneously, Party A returned with a new bid. A competitive process ensued where Anacor's Board pushed both parties for 'best and final' offers. Pfizer increased its bid from $95.00 to $97.50 and finally to $99.25 per share. Party A's final bid was $90.00 plus a $500 million CVR (estimated at $96.73 total value). The Board determined Pfizer's all-cash offer was superior in value and certainty, as Party A's bid included financing contingencies and stock election options. Regulatory clearance was obtained under the HSR Act, and the tender offer successfully closed with 76.7% of shares tendered.

## Rationale

The Anacor Board recommended the deal due to the significant cash premium (55% over the unaffected price), the certainty of value in an all-cash offer, and the risks associated with commercializing crisaborole as a standalone emerging company without an established commercial function. Pfizer sought the acquisition to bolster its inflammation and immunology portfolio with a near-term blockbuster candidate (crisaborole).

No confirmed pre-deal rumors or specific takeover speculation for Anacor Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (ANAC) appeared in major financial reporting outlets prior to the official acquisition announcement.

On **May 16, 2016**, Pfizer Inc. and Anacor Pharmaceuticals, Inc. announced a definitive merger agreement for Pfizer to acquire Anacor for **$99.25 per share** in cash (approximately $5.2 billion). 

While the broader market and analysts had speculated that Pfizer would seek "bolt-on" acquisitions following the collapse of its $160 billion merger with Allergan in April 2016, there were no specific reports or leaked rumors naming Anacor as the target before the deal was finalized. Post-announcement reporting from outlets such as *Reuters* and *The Guardian* noted that while some analysts had previously viewed Anacor as a logical target for companies like **Allergan** or **Novartis (Sandoz)** due to existing partnerships, no active rumors of a deal with Pfizer had circulated in the press.
