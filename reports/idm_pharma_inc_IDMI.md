## Summary

IDM Pharma, Inc. was acquired by Takeda America Holdings, Inc., a subsidiary of Takeda Pharmaceutical Company Limited, for $2.64 per share in cash. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger, aimed at integrating IDM's lead oncology product MEPACT into Takeda's European commercial infrastructure.

## Timeline

- 2006-11-28: IDM engages JMP Securities to explore strategic alternatives.
- 2006-12-08: IDM and Millennium (later a Takeda subsidiary) enter into a confidentiality agreement.
- 2007-05-09: FDA ODAC votes negatively on MEPACT; strategic discussions cease.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2006 but were halted in 2007 following a negative FDA advisory committee vote. After a positive European CHMP opinion in late 2008, IDM (led by CEO Timothy Walbert) and JMP Securities contacted 50 potential partners. Takeda (via its Millennium subsidiary) re-engaged. Competitive bidding involved 'Company A' and 'Company B'. Company B initially offered a higher headline value ($90M upfront + $25M CVR) but later reduced its upfront cash to $65M and proposed CVR milestones that IDM management deemed unlikely to be achieved. Takeda's final offer of $2.64 per share was supported by IDM's largest shareholders, Palo Alto Investors and Medarex. Negotiations in April 2009 focused on the Minimum Condition, termination fees ($2.25M), and the 'fiduciary out' provisions. Takeda's internal approval process in Japan caused a brief delay in late April, but final approval was secured on May 18, 2009.

## Rationale

The IDM Board recommended the deal due to the company's limited cash resources, which were expected to last only through Q3 2009, and the significant risks associated with commercializing MEPACT independently in Europe. Takeda sought the acquisition to add a late-stage oncology asset to its portfolio and utilize its existing European commercial infrastructure (TPEU).

## Evidence

- On May 17, 2009, IDM's board of directors unanimously (i) determined that the Merger, the Offer and the Merger Agreement are fair to and in the best interests of the Company and its stockholders (0001193125-09-118643)
- The total consideration to be paid for the shares of the Company common stock in the Offer and the Merger, excluding fees and expenses, consists of approximately $67,416,621. (0001104659-09-040297)

## Decision Notes

The deal value of $67,416,621 is explicitly stated in the 8-K filed upon completion. Shares outstanding (25,278,599) were sourced from the SC 14D9 as of May 18, 2009. The transaction utilized a Top-Up option to reach the 90% threshold for a short-form merger.
