## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, and hostile bids for Elan Corporation, PLC (ELN) prior to the confirmed deal announcement with Perrigo on July 29, 2013.

*   **January 9, 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Bloomberg*, *The Irish Times*, *PharmaTimes*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Pfizer
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specified (shares rose 9% in Dublin and 45% over the week on the rumor).
    *   **Details:** Speculation emerged that Pfizer might acquire Elan to improve its financial health and expand its biological medicines portfolio. Analysts noted Elan was at an "attractive price" following a significant share decline in 2008.

*   **May 16, 2011**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *BioSpace*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Johnson & Johnson (J&J)
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported.
    *   **Details:** Speculation arose that J&J might launch a bid for Elan after Elan merged its drug technologies business with Alkermes. J&J already owned 50.1% of Elan’s Alzheimer’s unit, and the divestment of debt-heavy units made Elan a "pure neuroscience play" and a cleaner target.

*   **February 22–25, 2013**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *The Motley Fool*, *Reuters*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Royalty Pharma
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** $11.00 per share ($6.6 billion total).
