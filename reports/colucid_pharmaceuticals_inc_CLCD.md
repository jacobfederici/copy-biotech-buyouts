## Summary

Eli Lilly and Company acquired CoLucid Pharmaceuticals for $46.50 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $957.7 million. The acquisition reunites Lilly with lasmiditan, a late-stage oral 5-HT1F agonist for the acute treatment of migraine that Lilly originally discovered and licensed to CoLucid in 2005.

## Timeline

- 2005-12-16: CoLucid in-licenses lasmiditan from Eli Lilly and Company.
- 2015-02-10: CoLucid and Lilly amend and restate the development and license agreement.
- 2016-06-30: CoLucid engages MTS Health Partners as financial advisor in anticipation of Phase 3 data.
- 2016-09-06: CoLucid announces positive top-line results from the SAMURAI Phase 3 trial; Lilly's Dr. Rob Conley contacts CoLucid CEO Tom Mathers.
- 2016-09-23: CoLucid and Lilly execute a Confidentiality Agreement.
- 2016-11-08: Lilly submits a preliminary non-binding indication of interest to acquire CoLucid for $880 million (~$42.70 per share).
- 2016-11-13: CoLucid Board establishes a Special Committee to oversee the strategic process.
- 2016-11-29: MTS sends process letters to nine interested parties setting a bid deadline of January 3, 2017.
- 2017-01-03: Lilly submits a revised bid of $46.00 per share; other parties (A, B, C, D, E) decline to bid citing labeling concerns.
- 2017-01-14: Lilly submits its 'best and final' offer of $46.50 per share.
- 2017-01-17: CoLucid Board and Special Committee unanimously approve the Merger Agreement.
- 2017-01-18: Joint press release announcing the transaction.
- 2017-01-31: Lilly commences the tender offer.
- 2017-02-28: Tender offer expires with 90.3% of shares tendered.
- 2017-03-01: Merger completed; CoLucid becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of Lilly.

## Interaction History

Following positive Phase 3 SAMURAI data in September 2016, Lilly expressed interest in re-acquiring lasmiditan. CoLucid's management, led by CEO Tom Mathers and Head of Strategy Linda Hogan, engaged in a broad market check, contacting 50 potential strategic acquirers. Nine parties entered confidentiality agreements. Lilly initially proposed $42.70 per share on November 8, 2016. The CoLucid Board formed a Special Committee due to Lilly's existing economic interests in funds associated with directors Art Pappas and Luc Marengere. Throughout December 2016, several parties (Parties A-E) conducted diligence but ultimately withdrew, primarily citing concerns that lasmiditan might not achieve a product label sufficiently differentiated from generic triptans. Lilly remained the sole bidder, raising its offer to $46.00 on January 3, 2017. After the Special Committee pushed for better terms and the removal of a closing condition related to a third-party contract, Lilly submitted a final offer of $46.50 per share on January 14, 2017. MTS Securities provided a fairness opinion on January 17, 2017, and the deal was signed that evening.

## Rationale

The CoLucid Board determined the all-cash offer maximized stockholder value and provided immediate liquidity, avoiding the significant risks and costs associated with independent commercialization, including the need for additional capital and the uncertainty of FDA labeling. For Lilly, the deal allowed them to regain a late-stage asset they originally discovered, complementing their existing pain management and neuroscience portfolio.

## Evidence

- At a meeting held on January 17, 2017... the Board (upon the unanimous recommendation of a special committee...) unanimously: (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereby... are fair to and in the best interests of CoLucid and its stockholders (0001193125-17-024367).
- On March 1, 2017... Purchaser was merged with and into CoLucid (the 'Merger'), with CoLucid being the surviving corporation. Upon completion of the Merger, CoLucid became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Lilly (0001193125-17-065899).

## Decision Notes

The transaction was completed via a Section 251(h) merger following a successful tender offer. Shares outstanding were sourced from the 14D-9 filing dated January 30, 2017. The fully diluted equity value of ~$957.7M includes the payout for options and RSUs as detailed in the 8-K.
