## Summary

Audentes Therapeutics, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Astellas Pharma Inc. for $60.00 per share in cash, representing a total equity value of approximately $3.0 billion. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, aimed at expanding Astellas' presence in the gene therapy sector.

## Timeline

- 2019-05-22: Astellas contacts Audentes CEO Matthew Patterson to propose a meeting regarding partnering opportunities.
- 2019-06-19: Management teams meet in San Francisco to discuss AT132 partnering.
- 2019-07-08: Astellas and Audentes execute a confidentiality agreement for partnering discussions.
- 2019-10-03: Astellas CSO Naoki Okamura delivers a non-binding proposal to acquire Audentes for $50.00 per share in cash.

- 2019-10-13: Audentes informs Astellas that the $50.00 price is insufficient but agrees to a management presentation.
- 2019-10-21: Astellas submits a revised proposal of $55.00 per share. Audentes counters with a request for $60.00 per share.
- 2019-10-25: Company C submits a proposal at $49.00 per share.
- 2019-10-28: Company C increases its proposal to $52.00 per share and is granted due diligence access.
- 2019-10-31: Astellas increases its offer to $60.00 per share, contingent on exclusivity; Audentes declines exclusivity but proceeds with diligence.
- 2019-11-22: Company C increases its proposal to $55.00 per share.
- 2019-11-25: Company C withdraws from the process after being told it must exceed $60.00 per share.
- 2019-12-01: Audentes Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement at $60.00 per share.
- 2019-12-02: Merger Agreement is executed and publicly announced.
- 2019-12-16: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2020-01-14: Tender offer expires at midnight.
- 2020-01-15: Merger completed; Audentes becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of Astellas.

## Interaction History

The transaction originated from partnering discussions for Audentes' lead asset, AT132, in mid-2019. On October 3, 2019, Astellas' Naoki Okamura proposed an acquisition at $50.00 per share. Audentes, advised by Centerview Partners and Fenwick & West, conducted a targeted market check, contacting three global pharmaceutical firms. Company A and B declined interest, while Company C engaged in a bidding process, eventually reaching $55.00 per share before withdrawing on November 25, 2019. Astellas increased its bid twice, first to $55.00 on October 21 and finally to $60.00 on October 31. Negotiations in late November focused on the 'Company Material Adverse Effect' definition, the termination fee (settled at 3.5% of equity value), and Astellas' requirement for retention agreements with key executives, including CEO Matthew Patterson and President Natalie Holles. The Board approved the final $60.00 offer on December 1, 2019, following a fairness opinion from Centerview.

## Rationale

The Audentes Board determined the cash offer provided immediate and certain value at a significant premium (110% over the unaffected price). The Board considered the risks of remaining independent, including the need for significant future capital raises, the novelty of gene therapy manufacturing, and regulatory uncertainties. Astellas viewed the acquisition as a key step in its 'Focus Area' strategy to establish a leading position in genetic regulation/gene therapy.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

Based on a review of historical market reports and SEC filings detailing the background of the merger, there were no public pre-deal rumors or media reports of a specific acquisition for Audentes Therapeutics, Inc. (BOLD) before the official announcement on December 2, 2019.

The acquisition by Astellas Pharma was conducted through private negotiations and a confidential bidding process. While the gene therapy sector was subject to general consolidation speculation following deals like Roche-Spark and Novartis-AveXis, no specific rumors naming Audentes as a target appeared in major financial or biotech news outlets prior to the deal.

**Confirmed Deal Announcement:**
*   **Date:** December 2, 2019
*   **Acquirer:** Astellas Pharma Inc.
*   **Price:** $60.00 per share in cash (approximately $3 billion total value).
*   **Premium:** 110% over the previous closing price.

Post-deal SEC filings (Schedule 14D-9) later revealed that a private bidding war had occurred between October and November 2019 involving Astellas and an unnamed "Company C," but these discussions remained confidential and did not leak to the press before the announcement.
