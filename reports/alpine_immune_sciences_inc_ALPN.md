## Summary

Alpine Immune Sciences, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Vertex Pharmaceuticals Incorporated for $65.00 per share in cash, representing an equity value of approximately $4.9 billion. The transaction was structured as a two-step tender offer under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, primarily driven by the clinical potential of Alpine's lead asset, povetacicept, a dual BAFF/APRIL antagonist for IgA nephropathy and other autoimmune diseases.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest in March 2024 when Vertex Executive Chairman Jeffrey Leiden contacted Alpine CEO Mitchell Gold. Vertex initially proposed $60.00 per share on March 19, 2024. The Alpine Board, advised by Centerview Partners and Fenwick & West, deemed this insufficient and engaged in a limited market check, contacting Company D (which eventually declined to bid) while Companies A, B, and C showed limited or no continued interest. On April 5, 2024, Vertex raised its offer to $64.00. Dr. Gold countered that the Board would likely require $65.00 to move forward. Vertex agreed to $65.00 later that day. Final negotiations focused on the termination fee (settled at 3.5% or $173 million) and employee severance benefits. No discussions regarding post-closing employment for Alpine executives occurred prior to the signing. Regulatory clearance was obtained via HSR Act expiration.

## Rationale

The Alpine Board determined the transaction was the best path to maximize stockholder value, citing the significant cash premium (67% over the unaffected price), the risks of remaining independent (including the need for substantial future capital raises of ~$700M), and the clinical/regulatory risks associated with the upcoming Phase 3 trial for povetacicept. Vertex sought the acquisition to bolster its specialty pipeline with a late-stage protein engineering platform and a potential blockbuster renal asset.

- On May 20, 2024, Parent completed its acquisition of the Company... with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation (0001193125-24-142285).
