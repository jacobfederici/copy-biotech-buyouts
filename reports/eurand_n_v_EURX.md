## Summary

Eurand N.V. entered into a Share Purchase Agreement with Axcan Holdings Inc. and its subsidiary Axcan Pharma Holding B.V. (an affiliate of TPG Capital) for an all-cash tender offer at $12.00 per share. The transaction includes a complex post-closing reorganization involving an asset sale and the eventual liquidation of Eurand N.V. under Dutch law to ensure non-tendering shareholders receive the same consideration.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in April 2010 following an approach by Party A, which later withdrew. Eurand's board, led by Warburg Pincus representatives Simon Turton and Jonathan Cosgrave, engaged Goldman Sachs to run a formal auction process, contacting 24 parties. Axcan (backed by TPG) emerged as the sole bidder for the whole company. Key negotiation points included the offer price, which moved from an initial $11.00-$11.75 range to a final $12.00, and deal certainty measures. A significant hurdle was the planned resignation of CEO Gearoid Faherty upon signing, which required Axcan to confirm its continued interest. The Board formed a Special Committee of independent directors (Angelo Malahias, Rolf Classon, and William Jenkins) to evaluate the deal due to potential conflicts with Warburg Pincus and management. The Special Committee engaged Jefferies for a separate fairness opinion. Final negotiations focused on the 'minimum condition' (set at 80% to ensure a majority of minority shareholders tendered) and termination fees ($30M for buyer breach, $50M for antitrust failure).

## Rationale

The Board and Special Committee recommended the deal because the $12.00 cash price provided immediate liquidity and a significant premium (38.7% over the price prior to Axcan's initial interest). They also considered the risks of remaining independent, including the FDA's delay of Ultrase approval and the competitive risks to ZENPEP's market share.

## Evidence

- The Board recommends that you accept the Offer and tender your Shares into the Offer. (SC 14D9, Item 4)

## Decision Notes

The deal value is calculated as $12.00 per share multiplied by 48,020,637 shares outstanding as of December 16, 2010. The transaction is structured as a tender offer followed by a Dutch-law asset sale and liquidation to ensure 100% ownership while providing the same cash value to non-tendering shares.
