## Summary

Ocata Therapeutics, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Astellas Pharma Inc. on November 10, 2015, under which Astellas' subsidiary Laurel Acquisition Inc. launched a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares of Ocata for $8.50 per share in cash. The transaction was structured as a two-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, requiring no stockholder vote. The deal closed on February 10, 2016, following the successful tender of approximately 53.6% of outstanding shares, making Ocata an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of Astellas.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began as licensing discussions for Ocata's Retinal Pigment Epithelium (RPE) program in early 2014. In early 2015, Ocata evaluated a corporate inversion proposal from 'Company A' but rejected it due to complexity and valuation. In August 2015, Astellas shifted from licensing interest to an acquisition proposal of $8.00 per share. Ocata's Board, advised by Jefferies LLC and Goodwin Procter LLP, pushed for higher valuation while Jefferies contacted nine other potential strategic partners; none expressed interest. Astellas increased its offer to $8.40 on October 19 and finally to $8.50 on October 27, 2015, characterizing it as 'best and final.' The Board deliberated on the risks of remaining independent, including a potential 4-month clinical delay in the AMD Phase 2 study and the need for significant capital. On November 9, 2015, Jefferies rendered a fairness opinion, and the Board approved the $8.50 per share cash deal.

## Rationale

The Ocata Board determined the merger was in the best interests of stockholders because the $8.50 cash price provided immediate liquidity and a significant premium (90.6%) over the market price. The Board considered the high risks of remaining independent, including the need for substantial future capital, the long development timelines for cell therapies, and a specific delay in patient randomization for the AMD Phase 2 study that would have necessitated a significant workforce reduction to preserve cash through 2016.

## Evidence

- At a meeting held on November 9, 2015... the Board... unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated... are advisable to and in the best interests of the Company and its stockholders (0001193125-15-381961).
- On February 10, 2016... Merger Sub merged with and into the Company, with the Company being the surviving corporation (0001193125-16-456606).

## Decision Notes

The deal value is calculated based on 42,300,462 shares outstanding as of November 18, 2015, multiplied by the $8.50 offer price. The transaction utilized Section 251(h) of the DGCL to bypass a shareholder vote. No CVRs were included in this transaction.
