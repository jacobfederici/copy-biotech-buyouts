## Summary

Albireo Pharma, Inc. was acquired by Ipsen S.A. via a tender offer for $42.00 per share in cash plus one contingent value right (CVR) worth $10.00 upon FDA approval of odevixibat for biliary atresia. The transaction was completed on March 2, 2023, following the successful tender of approximately 59% of outstanding shares, and was executed as a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2022 with Company A, which offered up to $65.00 (including CVR) but withdrew in May 2022. In late 2022, Albireo's board, led by CEO Ron Cooper and advised by Centerview Partners, engaged in a competitive process involving Company B, Company C, and Ipsen. Ipsen initially offered $32.00 plus a $5.00 CVR in November 2022. Following a structured 'auction' process in December 2022, Ipsen increased its bid to $38.00 plus a $10.00 CVR. On January 6, 2023, final bids were submitted. Ipsen and Company C both offered $42.00 upfront, but Ipsen's CVR was higher ($10 vs $8) and Ipsen was ready to sign immediately, whereas Company C required until the end of January for board approval. The Board determined Ipsen's offer was superior in value and certainty. Regulatory clearance was obtained via HSR Act expiration and German FCO approval.
