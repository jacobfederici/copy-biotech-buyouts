## Summary

KalVista Pharmaceuticals, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Chiesi Farmaceutici S.p.A. for $27.00 per share in cash, representing an implied total equity valuation of approximately $1.9 billion. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The acquisition was driven by Chiesi's interest in KalVista's HAE portfolio, specifically the recently FDA-approved oral therapy EKTERLY (sebetralstat).

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest in early 2024 with Party A, which eventually offered $23.00 per share before withdrawing in August 2024. Following the FDA approval of EKTERLY in July 2025, Chiesi (Parent) expressed strategic interest at the 2026 JPM Conference. On February 19, 2026, Giacomo Chiesi (Head of Global Rare Diseases) proposed $24.00 per share. KalVista's board, advised by Centerview Partners, conducted a market check involving Party A, Party B, and Party D. Party B offered $23.00 but withdrew when told the price needed to be in the high $20s. Party D also withdrew, citing valuation concerns. On March 18, Chiesi increased its offer to $26.50. On April 28, Chiesi attempted to bridge a valuation gap with a $26.50 cash plus $1.00 CVR proposal (linked to pediatric FDA approval). CEO Benjamin Palleiko countered at $27.50 cash, stating the board's disapproval of CVRs. Chiesi responded with a 'final' cash offer of $27.00, which the board accepted to ensure an all-cash certain value for stockholders.
