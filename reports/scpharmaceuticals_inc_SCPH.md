## Summary

scPharmaceuticals Inc. was acquired by MannKind Corporation for $5.35 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $1.00 per share. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, valuing the company at approximately $287.6 million upfront based on outstanding shares. The deal was driven by scPharmaceuticals' need for capital and the strategic fit of its heart failure product, FUROSCIX, within MannKind's portfolio.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2025 following an unsolicited approach by Party A. scPharmaceuticals engaged Leerink Partners to conduct a broad market check, contacting 25 parties. MannKind (Parent) entered the process in May 2025. Initial bids from MannKind and Party B were received on May 28. Throughout June and July, MannKind and scPharmaceuticals engaged in multiple rounds of price improvements, moving from $4.50 upfront to $5.25. A significant hurdle arose in late July when the device supplier (West Pharmaceutical Services) notified scPharmaceuticals of material price increases for the FUROSCIX infusor, causing MannKind to temporarily withdraw. After scPharmaceuticals successfully renegotiated the supply contract in mid-August, MannKind returned with a final offer of $5.35 cash plus a $1.00 CVR. The scPharmaceuticals board, led by CEO John Tucker, evaluated the offer against standalone risks, including the need for near-term financing and the impact of the new supply terms on margins. The board approved the deal on August 24, 2025.

## Rationale

The scPharmaceuticals board determined the transaction was the best path forward due to the company's significant near-term capital requirements and the risks associated with remaining independent, including increased costs of goods from the new supply agreement. MannKind sought to leverage its commercial infrastructure and expertise in drug-device combinations to maximize the value of FUROSCIX and the SCP-111 pipeline.

## Evidence

- At a meeting of the Company Board held on August 24, 2025, the Company Board, among other things, unanimously: (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the transactions... are advisable and fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company and its stockholders (SC 14D9).
- Parent completed the acquisition of the Company on October 7, 2025 (the 'Closing Date'), by causing Purchaser to merge with and into the Company (8-K, 2025-10-07).
