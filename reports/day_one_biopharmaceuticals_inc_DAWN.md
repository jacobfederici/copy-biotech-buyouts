## Summary

Day One Biopharmaceuticals, Inc. was acquired by Servier S.A.S. via a cash tender offer for $21.50 per share, representing an equity value of approximately $2.5 billion. The transaction followed a competitive bidding process involving multiple pharmaceutical companies and was completed as a two-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2025 with unsolicited interest from Company A. Day One's board, advised by Centerview Partners and Fenwick & West, rejected initial bids from Company A ($13.00-$15.45) and Company C ($14.00 mixed) as inadequate. Servier entered the process in early 2026, starting at $18.00. A competitive dynamic was maintained between Servier and Company A. On February 27, 2026, Servier bid $20.50 while Company A bid $17.80. The board directed Centerview to request $21.50 from Servier for exclusivity. Servier agreed on March 1, 2026. Despite a late unsolicited jump to $19.25 from Company A on March 2, Day One remained in exclusivity with Servier. The board approved the $21.50 offer on March 5, 2026, noting it represented a 114% premium to the unaffected price.

## Rationale

The board determined the transaction was the best path to maximize stockholder value, citing the significant cash premium (114% over unaffected price), the risks of remaining independent (commercialization hurdles for OJEMDA, clinical trial risks for Emi-Le, and the need for future capital), and the certainty of value provided by an all-cash offer from a well-capitalized buyer like Servier.
