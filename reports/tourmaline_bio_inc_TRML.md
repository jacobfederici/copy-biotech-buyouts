## Summary

Novartis AG acquired Tourmaline Bio, Inc. for $48.00 per share in cash, representing a total equity value of approximately $1.24 billion. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, primarily driven by the potential of Tourmaline's lead asset, pacibekitug, for cardiovascular and autoimmune indications.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2025 following the J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference. Company A initiated with a $28.00 bid in February, which was rejected. Following positive Phase 2 data in May 2025, a competitive process ensued involving Novartis, Company A, and Company B. Novartis initially proposed a CVR-heavy structure ($25 cash + $7 CVR) in July. The Board, advised by Leerink Partners and Cooley LLP, pushed for higher cash consideration and conducted a 'market check' of 12 potential buyers. Company A and Novartis engaged in a bidding war through August. On September 5, Novartis submitted a 'best and final' all-cash bid of $48.00, significantly outperforming Company A's $43.00 bid and Company B's licensing proposal. The Board granted Novartis exclusivity on September 6 to finalize the merger agreement.

## Rationale

The Tourmaline Board determined the cash offer provided immediate liquidity and a compelling premium (59% over the last trading day) while eliminating the significant capital requirements and clinical/regulatory risks of developing pacibekitug as a standalone company. Novartis sought to strengthen its cardiovascular and immunology pipeline with a Phase 3-ready asset.

## Evidence

- On September 8, 2025, the Company Board unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the consummation of the Transactions are advisable, fair to and in the best interest of, the Company and its stockholders (SC 14D9).
- On October 28, 2025, Parent, Purchaser and the Company... effected the merger of Purchaser with and into the Company (8-K, 2025-10-28).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a tender offer. Shares outstanding (25,799,160) were sourced from the SC 14D9 as of Sept 24, 2025. The fully diluted value includes options and RSUs as calculated in the Leerink fairness opinion. Early proposals included CVRs, but the final deal was all-cash.

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

No pre-deal rumors or public speculation regarding a buyout of Tourmaline Bio, Inc. (TRML) were reported by major financial or industry outlets prior to the official acquisition announcement.

*   **August 2025:** Rumors circulated that Novartis was on the cusp of an acquisition, but reporting outlets (such as *Pharmaphorum*) specifically linked the speculation to **Avidity Biosciences**, not Tourmaline Bio.
