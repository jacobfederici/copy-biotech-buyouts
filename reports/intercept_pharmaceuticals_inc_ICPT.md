## Summary

Intercept Pharmaceuticals entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Alfasigma S.p.A. for $19.00 per share in cash via a tender offer. The transaction followed Intercept's decision to discontinue its NASH program after receiving a Complete Response Letter from the FDA. The deal closed on November 8, 2023, following the successful tender of approximately 74.5% of outstanding shares.

## Interaction History

Following the failure of its NASH program in mid-2023, Intercept's board, advised by Barclays and Centerview, initiated a formal sale process. They contacted 18 potential counterparties. Seven expressed interest, leading to four formal bids on August 15, 2023. Alfasigma's initial bid was $16.75. During the second round, Party A and Party E withdrew when told they needed to significantly increase their offers. Party D withdrew later due to competitive concerns in the PBC space. A new entrant, Party I, bid $17.00 but could not meet the board's timeline for a definitive agreement. Alfasigma increased its bid to $18.50 on September 22 and finally to $19.00 on September 24 after Intercept's advisors pushed for a price closer to $20.00. One director, Gino Santini, recused himself from the process starting July 28 due to being considered for a board seat at Alfasigma. The board ultimately determined the $19.00 cash price provided superior certainty compared to the risks of remaining a standalone company facing PBC competition and regulatory hurdles.

## Rationale

The Intercept board recommended the deal due to the high 82% premium over the unaffected stock price, the immediate liquidity of cash consideration, and the significant risks of remaining standalone. These risks included the impact of the NASH program discontinuation, increasing competition in the PBC market, and the capital requirements needed to fulfill FDA postmarketing requirements for Ocaliva.

- On November 8, 2023, Alfasigma completed its acquisition of the Company by consummating the Merger without a meeting of stockholders of the Company in accordance with Section 251(h)... (0001104659-23-115782)

## Decision Notes

The deal was accepted as a completed acquisition. Shares outstanding (41,814,336) were sourced from the SC 14D9 filing dated October 11, 2023, reflecting the count as of October 3, 2023. No CVRs were included in the final Alfasigma deal, though they were proposed by other bidders during the process.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, and takeover chatter regarding Intercept Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (ICPT) prior to its confirmed acquisition by Alfasigma S.p.A. on September 26, 2023.

*   **December 2015**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Various (cited by Fox Business and S&P Capital IQ)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Shire PLC
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specified; shares "skyrocketed" during the period of the rumor.
    *   **Details:** Market chatter suggested Shire was interested in acquiring Intercept to bolster its gastrointestinal and rare disease portfolio. The rumors dissipated after Shire moved forward with other targets (Baxalta) and Intercept faced an FDA PDUFA date extension.

*   **February 11, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** FierceBiotech / Market Chatter
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** AstraZeneca
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specified; Intercept's market cap was approximately $2.2 billion at the time.
    *   **Details:** Rumors surfaced suggesting AstraZeneca was "taking a look" at the company due to its leadership position in the NASH (nonalcoholic steatohepatitis) drug development race. The report noted the idea did not rise above the level of "market chatter."

*   **2016–2019 (Ongoing Speculation)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Various (Bloomberg, Reuters, BioPharma Dive)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Gilead Sciences, Novartis, and Pfizer
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Often discussed in the context of Intercept's peak valuation (shares reached over $400 in 2014).
    *   **Details:** During the height of the NASH "gold rush," Intercept was frequently cited by analysts as a primary takeover target for large pharmaceutical companies looking to enter the liver disease space. Gilead Sciences was most frequently named due to its existing focus on liver diseases (Hepatitis C) and its large cash reserves.

*   **May 2023**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Market Chatter / Social Media Speculation
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Not specifically named (General "Big Pharma" speculation)
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specified.
    *   **Details:** Following a negative FDA Advisory Committee vote for Intercept's NASH candidate, speculation briefly shifted to whether the company would become a "distressed" acquisition target for its existing PBC (primary biliary cholangitis) business, Ocaliva.

*   **September 26, 2023**
    *   **Confirmed Deal:** Intercept announced a definitive agreement to be acquired by **Alfasigma S.p.A.** for **$19.00 per share** in cash (an 82% premium). No specific rumors naming Alfasigma appeared in major financial press immediately preceding this announcement.
