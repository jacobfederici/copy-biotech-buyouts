## Summary

Santarus, Inc. was acquired by Salix Pharmaceuticals, Ltd. via a cash tender offer for $32.00 per share, representing a total equity value of approximately $2.6 billion. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, which allowed the deal to close without a stockholder vote after Salix acquired approximately 76.4% of the outstanding shares.

## Timeline

- 2010-06-01: Salix CEO Carolyn Logan sends indication of interest to Santarus CEO Gerald Proehl for $4.50-$5.00 per share; Santarus board rejects as inadequate.
- 2012-02-10: Santarus retains Stifel as financial advisor due to concerns about undervaluation and takeover vulnerability.
- 2013-06-20: Jefferies, on behalf of Salix, contacts Santarus Chairman David Hale to express interest in an acquisition.
- 2013-07-19: Salix submits non-binding written indication of interest to acquire Santarus for $28.00-$30.00 per share in cash.
- 2013-07-26: Santarus board forms a special committee (Burgess, Hale, Snyder) to evaluate strategic alternatives.
- 2013-08-06: Santarus and Salix (via Intermediary) enter into a confidentiality agreement with a standstill provision.
- 2013-09-12: Salix submits updated indication of interest at $30.00 per share in cash.
- 2013-09-26: Salix increases offer to $32.00 per share in cash.
- 2013-10-01: Santarus board instructs Stifel to counter-propose $33.00 per share plus a $2.00 CVR for Ruconest milestones.
- 2013-10-08: Salix counters with $33.00 per share cash as 'best and final' offer, subject to diligence.
- 2013-10-09: Santarus board authorizes Stifel to contact 15 other potential buyers; only Party A signs a confidentiality agreement but makes no offer.
- 2013-10-29: Salix lowers offer to $31.00 cash (or $30.00 + $3.00 CVR) citing concerns over Ruconest regulatory pathway.
- 2013-11-01: Salix provides final offer of $32.00 per share cash; Santarus board authorizes management to proceed.
- 2013-11-06: Santarus board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement at $32.00 per share.
- 2013-11-07: Merger Agreement executed and transaction publicly announced.
- 2013-12-03: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2013-12-31: Tender offer expires with 76.4% of shares tendered.
- 2014-01-02: Merger completed; Santarus becomes an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Salix.

## Interaction History

Negotiations were led by Santarus CEO Gerald Proehl and Chairman David Hale, and Salix CEO Carolyn Logan. Initial interest in 2010 was rejected. In 2013, Salix re-engaged with a $28-$30 range. Santarus conducted a formal market check, contacting 15 parties (13 strategic, 2 financial), but no superior offers emerged. Price negotiations fluctuated between $30 and $33, with Santarus pushing for a Contingent Value Right (CVR) related to Ruconest. Salix eventually lowered its $33 'best and final' to $32 after diligence on Ruconest's regulatory pathway. A key component of the final deal was a concurrent License Amendment with Cosmo Technologies, where Santarus returned rights to Rifamycin SV MMX to Cosmo to resolve Salix's exclusivity concerns.

## Rationale

The Santarus board determined the cash offer provided immediate liquidity and certainty of value at a significant premium (36% over the unaffected price). They noted the risks of remaining independent, including clinical trial uncertainties and generic competition. Salix sought to expand its gastroenterology portfolio and leverage synergies between the two commercial organizations.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

Based on a review of historical financial news and market reports prior to the definitive merger announcement on November 7, 2013, there were no specific, widely reported buyout rumors or acquisition speculation for Santarus Inc. (SNTS) that included a named buyer or a rumored price.

The acquisition of Santarus by Salix Pharmaceuticals was largely characterized by market analysts as a "surprise" deal. While Santarus was frequently cited in "top picks" or "stocks to watch" lists due to the commercial success of its drug Uceris, there was no documented timeline of pre-deal rumors.

*   **November 7, 2013:** Salix Pharmaceuticals and Santarus Inc. officially announced a definitive merger agreement. Salix agreed to acquire Santarus for **$32.00 per share** in cash, valuing the company at approximately **$2.6 billion**.
*   **Market Reaction:** Following the announcement, Santarus shares rose approximately 37% in after-hours trading to match the offer price, confirming that the deal had not been priced into the stock by prior rumors.
*   **Reporting:** Major outlets including *The Wall Street Journal*, *Reuters*, and *Bloomberg* reported the deal as a fresh development on the day of the announcement.

**Conclusion:** No pre-deal rumors exist.
