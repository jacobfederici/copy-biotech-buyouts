## Summary

Icagen, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Pfizer Inc. on July 20, 2011, following a multi-year strategic review process. Pfizer, which already owned approximately 12% of Icagen and was a long-term collaborator, acquired all remaining shares via a cash tender offer at $6.00 per share. The transaction was structured as a two-step merger, with the tender offer followed by a short-form merger that concluded on October 27, 2011, making Icagen a wholly-owned subsidiary of Pfizer.

- 2011-10-27: Merger is completed and Icagen becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of Pfizer.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began informally in April 2011 when Barbara Dalton of Pfizer contacted Icagen director Anthony Evnin. Pfizer initially suggested a $4.00-$5.00 range, which Icagen's board deemed inadequate. Over several months, J.P. Morgan (representing Icagen) and Doug Giordano (representing Pfizer) negotiated price and structure. Pfizer briefly proposed a CVR structure in May 2011 before moving to an all-cash $6.00 offer on June 1. Icagen conducted a market check, contacting approximately 40 parties in 2009 and additional targets in 2010-2011; however, no competitive cash offers were received. Company A proposed a stock-for-stock merger at a lower premium, and Company B expressed interest only in specific assets. Following Pfizer's 13D filing on June 24, Icagen's stock price rose significantly, briefly trading above the offer price. Despite this, the Board determined $6.00 was the highest available price given the extensive market check and Pfizer's existing 12% stake and collaboration rights. Final negotiations in July resolved technical merger agreement terms, leading to the July 19 board approval.

## Rationale

The Icagen Board recommended the deal because the $6.00 price represented a 150% premium over the unaffected stock price of $2.40. The board cited the risks of remaining a standalone entity, including the need for significant additional capital, the volatility of equity markets, and the long timeline to commercialization (estimated 2018). Pfizer's deep knowledge of Icagen's programs through their existing collaboration made them the most logical acquirer, and the board believed $6.00 was the highest price achievable after a multi-year market check.

## Evidence

- The Board, at a meeting duly called and held on July 19, 2011, by unanimous vote: declared that the Offer and the Merger are fair to and in the best interests of Icagen and its stockholders (0001193125-11-210444).
- On October 27, 2011, Icagen, Inc... completed its merger with Eclipse Acquisition Corp... a wholly-owned subsidiary of Pfizer Inc. (0001193125-11-285007).

## Decision Notes

Deal value calculated based on $6.00 per share multiplied by 8,852,725 shares outstanding as of July 20, 2011. The filing notes Pfizer estimated a total of ~$51 million was needed for shares and in-the-money options. CVR milestones were discussed during negotiations but were ultimately excluded from the final $6.00 all-cash offer.
