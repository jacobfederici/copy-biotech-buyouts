## Summary

iTeos Therapeutics entered into a definitive merger agreement with Concentra Biosciences, an affiliate of Tang Capital Partners, following a strategic review triggered by the termination of its lead belrestotug program by GSK. The transaction was structured as a tender offer for $10.047 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) representing 80% of net proceeds from the disposition of legacy assets within six months and 100% of excess closing net cash. The deal closed on August 29, 2025, after approximately 72.17% of shares were tendered, resulting in iTeos becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Concentra.

## Interaction History

Following the failure of the belrestotug program in May 2025, iTeos conducted a broad market check contacting 20 potential counterparties. Three primary bidders emerged: Party A, Party C, and Concentra (Tang Capital). Party A's proposals were repeatedly rejected as inadequate. Party C submitted a proposal but required third-party debt financing and a longer closing period. Concentra, led by Kevin Tang, initially proposed $10.25 per share on June 12, 2025. Negotiations focused on the 'Cash Retention' (the difference between net cash and the payout to shareholders). iTeos management, including CEO Michel Detheux and the Transaction Committee chaired by David Hallal, pushed for a simplified CVR structure. On July 9, Concentra provided a draft agreement with a fixed price of $10.047 (maintaining a $10M cash retention based on updated share counts). The Board favored Concentra due to its track record of deal certainty and lack of a financing condition. TD Cowen provided a fairness opinion on July 18, 2025, primarily comparing the offer to a self-liquidation scenario.

## Rationale

The iTeos Board determined the offer was superior to a standalone liquidation. A self-liquidation was estimated to potentially yield $10.86 per share but would take over three years, involve significant execution risk, and require substantial holdbacks for unknown liabilities. The Concentra offer provided immediate liquidity, a 17.8% premium over the pre-announcement price, and a CVR that allowed shareholders to retain 80% of the upside from asset sales without the costs of managing a wind-down.

## Evidence

- On August 29, 2025, Parent completed a tender offer... a total of 32,226,407 Shares were validly tendered... representing approximately 72.17% of the outstanding Shares (0001193125-25-192031).

## Decision Notes

The deal value is anchored to the $465M limited guaranty cap. CVR payments are contingent on (1) Closing Net Cash exceeding $475M and (2) Dispositions of CVR products within 6 months of closing. Shares outstanding (44,171,194) sourced from the July 15, 2025 capitalization table in the 14D-9.

The sequence of events leading to the deal was as follows:
*   **May 13, 2025:** iTeos reported disappointing mid-stage clinical results for its lead drug candidate, belrestotug.
*   **May 28, 2025:** iTeos officially announced its intention to wind down operations and explore the sale of its assets to maximize shareholder value.
*   **July 21, 2025:** The company announced a definitive merger agreement to be acquired by Concentra Biosciences for $10.047 per share plus a Contingent Value Right (CVR).
