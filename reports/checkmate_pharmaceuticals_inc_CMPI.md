## Summary

Checkmate Pharmaceuticals, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc. for $10.50 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $250 million. The transaction focused on Checkmate's lead asset, vidutolimod, a TLR9 agonist being developed for various oncology indications. The deal followed a competitive process where several other pharmaceutical companies declined to bid, and Checkmate faced a declining cash position that threatened its ability to continue as a going concern beyond 2022.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest in early 2022 following a $7.50 per share unsolicited bid from Regeneron on February 1. Checkmate's Board, advised by Centerview Partners and Goodwin Procter, conducted a market check by contacting five other global pharmaceutical companies (Parties A, B, C, D, and E), all of whom declined to engage. Checkmate's Chief Business Officer, Kleem Chaudhary, led discussions with Regeneron's representatives. After Checkmate rejected the initial bid, Regeneron requested a counteroffer. Checkmate countered at $12.00 on March 15. Regeneron raised its bid to $8.66 on March 16, which Checkmate rejected, countering at $11.00. On March 18, Regeneron moved to $9.90. Checkmate made a final counter of $10.50, emphasizing the need for expediency due to competing business priorities. Regeneron accepted $10.50 and required a four-week exclusivity period, which was signed on March 22. Final diligence and merger agreement legalities were handled through mid-April, including negotiations over a $1.0 million retention pool and a 3.5% termination fee.

## Rationale

The Checkmate Board recommended the deal due to the significant 336% premium over the unaffected stock price and the immediate liquidity provided to stockholders. Factual considerations included Checkmate's precarious financial position, with cash expected to be depleted by the end of 2022, and the high risk/dilution associated with raising additional capital in a difficult market. The Board also noted the failure of a competitor's similar product candidate and the long timeline (late 2023) for their own clinical data readouts. Regeneron sought to acquire the TLR9 agonist technology to complement its oncology portfolio.

## Evidence

- On May 31, 2022, all conditions to the Offer having been satisfied or waived, Parent and Purchaser accepted for payment all shares of Common Stock validly tendered and not validly withdrawn (0001104659-22-066299).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a Section 251(h) merger following a successful tender offer where 83.8% of shares were tendered. Shares outstanding (22,031,231) were sourced from the Item 1 section of the SC 14D9. The $250M deal value includes the payout for in-the-money options.
