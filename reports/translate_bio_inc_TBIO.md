## Summary

Translate Bio, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Sanofi on August 2, 2021, under which Sanofi's subsidiary Vector Merger Sub, Inc. launched a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares for $38.00 per share in cash. The transaction, valued at approximately $2.7 billion, was completed on September 14, 2021, following the successful tender of approximately 72.89% of outstanding shares, resulting in Translate Bio becoming an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Sanofi.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began on May 28, 2021, when Sanofi CEO Paul Hudson proposed an acquisition at $28.00 per share. Translate Bio's Board, advised by Centerview Partners and Paul Weiss, rejected this as undervaluing the company. Throughout June 2021, Translate Bio reached out to three other large pharmaceutical companies (Companies B, C, and D), all of whom declined to participate. Sanofi raised its offer to $32.00 on June 15, which was also rejected. After a brief stalemate where Sanofi walked away on June 22, discussions resumed in July. Sanofi proposed $35.00 on July 16, which Translate Bio countered with $38.00. Sanofi agreed to the $38.00 price on July 22, 2021, subject to due diligence and support agreements from major shareholders (Baupost Group) and management. The Board approved the final terms on August 2, 2021.

## Rationale

The Translate Bio Board recommended the deal because the $38.00 cash price provided a substantial premium (30% over the last trading day and 67% over the price prior to Sanofi's mRNA center announcement) and immediate liquidity. The Board also considered the risks of remaining a standalone company, including the high costs of clinical development, regulatory uncertainties, and the need for significant future capital raises. Sanofi sought to fully integrate Translate Bio's mRNA platform into its 'mRNA Center of Excellence'.

## Evidence

- On September 14, 2021, Parent announced that the offering period of the Offer had expired... 51,467,612 Shares were validly tendered... representing approximately 72.89% of the outstanding Shares (0001193125-21-272462).
- The Board unanimously (a) determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions, including the Offer and the Merger, are advisable and fair to, and in the best interest of, Translate Bio and its stockholders (0001193125-21-247345).

## Decision Notes

The deal value of $2,735,376,686.00 is explicitly stated in the 8-K filed on 2021-09-14 as the total consideration paid for shares. Shares outstanding (75,590,892) were sourced from the 14D-9 as of August 9, 2021.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of rumors and speculation regarding the acquisition of Translate Bio, Inc. (TBIO) prior to the official deal announcement on August 3, 2021.

*   **August 2, 2021**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reuters
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported (though the report triggered a 70%+ surge in after-hours trading).
    *   **Details:** Reuters reported that Sanofi was in advanced talks to acquire its partner Translate Bio. The report indicated that an official announcement could come as early as the following day. This was the first specific report of an imminent takeover, occurring just hours before the formal announcement.

*   **August 2, 2021**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Bloomberg
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported.
    *   **Details:** Following the initial Reuters report, Bloomberg sources confirmed that Sanofi was nearing a deal to buy the biotech firm to bolster its mRNA vaccine capabilities.

**Note on Post-Deal Disclosures:**
While not "rumors" at the time, subsequent SEC filings (Schedule 14D-9) revealed that private acquisition interest began as early as **May 28, 2021**, when Sanofi CEO Paul Hudson first contacted Translate Bio CEO Ronald Renaud with an initial offer of **$28.00 per share**. These discussions remained strictly confidential and did not appear in public reporting or media speculation until after the deal was finalized.
