## Summary

ARIAD Pharmaceuticals entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Takeda Pharmaceutical Company Limited for $24.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $5.2 billion. The transaction focused on ARIAD's oncology portfolio, specifically Iclusig and the late-stage pipeline asset brigatinib, to strengthen Takeda's global oncology platform.

## Timeline

- 2015-05-01: Preliminary discussions between ARIAD and Takeda regarding a potential licensing transaction for brigatinib.
- 2015-09-01: ARIAD explores a possible sale; Takeda enters a confidentiality agreement but no formal offer is made.
- 2016-05-09: ARIAD announces divestiture of European operations to Incyte.
- 2016-07-27: Takeda representatives attend a management meeting at ARIAD headquarters regarding brigatinib partnering.
- 2016-11-29: Takeda CEO Christophe Weber expresses interest in a broader global partnership/acquisition during a dinner with ARIAD CEO Paris Panayiotopoulos.
- 2016-12-09: Takeda submits a non-binding offer to acquire ARIAD for $20.00 per share in cash.
- 2016-12-13: ARIAD Board rejects $20.00 offer as inadequate but authorizes management meetings and outreach to three other potential buyers (Company 1, 2, and 3).
- 2016-12-18: ARIAD and Takeda enter into a formal Confidentiality Agreement with a two-year standstill.
- 2016-12-22: Takeda increases offer to $22.00 per share.
- 2016-12-24: Takeda submits 'best and final' offer of $24.00 per share, contingent on exclusivity through January 9, 2017.
- 2016-12-26: ARIAD and Takeda enter into an Exclusivity Agreement.
- 2017-01-08: ARIAD Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement at $24.00 per share.
- 2017-01-09: Public announcement of the transaction.
- 2017-01-19: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2017-02-15: Tender offer expires with 81.4% of shares tendered.
- 2017-02-16: Merger completed via Section 251(h) of the DGCL; ARIAD becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of Takeda.

## Interaction History

Negotiations were led by ARIAD CEO Paris Panayiotopoulos and Takeda CEO Christophe Weber. Following a $20.00 initial bid on Dec 9, 2016, ARIAD's board, advised by J.P. Morgan and Paul Weiss, conducted a targeted market check involving three other pharmaceutical companies. Company 2 declined interest, while Company 3 showed interest but was eventually blocked by the exclusivity agreement granted to Takeda. Takeda increased its bid to $22.00 on Dec 22 and finally to $24.00 on Dec 24, labeling it 'best and final'. ARIAD negotiated a reduction in the termination fee from $1.00/share to $0.75/share (~3.125% of equity value) before granting exclusivity. Final board deliberations on Jan 7-8, 2017, included fairness opinions from J.P. Morgan, Goldman Sachs, and Lazard. A potential conflict regarding J.P. Morgan's affiliate holding convertible note hedges was disclosed and resolved by terminating those hedges with no net payment concurrently with the merger signing.

## Rationale

The ARIAD Board determined the all-cash offer provided certain and immediate value at a significant premium (74.7% over the last undisturbed price). Takeda sought to transform its global oncology portfolio by acquiring a high-potential late-stage lung cancer asset (brigatinib) and an established commercial product (Iclusig).

Before the confirmed acquisition of ARIAD Pharmaceuticals (ARIA) by Takeda Pharmaceutical Company on January 9, 2017, the company was the subject of several years of intense buyout speculation.

The following is a chronological timeline of reported rumors and takeover speculation:

*   **January 2014**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *FiercePharma*, *24/7 Wall St.*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unspecified "Big Pharma" (speculation often included Eli Lilly, GlaxoSmithKline, or Novartis).
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specifically reported, though shares rose on the buzz.
    *   **Details:** Following the FDA's decision to allow ARIAD to relaunch its leukemia drug Iclusig (after a temporary suspension due to safety concerns), rumors began circulating that the company was a prime acquisition target for larger oncology players.

*   **February 2015**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Forbes*, *FierceBiotech*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Activist-led sale (Sarissa Capital).
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported.
    *   **Details:** Activist investor Alex Denner of Sarissa Capital launched a proxy fight against ARIAD. Speculation intensified that Denner’s ultimate goal was to force a sale of the company to a larger pharmaceutical firm.

*   **August 28, 2015**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Bloomberg*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Baxalta Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Market cap rose from $1.5 billion to $1.7 billion on the news.
    *   **Details:** Reports surfaced that Baxalta was in active talks to acquire ARIAD. Analysts speculated the move was a "defensive" acquisition intended to make Baxalta itself less attractive to its own hostile suitor, Shire PLC.

*   **September 2, 2015**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Bloomberg*, *FierceBiotech*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Baxalta Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Rumored deal value was approximately $2 billion.
    *   **Details:** Reports indicated that merger talks between Baxalta and ARIAD had collapsed. The primary reason cited was a disagreement over the acquisition price and negative feedback from Baxalta's investors regarding the strategic fit.

*   **May 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *BioSpace*, *StreetInsider*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Incyte Corporation.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported.
    *   **Details:** While not a full acquisition rumor, ARIAD’s sale of its European operations to Incyte led to widespread speculation that Incyte or another partner would eventually return to buy the remainder of the company.

*   **December 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Daily Mail* (Market Rumors), *StreetInsider*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unspecified (speculation included Eli Lilly or Jazz Pharmaceuticals).
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Rumors suggested a price in the $20.00 per share range.
    *   **Details:** In the weeks leading up to the actual deal, market "chatter" increased regarding a potential year-end or J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference announcement. Speculation was fueled by ARIAD's hiring of investment bankers to review "strategic alternatives" following political pressure over drug pricing.
