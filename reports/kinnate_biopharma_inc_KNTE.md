## Summary

Kinnate Biopharma Inc. was acquired by XOMA Corporation via a tender offer for a cash amount based on the company's closing net cash plus a contingent value right (CVR). The transaction followed a strategic review process where Kinnate explored reverse mergers and asset sales, ultimately deciding on a cash-out merger with XOMA after other bidders, including major stockholders Foresite and OrbiMed, withdrew their interest in a whole-company acquisition.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in mid-2023 with a broad outreach to 119 parties. By late 2023, the process narrowed to three paths: a reverse merger with Party B, a buyout by major stockholders Foresite/OrbiMed, or a buyout by XOMA. Party B withdrew on Jan 12, 2024, citing internal plans. Foresite and OrbiMed initially proposed $2.17/share, eventually reaching a 'net cash minus $8.25M' formula before withdrawing on Jan 23, 2024, because the wind-down of Kinnate would impede their core activities. XOMA remained the sole viable bidder, offering a 'net cash minus $9.5M' structure. CEO Nima Farzan and XOMA CEO Owen Hughes negotiated the final cash components and CVR terms. The Special Committee, advised by Leerink Partners and Wilson Sonsini, recommended the XOMA deal as superior to a standalone liquidation.
