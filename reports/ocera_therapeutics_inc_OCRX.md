## Summary

Ocera Therapeutics was acquired by Mallinckrodt plc via a tender offer for $1.52 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $2.58 per share. The transaction followed a strategic review process initiated after Ocera's Phase 2b STOP-HE trial failed to meet its primary endpoint, leading to significant stock price depreciation and financing challenges. The deal was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL.

## Interaction History

Following the failure of the STOP-HE trial in January 2017, Ocera's board, led by the Strategic Committee (Eckard Weber, Anne VanLent, Steven James), conducted a broad outreach to 34 potential partners. Mallinckrodt and an unnamed 'Party A' emerged as the primary bidders. Negotiations were characterized by significant volatility in offer values as bidders analyzed post-hoc data and regulatory risks. Mallinckrodt initially offered $55M upfront in May, rising to $65M in July, before slashing the offer to $45M in August due to concerns that the FDA would require another Phase 2 trial. Ocera briefly entered exclusivity with Party A in September for a $1.60/share bid, but Party A withdrew on October 2. Ocera then re-engaged Mallinckrodt, which ultimately proposed $1.52/share upfront plus $75M in aggregate CVR milestones. The Board approved this final offer on November 1, 2017, noting it provided superior value compared to a highly dilutive standalone financing path.

## Rationale

The Ocera Board recommended the deal because the $1.52 upfront price represented a 52% premium over the prior day's close and provided immediate liquidity. The Board determined that remaining independent posed significant risks, including the need for substantial, highly dilutive capital raises to fund further clinical trials required by the FDA and to maintain Nasdaq listing. Mallinckrodt's established hospital and liver franchise was viewed as an ideal platform to maximize the value of OCR-002 and achieve CVR milestones.

## Evidence

- At a meeting held on November 1, 2017, after careful consideration, the Board, among other things, unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions contemplated by the Merger Agreement, including the Offer and the Merger, are fair to and in the best interests of the Company and its stockholders (0001193125-17-337114).
- On December 11, 2017, Purchaser accepted for payment all Shares validly tendered into and not validly withdrawn from the Offer... Purchaser acquired a sufficient number of Shares to consummate, and on December 11, 2017, did consummate, the Merger (0001193125-17-365690).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via Section 251(h) of the DGCL, bypassing a shareholder vote. CVR milestones are: $10M for IV Phase 3 start, $15M for Oral Phase 3 start, and $50M for $500M cumulative sales. Shares outstanding (26,514,134) sourced from Item 1 of the SC 14D9.
