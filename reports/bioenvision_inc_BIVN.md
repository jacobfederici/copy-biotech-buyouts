## Summary

Bioenvision, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Genzyme Corporation on May 29, 2007, under which Genzyme's subsidiary, Wichita Bio Corporation, launched a tender offer to acquire all outstanding common and preferred shares for $5.60 per common share in cash. The transaction aimed to consolidate Genzyme's worldwide rights to clofarabine (Evoltra), a leukemia treatment previously co-developed by the two companies. Following the tender offer and subsequent legal proceedings in the Delaware Court of Chancery regarding the counting of votes, the merger was completed in October 2007.

## Timeline

- 2001-03-12: Bioenvision and Genzyme (successor to Ilex) enter into a Co-Development Agreement for clofarabine.
- 2003-07-01: Bioenvision retains a financial advisor to explore strategic alternatives.
- 2004-06-01: Genzyme makes a verbal proposal for clofarabine rights; Bioenvision board rejects it as too low.
- 2005-10-17: Bioenvision and Genzyme execute a confidentiality agreement for due diligence.
- 2006-01-01: Genzyme withdraws from acquisition discussions citing regulatory and pricing risks in Europe.
- 2006-06-19: Company A submits a non-binding proposal of $6.25 to $6.75 per share, later withdrawn due to diligence concerns.
- 2007-04-11: Genzyme submits a written non-binding indication of interest at $5.25 per share.
- 2007-05-01: Bioenvision board creates a committee to oversee negotiations and a 'market check' of 10 potential partners.
- 2007-05-23: Company E, the only interested party from the market check, withdraws citing diligence concerns.
- 2007-05-24: Bioenvision and Southern Research Institute amend their agreement to improve economic terms, used as leverage for a higher price.
- 2007-05-25: Genzyme increases its offer to $5.60 per share (the 'Improved Genzyme Offer').
- 2007-05-28: Bioenvision board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement and recommends the offer to stockholders.
- 2007-05-29: Merger Agreement is signed and publicly announced.
- 2007-06-04: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2007-10-10: Delaware Court of Chancery issues an order resolving a petition related to the merger, facilitating the close.

## Interaction History

Negotiations were led by Dr. Christopher Wood (CEO) and Joseph Cooper (Independent Director) for Bioenvision, and Earl M. Collier, Jr. and Mark Enyedy for Genzyme. Financial advice was provided by UBS for Bioenvision and Banc of America Securities for Genzyme. The process involved multiple rounds of diligence and a formal 'market check' where 20 companies were contacted in 2006 and 10 in 2007. Genzyme initially offered $5.25 in April 2007. Bioenvision pushed for a higher price based on an improved royalty agreement with Southern Research Institute (the SRI Amendment). Genzyme eventually raised the price to $5.60 on May 25, 2007, contingent on a rapid signing by May 29. The board negotiated the termination fee down from 3.75% to approximately 2.6% ($9 million) to ensure it would not deter superior proposals.

## Rationale

The Bioenvision board recommended the deal because it provided immediate cash liquidity at a significant premium (50% over the 30-day average) and mitigated the substantial risks of remaining independent. These risks included the need for significant capital to build a commercial infrastructure in Europe, regulatory uncertainties for clofarabine in adult AML, and the potential for clinical trial delays. Genzyme sought the acquisition to gain full worldwide rights to clofarabine, consolidating its oncology portfolio.

## Evidence

- At a meeting of the Company’s Board of Directors held on May 28, 2007, the Company Board unanimously: (i) determined and declared that the Merger Agreement, the Offer and the Merger and the other transactions contemplated thereby are advisable, and in the best interests of the Company and its stockholders (SC 14D9, Item 4).
