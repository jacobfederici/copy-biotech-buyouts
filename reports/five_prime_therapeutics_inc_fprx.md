## Summary

Amgen Inc. announced on March 4, 2021 its agreement to acquire Five Prime Therapeutics, Inc., a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on oncology and gastrointestinal cancers, for $38.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer. The Five Prime Board of Directors, represented by Chairman William R. Ringo, unanimously recommended the transaction after a competitive process that evaluated multiple strategic alternatives including collaboration agreements and acquisition proposals from other parties. The tender offer commenced on March 18, 2021 and expired on April 16, 2021, achieving an 87.8% tender rate. The merger was completed on April 16, 2021 under Section 251(h) of the Delaware General Corporation Law without requiring a stockholder vote, with Five Prime surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Amgen.

## Interaction History

The acquisition of Five Prime Therapeutics emerged from a strategic process that began as an effort to identify collaboration partners for the company's lead program, bemarituzumab (Bema), following positive Phase 2 data in Q4 2020.

**Strategic Planning and Process Initiation (May–November 2020)**

Starting in May 2020, Five Prime engaged Lazard Frères & Co. LLC as a financial advisor to support strategic initiatives including potential collaborations, licenses, and in-licenses. The board met throughout the summer of 2020 to review strategic scenarios in light of expected data outcomes for Bema and FPT155 (a soluble CD80 fusion protein), with discussions of standalone commercialization, collaboration opportunities, and potential sale processes.

On November 8, 2020, Five Prime announced topline results from its FIGHT trial, demonstrating that bemarituzumab plus chemotherapy achieved statistically significant improvements in progression-free survival, overall survival, and overall response rate compared to placebo plus chemotherapy in front-line FGFR2b+/HER2- gastric and gastroesophageal junction (GEJ) cancer patients. Following the announcement, the company raised $174 million in a public stock offering. The positive data prompted the board, at its November 18–19, 2020 meetings, to authorize management to pursue collaboration opportunities for Bema and engage Lazard to support outreach efforts.

**Collaboration Process and Competitive Bidding (November 2020–January 2021)**

From November 23, 2020, through mid-December 2020, Five Prime's Dr. Tarak Mody (Vice President, Business Development and Alliance Management) contacted 15 potential collaboration partners, including Amgen. Management provided presentations to these parties following NDA executions. In mid-December, Mody issued a process letter requesting preliminary Bema Collaboration proposals by January 27, 2021.

Between November 23, 2020, and January 29, 2021, parties conducted due diligence through a virtual data room and held meetings with Five Prime management and Lazard representatives regarding the Bema collaboration scope. On January 14 and 25, 2021, Five Prime senior management met with executives of Party C, who on January 23 called Chairman William Ringo to express strong interest and confirm intention to submit a proposal by the following week. Party B indicated via email on January 22 its intention to submit a proposal, and Party D's representative stated on January 23 that it was finalizing its proposal.

By January 30, 2021, the strategic process had broadened. Five Prime received Collaboration Proposal A on January 24 and Collaboration Proposal B on January 26. On January 27, Party C called Ringo to state that it was interested in a strategic acquisition of Five Prime rather than a collaboration, and submitted Acquisition Proposal C at $25 per share cash. Party D submitted Collaboration Proposal D on January 28 and on January 29 expressed interest in a potential acquisition. On January 29, Amgen's Executive Director of Business Development, Chris DeRespino, contacted Mody to announce that Amgen would submit a Bema collaboration proposal that weekend. Amgen submitted its Collaboration Proposal on January 30.

**Strategic Pivot to Whole-Company Sale (January 30–February 2)**

At the January 30, 2021 Five Prime Board meeting, management and Lazard presented the developments and proposed broadening Lazard's engagement to cover a potential whole-company strategic acquisition process. The board approved the expanded engagement following discussion of Lazard's qualifications and experience. The board discussed the relative merits of the various collaboration and acquisition proposals, reviewed preliminary valuation analysis and Company forecasts, authorized the R&D committee to refine forecasts, and directed Lazard to reach out to all parties—including Amgen, Party D, Party E, and Party F—to inquire about acquisition interest. The board agreed to continue collaboration discussions with Party G (which had not yet submitted its Bema proposal) and determined to reject Collaboration Proposal B due to its low overall value.

On January 31, per board instruction, Lazard informed Amgen that Five Prime had received a third-party acquisition offer and invited Amgen's interest in an acquisition transaction. Amgen's Rachna Khosla (Vice President, Business Development) and DeRespino responded that they would discuss internally. Lazard also spoke with Party D, who reiterated acquisition interest.

On February 1, a Party A representative called director Garry Nicholson expressing broader strategic interest and confirming the call with CEO Thomas Civik. Amgen's Khosla contacted Lazard to state that Amgen was open to considering an acquisition. Party C emailed Ringo to follow up on Acquisition Proposal C, and Ringo responded that the company was continuing to assess the proposal. Lazard contacted Parties A, E, and F regarding acquisition interest; Party E indicated it was not interested in acquisition or collaboration, and Party B was declined.

At the February 2 Five Prime Board meeting, with management, Cooley LLP (Five Prime's outside counsel), and Lazard present, the board discussed the updated status of discussions. The board instructed Lazard to inform Party C that Acquisition Proposal C was undervalued but that Five Prime would provide selected due diligence information to allow Party C to increase its offer. The board instructed Lazard to invite Amgen to submit an indication of value for a whole-company sale. The board reviewed preliminary valuations and provided feedback on management forecasts.

**Acquisition Bidding Process (February 4–18)**

Between February 4 and February 12, Cooley negotiated non-disclosure agreements with Party C, Party D, and Amgen for potential acquisition discussions. Each NDA included customary standstill provisions that would fall away upon announcement or entry into a change-of-control transaction. Each party received access to a virtual data room covering the whole company and conducted diligence meetings on various subjects.

On February 9, Five Prime management provided a presentation to Party D. On February 10, management presented to Party C, and Mody and Lazard discussed with Party A how to improve its collaboration proposal and gauged its acquisition interest. On February 13, management presented to Amgen.

At the February 11 Five Prime Board meeting, Lazard updated the board on discussions (noting that Amgen, Parties C and D were exploring acquisitions; Party A remained more focused on collaboration; Parties E and F had declined participation; Party B had submitted a revised collaboration proposal; Party G had declined). The board determined that Lazard should notify Amgen, Party C, and Party D of a February 18, 2021 bid deadline for preliminary bids (Amgen and Party D) and a revised bid (Party C). Lazard executed its engagement letter with Five Prime, effective February 2, 2021. On February 11, Lazard verbally communicated the deadline to all three parties.

On February 14, Goldman Sachs (Amgen's financial advisor) and Lazard discussed the process, with Lazard noting that Five Prime had already received a competing proposal. On February 16, Party D's financial advisor indicated Party D was preparing to submit a proposal, and Party A submitted Revised Collaboration Proposal A. On February 18, Amgen submitted the Amgen Initial Acquisition Proposal at $32 per share. Later that day, Party D's financial advisor informed Lazard that Party D would not be submitting a proposal.

On February 19, Party C's representative called Ringo and verbally indicated Party C was in the high $20s to low $30s range but would not submit a revised written proposal. At the February 19 Five Prime Board meeting, with management, Cooley, and Lazard present, the board discussed the Revised Collaboration Proposal A, the Amgen Initial Acquisition Proposal ($32 per share), and the Verbal Acquisition Proposal C (high $20s to low $30s range). The board reviewed updated valuation analysis and determined to move forward with both Amgen and Party C with best and final bids due March 1, 2021. The board authorized Lazard to communicate to Party C that it would need to increase its price in line with recent biotech premiums to remain competitive.

On February 19, Lazard sent process letters and an initial draft Merger Agreement to Amgen and Party C with a March 1, 2021 deadline. Goldman Sachs and Lazard discussed next steps. Lazard spoke with Party C's financial advisors about the competitive nature of the process.

**Final Negotiations and Amgen Selection (February 20–March 3)**

On February 20, CEO Civik spoke with a Party D representative, who confirmed continued partnership interest but stated that Party D could not provide a competitive acquisition bid.

Cooley and Sullivan & Cromwell LLP (Amgen's counsel) had an introductory meeting on February 23 to discuss Merger Agreement terms, including tender offer structure, equity award treatment, regulatory matters, and termination rights. On February 25, Party C indicated it would submit a revised proposal by the deadline, and Cooley and Sullivan & Cromwell further discussed Amgen's positions on equity award treatment, Section 280G gross-ups for tax penalties, and regulatory matters. Lazard followed up with Party C's advisors emphasizing the importance of legal engagement given the targeted first-week-of-March signing date. On February 26, Sullivan & Cromwell sent a marked-up Merger Agreement draft to Cooley, which was shared with Company management and Lazard. Goldman Sachs and Lazard discussed process and competitive dynamics, with Lazard confirming the competitive nature and expectation of multiple proposals by March 1.

Also on February 26, the Compensation Committee met to approve the non-executive change-in-control severance plan and deferred annual executive equity awards pending receipt of final bids. The committee discussed and expressed support for 280G gross-up payments in connection with a potential strategic transaction, authorizing senior management and advisors to discuss such matters with counterparties.

On February 28, Cooley and Sullivan & Cromwell further discussed the draft Merger Agreement, including 280G gross-ups and cash awards (in lieu of equity) for executives and employees, and regulatory matters. Late on February 28, a Party C representative called Lazard to state that Party C would not be submitting a revised proposal, citing inability to offer more than the high $20s to low $30s per share range.

On March 1, Amgen submitted the Amgen Revised Acquisition Proposal at $38 per share, confirmed as "best and final." Goldman Sachs and Lazard discussed the bid and anticipated next steps. Sullivan & Cromwell shared revised regulatory language for the Merger Agreement.

At the March 2 Five Prime Board meeting, the board discussed the Amgen Revised Acquisition Proposal ($38 per share) and Revised Collaboration Proposal A. The board considered the relative merits of both proposals, including certainty of consummation. Lazard presented updated preliminary financial analysis. Cooley presented an overview of fiduciary duties and Merger Agreement terms. The board discussed whether to request a further price increase from Amgen and concluded not to do so, given Amgen's confirmation of "best and final" offer and risk that Amgen might retract. The board determined to proceed with final negotiations with Amgen and to terminate discussions with Party A. After the board meeting, Lazard informed Goldman Sachs of the board's determination to move forward with Amgen pending agreement on definitive terms.

Cooley sent a revised Merger Agreement and confidential disclosure letter to Sullivan & Cromwell on March 2. Evening discussions between Cooley and Sullivan & Cromwell addressed remaining open issues including termination fees and regulatory commitments.

On March 3, Sullivan & Cromwell and Cooley exchanged multiple drafts, and representatives finalized the Merger Agreement terms and exhibits. At the March 3 Five Prime Board meeting, Cooley updated the board on final negotiations and legal matters, including fiduciary duties and Merger Agreement provisions. Lazard reviewed its updated financial analysis and orally rendered its fairness opinion (subsequently confirmed in writing) that the $38.00 per share cash consideration was fair, from a financial point of view, to holders of shares (other than appraisal-exercising holders, treasury shares, or Amgen-affiliated shares).

The board unanimously determined that the Merger Agreement and Transactions (including the Offer and Merger) were advisable, fair to, and in the best interests of Five Prime and its stockholders. The board authorized execution and performance of the Merger Agreement, resolved that the Merger would be effected under DGCL Section 251(h), and resolved to recommend that stockholders tender their shares to Purchaser pursuant to the Offer.

On March 3, after the board meeting, Lazard informed Party A that Five Prime's senior management and board had decided not to focus on Collaboration Proposal A at this time.

**Execution and Consummation (March 4–April 16)**

On the morning of March 4, 2021, Five Prime, Amgen, and Purchaser executed the Merger Agreement. Also on March 4, prior to market opening, Amgen and Five Prime issued a joint press release announcing the transaction.

On March 18, 2021, Purchaser commenced the tender offer at $38.00 per share, with initial expiration set for April 16, 2021 (12:00 midnight New York Time, April 16 / 11:59 p.m. April 15). The Schedule TO and SC 14D9 were filed with the SEC.

On April 16, 2021, the tender offer expired. American Stock Transfer & Trust Company, LLC, as depositary, reported that 40,392,569 shares (87.8% of outstanding) had been validly tendered and not withdrawn, satisfying the Minimum Condition (requiring greater than 50% acceptance). Purchaser accepted for payment all validly tendered shares and paid within two business days. Following the offer's consummation, the Merger was effected immediately under DGCL Section 251(h) without a stockholder vote. Five Prime survives as a wholly owned subsidiary of Amgen. Each then-outstanding share not previously purchased in the Offer (other than treasury shares, Amgen-held shares, and appraisal-exercising holders' shares) was converted into the right to receive $38.00 per share cash.

**Notes on Shares Outstanding and Valuation:**

The SC 14D9 states that as of March 8, 2021, there were 45,941,900 shares issued and outstanding of Five Prime common stock, plus 4,859,903 shares subject to issuance pursuant to Five Prime's equity plans and 9,962 shares subject to rights under the Employee Stock Purchase Plan. The filing does not disclose a fully diluted share count used for valuation purposes. The $1.9 billion aggregate consideration mentioned in the 8-K (Item 2.01) reflects the cash paid for all validly tendered shares (40,392,569 shares at $38.00 = $1,534.9M) plus subsequent payment for non-tendered shares converted at closing. The $1,745.8 million figure represents the per-share offer price multiplied by the basic common shares outstanding as of the reference date in the SC 14D9.

Section 251(h) of the DGCL permitted completion of the Merger without a stockholder vote once Purchaser acquired greater than 50% of outstanding shares in the tender offer. The Minimum Condition was satisfied with 87.8% tender, and remaining shares were converted at the Offer Price at the Effective Time (April 16, 2021).

## Rationale

The acquisition addressed multiple strategic objectives for Five Prime and its stakeholders:

**For Five Prime and Its Shareholders:**

Five Prime's board determined that the $38 per share offer provided substantial and certain value relative to the risks and uncertainties of the standalone strategy. The board noted in the SC 14D9 that "the Offer Price was more favorable to Five Prime's stockholders than the potential value that might result from other alternatives available to Five Prime from a transaction with a different buyer or the continued operation of Five Prime on a standalone basis." The positive FIGHT trial results in November 2020 had created a strategic inflection point—the company had demonstrated efficacy but faced a path to Phase 3, manufacturing scale-up, regulatory approval, and commercialization with substantial capital requirements and execution risk. The filing states: "the Five Prime Board's assessment that Five Prime's standalone strategy (including a standalone path with a Bema Collaboration) was not reasonably likely to present opportunities for creating greater value for Five Prime's stockholders than the Offer Price, taking into account the time frame on which such value would be realized and the risks associated with the development, manufacturing, regulatory approval and commercialization of the Company's products and product candidates, as well as the competition Five Prime faces in the rapidly changing pharmaceutical industry."

The board also noted that "Five Prime may seek additional funding through future debt and equity financing or additional collaborations or strategic partnerships, any such fundraising could have a highly dilutive effect on Five Prime's existing stockholders, might only be available on unfavorable terms, or might not be available at all." The $38 offer provided immediate liquidity at a 79% premium to the March 3 closing price and a 99% premium to the 30-day VWAP.

**For Amgen:**

Amgen's strategic interest in Bemarituzumab reflected validation of the asset's efficacy in gastric and GEJ cancer, a large global market with limited treatment options. The Phase 2 data and scope of the Bema program (potential application in other FGFR2b+ cancers) made the asset attractive as either a standalone acquisition or a collaboration. Amgen's willingness to pay $38 per share (up from an initial $32 per share proposal on February 18) suggested confidence in the asset's development and commercialization potential. Amgen's acquisition of Five Prime added a late-stage oncology asset to its pipeline and eliminated a potential collaboration-only relationship by acquiring the full company and its science platform.

## Decision Notes

**Status: Completed**

The Amgen acquisition of Five Prime Therapeutics, Inc. is a completed acquisition. The transaction closed on April 16, 2021, when the tender offer expired, the Minimum Condition (greater than 50% of outstanding shares tendered) was satisfied with 87.8% tender, Purchaser accepted all validly tendered shares for payment, and the Merger was effected immediately under DGCL Section 251(h) without a stockholder vote. Five Prime survives as a wholly owned subsidiary of Amgen, and all remaining shareholders received $38.00 per share in cash.
