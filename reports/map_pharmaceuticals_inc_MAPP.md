## Summary

Allergan, Inc. acquired MAP Pharmaceuticals, Inc. for $25.00 per share in cash, totaling approximately $961 million. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a short-form merger, aimed at integrating MAP's migraine therapy LEVADEX into Allergan's neurology portfolio.

## Timeline

- 2011-01-28: MAP and Allergan enter into Collaboration and Co-Promotion Agreements for LEVADEX.
- 2012-10-29: Allergan CEO David Pyott sends a non-binding proposal to acquire MAP at $22.50 per share.
- 2012-11-02: MAP Board establishes a Special Committee to manage the acquisition process.
- 2012-11-08: MAP Board instructs Centerview to deliver a counterproposal of $25.00 per share.
- 2012-11-21: FDA accepts MAP's NDA resubmission for LEVADEX with a PDUFA date of April 15, 2013.
- 2012-11-28: Allergan indicates it is prepared to proceed with due diligence at $25.00 per share.
- 2012-12-17: Parties enter into a new Confidentiality Agreement with a standstill provision linked to exclusivity terms.
- 2013-01-22: MAP Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement at $25.00 per share.
- 2013-01-31: Purchaser commences the tender offer.
- 2013-02-28: Tender offer expires at midnight.
- 2013-03-01: Merger completed; MAP becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Allergan.

## Interaction History

The relationship began in 2009 with partnership discussions for LEVADEX. In late 2010, an unnamed 'Party 1' proposed an acquisition at $19-$21/share, prompting Allergan to express interest. Allergan initially bid $25.00 in Jan 2011 but withdrew it due to perceived FDA risks, opting instead for a commercial collaboration. In Aug 2011, Allergan proposed $21.00, later raised to $22.50, but MAP declined. Following the resubmission of the LEVADEX NDA in Oct 2012, Allergan returned with a $22.50 offer. MAP's CEO Timothy Nelson and the Special Committee (including Matthew McPherron, Scott Ward, and H. Ward Wolff) countered at $25.00. Negotiations involving Jeffrey Edwards (Allergan) and Centerview Partners (MAP) led to a non-binding agreement at $25.00 in Dec 2012. Final terms, including a $36 million breakup fee and a Top-Up Option, were negotiated by Latham & Watkins (MAP) and Gibson Dunn (Allergan) through Jan 2013.

## Rationale

The MAP Board determined the deal provided the highest value reasonably available, offering a 60% premium over the prior day's closing price. The board considered the risks of remaining independent, including the uncertainty of FDA approval for LEVADEX and the costs of commercial launch. Allergan sought to bolster its neurology franchise by adding a complementary acute migraine treatment to its existing BOTOX portfolio.

On **January 23, 2013**, Allergan, Inc. and MAP Pharmaceuticals, Inc. jointly announced a definitive merger agreement for Allergan to acquire MAP for **$25.00 per share** in cash (approximately $958 million). While the two companies had been in a co-promotion partnership for the migraine drug Levadex since January 2011, the acquisition itself was not preceded by public reports of a pending deal, price rumors, or specific competing bidders.
