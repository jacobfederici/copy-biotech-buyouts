## Summary

89bio, Inc. was acquired by Roche Holdings, Inc. via a tender offer for $14.50 per share in cash plus a non-tradeable contingent value right (CVR) of up to $6.00 per share. The transaction focused on 89bio's lead asset, pegozafermin, a Phase 3 FGF21 analog for the treatment of MASH and SHTG. The deal was completed on October 30, 2025, following the successful tender of approximately 60.49% of outstanding shares.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest in early 2025 following positive class data for FGF21 analogs. 89bio's CEO Rohan Palekar and Roche's Head of M&A Marc Buser led the discussions. Roche initially proposed a collaboration or license structure, but 89bio's board, advised by Centerview and Moelis, expressed a preference for an outright acquisition. Roche's initial bid of $13.00 (July 7) was rejected as insufficient. Subsequent rounds of bidding saw Roche introduce a CVR to bridge valuation gaps related to the Phase 3 pegozafermin program. On August 29, Roche delivered a 'best and final' offer of $14.50 cash plus a $6.00 CVR. 89bio attempted to negotiate a further extension of the CVR outside dates, which Roche partially accepted in the final August 29 proposal. Exclusivity was granted on August 29, and definitive agreements were finalized by September 17, 2025.

## Rationale

The 89bio board determined that the offer provided immediate liquidity and a significant premium (79% over the unaffected price) while mitigating the risks of operating as a single-asset company. The board noted the substantial capital requirements (~$1.1 billion) needed to reach commercialization and the potential for future dilutive financing. Roche sought to strengthen its metabolic disease pipeline with a late-stage asset (pegozafermin) that has Breakthrough Therapy Designation.
