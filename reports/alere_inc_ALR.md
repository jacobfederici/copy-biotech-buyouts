## Summary

Abbott Laboratories acquired Alere Inc. for $51.00 per common share in cash, representing an aggregate consideration of approximately $4.6 billion. The transaction followed a protracted period between the original January 2016 agreement and a subsequent April 2017 amendment. To secure regulatory approval from the FTC and European Commission, Alere divested its Triage MeterPro, BNP, and Epocal businesses to Quidel Corporation and Siemens Healthineers, respectively.

- 2017-10-03: Merger completed; Alere becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Abbott.

## Interaction History

The transaction was initially announced on January 30, 2016. Following the initial agreement, the parties engaged in extended negotiations and legal proceedings that led to an amendment on April 13, 2017. Regulatory scrutiny from the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) and the European Commission (EC) necessitated significant divestitures. Alere, led by CEO Namal Nawana and the board, negotiated the sale of the Triage MeterPro and BNP businesses to Quidel Corporation for approximately $400 million plus installment payments, and the Epocal business to Siemens. On July 7, 2017, 72.5% of Alere's outstanding common stock voted in favor of the merger. Abbott also conducted a separate tender offer for the Series B Preferred Stock to simplify the capital structure post-merger. The merger closed on October 3, 2017, after all regulatory conditions and divestiture milestones were addressed.

## Rationale

The Alere board determined that the merger was in the best interests of the stockholders. The transaction provided immediate liquidity at a significant premium. For Abbott, the acquisition expanded its leadership in point-of-care diagnostics. The divestitures were a strategic necessity to satisfy the anti-competition concerns of the FTC and EC.

## Evidence

- On October 3, 2017 (the 'Closing Date'), Abbott Laboratories... completed its acquisition of Alere Inc. (0001104659-17-060404)
- Each share of Alere’s common stock... was automatically converted into the right to receive $51.00 in cash per Share (0001104659-17-060404)
- The proposal to adopt the Merger Agreement was approved, receiving the affirmative vote of approximately 72.5% of the shares (0001193125-17-224356)

## Decision Notes

The deal value of $4.6B includes $4.5B for common shares and $100M for equity awards. Shares outstanding (87,368,721) sourced from the July 7, 2017 8-K reporting the special meeting results. Preferred stock was handled via a separate tender offer at $402.00/share.
