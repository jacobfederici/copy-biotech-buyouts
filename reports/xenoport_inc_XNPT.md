## Summary

XenoPort, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Arbor Pharmaceuticals, LLC on May 21, 2016, under which Arbor's subsidiary, AP Acquisition Sub, Inc., launched a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares of XenoPort for $7.03 per share in cash. The transaction was valued at approximately $449 million and was structured as a two-step acquisition under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, requiring no stockholder vote. Following the successful tender of approximately 62.95% of outstanding shares, the merger was completed on July 5, 2016, making XenoPort a wholly owned subsidiary of Arbor.

## Interaction History

Following a strategic shift in late 2015, XenoPort's management and Centerview Partners contacted over 30 potential counterparties. Initial interest from Party A at $7.25-$7.50 in stock was withdrawn in January 2016 due to market rumors. Party B engaged in extensive diligence but offered a lower cash price of $5.65, later raised to $6.40 plus CVRs, which the board found less certain than Arbor's all-cash bids. Arbor Pharmaceuticals entered the process in late March 2016. Negotiations led by Vincent Angotti (XenoPort CEO) and Ed Schutter (Arbor CEO) saw Arbor's bid move from $6.20 to $6.85 and finally to $7.03. The XenoPort board, advised by Weil, Gotshal & Manges and Centerview, pushed for higher value and the removal of financing conditions. Arbor secured debt financing from Deutsche Bank. On May 21, 2016, the board determined the $7.03 offer was fair and superior to remaining independent or accepting Party B's contingent offer.

## Rationale

The XenoPort board recommended the deal because the $7.03 cash price provided immediate liquidity and a 60% premium over the unaffected stock price. The board considered the risks of remaining independent, including the challenges of growing HORIZANT sales and potential generic competition. They also noted that the sale process was thorough, involving 30+ parties, and Arbor's offer was the highest certain value obtained.

Prior to the definitive acquisition agreement with Arbor Pharmaceuticals on May 23, 2016, XenoPort, Inc. (XNPT) was the subject of several reports regarding its exploration of a sale and pressure from activist investors.

The following is a chronological timeline of rumors and speculation:

*   **January 26, 2015**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** 13D Monitor / The Activist Report
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Not specified (Strategic Buyer)
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not reported
    *   **Details:** Reported that activist investor **Clinton Group, Inc.** was seeking to replace three directors on XenoPort's board. Clinton Group expressed the belief that the board should hire a financial advisor to explore a sale of the company if it could not successfully execute a new capital allocation strategy focused on its pipeline candidate XP23829.

*   **January 2016 (Early 2016)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** FierceBiotech / FirstWord Pharma (referencing earlier reports)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Not specified
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not reported
    *   **Details:** Multiple outlets later confirmed that "reports suggested" or there were "reports earlier this year" that XenoPort was in the early stages of exploring a strategic sale. These reports followed a series of R&D disappointments and the retirement of long-time CEO Ronald Barrett in late 2015.

*   **March 28, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Various (Market Speculation following Dr. Reddy's deal)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Not specified
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not reported
    *   **Details:** Following XenoPort's announcement of a $490 million licensing deal with **Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories** for its pipeline drug XP23829, market speculation increased that the company was "cleaning up" its balance sheet and narrowing its focus to its sole commercial asset, Horizant, to make itself a more attractive acquisition target for a specialty pharmaceutical company.

*   **May 23, 2016 (Pre-Market/Early Morning)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** StreetInsider / Bloomberg
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Arbor Pharmaceuticals
    *   **Rumored Price:** $7.03 per share
    *   **Details:** Just prior to the formal press release, reports circulated that XenoPort had reached a definitive agreement to be acquired by **Arbor Pharmaceuticals**. The rumored price was a significant premium (approximately 60%) over the previous closing price of $4.40. (This speculation immediately preceded the official announcement on the same day).
