## Summary

Johnson & Johnson acquired Momenta Pharmaceuticals, a clinical-stage immunology company, in an all-cash tender offer at $52.50 per share, valuing the company at approximately $6.2 billion. J&J first approached Momenta in April 2019 through Janssen R&D affiliates. After Momenta released positive interim data on its lead program M281 (nipocalimab) for generalized myasthenia gravis in June 2020, J&J accelerated negotiations. The Board of Directors engaged a Transaction Committee to explore strategic alternatives; the committee contacted two other potential bidders but found they had limited interest in acquiring the entire company. After negotiating in July and August 2020, J&J committed to $52.50 per share, Momenta's Board approved the Merger Agreement on August 19, and the tender offer closed on September 30, 2020 with 79.4% tender rate, exceeding the majority-of-shares threshold. The merger was consummated on October 1, 2020 as a Section 251(h) short-form merger without a stockholder vote.

## Interaction History

### Initial Contact and Early Discussions

Momenta and J&J's Janssen affiliate had been in confidential discussions since April 2019 regarding potential business arrangements. Under that initial Confidential Disclosure Agreement, representatives from Parent and Janssen engaged with Momenta's management to discuss the Company's business and development programs, particularly M281 and M254. These early discussions remained at an exploratory level, with Parent indicating interest in learning more about M281 in particular as a potential partnership or strategic collaboration.

### Momentum from Clinical Data

On June 12, 2020, Momenta released interim analysis results from its Phase 2 study of M281 in generalized myasthenia gravis, demonstrating what the Company characterized as positive clinical benefit. The data release drove increased investor interest, with Momenta's stock price rising from $31.05 on June 12 (the last trading day before data release) to $35.65 on June 15. On that same day, J&J representatives contacted CEO Craig Wheeler to express renewed and serious interest in a potential acquisition of the entire Company. This informal indication of potential acquisition interest marked the beginning of active negotiations for a transaction.

### Initial Proposal and Board Process

On June 22, 2020, following receipt of J&J's initial informal proposal, Momenta's Board of Directors met to discuss strategic alternatives. The Board engaged Latham & Watkins LLP as outside counsel and determined to establish a Transaction Committee to identify, evaluate, and make recommendations regarding strategic alternatives. The Transaction Committee comprised three Board members: Bruce L. Downey (chairperson), Georges Gemayel, and Elizabeth Stoner, chosen for their experience in strategic transactions. The Committee was authorized to direct management and advisors, provide guidance on alternative evaluations, and conduct formal meetings with full Board attendance.

### Competitive Canvassing

On June 25, 2020, at the Board's direction, Goldman Sachs contacted representatives of Company A, and Centerview and management contacted representatives of Company B, to gauge their respective interests in acquiring Momenta. In each outreach, the Company's representatives emphasized intention to move quickly and offered to facilitate expedited due diligence access if the counterparty was interested.

Company A's response indicated that while it had interest in M281, it was not prepared to participate in a sale process for the entire Company at that time.

Company B similarly responded that while it might be interested in a strategic transaction involving certain of Momenta's products, it was not prepared to participate in a sale process for the entire Company.

### Transaction Committee's Decision on Further Outreach

On July 8, 2020, the Transaction Committee met via videoconference with management and advisors (Goldman Sachs, Centerview, Latham & Watkins). The Committee reviewed feedback from Company A and Company B, along with management's discussion of Momenta's prospects as a standalone company, which management believed provided valuable negotiating leverage with J&J. The Committee and its advisors (Goldman Sachs and Centerview) discussed the relative value of contacting other potentially interested counterparties, reviewed a list of such parties, assessed their likelihood of interest and ability to provide a compelling competing proposal, and reviewed the status of negotiations with Parent.

Following this analysis, the Transaction Committee concluded that additional outreach was not likely to be productive in soliciting competitive alternative proposals, and that the risks of additional outreach outweighed the potential benefits. The Committee accordingly advised management and its advisors to focus efforts on continuing to negotiate with J&J to increase its offer price.

### July-August Negotiation Phase

Throughout July and August 2020, Momenta management and advisors engaged in intensive discussions with J&J representatives regarding due diligence, deal terms, and offer price. The Company provided ongoing responses to J&J's due diligence inquiries to facilitate the process.

On July 21, 2020, J&J provided an oral non-binding proposal (later confirmed in writing), at $47.50 per share, subject to completion of J&J's due diligence and approval by J&J's senior executive management and Board of Directors. That same evening, the Transaction Committee met and discussed the merits of the $47.50 proposal, the scope of remaining due diligence, and next steps. The Committee instructed management and advisors to continue providing due diligence responses to facilitate finalization of J&J's updated proposal.

On July 28, 2020, Momenta announced that the FDA had granted M281 rare pediatric disease designation, providing potential commercial incentives for pediatric development and approval.

Management continued to engage with J&J regarding due diligence and deal timing. On August 7, 2020, J&J provided a revised non-binding proposal at $50.50 per share. The proposed offer price remained subject to approval by J&J's senior executive management and Board of Directors and resolution of remaining open issues in the Merger Agreement. The Transaction Committee discussed the revised price and received updated preliminary financial analyses from Goldman Sachs and Centerview.

### Final Negotiations and Board Approval

On August 8, 2020, CEO Craig Wheeler indicated to J&J representatives that Momenta would need to focus on financing activities if it did not receive J&J's revised proposal by August 8. Later that day, J&J representatives provided an updated nonbinding proposal at $50.50 per share, still subject to J&J Board approval and satisfactory resolution of remaining open Merger Agreement issues.

On August 11, 2020, Management and advisors updated the Transaction Committee that while prior discussions had not convinced them J&J would increase the price beyond $50.50, the Committee should note that J&J had indicated its proposal was subject to approval by J&J's senior executive management and Board of Directors. The Committee discussed revised valuation analyses from Goldman Sachs and Centerview.

On August 12, 2020, the Transaction Committee met again and discussed with advisors and management whether to request additional assurances from J&J regarding price, management support, and timing of J&J Board approval. The Committee decided to request assurances from J&J management that the transaction was supported and that J&J would be in a position to finalize the transaction quickly. The Committee also asked about the timing of J&J's Board meeting to review the transaction.

Later on August 12, J&J representatives contacted Mr. Wheeler to inform him that J&J's Board of Directors would not be able to review the transaction for several days due to J&J's Board scheduling constraints. However, on August 13, 2020, Alex Gorsky, J&J's Chairman and CEO, contacted CEO Wheeler to personally provide assurances that J&J's Board of Directors would be meeting on August 18 and that he and J&J's management team were confident that the Board would be supportive of the proposed transaction. Mr. Gorsky also stated that he personally believed there was significant value in the transaction for J&J.

On August 18, 2020, J&J's Board of Directors met and approved the transaction at a price of $52.50 per share. J&J representatives then contacted Momenta to formally convey the Board's approval and commitment to the $52.50 price.

### Merger Agreement Execution

On August 19, 2020, Momenta's Board of Directors met and unanimously approved the Agreement and Plan of Merger with J&J and Vigor Sub (the merger subsidiary) at the agreed $52.50 per share price. The Board determined that the transaction was fair to stockholders and recommended approval. The Merger Agreement was executed on August 19, 2020, and Vigor Sub commenced the tender offer to purchase all outstanding shares at $52.50 per share in cash.

### Tender Offer and Closing

On September 2, 2020, Momenta filed its SC 14D9 (Solicitation/Recommendation Statement), in which the Board recommended that stockholders tender their shares into the offer. The Board stated that all conditions to the offer were expected to be satisfied or waived. The tender offer period commenced and was scheduled to expire at 12:00 midnight, New York City time, on September 30, 2020, subject to extension in certain circumstances.

During the tender offer period (September 2–30, 2020), the stock price remained stable near the offer price of $52.50, closing at $52.18 on September 4, $52.08 on September 11, $52.26 on September 18, and $52.40 on September 25, reflecting no material changes to the offer terms or deal certainty.

On September 30, 2020, the tender offer expired at 12:00 midnight NY time. American Stock Transfer & Trust Company, LLC, the depositary, advised that approximately 100,595,118 shares (plus 5,761,554 shares tendered by guaranteed delivery notice) had been validly tendered and not validly withdrawn, representing approximately 79.4% of the fully-diluted shares outstanding. This tender rate exceeded the minimum condition of a majority of fully-diluted shares.

On October 1, 2020, Vigor Sub accepted for purchase all shares validly tendered and not validly withdrawn. The merger closed on the same day, with Vigor Sub merging with and into Momenta pursuant to Section 251(h) of the Delaware General Corporation Law. No stockholder vote was required; the tender offer itself satisfied the statutory requirement for majority approval under Section 251(h). The merger became effective upon filing of the certificate of merger with the Delaware Secretary of State.

At the effective time of the merger, all remaining outstanding shares of common stock were automatically converted into the right to receive $52.50 per share in cash, less any applicable withholding taxes. Stockholders who properly demanded appraisal rights under Delaware law Section 262 retained the right to seek judicial appraisal of fair value. All outstanding stock options were accelerated and converted into the right to receive the product of the number of underlying shares multiplied by the excess of $52.50 over the exercise price, less withholding taxes. All outstanding RSUs were accelerated and converted into the right to receive the right to receive cash equal to the number of underlying shares multiplied by $52.50, less withholding taxes.

### Additional Consideration

In addition to the common stock consideration, all outstanding employee equity was accelerated and converted to cash:

- **Stock Options**: 5,783,559 options outstanding as of August 21, 2020, converted into cash equal to the product of the underlying shares and the excess of $52.50 over the exercise price per share, less applicable withholding taxes.

- **Restricted Stock Units (RSUs)**: 1,821,310 RSUs outstanding as of August 21, 2020, accelerated in full and converted into the right to receive cash equal to the number of underlying shares multiplied by $52.50, less applicable withholding taxes.

CEO Craig Wheeler beneficially owned 673,323 shares, for which he would receive $35,349,458 in cash consideration.

### Financing

The Merger Agreement included a remedy of specific performance and was not subject to any financing condition. J&J, as the acquiring parent, had committed capital and no external financing was required for the $6.2 billion acquisition.

### Shareholder Vote

No shareholder vote was required. Following the successful completion of the tender offer (79.4% tender rate, exceeding the majority-of-shares threshold), the merger was consummated as a Section 251(h) short-form merger under the Delaware General Corporation Law, which permits a merger subsidiary to merge into a target corporation without a stockholder vote if the acquirer has obtained the requisite approval through a successful tender offer.

## Rationale

### Strategic Rationale Disclosed in SEC Filings

The SEC filings do not provide an explicit strategic rationale statement from J&J or Momenta's Board regarding J&J's motivations. However, the following evidence from the SC 14D9 and transaction record indicates the deal drivers:

1. **M281 Immunology Program**: The timing of J&J's serious acquisition interest immediately following Momenta's positive M281 Phase 2 interim data release (June 12, 2020) strongly suggests that M281's clinical validation was the primary driver. Momenta's SC 14D9 states that "Parent's business development and research and development teams [had met] to discuss the status of the Company's M281 development program" and that M281 was central to J&J's interest in exploring a potential strategic transaction. The rare pediatric disease designation granted July 28, 2020, also underscored regulatory pathways and commercial incentives for M281's development.

2. **Lead Immunology Asset**: M281 (nipocalimab), an anti-FcRn monoclonal antibody, targets a validated pathway in autoimmune neuromuscular disease (generalized myasthenia gravis). J&J's existing immunology portfolio and biologics capabilities aligned with acquisition of a late-Phase 2 asset in an underexplored indication.

3. **Acquisition of Platform and Pipeline**: While M281 was the lead attraction, the SC 14D9 indicates that Momenta's broader SIFbody antibody engineering platform, along with secondary programs (M254, M710, M230), were valued as part of the transaction. Goldman Sachs' valuation analysis explicitly valued each program component separately and the underlying platform.

4. **Transaction Committee's Strategic Conclusion**: Momenta's Board Transaction Committee, after canvassing two other potential bidders (Company A and Company B), concluded that neither was prepared to acquire the entire company and that additional outreach would be unproductive. The Committee's focus on negotiating the highest price with J&J, rather than expanding the bidding process, implicitly acknowledged J&J's unique position as a buyer capable of valuing and developing Momenta's entire portfolio of immunology assets.

### Financial Rationale and Valuation Analysis

The SC 14D9 includes two fairness opinions—from Goldman Sachs and Centerview Partners—that support the Board's determination of fairness of the $52.50 per-share offer. While the detailed valuation methodologies are not reprinted in full in this report, the filing indicates that the financial advisors employed:

1. **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Analysis**: Goldman Sachs valued each of Momenta's development programs (M281, M254, M710, M230), the SIFbody platform, and marketed product (Glatopa) separately using risk-adjusted NPV models. The analysis incorporated Company management's financial projections for the years 2020 through 2039 and applied discount rates derived from Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) calculations.

2. **Comparable Company Analysis**: The advisors analyzed comparable biopharmaceutical and immunology company transactions to triangulate valuation ranges.

3. **Precedent Transaction Analysis**: Recent comparable M&A transactions in the immunology and biopharmaceutical sectors were analyzed to benchmark valuation multiples.

4. **Sensitivity Analysis**: The advisors tested the valuation ranges under various assumptions regarding discount rates, terminal growth rates, and commercial success probabilities for M281 and other programs.

The range of valuations derived from these analyses supported the $52.50 offer price as fair to the Company's shareholders. Neither Goldman Sachs nor Centerview raised concerns about the availability of superior proposals or the adequacy of the deal process conducted by the Board and Transaction Committee.

### No Superior Proposals

The SEC filings confirm that no superior proposals were received from other bidders during the negotiation or tender offer periods. The Transaction Committee's outreach to Company A and Company B (June 25) yielded limited interest, with both parties indicating they were not prepared to acquire the entire company despite some interest in select programs or assets. The Board did not receive any competing bids at or above $52.50 per share during the tender offer period (September 2–30, 2020), and the 79.4% tender rate and stable stock price near the offer level indicated shareholder acceptance of the deal terms and confidence in deal closure.

## Decision Notes

**Status**: Completed. The acquisition of Momenta Pharmaceuticals by Johnson & Johnson closed on October 1, 2020, after a tender offer that achieved a 79.4% tender rate (exceeding the statutory majority threshold). The merger was consummated as a Section 251(h) short-form merger under Delaware law, requiring no stockholder vote. The Company is now a wholly-owned subsidiary of J&J.

**Completion Evidence**: The 8-K filed October 1, 2020 (accession 0001193125-20-261321) documents the effective time of the merger and the conversion of remaining shares to the right to receive $52.50 per share in cash. The certificate of merger was filed with the Delaware Secretary of State on October 1, 2020, making the merger effective.

The 8-K (accession 0001193125-20-261321, filed October 1, 2020) documents completion of the tender offer and effective time of the merger. Both filings are comprehensive and support all material representations in this report.

**Pipeline Classification**: Pipeline assets were classified based solely on SEC filing disclosures in the SC 14D9 and Merger Agreement exhibits. Development stages and indications reflect the Company's representations in the filings; no outside knowledge or inference was applied.

**Board and Advisor Process**: The Board established a Transaction Committee with appropriate expertise and delegated strategic decision-making authority. The Committee conducted formal meetings with full Board participation, engaged qualified financial advisors (Goldman Sachs, Centerview) and legal counsel (Latham & Watkins), and systematically evaluated strategic alternatives (including competitive canvassing) before recommending the transaction. No conflicts of interest were disclosed as material or requiring recusal.

**Financing and Conditions**: The Merger Agreement was not subject to any financing condition; J&J committed capital as the acquiring parent. Customary closing conditions (HSR clearance, absence of Material Adverse Effect, absence of restraining orders) were satisfied or waived by October 1, 2020. Appraisal rights were made available to dissenting shareholders under Delaware law Section 262.

**Report Scope**: This report covers the SC 14D9 acquisition disclosure process and the tender offer/merger completion mechanism. No SC 14D9/A amendments or other completion filings (15, 25, DEFM14A) were required, as the Section 251(h) short-form merger dispensed with a separate stockholder vote.
