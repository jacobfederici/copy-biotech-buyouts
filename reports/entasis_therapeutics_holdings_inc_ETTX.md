## Summary

Entasis Therapeutics Holdings Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with its majority shareholder, Innoviva, Inc., under which Innoviva acquired all outstanding shares of Entasis not already owned by Innoviva for $2.20 per share in cash. The transaction was structured as a two-step tender offer followed by a merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The deal followed a period of significant capital constraints for Entasis and an extensive market check that failed to produce alternative bidders.

## Timeline

- 2020-04-12: Innoviva makes initial private placement investment in Entasis, becoming a majority shareholder.
- 2021-09-27: Entasis engages MTS Health Partners to explore strategic alternatives.
- 2021-10-25: Entasis Board establishes a Special Transactions Committee to assist in exploring strategic transactions.
- 2022-02-01: Innoviva submits a non-binding proposal to acquire Entasis for $1.80 per share in cash.
- 2022-02-17: Entasis enters into a $15 million bridge financing agreement with Innoviva via a convertible promissory note.
- 2022-03-15: Innoviva increases its offer to $2.00 per share in cash.
- 2022-03-21: Willkie Farr & Gallagher (Innoviva counsel) sends initial draft Merger Agreement to Covington & Burling (Entasis counsel).
- 2022-05-22: Innoviva increases its offer to $2.20 per share, designated as its 'best and final' offer; Special Transactions Committee and Entasis Board approve the transaction.
- 2022-05-23: Merger Agreement and Amendment No. 1 to the Investor Rights Agreement are executed and announced.
- 2022-06-07: Innoviva commences the tender offer.
- 2022-07-07: Tender offer expires with approximately 60.45% of the unaffiliated shares tendered.
- 2022-07-11: Merger completed; Entasis becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Innoviva.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2022 after Entasis faced difficulties raising capital through 'at-the-market' offerings and private placements with third parties. On February 1, 2022, Innoviva proposed an acquisition at $1.80 per share. The Entasis Special Transactions Committee, advised by MTS Securities and Covington & Burling, oversaw the process. MTS contacted 21 potential counterparties, and Innoviva facilitated introductions to 13 others; however, no other firm offers emerged. Innoviva increased its bid to $2.00 on March 15, 2022. Negotiations continued over several months regarding deal structure, employee retention, and bridge financing. On May 22, 2022, Innoviva delivered a 'best and final' offer of $2.20 per share. The Special Transactions Committee recommended the offer to the full Board, which then unanimously approved the Merger Agreement, noting that Innoviva's 60% ownership made alternative transactions impossible without their consent.

## Rationale

The Entasis Board determined the deal was fair due to the company's substantial future financing needs for commercializing SUL-DUR and its historical difficulty in raising capital. The Board noted that as a controlled company (60% owned by Innoviva), any alternative transaction was impossible without Innoviva's consent. The cash offer provided immediate liquidity and a premium over the trading price prior to the initial February proposal. Innoviva sought to consolidate its ownership to fully capture the potential value of the Entasis pipeline.

## Evidence

- The Entasis Board unanimously adopted resolutions (1) determining that the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated by the Merger Agreement, including the Offer and the Merger, are fair to and in the best interests of the Company and its stockholders (other than the Excluded Holders) (0001140361-22-022140)
- On July 11, 2022, following consummation of the Offer, Purchaser merged with and into the Company, with the Company as the Surviving Corporation. (0001140361-22-025725)

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, and non-binding offers for Entasis Therapeutics Holdings Inc. (ETTX) that occurred prior to the definitive merger agreement announced on May 23, 2022.

*   **February 1, 2022**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** SEC Filing (Schedule 13D/A) / Endpoints News
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Innoviva, Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $1.80 per share in cash.
    *   **Details:** Innoviva, which already owned approximately 60% of Entasis, submitted a non-binding proposal to acquire all remaining outstanding shares it did not already own. The offer represented a 23% premium over the previous day's closing price of $1.47. Innoviva also expressed a willingness to provide at least $15 million in interim financing to support operations during the negotiation period.

*   **February 2, 2022**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Endpoints News / Seeking Alpha
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Innoviva, Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $1.80 per share.
    *   **Details:** Media outlets reported on the "small-dollar buyout offer" revealed in the SEC filing. Reports highlighted that the cash-strapped biotech was facing a liquidity crunch (with cash expected to last only until Q2 2022) and that the $1.80 offer was a fraction of its 2018 IPO price of $15.00.

*   **March – May 2022**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Various Financial News (e.g., Fierce Biotech, Streetwise Reports)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Innoviva, Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $1.80 per share (initial bid).
    *   **Details:** Following the initial February bid, market speculation continued regarding whether Entasis would accept the $1.80 offer or negotiate for a higher premium. Analysts noted that the company was "fielding offers" and that a deal was likely given Innoviva's majority stake and the biotech's need for capital to fund the NDA submission for its lead asset, SUL-DUR.

*   **May 23, 2022 (Pre-Market/Early Morning)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** GlobeNewswire / Bloomberg
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Innoviva, Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $2.20 per share.
    *   **Details:** Immediately preceding the formal confirmation of the merger, reports circulated that the two companies had reached an improved agreement. This final speculation was confirmed shortly thereafter by the official announcement of a definitive merger agreement at $2.20 per share, a 50% premium to the unaffected price on January 31, 2022.
