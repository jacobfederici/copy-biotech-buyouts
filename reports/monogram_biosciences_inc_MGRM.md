## Summary

Monogram Biosciences, Inc. was acquired by Laboratory Corporation of America Holdings (LabCorp) via a cash tender offer of $4.55 per share, followed by a short-form merger. The transaction was valued at approximately $105 million and aimed to integrate Monogram's specialized HIV and oncology molecular diagnostic capabilities into LabCorp's clinical laboratory network.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2008 as Monogram faced significant capital requirements and $55 million in maturing convertible debt (Pfizer and Highbridge). David King (LabCorp CEO) and William Young (Monogram CEO) led the discussions. Initial offers in the $3.00-$4.00 range were rejected as undervaluing the company. After scientific diligence on the VeraTag oncology platform, LabCorp raised its offer to $5.25 in March 2009. However, in April 2009, LabCorp lowered the price to $4.35 after re-evaluating the cost of the 'put right' on Monogram's 0% notes. Monogram countered with various Contingent Value Right (CVR) structures tied to HERmark revenue milestones. Ultimately, LabCorp offered a choice between a CVR-heavy structure and a $4.55 all-cash price. The Monogram board chose the all-cash certainty over the illiquid CVRs. Lazard Freres provided the fairness opinion. Regulatory clearance was obtained under the HSR Act, and the deal closed via a short-form merger after LabCorp reached over 90% ownership through the tender and a Top-Up Option exercise.

The Monogram board recommended the deal due to the significant 151% premium over the current stock price and the immediate cash liquidity provided to stockholders. The board also considered the company's precarious financial position, including $55 million in debt maturing in 2010 and the high dilution (estimated at 50%) that would result from alternative standalone restructuring and financing plans. LabCorp sought to acquire Monogram to expand its specialized diagnostic testing capabilities in personalized medicine, particularly in HIV and oncology.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

No pre-deal rumors exist for the acquisition of Monogram Biosciences, Inc. (MGRM) by Laboratory Corporation of America Holdings (LabCorp).

The acquisition was announced on **June 23, 2009**, via a definitive agreement for LabCorp to acquire Monogram for $4.55 per share in cash. Financial news reports from that time, such as those from *Dark Daily* and various market analysts, noted that Monogram's shares had been trading under $2.00 in the weeks leading up to the announcement, and the deal was characterized as a surprise to the broader market rather than the culmination of public speculation. 

While Monogram had high-profile collaborations with companies like **Pfizer** (specifically for the *Trofile* companion diagnostic for the HIV drug Selzentry), these were established commercial partnerships and did not generate documented acquisition rumors in major financial outlets prior to the LabCorp deal. 

*(Note: There is a separate company, Monogram Technologies Inc., which also uses the ticker MGRM and was the subject of acquisition news by Zimmer Biomet in 2025; however, this is a distinct entity from the Monogram Biosciences acquired in 2009.)*
