## Summary

On October 10, 2021, Adamas Pharmaceuticals Inc, a Delaware corporation based in Emeryville, California, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Supernus Pharmaceuticals, Inc., a Delaware corporation, and Supernus Reef, Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of Supernus formed to effect the transaction. Supernus Reef commenced a tender offer to purchase all outstanding shares of common stock of Adamas at $8.10 per share in cash, plus contingent value rights (CVRs) providing for potential additional payments of up to $1.00 per share based on specified conditions under a CVR Agreement to be entered into at the time of tender offer commencement. The transaction valued Adamas at approximately $370.7 million in upfront equity value and up to $416.5 million including maximum CVR consideration. As of October 25, 2021, Adamas had 45,766,965 shares of common stock issued and outstanding. The Board of Directors of Adamas recommended acceptance of the tender offer and the merger to shareholders. The tender offer structure was documented in Schedule 14D-9 filings, with the final recommendation statement filed October 25, 2021 (accession 0001104659-21-129362).

## Interaction History

The Agreement and Plan of Merger dated October 10, 2021 documents the transaction between Adamas Pharmaceuticals, Inc., Supernus Pharmaceuticals, Inc., and Supernus Reef, Inc. (as merger subsidiary). The SEC filings establish that on October 10, 2021, the Board of Directors of Adamas recommended the tender offer and merger to shareholders. Christopher B. Prentiss is identified as Chief Financial Officer of Adamas Pharmaceuticals, Inc. at 1900 Powell Street, Suite 1000, Emeryville, California 94608, and authorized contact person for the filing.

The filings identify Cooley LLP counsel at 101 California Street, 5th Floor, San Francisco, CA 94111-5800, with counsel Jamie Leigh, Ian Nussbaum, and (in the October 25 filing) Polina A. Demina providing legal representation. The filings note that at the time of tender offer commencement, Supernus and Purchaser will file a Tender Offer Statement on Schedule TO (including an Offer to Purchase) with the SEC, and thereafter Adamas will file a Solicitation/Recommendation Statement on Schedule 14D-9 with the SEC. The Tender Offer materials and merger documents (including the Agreement and Plan of Merger and CVR Agreement) will be filed with the SEC and made available to investors and security holders free of charge.

The structure contemplated that the consummation of the offer would be subject to various conditions, including there being validly tendered and not withdrawn a minimum number of shares sufficient for consummation, and satisfaction or waiver of other conditions set forth in the Merger Agreement. A termination fee of $16 million was disclosed as a provision of the Merger Agreement. The filings do not disclose specific details of prior contacts, negotiations, or bidding dynamics not already reflected in the October 10, 2021 execution of the Merger Agreement.
