## Summary

Applied Therapeutics, Inc. was acquired by Cycle Group Holdings Limited via a tender offer for $0.088 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $0.40 per share based on regulatory and commercial milestones. The transaction was driven by the target's distressed financial state and regulatory setbacks for its lead candidate, govorestat.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest in late 2025 after Applied Therapeutics faced a liquidity crisis following an FDA Complete Response Letter. Interim CEO Les Funtleyder and CBO Constantine Chinoporos engaged with multiple parties (Parties A-H). Cycle Group Holdings, led by CEO James Harrison, emerged as the primary bidder. Initial proposals from Cycle in November 2025 suggested $0.10 per share. Applied Therapeutics countered to secure an unsecured bridge loan (Promissory Note) to maintain operations through closing. A competing bid from 'Party E' for $20 million cash was rejected because it required 6-8 weeks of diligence that the company's cash runway could not support. Final negotiations on December 11, 2025, resulted in a price adjustment from $0.093 to $0.088 per share to account for dilution from At-The-Market (ATM) equity sales. The board approved the deal on December 11, 2025, citing the risk of bankruptcy as the primary alternative.

## Rationale

The Board recommended the deal due to the company's 'challenged financial position,' including a stockholders' deficit and total liabilities exceeding assets. Management advised that without the sale, the company would need to pursue a wind-down or bankruptcy, which would likely result in zero recovery for stockholders. The Cycle offer provided immediate liquidity via an $8.5 million promissory note and potential future upside through CVRs.
