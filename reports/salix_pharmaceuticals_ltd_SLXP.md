## Summary

Salix Pharmaceuticals, Ltd. was acquired by Valeant Pharmaceuticals International via a cash tender offer and subsequent merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The transaction was initially valued at $158.00 per share but was increased to $173.00 per share following a competing interest period, resulting in an aggregate transaction value of approximately $11.3 billion.

## Timeline

- 2013-11-07: Salix announces Santarus acquisition; Party A CEO contacts Salix CEO Carolyn Logan for a meeting.
- 2014-07-08: Salix enters into a merger agreement with Cosmo Pharmaceuticals S.p.A.
- 2014-07-29: Party A makes an oral offer to acquire Salix at $180.00 per share in cash.
- 2014-08-26: Party A increases offer to $205.00 per share in exchange for exclusivity.
- 2014-09-23: Party A reduces offer to $175.00 per share citing wholesaler inventory concerns; Party B expresses interest.
- 2014-10-02: Salix terminates the Cosmo merger agreement.
- 2014-11-06: Salix announces Audit Committee review of inventory disclosures and CFO resignation; stock price drops ~35%.
- 2015-01-20: J.P. Morgan contacts Valeant CEO J. Michael Pearson to gauge interest.
- 2015-02-08: Valeant CEO Pearson conveys oral indication of interest at $150.00 per share in cash.
- 2015-02-17: Salix Board authorizes counterproposal to Valeant at $160.00 per share.
- 2015-02-20: Salix and Valeant agree to $158.00 per share; Merger Agreement executed.
- 2015-03-16: Merger Agreement amended to increase price to $173.00 per share to ensure rapid closing.
- 2015-04-01: Tender offer expires; Merger completed.

## Interaction History

Negotiations involved multiple parties (A through G). Initial high-value interest from Party A at $205.00 in mid-2014 collapsed due to Salix's wholesaler inventory issues. Following a significant stock price decline in November 2014, Salix re-engaged with potential buyers. Valeant emerged in January 2015, initially offering $150.00. Salix Acting CEO Thomas D'Alonzo negotiated the price up to $158.00 by February 20, 2015. During the tender period, amid market speculation of other bidders, Valeant and Salix amended the agreement on March 16, 2015, to increase the price to $173.00 per share, contingent on a closing by April 8, 2015, to provide certainty and preempt competing bids. Regulatory clearance was obtained via HSR filings on February 27 and March 2, 2015.

## Rationale

The Salix board determined the deal was superior to a standalone plan due to significant risks including: liquidity pressure from $345M in notes due May 2015, management instability following the CEO retirement and CFO resignation, regulatory uncertainty regarding Xifaxan 550's IBS-D approval, and the ongoing Audit Committee review into wholesaler inventory levels which threatened distributor relationships.

## Evidence

- On April 1, 2015, Purchaser merged with and into Salix (the 'Merger'), with Salix surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of VPI (0001193125-15-116335).
- Pursuant to the Amendment, among other things, (i) the offer price was increased from $158.00 per share to $173.00 per share (0001193125-15-093991).

## Decision Notes

The deal was accepted based on the final $173.00 price. Shares outstanding (64,248,604) and restricted stock (1,361,497) were sourced from the Item 1 'Securities' section of the SC 14D9. The $11.3B value includes the cash-out of options and restricted stock.
