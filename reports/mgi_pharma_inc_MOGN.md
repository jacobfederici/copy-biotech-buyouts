## Summary

MGI PHARMA INC was acquired by Eisai Co., Ltd. via a cash tender offer for $41.00 per share, valuing the company at approximately $3.3 billion. The transaction followed a competitive strategic review process initiated by MGI PHARMA after receiving unsolicited interest from Eisai. The deal was structured as a two-step merger, with a tender offer followed by a short-form merger after Eisai acquired over 90% of the outstanding shares.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2005 with collaborative discussions between Eisai and MGI PHARMA. In September 2006, Eisai CEO Haruo Naito formally proposed an acquisition to MGI CEO Leon Moulder. MGI initially resisted, focusing on its independent business plan. In June 2007, Eisai offered $33.00/share, prompting MGI to hire Lehman Brothers and conduct a broad 'sale process' involving 18 potential buyers. Eight parties signed NDAs and attended management presentations. By October 2007, Eisai offered $37.00 while 'Company B' offered $40.00. During final diligence in November/December, Company B withdrew, citing lack of strategic fit. Eisai raised its bid to $41.00 on December 5, 2007. MGI's legal counsel (Hogan & Hartson) and Lehman Brothers negotiated to reduce the termination fee from 3.65% to 3.3% and shorten the matching period. The MGI Board approved the $41.00 offer on December 9, 2007, after receiving a fairness opinion from Lehman Brothers.

## Rationale

Eisai sought to expand its worldwide oncology business and U.S. operations to mitigate the impact of future generic competition for its Alzheimer's drug, Aricept. MGI PHARMA's board determined that the $41.00 cash price provided a significant premium (38.7% over the pre-announcement price) and immediate liquidity, outweighing the risks of remaining an independent entity.

## Evidence

- On January 28, 2008, pursuant to the terms of the Merger Agreement, Purchaser merged with and into MGI PHARMA (the 'Merger'), with MGI PHARMA continuing as the surviving corporation (0001104659-08-005146).
- The MGI PHARMA Board unanimously recommends that you accept the Offer and tender your Shares into the Offer (0001193125-07-270122).

## Decision Notes

The deal value is computed as $41.00 per share multiplied by 81,268,043 shares outstanding as of Nov 30, 2007. The 8-K filing on Jan 28, 2008, confirms the total value of shares purchased was approximately $3.1 billion in the offer plus $206.8 million in the merger, totaling ~$3.3 billion.
