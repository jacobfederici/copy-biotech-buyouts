## Summary

Gilead Sciences, Inc. acquired Arcellx, Inc. through a tender offer for $115.00 per share in cash plus one CVR for $5.00, contingent on anito-cel sales. The transaction, valued at approximately $7.1 billion, closed on April 28, 2026, following the unanimous recommendation of Arcellx's Board of Directors.

## Timeline

- 2022-12-08: Arcellx and Kite Pharma, a subsidiary of Parent, entered into a Collaboration and License Agreement to co-develop and co-commercialize anito-cel and next-generation CAR-T cell therapy products. Parent and Arcellx also entered into a Common Stock Purchase Agreement and a Standstill Agreement.
- 2023-11-15: Arcellx and Kite amended the Collaboration Agreement, expanding its scope to include lymphomas. Kite exercised its option to negotiate a license for ACLX-001. Parent and Arcellx entered into a Second Stock Purchase Agreement and an Amended and Restated Standstill Agreement.
- 2026-02-13: Daniel O’Day, Chairman and CEO of Parent, informed Rami Elghandour, Chairman and CEO of Arcellx, of Parent's non-binding indication of interest to acquire Arcellx for $98.00 per Share in cash (Initial Parent Proposal). Parent delivered the written proposal, an initial draft of the Merger Agreement, and a draft non-disclosure agreement. Arcellx Board held a meeting to discuss the proposal, determining not to conduct broad outreach but directed Centerview to contact Party A.
- 2026-02-14: Centerview contacted Party A, who declined to submit a proposal. Centerview met with BofA Securities, Parent's financial advisor, who reiterated Parent's accelerated timeline and the impact of the Collaboration Agreement on valuation. Arcellx Board held a meeting, reviewed management's long-range plan (Projections), and directed management and Centerview to inform Parent that the Initial Parent Proposal was insufficient, seeking a valuation in the 'double-digit' billions (approximately $150.00 per Share). Mr. Elghandour conveyed this to Mr. O’Day, who rejected the valuation but suggested a CVR could bridge the gap. Arcellx Board held an informal update meeting.
- 2026-02-15: Centerview and BofA Securities held several conversations to discuss potential valuation terms and structuring. Arcellx Board held an informal update meeting.
- 2026-02-16: Mr. Elghandour spoke with Mr. O’Day, suggesting the Board would consider a proposal with at least $8.0 billion in upfront consideration (approximately $118.00 per Share) plus an attractive CVR. Mr. O’Day countered with $110.00 per Share in cash plus a CVR. Arcellx Board instructed Mr. Elghandour and advisors to seek at least $118.00 per Share upfront. Centerview and BofA Securities continued discussions. BofA Securities informed Centerview that Parent would increase the offer to $110.00 per Share in cash plus a CVR of up to $10.00 per Share (Second Parent Proposal), which Centerview indicated was still insufficient.
- 2026-02-17: Arcellx Board held an update meeting, directing Mr. Elghandour and advisors to seek at least $120.00 per Share, comprising at least $115.00 per Share upfront and $5.00 CVR. Centerview and BofA Securities discussed terms, with Centerview suggesting $125.00 per share ($115.00 upfront + $10.00 CVR). BofA Securities presented Parent's 'best and final' offer: $115.00 per Share upfront cash plus a $5.00 CVR, payable on March 31, 2030, subject to $6.0 billion cumulative net sales of anito-cel by December 31, 2029 (Final Parent Proposal). Arcellx Board held a meeting, discussed the Final Parent Proposal, and decided not to contact other potential acquirors due to various factors including the Collaboration Agreement, Parent's accelerated timeline, and the belief that the offer was the best reasonably obtainable. The Board instructed management and Wilson Sonsini to negotiate definitive agreements. Mr. Elghandour informed Mr. O’Day of the Board's decision to accept the Final Parent Proposal. Ropes & Gray provided an initial draft of the Tender and Support Agreement to Wilson Sonsini.
- 2026-02-18: Parent and Arcellx entered into a Confidentiality Agreement. Parent and its representatives were granted access to an online data room. Wilson Sonsini distributed a revised draft of the Merger Agreement to Ropes & Gray, proposing a 1.0% termination fee, a more Company-favorable 'Material Adverse Effect' definition, a more Company-favorable 'no-shop' provision, and a 'clear skies' antitrust covenant obligation on Parent. Negotiations on the Merger Agreement and other transaction documents continued.
- 2026-02-19: Centerview's relationship disclosure letter was circulated to the Arcellx Board.
- 2026-02-20: Ropes & Gray distributed a draft of the CVR Agreement to Wilson Sonsini. Negotiations on the CVR Agreement terms, including Parent's efforts to achieve the Milestone and Rights Agent audit rights, commenced. Arcellx Board held an update meeting.
- 2026-02-21: Arcellx Board held an update meeting.
- 2026-02-22: Arcellx Board held a meeting, approved Centerview's engagement, received Centerview's financial analysis and oral opinion (later confirmed in writing on February 23, 2026) that the Merger Consideration was fair. Wilson Sonsini reviewed fiduciary duties and final transaction documents. The Board unanimously approved the Merger Agreement and recommended the Offer. Arcellx, Parent, and Purchaser signed the Merger Agreement. Parent and Purchaser entered into Tender and Support Agreements with Supporting Stockholders.
- 2026-02-23: Arcellx and Parent publicly announced the execution of the Merger Agreement.
- 2026-03-06: Purchaser commenced the Offer.
- 2026-04-27: The Offer expired at 5:00 p.m., Eastern Time. 38,795,604 Shares were validly tendered and not validly withdrawn, representing approximately 77.2% of outstanding Shares. All conditions to the Offer were satisfied or waived, and Parent and Purchaser accepted all validly tendered Shares.
- 2026-04-28: Purchaser merged with and into Arcellx, Inc. pursuant to Section 251(h) of the DGCL. Arcellx became a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent. All directors and officers of Arcellx resigned, and the directors and officers of Purchaser became the directors and officers of Arcellx. Arcellx's certificate of incorporation and bylaws were amended and restated. Arcellx notified NASDAQ to suspend trading of Shares and initiated delisting and deregistration processes.

## Interaction History

On February 13, 2026, Daniel O’Day, Chairman and CEO of Parent, contacted Rami Elghandour, Chairman and CEO of Arcellx, to inform him of Parent's non-binding indication of interest to acquire Arcellx for $98.00 per Share in cash, implying an equity value of approximately $6.5 billion (the 'Initial Parent Proposal'). Mr. Elghandour conveyed the Arcellx Board's view that this valuation was insufficient. Parent's written proposal indicated a desire to complete the acquisition by the end of February 2026, prior to the potential launch of anito-cel later in 2026, and stated that Parent's interest would diminish if a definitive agreement was not reached by then. The initial Merger Agreement draft proposed an all-cash tender offer followed by a short-form merger and included a 3.5% termination fee. The Arcellx Board, with Centerview Partners and Wilson Sonsini, discussed the proposal and decided against broad outreach to other potential acquirors due to the accelerated timeline and the terms of the Collaboration Agreement. They directed Centerview to contact one large pharmaceutical company ('Party A'), which subsequently declined to submit a proposal. On February 14, 2026, Centerview and BofA Securities, Parent's financial advisor, discussed the accelerated timeline and the impact of the Collaboration Agreement on Parent's valuation. The Arcellx Board, after reviewing management's long-range plan (Projections), instructed Mr. Elghandour and Centerview to communicate that the Initial Parent Proposal was insufficient and that the Board sought a valuation in the 'double-digit' billions (approximately $150.00 per Share). Mr. Elghandour conveyed this to Mr. O’Day, who rejected the valuation but suggested a contingent value right (CVR) could bridge the valuation gap. On February 16, 2026, Mr. Elghandour proposed at least $8.0 billion in upfront consideration (approximately $118.00 per Share) plus an attractive CVR. Mr. O’Day countered with $110.00 per Share in cash plus a CVR. The Arcellx Board instructed its advisors to continue seeking at least $118.00 per Share upfront. BofA Securities then presented Parent's 'Second Parent Proposal' of $110.00 per Share in cash plus a CVR of up to $10.00 per Share, which Centerview indicated was still insufficient. On February 17, 2026, the Arcellx Board directed its advisors to seek at least $120.00 per Share, comprising at least $115.00 per Share upfront and a $5.00 CVR. BofA Securities then presented Parent's 'best and final' offer, the 'Final Parent Proposal,' of $115.00 per Share in upfront cash consideration, with a CVR of $5.00 per Share, payable on March 31, 2030, subject to achievement of cumulative net sales of anito-cel of at least $6.0 billion through December 31, 2029. The Arcellx Board, after considering the negotiations, the unlikelihood of a superior offer given the Collaboration Agreement and Parent's timeline, and the risks of remaining independent, unanimously determined to accept the Final Parent Proposal. Mr. Elghandour informed Mr. O’Day of this decision. Negotiations on the Merger Agreement and CVR Agreement continued, with key areas of discussion including the termination fee, definition of 'Material Adverse Effect,' antitrust obligations, and non-solicitation provisions. On February 22, 2026, the Arcellx Board formally approved the Merger Agreement and recommended the Offer, following Centerview's fairness opinion. The Merger Agreement and Tender and Support Agreements were signed on the same day.

## Rationale

The Arcellx Board of Directors believed the Offer Price represented the best value reasonably obtainable for the Shares, providing certainty of value and liquidity while eliminating risks associated with the Company's business plan and financial markets. The CVR offered an opportunity for additional value realization. The Board considered the Company's competitive position, the terms of the existing Collaboration Agreement with Kite (a Parent subsidiary), and the accelerated timeline for Parent's offer, concluding that alternative strategies or acquirors were unlikely to provide superior value. The Board also weighed the significant execution risks of remaining an independent public company and the potential for litigation.
