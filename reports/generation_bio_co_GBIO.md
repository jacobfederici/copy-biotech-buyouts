## Summary

Generation Bio Co. was acquired by XOMA Royalty Corporation via a tender offer for $4.2913 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) representing potential payments from net cash adjustments, lease recoveries, and future proceeds from the Moderna collaboration and legacy assets. The transaction followed a strategic review process initiated after the company announced early-stage data for its cell-targeted lipid nanoparticle system and sought to maximize value for shareholders amidst a wind-down of independent operations.

## Interaction History

Following a data announcement in August 2025, Generation Bio and TD Cowen contacted 71 potential counterparties. Negotiations involved multiple rounds with Party A, Party B, Party C, and Party D. Party A initially offered $82 million but required high net cash levels. Party C offered $70 million. XOMA entered the process in October 2025. The Transaction Committee, led by Interim CEO Yalonda Howze and the Board, focused on the 'spread' between the company's net cash and the amount paid to stockholders. XOMA's initial proposal included a $2.5 million fee/spread. After Party D submitted a $0 spread proposal on December 7, XOMA improved its terms on December 8 to a $1.25 million spread. Final negotiations focused on the treatment of the Binney Lease liability, which was reserved at $20 million in the net cash calculation, with 90% of any savings to be returned via CVR. The Board approved the deal on December 15, 2025, after TD Cowen provided a fairness opinion.

The Board determined the transaction was the best available alternative to maximize stockholder value compared to a standalone liquidation or continued independent operation. Key factors included the immediate liquidity of the cash payment, the potential upside from the CVRs (particularly the Moderna collaboration), the company's substantial capital needs for further development, and the risks associated with clinical trials and regulatory processes.
