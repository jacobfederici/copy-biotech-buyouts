## Summary

Genmab A/S acquired Merus N.V. for $97.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer followed by a back-end merger and cancellation of shares. The transaction, valued at approximately $6.9 billion, aimed to integrate Merus's proprietary bispecific antibody platform and clinical-stage oncology assets into Genmab's portfolio.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2025 as a search for collaboration partners for petosemtamab. Genmab initiated acquisition interest in August 2025 with an $81.00 offer. Merus management, led by CEO Dr. Bill Lundberg and COO Peter Silverman, engaged in a competitive process involving three other parties (Parties A, B, and D). Party A bid $94.00 and Party B bid up to $90.00, but both eventually declined to submit revised bids following due diligence. Genmab increased its offer sequentially to $87.00, $95.00, and finally $97.00 after Merus requested a higher price and CVRs. The Merus Board, advised by Jefferies and Latham & Watkins, pushed for the $97.00 cash price as Genmab's 'best and final' offer. Regulatory clearances included HSR Act expiration and a 'no further questions' response from the UK CMA. The deal also required consultation with Dutch works councils and a specific tax ruling from the Dutch Tax Authority to facilitate the back-end structure.

## Rationale

The Merus Board determined the offer was in the best interests of the company and its stakeholders, providing immediate liquidity and a significant premium (41% over the last closing price). The board noted the risks of remaining a standalone clinical-stage company, including the need for substantial future capital and the inherent uncertainty of clinical trials and regulatory approvals. Genmab sought the acquisition to gain full control of Merus's bispecific antibody platform and clinical assets to realize operational and commercial synergies.

## Evidence

- On December 12, 2025, Purchaser accepted for payment all Common Shares validly tendered... resulting in a change of control. (8-K, 2025-12-12)
- The Merus Board hereby recommends that Merus’ shareholders accept the Offer and tender all of their Common Shares pursuant to the Offer. (SC 14D9)
- As a result of the Back-End Merger and the Back-End Cancellation, each Common Share held by shareholders... was cancelled... and converted into the right to receive an amount in cash equal to the Offer Consideration. (8-K, 2025-12-30)

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a Dutch-law specific back-end structure involving a merger and share cancellation. Shares outstanding (75,842,847) were sourced from the SC 14D9 as of October 13, 2025. CVRs were requested by Merus during negotiations but rejected by Genmab in favor of a higher upfront cash price.
