## Summary

GreenLight Biosciences Holdings, PBC was acquired by SW ParentCo, Inc., an affiliate of Fall Line Capital, LLC, via a cash tender offer for $0.30 per share. The transaction followed a period of severe capital constraints for GreenLight, which faced potential insolvency. The deal included a rollover of approximately 79.46% of the company's shares by existing stockholders and a $100 million convertible debt financing commitment to fund ongoing operations.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in February 2023 when Eric O’Brien (Fall Line Managing Director and GreenLight board member) approached CEO Andrey Zarur. A Special Committee was formed on March 8, 2023. Fall Line initially proposed $0.60 per share on March 29, but as GreenLight's cash position deteriorated and public stock prices fell, Fall Line lowered the bid to $0.40 on May 2 and finally to $0.30 on May 17. GreenLight management conducted an extensive market check, contacting 18 strategic parties and 120 potential investors; however, no other firm offers materialized. Party A (a multinational manufacturer) and Party B (a life science company) engaged in preliminary diligence but both withdrew citing the tight timeline and resource requirements. The Special Committee, advised by Roth Capital and Foley Hoag, ultimately accepted the $0.30 offer as the only viable alternative to bankruptcy. The deal was structured to include a $15 million immediate cash advance via notes to keep the company operational through the closing.

## Rationale

The Board and Special Committee recommended the deal because GreenLight was facing near-term insolvency and bankruptcy was the most likely alternative. The $0.30 price represented a 50% premium to the trading price at the time of approval. The transaction provided immediate liquidity to unaffiliated stockholders and secured $100 million in new financing to continue the company's public benefit purpose and mRNA research.

## Evidence

- On July 24, 2023, following consummation of the Offer, Merger Sub merged with and into the Company, with the Company surviving the merger (0001193125-23-191971).
- The Board unanimously recommended that the GreenLight stockholders tender their Shares to Merger Sub pursuant to the Offer (0001193125-23-171468).

## Decision Notes

The deal value of $9.3 million refers to the cash paid for non-rollover shares. The total implied equity value is higher (~$45.5M) but 79.46% of shares were rolled over into the new private entity rather than cashed out. Shares outstanding sourced from Item 1 of SC 14D9.
