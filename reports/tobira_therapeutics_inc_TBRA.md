## Summary

Tobira Therapeutics entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Allergan plc for an upfront cash payment of $28.35 per share plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $49.84 per share based on clinical and commercial milestones. The transaction was structured as a two-step tender offer under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, following a competitive bidding process involving multiple pharmaceutical companies.

## Timeline

- 2016-06-21: Allergan CEO Brenton Saunders meets with Tobira Chairman Dennis Podlesak and Director Eckard Weber.
- 2016-07-05: Allergan submits initial proposal of $310M upfront plus $740M in CVRs.
- 2016-07-25: Tobira announces Phase 2b CENTAUR trial results; stock price drops from $11.25 to $4.50.
- 2016-08-26: Allergan submits revised proposal of $400M upfront plus $1.0B in CVRs.
- 2016-09-04: Allergan orally submits revised proposal of $450M upfront plus $650M in CVRs.

- 2016-09-12: Party A submits indication of interest for $400M upfront plus $1.0B in CVRs.
- 2016-09-13: Party A increases offer to $500M upfront plus $900M in CVRs.
- 2016-09-17: Party A increases offer to $500M upfront plus $1.025B in CVRs; Allergan counters with $550M upfront plus $950M in CVRs.
- 2016-09-19: Final bids: Party A offers $550M upfront + $1.05B CVR; Allergan offers $595M upfront + $1.1B CVR. Tobira Board approves Allergan deal.

- 2016-10-31: Tender offer expires with 92.2% of shares tendered.
- 2016-11-01: Merger completed; Tobira becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Allergan.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2014 with Party A and Party B while Tobira was private. Following the July 2016 CENTAUR data release, Tobira engaged with 11 parties. Allergan, led by CEO Brenton Saunders, initially proposed a deal on July 5, 2016, which Tobira rejected as insufficient. After the stock price decline, Allergan re-engaged in August. A competitive process ensued between Allergan and 'Party A'. Tobira's Strategic Committee (Podlesak, Heron, Legault) and financial advisors (Centerview and Citi) pushed both bidders to increase upfront cash and near-term milestones. Party A submitted multiple bids, reaching $550M upfront on Sept 19. Allergan ultimately won the process by submitting a final bid on Sept 19 of $595M upfront plus $1.1B in CVR milestones. The board determined Allergan's bid had a higher probability-adjusted net present value ($1.082B vs $1.009B).

## Rationale

The Tobira Board recommended the deal due to the significant upfront premium (518% over the unaffected price), the immediate liquidity provided by the cash component, and the opportunity for stockholders to participate in future upside via CVRs. They also considered the high costs and risks of independent Phase 3 development and commercialization, noting Tobira's lack of global infrastructure and the need for substantial dilutive capital raises if remaining standalone.

## Evidence

- At a meeting of the Tobira Board held on September 19, 2016, the Tobira Board unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement, the Offer and the Merger are fair to, advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its stockholders (0001193125-16-728492).
- On November 1, 2016, Parent completed its acquisition of Tobira by consummating the Merger, without a meeting of stockholders of Tobira in accordance with Section 251(h) (0001193125-16-754602).
