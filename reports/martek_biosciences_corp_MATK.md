## Summary

Martek Biosciences Corporation entered into a definitive merger agreement with Koninklijke DSM N.V. on December 20, 2010, under which DSM's subsidiary, Greenback Acquisition Corporation, launched a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares of Martek for $31.50 per share in cash. The transaction, valued at approximately $1.09 billion, was structured as a two-step acquisition involving a tender offer followed by a short-form merger. The deal closed on February 25, 2011, after the purchaser exercised a top-up option to reach the 90% ownership threshold required for a short-form merger under Delaware law.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began with informal strategic alliance discussions between Martek CEO Steve Dubin and DSM's Alexander Wessels in late 2009. In late 2010, Martek's Strategic Review Committee (Messrs. Flanagan, Beery, D'Andrea, and MacMaster) oversaw a process involving DSM and several private equity firms (Parties B, C, D, and E). Party B and DSM emerged as the primary bidders. Party B's $31.00 proposal was contingent on financing and a 45-day exclusivity period. DSM initially offered $27-$30 but increased to $31.50 on December 8, 2010. Martek pushed for a 'go-shop' provision and lower termination fees. DSM refused the go-shop but agreed to a 'package' on December 16 that delayed the tender launch to allow a 'window shop' period, reduced the termination fee to 3.5% ($38 million), and shortened matching rights to three days. The Board favored DSM over Party B due to the higher certainty of closing without a financing contingency. Regulatory clearances were required in the US (HSR), Brazil, Ireland, Netherlands, Austria, and Spain.

## Rationale

The Martek board determined the deal was superior to remaining independent due to declining revenues and earnings in the core infant formula business caused by competition, pricing adjustments, and upcoming patent expirations. The board believed the $31.50 cash price provided immediate certainty of value and a significant premium (35% over the December 20 closing price) that the market was unlikely to reflect in the foreseeable future. DSM sought the acquisition to integrate its ARA supply chain and expand its nutritional ingredients portfolio.

## Decision Notes

The deal value of $1.09 billion is explicitly stated in the 8-K (Item 5.01). Shares outstanding (33,532,053) were sourced from the SC 14D9 Item 1(b). The transaction utilized a Top-Up Option to facilitate a short-form merger after the tender offer fell short of 90% but exceeded the majority threshold.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The acquisition of Martek Biosciences Corporation (MATK) by Royal DSM N.V. was officially announced on **December 21, 2010**. Prior to this announcement, there were no widely reported specific rumors naming DSM as the buyer or detailing a specific price. However, the company was the subject of general takeover speculation and unusual trading activity in the days immediately preceding the deal.

The following timeline details the rumors and speculative activity that appeared before the confirmed deal:

*   **2007 – 2009 (General Speculation)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Various financial commentators/analysts (referenced in retrospect by *Wikipedia* and *Reuters*).
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Not specified (General "Strategic Buyers").
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not reported.
    *   **Details:** During this period, analysts frequently labeled Martek a "pure play" investment target and a logical acquisition candidate for larger food or life sciences companies. This was due to Martek's near-monopoly (approx. 70-80% market share) on DHA and ARA algal oils used in infant formula, making it an attractive "bolt-on" for a diversified nutrition company.

*   **December 10 – December 15, 2010 (Unusual Options Activity)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reported post-deal by the *U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)* and *WholeFoods Magazine*.
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unknown at the time.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not reported.
    *   **Details:** While not a public "rumor" in the press, significant insider speculation occurred in the options market. An anonymous trader (later identified in an SEC insider trading lawsuit) purchased 2,615 call options on Martek stock. This unusual volume indicated market "whispers" or non-public knowledge of an impending takeover.

*   **December 20, 2010 (Pre-Announcement Market Speculation)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Bloomberg* / *Reuters* (Market monitoring).
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Not specified.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not reported.
    *   **Details:** On the day before the official announcement, Martek's stock experienced a notable rise in volume and price (closing at $23.36), leading to increased chatter among traders and on financial message boards (such as *InvestorVillage* and *Yahoo Finance*) regarding a potential year-end buyout, though no specific suitor was named in the mainstream press until the following morning.

**Confirmed Deal Announcement:**
On **December 21, 2010**, Royal DSM N.V. and Martek Biosciences officially announced a definitive agreement for DSM to acquire Martek for **$31.50 per share** in cash, representing a **35% premium** over the December 20 closing price.
