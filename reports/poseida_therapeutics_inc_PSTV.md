## Summary

Poseida Therapeutics entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Roche for $9.00 per share in cash plus a non-transferable contingent value right (CVR) of up to $4.00 per share. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The deal aimed to integrate Poseida's cell and gene therapy platforms, specifically its allogeneic CAR-T programs, into Roche's portfolio following a multi-year collaboration between the two companies.

## Timeline

- 2021-03-09: Poseida and F. Hoffmann-La Roche Ltd enter into a mutual non-disclosure agreement.
- 2022-07-30: Poseida and Roche enter into a strategic global collaboration and license agreement for allogeneic CAR-T cell therapies.
- 2023-04-17: Poseida CEO Mark Gergen informs Roche of unsolicited interest from another party; Roche indicates acquisition is not of current interest.
- 2023-08-08: Astellas makes a $50 million strategic investment in Poseida, gaining board observer and notification rights.
- 2024-05-22: Roche's James Sabry indicates interest in evaluating strategic options, including a potential acquisition.
- 2024-07-29: Poseida management gives a presentation to Roche executive leadership on proprietary assets outside the existing collaboration.
- 2024-08-27: Roche submits an initial non-binding proposal to acquire Poseida for $5.75 per share in cash.
- 2024-08-29: Poseida Board rejects the $5.75 proposal as insufficient.
- 2024-09-12: Roche submits a revised proposal of $7.50 per share plus $5.50 in CVRs.
- 2024-09-18: Poseida Board rejects the $7.50 proposal, seeking aggregate value in the 'teens'.
- 2024-10-29: Poseida counters with $9.50 upfront plus $7.00 in CVRs.
- 2024-11-01: Roche counters with $9.00 upfront plus $3.00 in CVRs.
- 2024-11-07: Roche submits final offer of $9.00 upfront plus $4.00 in CVRs; Poseida Board authorizes proceeding.
- 2024-11-25: Poseida Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement; definitive documents are executed.
- 2024-11-26: Public announcement of the transaction.
- 2024-12-09: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2025-01-07: Tender offer expires at 11:59 p.m. ET.
- 2025-01-08: Roche completes the acquisition of Poseida.

## Interaction History

Negotiations were led by Mark Gergen (Executive Chairman) and Kristin Yarema (CEO) for Poseida, and Boris Zaïtra for Roche. Following a 2022 collaboration, Roche initially declined acquisition interest in 2023 but re-engaged in May 2024. Roche's first formal bid of $5.75 (Aug 27) was rejected immediately. A second bid of $7.50 + $5.50 CVR (Sept 12) was also rejected as the Board believed the company was undervalued. After Poseida countered with $9.50 + $7.00 CVR (Oct 29), Roche moved to $9.00 + $3.00 CVR (Nov 1). Final negotiations centered on the CVR milestones and efforts clauses, resulting in the final $9.00 + $4.00 CVR agreement. Poseida also contacted 'Party X' and Astellas during the process; Party X declined interest on Nov 15, and Astellas declined on Nov 21.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

No pre-deal rumors exist for Poseida Therapeutics, Inc. (PSTX).

While the company was officially acquired by **Roche** in a deal announced on **November 26, 2024**, there were no public reports of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, or leaked takeover details in the media prior to the formal announcement.

Post-deal regulatory filings (specifically the **Schedule 14D-9** filed with the SEC) later revealed that Poseida had engaged in private discussions and received unsolicited interest in the years leading up to the sale, but these events were not reported as "rumors" by news outlets at the time they occurred:

*   **April 2023:** Poseida received an unsolicited acquisition proposal from an unnamed "Big Pharma" company. This remained confidential and was not reported by the press until after the Roche deal was announced.
*   **August 2023:** **Astellas Pharma** made a $50 million strategic investment in Poseida. While this established a partnership, it was reported as a financing and collaboration deal, not as a rumored takeover.
*   **August 2024:** Roche privately submitted its first acquisition proposal to Poseida at $5.75 per share. This remained a private negotiation and did not leak to the public or reporting outlets.

The acquisition was a "surprise" to the market, as evidenced by the stock's **215% surge** on the day of the announcement, which typically indicates that no credible rumors had been priced into the stock beforehand.
