## Summary

Concert Pharmaceuticals entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Sun Pharma for $8.00 per share in cash plus a non-transferable CVR worth up to $3.50 per share based on sales milestones for deuruxolitinib. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, aimed at providing Concert with the resources needed to commercialize its lead alopecia areata candidate while mitigating its declining cash position.

## Timeline

- 2022-07-12: Concert and Sun Pharma enter into a mutual Confidential Disclosure Agreement.
- 2022-08-30: Concert Board approves engagement of MTS Health Partners and Chestnut Partners; establishes Transaction Committee.
- 2022-09-11: MTS begins contacting 39 potential counterparties for a strategic transaction.
- 2022-11-11: Sun Pharma submits a non-binding proposal for a worldwide license of deuruxolitinib.
- 2022-11-23: Sun Pharma submits a non-binding proposal to acquire Concert for $7.00 per share plus a $1.00 CVR.
- 2022-12-09: Concert makes a counteroffer of $8.00 per share plus a $3.50 CVR.
- 2022-12-13: Sun Pharma accepts the counteroffer terms and enters into a new Confidentiality Agreement with a standstill provision.
- 2022-12-16: Concert and Sun Pharma enter into an Exclusivity Agreement through January 16, 2023.
- 2023-01-18: Concert Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement and CVR Agreement.
- 2023-01-19: Merger Agreement is executed and publicly announced.
- 2023-02-02: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2023-03-03: Tender offer expires with 75.2% of shares tendered; HSR waiting period expires.
- 2023-03-06: Merger completed; Concert becomes a wholly owned indirect subsidiary of Sun Pharma.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2021 with informal discussions. In August 2022, facing a cash runway extending only into Q2 2023, the Concert Board initiated a formal strategic process. MTS Health Partners contacted 39 parties. Sun Pharma initially proposed a licensing deal on November 11, 2022, which Concert rejected in favor of an acquisition. Sun Pharma's first acquisition bid on November 23 was $7.00 cash + $1.00 CVR. Concert's Transaction Committee (Richard Aldrich, Thomas Auchincloss, and Roger Tung) countered on December 9 with $8.00 cash + $3.50 CVR. Sun Pharma agreed to these economic terms on December 13, 2022, contingent on exclusivity. During the exclusivity period, Sun Pharma conducted deep IP due diligence. Final negotiations in January 2023 settled employee retention pools and the CVR Agreement terms. The Concert Board approved the deal on January 18, 2023, following a fairness opinion from MTS Securities.

## Rationale

The Concert Board determined the deal was superior to a standalone path due to the company's declining cash position (expected depletion by Q2 2023), the high cost and risk of commercializing a lead asset against global competitors, and the ongoing IP litigation with Incyte. The cash offer provided immediate liquidity at a premium, while the CVR allowed shareholders to retain upside from deuruxolitinib's potential commercial success.

## Evidence

- On March 6, 2023, Sun Pharma completed its acquisition of Concert by consummating the Merger without a meeting of the stockholders of Concert in accordance with Section 251(h) of the Delaware General Corporation Law (0001193125-23-060815).
- The Concert Board unanimously recommends that Concert’s stockholders accept the Offer and tender their Shares to Purchaser pursuant to the Offer (0001193125-23-022105).
