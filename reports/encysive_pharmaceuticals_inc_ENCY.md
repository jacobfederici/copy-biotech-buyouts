## Summary

Pfizer Inc. acquired Encysive Pharmaceuticals Inc. for $2.35 per share in cash, representing an equity value of approximately $195 million. The acquisition was structured as a cash tender offer followed by a second-step merger, primarily driven by Pfizer's interest in Encysive's pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) treatment, THELIN (sitaxsentan sodium), which was already marketed in the EU but had received multiple approvable letters from the FDA in the U.S.

## Interaction History

Following a third FDA 'approvable' letter for THELIN in June 2007, Encysive's board, led by CEO George W. Cole, initiated a strategic review process with Morgan Stanley. They contacted over 70 potential bidders. By December 2007, eleven preliminary bids were received. Pfizer and several others (including 'Bidder B' and 'Bidder C') entered a second round of due diligence. Bidder C initially proposed an asset purchase of European rights for $200M upfront. Pfizer's initial whole-company bid was $1.25/share. Throughout February 2008, a competitive auction ensued. Pfizer raised its bid to $1.65, then $2.00. Bidder B emerged with a $2.10 offer on Feb 15. Encysive used this to leverage Pfizer, which eventually submitted a 'best and final' offer of $2.35 on Feb 19, 2008. Bidder B's final offer was $2.26. The board determined Pfizer's offer was superior in price and certainty, as asset-sale alternatives (like Bidder C's) would trigger convertible note repurchases and leave the company undercapitalized for U.S. Phase III trials.

## Rationale

Encysive's board recommended the deal because the company faced a projected inability to meet working capital requirements by Q3 2008 without significant financing. The $2.35 price represented a 118% premium over the pre-announcement close. For Pfizer, the deal added growing revenue from the European market and a product (THELIN) that complemented its existing PAH treatment, REVATIO.

## Evidence

- On February 20, 2008, the Board of Directors of Encysive (the 'Company Board') unanimously approved the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereby (SC 14D9, Item 3).
- On June 10, 2008, Encysive Pharmaceuticals Inc... completed its merger (the 'Merger') with Explorer Acquisition Corp... a wholly-owned subsidiary of Pfizer Inc. (8-K, 2008-06-16).
