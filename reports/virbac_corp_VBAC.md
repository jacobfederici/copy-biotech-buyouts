## Summary

Virbac S.A., the majority owner of Virbac Corporation, acquired all remaining outstanding shares of the company not already owned by it or its subsidiaries through its indirect wholly owned subsidiary, Labogroup Holding, Inc. The transaction was structured as an all-cash tender offer followed by a short-form merger. Initially proposed at $4.15 per share in December 2005, the offer price was negotiated up to $5.25 and finally increased to $5.75 per share in October 2006 to resolve litigation and secure the necessary 90% ownership threshold for a short-form merger.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began on December 12, 2005, when Virbac S.A. (Parent) proposed acquiring the minority interest at $4.15 per share. The Virbac Corporation Board formed a Special Committee of independent directors on March 14, 2006, to evaluate the offer. The Special Committee, advised by Houlihan Lokey Howard & Zukin, engaged in arms-length negotiations with Parent. On July 10, 2006, Parent submitted a revised proposal. Following further negotiations, the price was increased to $5.25 per share. On August 7, 2006, Houlihan Lokey provided a fairness opinion, and the Special Committee recommended the deal. The Board approved the Merger Agreement on August 10, 2006. During the tender period, stockholders filed derivative actions in Delaware and Texas seeking to enjoin the offer. While courts denied the injunctions, Parent voluntarily increased the offer price to $5.75 per share on October 10, 2006, to ensure the 90% Minimum Condition was met. The offer successfully closed on October 31, 2006, with Parent reaching 97.2% ownership.

## Rationale

The Special Committee and Board recommended the deal due to the Company's financial condition, the relatively low trading volume of the stock, the risks and costs associated with research and development of new products, and the fact that the offer provided a significant premium over historical trading prices. The cash offer provided immediate liquidity to unaffiliated stockholders at a fair price as determined by financial analysis.

## Evidence

- On August 10, 2006, the Board... unanimously approved the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereby, including the Offer and the Merger (SC 14D9).
- On November 1, 2006, Virbac Corporation... and Virbac S.A... issued a joint press release announcing that Labogroup Holding, Inc... had successfully completed its cash tender offer (8-K dated 2006-11-03).

## Decision Notes

The deal_value of $47.4 million is the aggregate cash purchase price for the minority shares as stated in the 8-K filed August 8, 2006. The per-share consideration was later increased from $5.25 to $5.75. Shares outstanding (22,644,240) were sourced from the SC 14D9 Item 1. The implied upfront equity value of ~$130.2M is calculated based on the final $5.75 price multiplied by total shares outstanding.
