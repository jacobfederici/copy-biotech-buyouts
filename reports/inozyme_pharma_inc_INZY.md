## Summary

Inozyme Pharma, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with BioMarin Pharmaceutical Inc. on May 16, 2025, under which BioMarin acquired all outstanding shares of Inozyme for $4.00 per share in cash. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The deal was driven by Inozyme's need for significant capital to advance its lead candidate, INZ-701, and BioMarin's strategic interest in strengthening its enzyme therapy portfolio. The acquisition closed on July 1, 2025, after approximately 69.8% of shares were tendered.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest in early 2025 following a period of business development outreach. Inozyme's Board, led by CEO Douglas Treco and advised by Centerview Partners and Goodwin Procter, engaged with multiple parties including BioMarin and 'Party A'. Party A's offers were consistently viewed as less compelling due to the inclusion of stock consideration and contingent value rights (CVRs). BioMarin initially offered $2.00 per share on April 30, 2025. Following a competitive process and the sharing of interim clinical data, BioMarin increased its offer to $3.55 on May 9, 2025. Inozyme countered at $5.00. BioMarin then provided a 'best and final' offer of $4.00 per share contingent on immediate exclusivity. Despite a brief pause to release earnings and clinical data on May 14, the Board ultimately determined that $4.00 represented the highest value obtainable given the company's capital needs and standalone risks. The final Merger Agreement included a 3.25% termination fee ($8.7 million).

## Rationale

The Inozyme Board determined the transaction was in the best interest of stockholders due to the significant capital required to fund Phase 3 trials and commercialization of INZ-701, the risks of dilution from independent fundraising, and the high premium offered by BioMarin. BioMarin sought to leverage its expertise in rare disease enzyme therapies to accelerate the development of INZ-701.

## Evidence

- On July 1, 2025, BioMarin Pharmaceutical Inc. completed its acquisition of Inozyme Pharma, Inc. pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated May 16, 2025 (8-K, 2025-07-01).

## Decision Notes
