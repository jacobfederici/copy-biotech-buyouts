## Summary

Bristol-Myers Squibb acquired Turning Point Therapeutics for $76.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $4.1 billion. The acquisition centers on Turning Point's lead asset, repotrectinib, a next-generation tyrosine kinase inhibitor for ROS1-positive non-small cell lung cancer, which had recently reported positive registrational data.

## Timeline

- 2022-01-12: Introductory meeting between Turning Point and BMS at J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference.
- 2022-02-15: Turning Point initiates a formal partnering process for repotrectinib, contacting 33 parties including BMS.
- 2022-03-06: Turning Point and BMS enter into a mutual non-disclosure agreement with a standstill provision.
- 2022-04-12: Turning Point announces positive top-line BICR data for repotrectinib from the TRIDENT-1 study.
- 2022-04-28: BMS submits a non-binding partnering proposal including a $700M upfront payment and equity investment.
- 2022-05-06: BMS shifts from partnering interest to an acquisition proposal, offering $58.00 per share in cash.
- 2022-05-09: Turning Point Board rejects the $58.00 offer as inadequate.
- 2022-05-14: BMS increases its acquisition offer to $70.00 per share.
- 2022-05-15: Turning Point Board rejects the $70.00 offer, providing guidance in the low $80s range.
- 2022-05-26: BMS submits a revised offer of $75.00 per share and requests exclusivity.
- 2022-06-02: BMS communicates a 'best and final' offer of $76.00 per share; Turning Point Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement.
- 2022-06-03: Joint press release announcing the transaction.
- 2022-06-17: BMS commences the tender offer.
- 2022-08-15: Tender offer expires with approximately 84% of shares tendered.
- 2022-08-17: Merger completed; Turning Point becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of BMS.

## Interaction History

Negotiations were led by Turning Point CEO Dr. Athena Countouriotis and BMS EVP Elizabeth Mily. Turning Point initially sought a global partnering deal for repotrectinib to retain U.S. rights. BMS was one of 33 parties contacted. After positive clinical data in April 2022, BMS submitted a partnering term sheet but quickly pivoted to an acquisition bid of $58.00 on May 6. The Turning Point Board, advised by Goldman Sachs and Cooley LLP, rejected multiple bids ($58, $70, $75) while continuing parallel partnering talks with 'Company A' and 'Company B'. BMS eventually increased its bid to $76.00 on June 2, 2022, which the Board accepted after determining that Company A's partnering offer was contingent on future FDA meeting minutes and that the BMS cash offer provided superior certain value. Regulatory clearance was obtained under the HSR Act and German antitrust laws.

## Rationale

The Turning Point Board determined the cash offer provided immediate and certain value that outweighed the execution risks of remaining a standalone company, including the challenges of transitioning to a commercial-stage entity and the speculative nature of future clinical/regulatory milestones. BMS sought to strengthen its oncology portfolio with a potential best-in-class ROS1/NTRK inhibitor.
