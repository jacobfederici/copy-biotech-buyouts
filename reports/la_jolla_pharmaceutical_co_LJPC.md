## Summary

La Jolla Pharmaceutical Company was acquired by Innoviva, Inc. for $6.23 per share in an all-cash tender offer followed by a merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The transaction was valued at approximately $155.3 million on a fully diluted basis, representing an 84% premium to the company's closing price prior to the announcement. The deal followed a competitive bidding process involving multiple strategic parties and was facilitated by the divestiture of a non-core minority interest in a contract manufacturer to maximize cash proceeds for stockholders.

## Interaction History

Beginning in late 2021, La Jolla sought to acquire assets to leverage its hospital sales force but pivoted to a sale process after receiving unsolicited interest. Cowen contacted 25 parties; 8 entered NDAs. Negotiations were highly competitive. Company A initially led with a $6.00 bid but later reduced it to $5.50 after due diligence. Company B and C offered structures involving Contingent Value Rights (CVRs) related to GIAPREZA and XERAVA sales and regulatory milestones, which the Board viewed as uncertain. Innoviva entered the process later, initially bidding $4.90 on June 17. Following feedback that they needed to be in the 'high $5.00 range,' Innoviva increased its bid to $5.95 on July 1. A key negotiation point involved a minority interest in a contract manufacturer; La Jolla successfully redeemed this interest for $10.4 million in total proceeds. Innoviva agreed to add these proceeds to the purchase price, resulting in the final $6.23 per share offer. Company H remained competitive at $5.83 but declined to increase its price. On July 10, the Board accepted Innoviva's offer as the highest and most certain value.

## Rationale

The La Jolla Board determined the all-cash offer provided immediate and certain value at a significant premium (84%) compared to the risks of remaining independent. Key risks included the difficulty of scaling the hospital-based sales force through further acquisitions, the uncertainty of Paragraph IV litigation regarding GIAPREZA, and general market volatility. The Board also noted that Innoviva's offer was superior to other bids which were either lower in value or contained highly uncertain CVR components.

The deal was completed via a tender offer and 251(h) merger. No CVRs were included in the final Innoviva deal, though they were proposed by other bidders (Companies B and C).
