## Summary

Chattem, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with sanofi-aventis on December 20, 2009, under which sanofi-aventis acquired all outstanding shares of Chattem for $93.50 per share in cash. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a short-form merger, positioning Chattem as the platform for sanofi-aventis's over-the-counter (OTC) business in the United States.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in August 2009 when Goldman Sachs contacted Chattem CEO Zan Guerry. Initial discussions focused on a strategic relationship for specific brands, but on November 5, 2009, sanofi-aventis CEO Christopher Viehbacher proposed an acquisition at $85-$90 per share. The Chattem Board, advised by Morgan Stanley and Skadden Arps, pushed for a higher valuation, arguing the initial range did not reflect the company's full value. Sanofi-aventis emphasized that management retention was critical, leading to the negotiation of retention agreements for key executives including Zan Guerry and Robert Bosworth. After diligence and multiple price increments, sanofi-aventis moved to $93.50 on December 15, 2009. The Board approved the deal on December 20, 2009, noting the significant premium and the strategic fit of Chattem serving as sanofi's U.S. OTC headquarters.

## Rationale

The Chattem Board recommended the deal because it provided immediate cash value at a 33.6% premium to the market price. Strategically, the merger allowed Chattem to become the platform for sanofi-aventis's U.S. OTC business, specifically facilitating the high-value Rx-to-OTC switch of Allegra. Sanofi-aventis sought the acquisition to establish a significant presence in the U.S. consumer healthcare market.

## Evidence

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

There were no widely reported public rumors or media speculation regarding a takeover of Chattem Inc. (CHTT) by Sanofi-Aventis prior to the official deal announcement on December 21, 2009. The acquisition was described by financial analysts at the time as a "well-kept secret" and a surprise to the market.

However, subsequent investigations by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the Department of Justice revealed that non-public information had leaked to specific individuals, leading to several insider trading cases. The following timeline details the private leaks and unusual market activity that occurred before the public announcement:

*   **November 26, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *PharmaTimes* / *Business Standard*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sun Pharmaceutical Industries
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not reported (Private transaction)
    *   **Details:** Reports surfaced that Sun Pharma was acquiring **Chattem Chemicals Inc.**, a subsidiary of Elcat. While this shared the "Chattem" name and was based in Chattanooga, it was a separate entity from the publicly traded consumer healthcare company Chattem Inc. (CHTT). This often caused confusion in historical search results but did not pertain to a takeover of the parent company CHTT.

*   **December 7 – December 17, 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** N/A (Later reported in SEC Civil Complaint, Dec 2009)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi-Aventis
    *   **Rumored Price:** N/A
    *   **Details:** According to SEC filings, two French nationals (Nicolas Patrick Benoit Condroyer and Gilles Robert Roger) began purchasing 2,910 call options on Chattem shares. This activity was based on leaked "secret knowledge" of the upcoming takeover but was not reported by any news outlet as a rumor at the time.

*   **December 18, 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** N/A (Market Data / Later reported by *CBS MoneyWatch*)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Undisclosed
    *   **Rumored Price:** N/A
    *   **Details:** On the final trading day before the announcement, Chattem’s trading volume more than doubled, jumping from approximately 133,200 shares to 315,600 shares. Despite the spike in volume, no specific rumors were published in the financial press to explain the move until after the deal was made public.

*   **December 21, 2009 (Pre-Market)**
    *   **Confirmed Deal:** Sanofi-Aventis officially announced the acquisition of Chattem Inc. for **$93.50 per share** in cash, a 34% premium over the December 18 closing price.
