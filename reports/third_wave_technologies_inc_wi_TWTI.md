## Summary

Third Wave Technologies, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Hologic, Inc. on June 8, 2008, under which Hologic's subsidiary, Thunder Tech Corp., launched a cash tender offer for all outstanding shares at $11.25 per share. The transaction, valued at approximately $600 million, followed a strategic review process and the successful achievement of clinical endpoints for Third Wave's HPV diagnostic tests. The offer was completed on July 17, 2008, with approximately 92% of shares tendered, leading to a change in control and the appointment of Hologic designees to the board.

## Interaction History

The transaction resulted from a multi-year strategic review led by CEO Kevin Conroy and the board. After rejecting early low-value bids in 2006, the company focused on its HPV clinical trials. In March 2008, following positive trial data, the board engaged Merrill Lynch and XMS Capital Partners to contact 11 potential buyers. Hologic emerged as the primary bidder, initially proposing $11.50 per share in April 2008. Following extensive due diligence and site visits in May 2008, Hologic lowered its offer to $11.25 on June 2, 2008, citing diligence findings. Third Wave's board, represented by counsel from Kirkland & Ellis and regular counsel Kennedy Covington, attempted to negotiate the price back to $11.50, but Hologic remained firm. Negotiations also focused on restructuring executive compensation (LTIPs and RSUs) to defer payments until FDA approval of the HPV products, a key condition for Hologic. The board approved the $11.25 offer on June 8, 2008, after receiving a fairness opinion from Merrill Lynch.

## Rationale

The board recommended the deal because it provided immediate cash value at a significant premium (58.5% over the pre-HPV trial data price) and eliminated the risks associated with remaining independent, such as the need for significant capital to launch the HPV products and the uncertainty of FDA approval. Hologic sought to expand its diagnostic portfolio with Third Wave's molecular testing platform and HPV assays.
