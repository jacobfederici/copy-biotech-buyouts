## Summary

2seventy bio, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by its long-term collaboration partner Bristol-Myers Squibb for $5.00 per share in cash. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, resulting in 2seventy bio becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Bristol-Myers Squibb.

## Interaction History

Negotiations were led by CEO Chip Baird and the Transaction Committee (Eli Casdin, Charles Newton, and Nick Leschly). In early 2025, 2seventy bio was in advanced talks with 'Party A' for an all-stock merger of equals. On February 28, 2025, 2seventy bio reached out to BMS (Lynelle Hoch and Julie Rozenblyum) to see if they would compete. BMS initially offered $4.75 on March 5. After a counter-offer of $5.50, BMS moved to $5.25 on March 6. However, following confirmatory diligence on the company's 'Prime Lease' at 60 Binney Street and updated capitalization data, BMS (via advisor DyalCo) lowered the offer to $5.00 on March 8. 2seventy bio Chairman Nick Leschly attempted to negotiate the price back up with BMS CFO David Elkins, but BMS remained firm. The board ultimately accepted $5.00, citing the certainty of cash and the assumption of significant lease liabilities by BMS.
