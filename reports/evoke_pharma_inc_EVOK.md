## Summary

Evoke Pharma, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with QOL Medical, LLC on November 3, 2025, under which QOL Medical acquired all outstanding shares of Evoke for $11.00 per share in cash. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, which was completed on December 17, 2025, after approximately 67.63% of shares were tendered.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2025 with Party A, which initially offered $8.17 and then $10.00 per share. After Party A lowered its bid to $7.50 plus CVRs in August 2025, Evoke re-engaged with QOL Medical (Parent) and Party B. Parent initially offered $11.00, then increased to $12.00 in September. However, following diligence on transaction-triggered expenses (warrant/option cancellations and severance), Parent reduced its offer back to $11.00 on October 1, 2025. Party B withdrew due to pricing constraints. Evoke's Board, advised by Stifel and Latham & Watkins, negotiated terms regarding the Eversana commercial relationship and executive transition. On November 3, 2025, the Board approved the $11.00 cash offer, noting it was the highest value reasonably obtainable.
