## Summary

Lumos Pharma, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by DPV Parent, Inc., a subsidiary of Double Point Ventures LLC, for $4.25 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) representing up to $4.50 in potential milestone payments. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The deal included a $7.5 million clinical trial funding agreement to support Lumos's lead program, LUM-201, during the interim period between signing and closing.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2023 as Lumos sought financing for its Phase 3 trial of LUM-201. DPV, introduced via Party B, initially proposed a take-private transaction in January 2024, which Lumos management (Richard Hawkins, Lori Lawley) initially resisted in favor of a PIPE. After a formal market check by Piper Sandler involving 51 potential buyers, three primary contenders emerged: DPV, Party A (a China-based pharma), and Party C (a private drug discovery company). Party C proposed a reverse merger in July 2024 but failed to secure investor backing. Party A offered $3.57 per share in September 2024 but lacked interim financing. DPV's final offer of $4.25 per share plus a CVR and a $7.5M Clinical Trial Funding (CTF) agreement was deemed superior by the Board due to higher upfront value, certain interim funding, and lower regulatory risk compared to a cross-border deal with Party A. Final negotiations in October 2024 focused on the treatment of out-of-the-money options and the threshold for the dissenting shares condition (settled at 10%).

## Rationale

The Lumos Board recommended the deal because it provided immediate liquidity at a premium (7.6% over the last closing price and 193% over the 52-week low) while mitigating the substantial risk and dilution associated with raising the ~$242 million required to fund LUM-201 through commercialization independently. The Board also noted that the offer was superior to a potential liquidation, which was estimated to yield only $0.09 to $1.19 per share.
