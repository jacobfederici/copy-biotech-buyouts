## Summary

CollaGenex Pharmaceuticals entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Galderma Laboratories, a subsidiary of Galderma Pharma S.A., for $16.60 per share in cash. The transaction was structured as a two-step acquisition involving a tender offer followed by a short-form merger. The deal provided immediate liquidity to stockholders at a significant premium and allowed Galderma to expand its dermatology portfolio, specifically acquiring the rosacea treatment Oracea.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began informally in 2005 regarding product rights but shifted to acquisition interest in late 2007. Galderma, led by CEO Humberto Antunes and President Albert Draaijer, initially proposed $12.50 per share in December 2007. CollaGenex CEO Colin Stewart and the Board, advised by Cowen and Milbank Tweed, rejected this and initiated a broad market check, contacting 13 additional parties. Four other companies signed NDAs and conducted diligence. Galderma incrementally raised its bid to $13.50, then $14.00, and finally $16.60 on February 15, 2008. A competing bid from a private dermatology company was slightly higher but deemed inferior due to financing contingencies and a slower one-step merger structure. Final negotiations focused on regulatory risk allocation, termination fees ($12.6M), and the treatment of Series D-1 Preferred Stock. The Board unanimously approved the $16.60 offer on February 25, 2008, after Cowen delivered a fairness opinion.

## Rationale

The CollaGenex Board recommended the deal because the $16.60 cash price provided a 45% premium over the 20-day average trading price and immediate liquidity. The Board noted limited organic growth opportunities in the dermatology sector and the risks of remaining independent. For Galderma, the acquisition secured full control of Oracea and leveraged CollaGenex's established sales and marketing infrastructure in the U.S. dermatology market.
