## Summary

Radius Health, Inc. was acquired by a joint venture between Gurnet Point Capital and Patient Square Capital via a tender offer for $10.00 per share in cash plus a $1.00 CVR. The transaction followed a strategic review process involving over 70 parties, initiated after the clinical failure of the abaloparatide transdermal patch and challenging market conditions. The merger was completed on August 15, 2022, following the successful tender of approximately 52.1% of outstanding shares.

## Timeline

- 2021-12-01: Radius announces the failure of the abaloparatide transdermal patch (the 'Patch') Phase III trial.
- 2022-01-26: Radius enters into a Confidential Disclosure Agreement with Gurnet Point Capital.

- 2022-05-27: Gurnet Point lowers cash offer to $9.00 per share plus a $2.75 CVR due to declining stock price and updated projections.
- 2022-06-10: Gurnet Point submits Fourth Offer of $10.00 per share plus a $1.50 CVR.

- 2022-06-23: Merger Agreement is executed and publicly announced.
- 2022-07-13: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2022-08-10: Tender offer expires with 52.1% of shares tendered.
- 2022-08-15: Merger completed; Radius becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of Ginger Acquisition Inc.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2021 following the failure of the abaloparatide transdermal patch. Radius, advised by J.P. Morgan, contacted 15 potential bidders for a whole-company or asset sale. Gurnet Point Capital emerged as the primary bidder, initially offering $10.50 cash plus a $1.75 CVR in March 2022. A competing strategic bidder, Party B, offered an asset-only deal for TYMLOS which the board deemed inadequate. During April and May 2022, Radius faced a proxy contest from Velan Capital and Repertoire Partners. Gurnet Point lowered its offer to $9.00 cash in May 2022 citing market volatility and updated TYMLOS projections. Radius counter-offered $10.00 cash. On June 10, Gurnet Point offered $10.00 cash plus a $1.50 CVR milestone. Radius negotiated the CVR threshold down to $300M in net sales to make it more achievable, resulting in a $1.00 CVR. Patient Square Capital joined as a co-sponsor on June 21 to provide additional funding and expertise. The board approved the final terms on June 22, 2022.

## Rationale

The board determined the merger was the best path forward due to the significant capital required to expand TYMLOS sales and develop the pipeline, the company's high debt burden ($193M in convertible notes), and the 70% loss in market value following the Patch trial failure. The cash consideration provided immediate liquidity at a 45% premium to the 30-day VWAP, while the CVR allowed shareholders to participate in the potential upside of TYMLOS commercial success.

## Evidence

- At a meeting of the Company Board held on June 22, 2022, the Company Board, among other things, unanimously resolved: (1) that the Merger Agreement and contemplated transactions are fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company and its stockholders (0001193125-22-192655).
- On August 15, 2022, following consummation of the Offer, Purchaser merged with and into the Company (the 'Merger'), with the Company as the surviving corporation (0001193125-22-220887).

## Decision Notes

The deal value is calculated based on the maximum potential consideration ($11.00) multiplied by the 47,607,604 shares outstanding as of June 30, 2022. The CVR milestone is a single payment of $1.00 if net sales of TYMLOS in the US plus Japan royalties/supply payments exceed $300 million in any consecutive 12-month period ending on or before December 31, 2025.
