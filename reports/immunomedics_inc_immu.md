## Summary

Gilead Sciences, Inc. acquired Immunomedics, Inc. through a tender offer for $88.00 per share in cash, totaling approximately $21 billion. The acquisition was completed on October 23, 2020, following the successful tender of 81.38% of Immunomedics' outstanding shares.

## Interaction History

On January 9, 2019, Andrew Dickinson, then Executive Vice President, Corporate Development and Strategy of Gilead, met informally with representatives of Immunomedics at an industry conference to discuss Gilead's interest in oncology. Following this, Gilead and Immunomedics entered into a mutual confidential disclosure agreement, effective January 1, 2019, which was amended on June 30, 2019, to facilitate information sharing for a potential business transaction. Throughout 2019 and early 2020, members of senior management from both companies held periodic discussions covering clinical studies of Trodelvy, commercialization preparations, Immunomedics' pipeline, and Gilead's oncology strategy.

In late 2019, the Immunomedics Board and management began exploring a broader licensing and collaboration arrangement with a larger pharmaceutical company. In January 2020, Immunomedics management, including Dr. Behzad Aghazadeh (Executive Chairman) and Mr. Usama Malik (Chief Financial and Business Officer), attended the J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference, engaging with various pharmaceutical companies, including Party A, Party B, and Party C, regarding potential partnering arrangements.

On February 26, 2020, the Immunomedics Board met to review the Company's financial performance, strategic trajectory, and the possibility of a collaboration. Mr. Malik provided an overview of discussions from the JPM Conference. Following the FDA approval of Trodelvy on April 22, 2020, Immunomedics management, at the Board's direction, contacted 12 pharmaceutical companies, including Gilead, Party A, Party B, and Party C, in May and June 2020 to gauge interest in a Trodelvy collaboration. Process letters were sent, requesting indications of interest by June 29, 2020, and preliminary proposals by August 10, 2020. Gilead expressed interest on June 27, 2020.

From July 6, 2020, through early August, certain Potential Counterparties, including Gilead, Party A, Party B, and Party C, received due diligence materials and participated in management meetings. On August 5, 2020, the Immunomedics Board met, and Centerview Partners LLC and BofA Securities, Inc. presented preliminary perspectives on strategic opportunities. The Board authorized continued engagement with Potential Counterparties. Around August 10, 2020, Gilead, Party A, Party B, and Party C submitted preliminary collaboration proposals. Immunomedics management updated the Board, and engagement continued. Around August 20, 2020, these parties received access to additional confidential data, including from the ASCENT Study and a Phase 2 TROPHY study.

On September 7, 2020, representatives of Party A informed Dr. Aghazadeh and Mr. Malik of their intent to submit a written acquisition proposal for $55.00 per share in cash, contingent on an announcement before September 14, 2020. Dr. Aghazadeh informed the Board. Later that day, a representative of Centerview informed Gilead that Immunomedics had received an acquisition proposal. Daniel O’Day, Chairman and CEO of Gilead, then expressed Gilead's interest in acquiring Immunomedics to Dr. Aghazadeh.

On September 8, 2020, Mr. O’Day informed Dr. Aghazadeh that Gilead would submit a written acquisition proposal the next day. The Immunomedics Board met to discuss Party A's proposal and the anticipated Gilead proposal. The Board considered the merits of Party A's offer and the possibility of contacting Party B and Party C for whole company transactions, but decided against contacting Party C due to lower interest and less due diligence. Party A delivered draft transaction documents to Wachtell Lipton on the evening of September 8, 2020.

On September 9, 2020, Mr. O’Day conveyed Gilead’s preliminary, non-binding proposal to acquire Immunomedics for $82.00 per share, followed by a written proposal and a draft merger agreement. Centerview informed Party A that its proposal was substantially lower, leading to discussions where Party A indicated willingness to consider an increased proposal in the mid to high $60s per share.

On September 10, 2020, Dr. Aghazadeh contacted Party B's CEO, informing him of the two acquisition proposals and Immunomedics' focus on a whole company sale. Party B's CEO later only reiterated interest in a collaboration. The Immunomedics Board held a special meeting, where Centerview and BofA Securities summarized the proposals. Wachtell Lipton noted Gilead's draft merger agreement terms were more favorable. The Board decided to focus on negotiating with Gilead. Dr. Aghazadeh was authorized to propose an increased purchase price to Mr. O’Day in exchange for a 72-hour exclusivity commitment. After negotiations, Mr. O’Day and Dr. Aghazadeh agreed on $88.00 per share, and Immunomedics entered into an Exclusivity Agreement with Gilead. Gilead and its advisors were granted access to additional diligence materials.

On September 11, 2020, Wachtell Lipton delivered a revised draft of the merger agreement to Davis Polk & Wardwell LLP, Gilead's counsel. Over the next 48 hours, revised drafts were exchanged, and discussions were held on open issues, including regulatory approvals, termination fees, interim operating restrictions, and employee provisions. A due diligence call was also held.

On September 12, 2020, the Immunomedics Board held a special meeting. Mr. Malik reviewed financial forecasts. Dr. Aghazadeh updated on discussions with Gilead, noting an impending media report on the potential transaction. The Board approved working towards an announcement on September 13, 2020. Later that afternoon, financial media reported speculation about the acquisition. The Board reconvened, and Centerview and BofA Securities presented their financial analyses and rendered oral fairness opinions, later confirmed in writing. Wachtell Lipton summarized the final merger agreement and reviewed fiduciary duties. The Board unanimously approved the merger agreement and recommended the offer. Immunomedics also entered into revised employment agreements and make-whole agreements with executive officers.

On September 13, 2020, shortly after midnight, Immunomedics, Gilead, and Purchaser executed the final merger agreement. Later that day, Immunomedics and Gilead issued a joint press release announcing the transaction.

## Rationale

The Immunomedics Board unanimously recommended the offer, citing the substantial and compelling premium of $88.00 per share (108% premium to the closing price on September 11, 2020), certainty of value through an all-cash offer, and immediate liquidity for stockholders. The Board determined that the offer was the best strategic alternative for maximizing stockholder value, especially when considering the risks and uncertainties of remaining a standalone public company, including the costs and risks associated with commercializing Trodelvy, achieving full FDA approval, and developing other product candidates. The Board also considered the high likelihood and speed of consummation due to the tender offer structure, Gilead's financial strength, and the limited closing conditions. The ability to receive unsolicited alternative proposals and terminate the merger agreement for a superior offer, subject to a reasonable termination fee, was also a factor. The availability of appraisal rights under Delaware law provided an additional safeguard for stockholders.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, and reported takeover interest regarding Immunomedics Inc. (IMMU) prior to the official announcement of its acquisition by Gilead Sciences on September 13, 2020.

*   **January 25, 2017**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *TheStreet* (Adam Feuerstein)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unspecified (Strategic buyers)
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specified.
    *   **Details:** Reported that a potential sale of Immunomedics was being hindered by a proxy battle between the company’s board and activist investor venBio (now Avoro Capital). The report suggested that while the company was a logical acquisition target due to its lead drug sacituzumab govitecan, the internal conflict and the role of the company's founder were significant obstacles to a deal.

*   **February 10, 2017**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *BioPharma Dive* / Press Release (Contextual)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Seattle Genetics (now Seagen)
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** $1.7 billion in milestones + $250 million upfront (Licensing/Equity stake).
    *   **Details:** While technically a licensing deal, it was widely speculated in the market as a "creeping takeover" or a precursor to an acquisition. Seattle Genetics agreed to pay $250 million upfront for rights to IMMU-132 and took a 2.8% equity stake with options to increase it to roughly 11%. This deal was later scrapped following a successful proxy fight by activist investors who believed the company should be sold outright or developed independently for a higher value.

*   **April – August 2020**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** SEC Filings (Schedule 14D-9, reported post-deal but detailing pre-deal rumors/activity)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Gilead Sciences and three unnamed "Major Pharmaceutical Companies" (referred to as Party A, Party B, and Party C).
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not publicly leaked at the time, but later revealed to have involved bids starting at $55.00 per share.
    *   **Details:** During this period, speculation intensified in the biotech community following the FDA approval of Trodelvy. Internal SEC documents later confirmed that Gilead began discussions in April 2020, and by August, multiple parties were conducting due diligence under confidentiality agreements.

*   **September 7, 2020**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** SEC Filings (Schedule 14D-9)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unnamed "Party A"
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** $55.00 per share (Cash).
    *   **Details:** An unnamed large pharmaceutical company submitted a formal, unsolicited proposal to acquire Immunomedics. This offer was kept private but triggered the final competitive bidding process.

*   **September 9, 2020**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** SEC Filings (Schedule 14D-9)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Gilead Sciences
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** $82.00 per share (Cash).
    *   **Details:** Gilead submitted a formal proposal to acquire the company, significantly outbidding the initial $55.00 offer from Party A.

*   **September 12, 2020 (The "Final Rumor")**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *The Wall Street Journal*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Gilead Sciences
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** "More than $20 billion" (implied $80+ per share).
    *   **Details:** Reported that Gilead was in advanced talks to acquire Immunomedics for a price exceeding $20 billion. The report noted that the deal could be announced as early as Monday (September 14) and that the discussions had shifted from a partnership to a full takeover following positive clinical data.
