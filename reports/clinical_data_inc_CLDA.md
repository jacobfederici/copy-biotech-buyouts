## Summary

Clinical Data, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Forest Laboratories, Inc. under which Forest acquired the company via a tender offer for $30.00 per share in cash plus a Contingent Value Right (CVR) worth up to $6.00 based on sales milestones for the antidepressant Viibryd. The transaction followed the FDA approval of Viibryd in January 2011 and a competitive bidding process involving multiple pharmaceutical companies.

## Timeline

## Interaction History

Following the FDA approval of Viibryd in January 2011, J.P. Morgan contacted 23 pharmaceutical companies. Forest Laboratories, which had previously declined interest in 2009, emerged as a lead bidder. On February 17, 2011, Forest bid $30.00 per share cash. Clinical Data's Chairman, Randal J. Kirk, and J.P. Morgan pushed for a higher price; Forest refused to increase the upfront cash but offered a CVR based on Viibryd sales. Negotiations between Cooley LLP (Clinical Data's counsel) and Covington & Burling LLP (Forest's counsel) finalized the CVR terms and a 3.5% termination fee. The Board reviewed a fairness opinion from J.P. Morgan, which valued the CVR at a present value of ~$3.14. Regulatory clearance was obtained via HSR filing on March 2, 2011, with the waiting period expiring March 17, 2011.

## Rationale

The Board recommended the deal because the $30.00 cash price provided immediate liquidity and a significant premium over the pre-approval stock price. Additionally, the company only had cash to fund operations through May 2011, and launching Viibryd independently would require highly dilutive capital raises and significant execution risk. Forest's existing infrastructure for Lexapro was seen as an ideal platform to maximize Viibryd's commercial potential, captured for stockholders via the CVR.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors and acquisition speculation for Clinical Data Inc. (CLDA) prior to the official announcement of its acquisition by Forest Laboratories on February 22, 2011.

*   **January 21, 2011**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Various (Analyst reports and market commentary following FDA approval)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unspecified "Big Pharma" or specialty pharmaceutical companies (Forest Laboratories, Pfizer, and AstraZeneca were frequently cited by analysts as logical fits).
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** No specific price was initially reported, but analysts noted the stock's 70% surge on the day of Viibryd's approval as a signal of an impending "takeover premium."
    *   **Details:** Following the FDA approval of the antidepressant Viibryd, analysts from firms such as Lazard Capital and Wedbush Securities publicly speculated that Clinical Data had become a "prime acquisition target." Reports highlighted that the company lacked the sales force to launch the drug independently, making a buyout the most likely outcome.

*   **January 24, 2011**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Clinical Data Inc. (Official Press Release/SEC Filing)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Multiple potential bidders.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specified.
    *   **Details:** In a move that fueled intense market speculation, Clinical Data officially announced it had hired J.P. Morgan to "evaluate strategic alternatives," including a potential sale of the company. This confirmed to the market that the company was actively seeking a buyer.

*   **February 18, 2011**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Bloomberg / Market Rumors
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Forest Laboratories.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Speculation centered on a price in the $30.00 range.
    *   **Details:** Rumors intensified in the days leading up to the deal, with trading volume spiking as reports circulated that Forest Laboratories was the frontrunner in the bidding process. Market participants noted that Forest needed a replacement for its blockbuster antidepressant Lexapro, which was nearing patent expiry.

*   **February 21, 2011**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reuters / Financial Blogs
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Forest Laboratories.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** $30.00 per share plus contingent value rights (CVRs).
    *   **Details:** Reports emerged late in the day suggesting a deal with Forest Laboratories was imminent and would be announced before the market opened the following morning. The rumored structure included an upfront cash payment and additional payments based on future sales milestones.
