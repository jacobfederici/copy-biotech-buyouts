## Summary

Vitae Pharmaceuticals entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Allergan plc for $21.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer. The transaction was valued at approximately $639 million and focused on Vitae's dermatology pipeline, specifically its RORgt and LXR programs. The deal closed on October 25, 2016, following the successful tender of approximately 90.3% of outstanding shares.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2016 as partnership discussions for Vitae's RORgt asset. Allergan, initially not in the top group of potential partners, requested inclusion and later proposed a partnership for both RORgt and LXR assets. After Vitae declined partnership terms in June 2016, Allergan CEO Brenton Saunders met with Vitae CEO Jeffrey Hatfield on July 18, 2016, suggesting a 100% premium acquisition. Allergan submitted a written offer of $20.50 on July 27. Vitae's board, advised by J.P. Morgan and Gunderson Dettmer, pushed for more value through due diligence. On September 4, Allergan raised its offer to $21.00, labeling it 'best and final.' J.P. Morgan conducted a targeted market check with three other parties (including 'Company A'), but no other bids were received. The board approved the $21.00 offer on September 13, 2016.

## Rationale

The Vitae Board determined the all-cash offer provided immediate liquidity and a significant premium (159% over the prior day's close) while eliminating the risks inherent in independent drug development, such as regulatory hurdles, clinical trial failure, and the need for substantial future capital. Allergan sought to bolster its dermatology pipeline with Vitae's RORgt and LXR programs.

## Evidence

- On September 13, 2016, the Vitae Board unanimously (i) approved and declared advisable the Merger Agreement, the Offer, the Merger and the other Transactions (0001193125-16-720011)
- On October 25, 2016, Parent completed its acquisition of Vitae by consummating the Merger (0001104659-16-151917)

No pre-deal rumors exist for Vitae Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (VTAE).

The acquisition of Vitae Pharmaceuticals by Allergan plc for $21.00 per share (approximately $639 million) was announced on September 14, 2016.
