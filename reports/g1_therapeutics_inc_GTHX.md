## Summary

Pharmacosmos A/S, through its indirect wholly-owned subsidiary Genesis Merger Sub, Inc., acquired G1 Therapeutics, Inc. for $7.15 per share in an all-cash tender offer, valuing the company at approximately $405 million. The acquisition was completed on September 18, 2024, following the successful tender of 74.64% of G1 Therapeutics' outstanding shares.

## Interaction History

The Board and senior management of G1 Therapeutics, Inc. (the "Company") regularly reviewed strategic alternatives to enhance stockholder value. In July 2020, an ad hoc Transaction Committee was formed to evaluate potential licensing, partnering, joint ventures, spin-outs, and other transactions. Between September 2022 and February 2023, the Company engaged in discussions with six large pharmaceutical companies (Party A, Party B, Party C, Party D, Party E, and Party F) regarding potential strategic opportunities, particularly contingent on the results of the Company's Phase 3 PRESERVE 1 trial of trilaciclib in colorectal cancer (the "CRC Trial").

In January 2023, following an introductory email in December 2022, Company management met with representatives of Pharmacosmos A/S ("Parent") to discuss partnering opportunities. The Company indicated it would not explore such transactions until after the CRC Trial readout, expected in February 2023. No acquisition discussions occurred at this time. In late January 2023, Party F contacted the Company about a potential business combination, leading to a confidentiality agreement on February 9, 2023, which did not include a standstill provision. However, Party F never made a formal acquisition proposal.

On February 10, 2023, the Board met to discuss the CRC Trial results and strategic alternatives. On February 13, 2023, the Company announced the discontinuation of the CRC Trial due to unfavorable results. Following this, Party A, Party B, and Party D indicated they were not interested in further discussions until readouts from other clinical programs. Party C also declined further discussions. Party G made an inbound inquiry, and a confidentiality agreement was signed on February 17, 2023, with a standstill provision that later expired. Party H also contacted CEO Jack Bailey, who indicated the Company would explore strategic opportunities. On February 16, 2023, Party F withdrew from business combination discussions. The Company continued discussions with Party E, leading to an amendment of an existing license agreement in April 2023, providing the Company with a $30 million non-refundable payment.

In April 2023, Party H proposed acquiring certain IP for $20 million, which the Company deemed a substantial undervaluation and did not respond. Throughout 2023, the Company engaged in ordinary course discussions with potential partners, including Parent, but these did not result in formal acquisition proposals, though many expressed interest contingent on future clinical trial outcomes.

On September 20, 2023, Party J contacted Mr. Bailey to explore a potential transaction, and a confidentiality agreement was signed on September 21, 2023, without a standstill. Party J began due diligence. In early October 2023, Party K expressed interest in licensing and co-promotion opportunities. On October 26, 2023, Party J submitted a non-binding indication of interest to acquire the Company at $2.25 per Share plus a $1.00 Contingent Value Right ("CVR") for FDA approval of COSELA for TNBC. The Board, with Centerview Partners LLC ("Centerview") and Ropes & Gray LLP ("Ropes & Gray"), rejected this proposal on October 30, 2023, as undervalued. Mr. Bailey communicated the rejection to Party J on November 2, 2023.

On November 14, 2023, Party J submitted an updated non-binding indication of interest at $2.25 per Share plus $3.00 in CVRs for multiple milestones. The Board, with Centerview and Ropes & Gray, rejected this on December 1, 2023, also deeming it undervalued. Mr. Bailey communicated the rejection on December 4, 2023.

On January 8, 2024, Company and Parent management met again. On January 10, 2024, Parent's President and CEO, Tobias S. Christensen, expressed strong interest and requested inclusion in any formal process, leading to a confidentiality agreement on February 6, 2024, which was later amended on July 11, 2024, to facilitate acquisition discussions. This agreement did not contain a standstill provision.

On February 12, 2024, the Company announced its Interim TNBC Trial Data, recommending continuation of the trial. Management engaged with Parent, Party A, Party B, Party D, and Party L. Parent expressed continued interest. Party M also met with the Company on February 15, 2024, and signed a confidentiality agreement on February 18, 2024, but no further discussions occurred after April 18, 2024.

Throughout the first quarter of 2024, the Company explored capital raising opportunities with various parties, including Parent, Party K, and Party N, but no equity investments were undertaken. On March 8, 2024, Mr. Bailey discussed equity investment interest with Parent, and Mr. Christensen confirmed Parent's interest. On March 11-12, 2024, the Company informed Party K of third-party interest, and Party K expressed interest in co-promotion and considering an equity investment. On March 13, 2024, Party J reaffirmed its interest in acquiring the Company.

On March 18, 2024, Party J submitted another updated non-binding indication of interest at $5.00 per Share plus $3.00 in CVRs. The Transaction Committee, with Centerview and Ropes & Gray, rejected this on March 20, 2024, citing undervaluation. Party K also declined an equity investment but wished to continue exploring partnering. On March 21, 2024, Mr. Bailey communicated the rejection to Party J. On March 27, 2024, Party J submitted a further updated non-binding indication of interest at $5.50 per Share plus $5.00 in CVRs. The Transaction Committee rejected this on April 16, 2024, as not a material improvement. Mr. Bailey communicated the rejection on April 17, 2024. ## Rationale

The Board of G1 Therapeutics, Inc. recommended the all-cash offer due to the attractive price, which represented a substantial premium to recent market prices of the Common Stock (81% premium to the closing price on August 5, 2024, and 151% premium to the 30-trading day volume weighted average price). The Board also valued the certainty and immediate liquidity provided by the all-cash offer, especially in light of macroeconomic conditions and the risks associated with operating independently. The Board considered the thorough review of alternative strategic options, including continuing as a standalone company, and concluded that the offer represented the best available prospect for maximizing stockholder value. Key factors included the competitive landscape, the high costs and risks of drug development, the Company's need for additional capital and associated dilution risks, intellectual property risks, and the unfavorable results of the Phase 3 PRESERVE 2 trial for trilaciclib in TNBC. The Board believed that the offer was the result of robust arm's-length negotiations and represented the highest price reasonably obtainable under the circumstances, with a high likelihood of closing due to limited conditions and no financing contingency. The Board also considered the fairness opinion from Centerview Partners LLC.
