## Summary

Sanofi entered into a Heads of Agreement to acquire Ablynx NV via a friendly tender offer for €45.00 per share in cash. The transaction followed an unsolicited and rejected proposal from Novo Nordisk and a subsequent competitive process that identified Sanofi as the preferred partner. The offer includes a U.S. offer for Shares held by U.S. holders and all ADSs, and a simultaneous Belgian offer for all outstanding securities including warrants and convertible bonds.

## Timeline

- 2017-12-07: Novo Nordisk Chairman Goran Ando contacts Ablynx Chairman Peter Fellner; Novo submits a non-binding proposal of €26.75 per share.
- 2017-12-13: Ablynx Board determines the Novo offer fundamentally undervalues the company and forms a Defense Committee.
- 2017-12-22: Novo submits a revised proposal of €28.00 cash plus a €2.50 CVR; Ablynx Defense Committee rejects the offer as inadequate.
- 2018-01-08: Novo publicly discloses its proposal; Ablynx confirms rejection and J.P. Morgan contacts eight other potential parties, including Sanofi.
- 2018-01-10: Ablynx CEO Edwin Moses meets with Sanofi CEO Olivier Brandicourt at the J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference.
- 2018-01-19: Sanofi Board approves a non-binding proposal in the range of €43.00 to €45.00 per share.
- 2018-01-22: Ablynx and Sanofi enter into a Confidentiality Agreement and Exclusivity Agreement; Sanofi submits formal proposal of €43.00-€45.00.
- 2018-01-28: Ablynx Board unanimously approves the Heads of Agreement at €45.00 per share.
- 2018-01-29: Joint press release announcing the transaction.
- 2018-02-27: German Federal Cartel Office clears the transaction.
- 2018-02-28: HSR Act waiting period expires, satisfying the Antitrust Condition.
- 2018-04-04: Commencement of the U.S. and Belgian tender offers.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began following an unsolicited approach by Novo Nordisk in December 2017. After Ablynx rejected Novo's €28.00 + CVR offer, Novo went public on January 8, 2018. Ablynx's advisor, J.P. Morgan, then reached out to eight potential buyers. Sanofi, which already had a 2017 collaboration agreement with Ablynx, expressed immediate interest. Meetings between CEOs Edwin Moses (Ablynx) and Olivier Brandicourt (Sanofi) occurred during the J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference. Sanofi conducted due diligence in mid-January and initially proposed a range of €43-€45. On January 26, Sanofi confirmed the €45.00 price. The Ablynx Board met on January 28, 2018, to approve the Heads of Agreement. Regulatory clearances were obtained from the German FCO on February 27 and the U.S. FTC/DOJ on February 28, 2018.

## Rationale

The Ablynx Board recommended the deal because the €45.00 price represented a significant premium (112.3% over the pre-Novo announcement price) and provided immediate cash liquidity. Sanofi sought to acquire Ablynx to gain full control of caplacizumab and the proprietary Nanobody platform, building on their existing 2017 research collaboration.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, unsolicited bids, and takeover speculation for Ablynx NV (ABLX) prior to the confirmed acquisition announcement by Sanofi on January 29, 2018.

*   **December 7, 2017**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Disclosed retrospectively by Novo Nordisk and various outlets (e.g., *FirstWord Pharma*, *Fierce Biotech*) on January 8, 2018.
    *   **Rumored/Unsolicited Buyer:** Novo Nordisk A/S.
    *   **Rumored Price:** €26.75 per share in cash.
    *   **Details:** Novo Nordisk made a private, unsolicited proposal to the Ablynx Board of Directors. The offer was rejected by Ablynx on December 14, 2017, on the grounds that it fundamentally undervalued the company.

*   **December 22, 2017**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Disclosed retrospectively by Novo Nordisk on January 8, 2018.
    *   **Rumored/Unsolicited Buyer:** Novo Nordisk A/S.
    *   **Rumored Price:** €28.00 per share in cash, plus a Contingent Value Right (CVR) of up to €2.50 per share.
    *   **Details:** Novo Nordisk submitted a revised private proposal. This second offer was also rejected by the Ablynx Board on December 23, 2017.

*   **January 8, 2018**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Bloomberg*, *Reuters*, *Financial Times*, and a formal press release from Novo Nordisk.
    *   **Rumored/Unsolicited Buyer:** Novo Nordisk A/S.
    *   **Rumored Price:** €28.00 per share in cash plus €2.50 in CVRs (Total value approx. €2.6 billion).
    *   **Details:** After being twice rebuffed in private, Novo Nordisk went public with its offer to pressure the Ablynx board to enter negotiations. Ablynx issued a same-day response confirming it had rejected the "unsolicited and conditional" proposal, stating it did not provide a basis for further discussion.

*   **January 9–25, 2018**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Reuters*, *The Times*, and various market analysts (e.g., Jefferies).
    *   **Rumored/Potential Buyers:** Sanofi, Roche, Merck KGaA, and Novartis.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Speculation of a "white knight" bid exceeding €35–€40 per share.
    *   **Details:** Following Novo Nordisk's public bid, intense speculation emerged regarding a potential bidding war. Reports suggested Ablynx was working with J.P. Morgan to solicit interest from other pharmaceutical giants. Analysts specifically highlighted Sanofi as a logical suitor due to their existing research partnership (signed in July 2017).

*   **January 26, 2018**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Bloomberg News*.
    *   **Rumored/Potential Buyer:** Sanofi.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified, but reported as a "superior offer" to Novo Nordisk.
    *   **Details:** Reports surfaced that Sanofi was in advanced talks to acquire Ablynx, potentially outbidding Novo Nordisk. This speculation followed Sanofi's recent $11.6 billion acquisition of Bioverativ, signaling the company's aggressive move into rare blood disorders.

***

**Confirmed Deal Announcement:** On **January 29, 2018**, Sanofi and Ablynx officially announced a definitive agreement for Sanofi to acquire all outstanding shares of Ablynx for **€45.00 per share** in cash, totaling approximately **€3.9 billion**.
