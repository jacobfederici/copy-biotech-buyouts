## Summary

Alpharma Inc. was acquired by King Pharmaceuticals, Inc. for $37.00 per share in cash, totaling approximately $1.6 billion. The transaction followed a hostile pursuit by King that began in July 2008 at $33.00 per share, which Alpharma's board initially rejected as inadequate. After Alpharma initiated a strategic review and auction process, King increased its offer to $37.00. The parties entered into a definitive merger agreement on November 23, 2008, and the deal was completed via a tender offer followed by a short-form merger on December 29, 2008.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began with an informal approach by King CEO Brian Markison to Alpharma CEO Dean Mitchell in July 2008. King initially offered $33.00 per share, which Alpharma's board, advised by Banc of America Securities and Simpson Thacher & Bartlett, rejected as undervaluing the company's pipeline, specifically EMBEDA. King went public with its offer on August 22, 2008, prompting Alpharma to adopt a poison pill and launch an auction process. During this period, Alpharma engaged with 'Company X' and other third parties. King increased its bid to $37.00 but initially refused to participate in the auction or sign a standstill. On September 12, King launched a hostile tender offer at $37.00. Following further negotiations and the realization of the auction process, Alpharma eventually agreed to a friendly deal with King at the $37.00 price point on November 23, 2008, which included a $60 million termination fee and a 'no-shop' provision.

## Rationale

King sought the acquisition to create scale and improved efficiencies by combining complementary products, pipelines, and research capabilities in the pain management sector. Alpharma's board initially resisted, citing the value of its EMBEDA priority review and other pipeline assets, but eventually recommended the $37.00 offer after an auction process failed to produce a superior definitive agreement, concluding the price was fair and in the best interests of shareholders.

The deal value is explicitly stated as approximately $1.6 billion in the 8-K filing dated January 5, 2009. Shares outstanding (41,827,154) were sourced from the SC 14D9 filed September 26, 2008. ## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, unsolicited offers, and takeover speculation for Alpharma Inc. (ALO) prior to the confirmed merger agreement with King Pharmaceuticals on November 24, 2008.

*   **July – Early August 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reported retrospectively by *Forbes* and *Drug Discovery News* (referencing letters from King Pharmaceuticals).
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** King Pharmaceuticals.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not publicly disclosed at the time; later revealed to be private approaches.
    *   **Details:** King Pharmaceuticals made at least two private, unsolicited approaches to Alpharma’s board regarding a potential acquisition. These were rejected by Alpharma without public disclosure at the time.

*   **August 22, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Reuters*, *Forbes*, *BioSpace*.
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** King Pharmaceuticals (Unsolicited Bid).
    *   **Rumored Price:** $33.00 per share in cash (approx. $1.4 billion).
    *   **Details:** King Pharmaceuticals took its interest public by announcing an unsolicited all-cash offer. The bid represented a 37% premium over the previous day's closing price. Alpharma’s board immediately rejected the offer, stating it "fundamentally undervalues" the company.

*   **August 22, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Forbes* (citing analysts from RBC Capital Markets and Cowen & Co).
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Endo Pharmaceuticals and Cephalon.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified (speculated to be higher than $33.00).
    *   **Details:** Following King’s public bid, analysts speculated that Alpharma was now "in play" and identified Endo Pharmaceuticals and Cephalon as potential white knight suitors or competing bidders due to their presence in the pain management market.

*   **August 27, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *FierceBiotech*, *CFO.com*.
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** King Pharmaceuticals.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $33.00 per share.
    *   **Details:** King Pharmaceuticals filed with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) stating its intent to acquire a majority of Alpharma’s common stock, signaling a move toward a hostile takeover.

*   **September 2, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Business Wire*, *FierceBiotech*.
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** King Pharmaceuticals.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $33.00 per share.
    *   **Details:** Alpharma announced the adoption of a "poison pill" (shareholder rights plan) specifically to thwart King’s unsolicited $33.00 per share bid, which the board continued to label as inadequate.

*   **September 11–12, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *SEC Filings*, *MarketWatch*.
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** King Pharmaceuticals.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $37.00 per share.
    *   **Details:** King Pharmaceuticals increased its unsolicited offer to $37.00 per share in cash (approx. $1.6 billion) and officially launched a hostile tender offer directly to Alpharma shareholders.

*   **September 26, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Forbes*.
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** "Unnamed Suitors" / "Masked Suitors."
    *   **Rumored Price:** Speculated to be higher than $37.00.
    *   **Details:** Alpharma’s board rejected King’s $37.00 offer and announced it had begun a "strategic alternatives" review. The company reported it had entered into confidentiality agreements and begun due diligence with "several other suitors" who had expressed interest at prices potentially higher than King's offer.

*   **October – November 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Various (Speculation during tender offer period)*.
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** King Pharmaceuticals.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $37.00 per share.
    *   **Details:** Speculation continued as King repeatedly extended its tender offer deadline (originally set for mid-October, then November 21) while Alpharma continued to search for a higher bidder. No superior offer materialized during this period.
