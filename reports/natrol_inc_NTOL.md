## Summary

Natrol, Inc. was acquired by Plethico Pharmaceuticals Limited, an Indian public limited company, through a two-step transaction consisting of a $4.40 per share cash tender offer followed by a short-form merger. The deal provided Natrol with the scale and resources of a larger pharmaceutical entity to overcome challenges in organic growth and international market penetration.

## Timeline

- 2007-04-17: Brocair Partners contacts Natrol CFO Dennis Jolicoeur regarding a strategic purchaser candidate in India.
- 2007-05-16: Natrol CEO Wayne Bos meets with Plethico management in New York City.
- 2007-06-21: Plethico submits a draft letter of intent proposing approximately $3.80 per share; Natrol declines the offer as inadequate.
- 2007-07-11: Wayne Bos visits Plethico in Mumbai; indicates Natrol will not accept less than $5.00 per share.
- 2007-07-26: Plethico and Natrol enter into a revised confidentiality agreement with an 18-month standstill.
- 2007-08-09: Plethico informs Natrol that global credit market turmoil has disrupted its financing; discussions are suspended.
- 2007-10-15: Plethico raises $75 million via convertible bonds, securing funds for the acquisition.
- 2007-11-14: Plethico offers $3.50 per share citing adverse financial changes; Natrol Board rejects the offer.
- 2007-11-16: Plethico increases offer to $4.40 per share as its 'best and final' price.
- 2007-11-18: Natrol Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement at $4.40 per share.
- 2007-11-27: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2007-12-27: Tender offer expires with 91.9% of shares tendered.
- 2007-12-28: Short-form merger completed; Natrol becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Plethico Global Holdings B.V.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in April 2007 via Brocair Partners. Initial discussions involved Plethico CFO Sanjay Pai and Chairman Shashikant Patel. An initial June proposal of $3.80 per share was rejected by Natrol CEO Wayne Bos. In July, Bos demanded $5.00 per share, which Plethico agreed to consider subject to due diligence. However, in August, Plethico suspended talks due to credit market instability. After Plethico successfully raised $75 million in October, negotiations resumed. On November 14, Plethico lowered its bid to $3.50 following Natrol's disappointing Q3 results. After further in-person negotiations in Mumbai, Plethico raised the offer to $4.40 on November 16. The Natrol Board, advised by Morgan Joseph and Greenberg Traurig, accepted this final offer on November 18, 2007. Regulatory clearance from the Reserve Bank of India was required and obtained via the 'automatic route' under FEMA.

## Rationale

The Natrol Board determined that the company faced significant risks as an independent entity, including disappointing Q3 2007 results, limited resources compared to larger competitors, and an inability to penetrate foreign markets effectively. The $4.40 cash price represented a 94% premium over the closing price prior to the announcement and provided immediate liquidity and certainty of value for stockholders.

## Evidence

- On November 18, 2007, the Company Board, by unanimous vote, determined that the Offer, the Merger and the transactions contemplated by the merger agreement were advisable, fair to and in the best interests of the stockholders (SC 14D9).
- On December 28, 2007, the acquisition of the Company by Parent was completed by means of a 'short-form' merger of Purchaser with and into the Company (8-K, 2007-12-28).

The acquisition of Natrol by Plethico Pharmaceuticals was announced on November 19, 2007, as a "friendly" and "definitive" merger agreement. Financial news reports from that period, including the *Los Angeles Business Journal*, noted that the offer was unexpected and sent the stock up 89% in a single day, indicating a lack of prior public speculation or leaked rumors. 

While Natrol was active in the M&A market in the years leading up to its sale, it was primarily as an **acquirer** rather than a target:
*   **October 13, 2006:** Natrol announced the acquisition of the Nu Hair and Shen Min brands from Biotech Corporation.
*   **June 5, 2007:** Natrol announced the acquisition of Medical Research Institute (MRI).

The 2007 sale to Plethico was the first confirmed deal for the company itself, and no documented rumors of a takeover or buyout appeared in major financial outlets or trade publications before the official November 19, 2007, announcement.
