## Summary

Turnstone Biologics Corp. entered into a merger agreement with XOMA Royalty Corporation following a strategic review process initiated after the failure of its lead TIL therapy candidate, TIDAL-01. XOMA acquired Turnstone via a tender offer for $0.34 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) representing potential tax receivables and lease security deposits. The transaction was structured as a two-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, effectively liquidating the company's remaining cash and assets for stockholders as an alternative to a formal dissolution.

## Interaction History

Following the failure of TIDAL-01 data in August 2024, Turnstone management, led by CEO Sammy Farah, explored financing and strategic options. Between December 2024 and January 2025, Leerink Partners contacted 41 companies. Turnstone initially pursued a reverse merger with 'Company D,' signing a term sheet in March 2025, but Company D withdrew in April 2025 citing market conditions. Turnstone then pivoted to financial acquirors. XOMA and 'Company F' submitted bids in late April 2025. XOMA's initial bid was ~$8.5M cash or a stock-for-stock deal. Company F's bid was $9.8M but required debt financing and stockholder indemnification. On May 27, 2025, XOMA revised its offer to a 'net cash minus $2.0 million' formula. The Transaction Committee (directors Jerel Davis, Rishi Gupta, and William Waddill) rejected Company F due to lack of committed financing and chose XOMA. Negotiations in June 2025 focused on the 'Net Cash' definition and the CVR terms. The final price was set at $0.34 per share based on a June 24 Net Cash Schedule. Concurrently, Turnstone negotiated an asset sale of TIDAL-01 assets to Moffitt Cancer Center to settle outstanding liabilities.

## Rationale

The Turnstone Board determined the company had no viable standalone business plan following the discontinuation of TIDAL-01. A dissolution analysis estimated a liquidation value of $0.33 per share (discounted), making XOMA's $0.34 upfront offer plus CVR upside more favorable and certain. The board also noted the lack of financing for alternative reverse merger options.

## Evidence

- On June 26, 2025, the Turnstone Board unanimously (i) determined that the Transactions are fair to and in the best interest of Turnstone and its stockholders... and (iv) recommended that Turnstone Company’s stockholders accept the Offer (0001193125-25-165140).
- On August 11, 2025 (the 'Closing Date'), Merger Sub merged with and into the Company (the 'Merger')... the Company continued as the surviving corporation... and a wholly owned subsidiary of Purchaser (0001193125-25-177597).

## Decision Notes

The deal value is calculated based on 23,140,691 shares outstanding as of July 10, 2025. The CVR is estimated by the company to have a maximum pre-tax value of $0.05 per share, though they benchmarked it at $0.03 for internal analysis. The transaction included a concurrent asset sale to Moffitt Cancer Center to resolve liabilities.
