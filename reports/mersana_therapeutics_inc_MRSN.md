## Summary

Mersana Therapeutics, Inc. was acquired by Day One Biopharmaceuticals, Inc. via a tender offer for $25.00 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $30.25 per share. The transaction followed a strategic review process where Mersana faced significant capital needs and a 'going concern' qualification, ultimately choosing Day One's all-cash offer over a mixed cash-and-stock proposal from another bidder.

## Interaction History

In early 2025, Mersana sought capital to fund its lead candidate, Emi-Le. After a workforce reduction in May 2025, the company engaged TD Cowen to run a sale process, contacting 32 parties. Party A initially proposed a $30M upfront licensing deal but later withdrew. Party B emerged as a serious bidder in September 2025, proposing a mixed cash/stock deal. Day One (Parent), which had paused talks in June, re-engaged in October. Day One initially offered $12.00/share cash plus $10.00 CVR. Mersana countered, leading Day One to increase its offer to $21.00 and finally $25.00 cash upfront plus a $30.25 CVR. The Mersana Board favored Day One's proposal over Party B's because it was all-cash and included more near-term CVR milestones. Party B declined to increase its offer on the required timeline. The deal was signed on November 12, 2025, and closed on January 6, 2026, following the expiration of the HSR waiting period on December 26, 2025.

## Rationale

The Mersana Board determined the deal was the best available alternative due to the company's limited cash runway and 'going concern' audit qualification. The board noted that the all-cash upfront payment provided certainty of value, while the CVR allowed shareholders to participate in the potential upside of Emi-Le. Day One sought to expand its oncology pipeline with a clinical-stage ADC candidate.
