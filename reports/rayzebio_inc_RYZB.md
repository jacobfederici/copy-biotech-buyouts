## Summary

RayzeBio, Inc. was acquired by Bristol-Myers Squibb Company for $62.50 per share in an all-cash tender offer followed by a merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The transaction, valued at approximately $4.1 billion on a fully diluted basis, focused on RayzeBio's radiopharmaceutical platform and its lead Phase 3 candidate, RYZ101, for gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine tumors.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2023 as partnership discussions involving BMS, Party A, Party B, and Party C. On December 15, 2023, Party B and BMS both shifted to acquisition proposals ($36.00 and $40.00 respectively). RayzeBio's CEO, Ken Song, and financial advisor, Centerview Partners, managed a rapid competitive process. Party A entered with a CVR-based bid on December 22, but BMS remained the lead bidder. On December 24, BMS CEO Chris Boerner delivered a 'best and final' offer of $62.50 per share, representing a 104% premium, contingent on signing by the next morning. The Board evaluated the risk of losing this high premium against the lower, diligence-contingent bids from Party A and B. Regulatory considerations favored BMS as they lacked a competing radiopharmaceutical pipeline, reducing antitrust risk compared to other bidders. The deal was signed on Christmas Day 2023.

## Rationale

The Board determined the $62.50 cash price provided immediate and certain value at a substantial premium (104%) over the standalone trading price. They noted the significant risks of remaining independent, including the capital-intensive nature of radiopharmaceutical manufacturing and the clinical/regulatory hurdles for RYZ101. BMS sought to acquire RayzeBio to establish a leading position in the high-growth radiopharmaceutical therapy (RPT) sector, leveraging RayzeBio's actinium-based platform and manufacturing capabilities.
