## Summary

Adams Respiratory Therapeutics, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Reckitt Benckiser Group plc on December 10, 2007, under which Reckitt Benckiser's subsidiary, Twickenham Inc., launched a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares of Adams for $60.00 per share in cash. The transaction, valued at approximately $2.3 billion including fees and expenses, was completed on January 29, 2008, following a successful tender offer in which approximately 95.9% of shares were acquired, followed by a short-form merger.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in August 2007 when Company A approached Adams CEO Michael Valentino. Adams engaged Morgan Stanley and initiated a structured solicitation process in October 2007, contacting nine potential strategic acquirors. Four parties signed confidentiality agreements and conducted due diligence. On November 19, 2007, Company A and Reckitt Benckiser submitted preliminary cash bids. Following a second phase of diligence, final bids were requested by December 6, 2007. Reckitt Benckiser submitted the highest offer at $60.00 per share, supported by a $2.5 billion credit facility from Barclays. The Adams Board, advised by Alston & Bird on fiduciary duties and Morgan Stanley on financial fairness, met on December 9, 2007, to approve the deal. The parties finalized the Merger Agreement on December 10, 2007. Regulatory clearance was obtained via HSR Act filings in late December 2007.

## Rationale

The Adams Board recommended the deal because the $60.00 cash price represented a significant premium (37.4% over the last closing price and 20.6% over the all-time high), eliminated valuation uncertainty through cash payment, and followed a robust solicitation process that determined no superior proposals were likely. Management also believed the corporate cultures were similar and that Reckitt Benckiser's financial strength would benefit employees and customers.
