## Summary

Regeneron Pharmaceuticals acquired Decibel Therapeutics via a tender offer for $4.00 per share in cash plus a non-tradeable contingent value right (CVR) of up to $3.50 per share. The deal, valued at approximately $109 million in upfront cash, was structured to address Regeneron's concerns regarding clinical trial enrollment risks for Decibel's lead gene therapy candidate, DB-OTO. Following the tender of approximately 86.12% of outstanding shares, the merger was completed under Section 251(h) of the DGCL on September 25, 2023.

## Timeline

- 2017-11-15: Decibel and Regeneron enter into a strategic license and collaboration agreement.
- 2023-03-30: Regeneron's Nouhad Husseini advises Decibel CEO Laurence Reid that Regeneron intends to propose an acquisition rather than further collaboration amendments.
- 2023-04-12: Regeneron submits a non-binding proposal to acquire Decibel for $5.00 per share in cash.

- 2023-06-16: Regeneron CEO Leonard Schleifer proposes $6.00 cash plus $1.00 CVR for clinical proof of concept.
- 2023-07-09: Regeneron proposes $6.50 per share in all-cash with no CVR.
- 2023-07-19: Regeneron expresses concerns regarding DB-OTO patient enrollment and requests additional due diligence interviews.
- 2023-07-24: Regeneron reduces offer to $3.25 cash plus $3.25 CVR due to perceived enrollment risks.
- 2023-07-31: Regeneron submits 'best and final' proposal of $4.00 cash plus $3.50 CVR.

- 2023-08-25: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2023-09-22: Tender offer expires with 86.12% of shares tendered.
- 2023-09-25: Merger completed; Decibel becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Regeneron.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2023 as discussions for collaboration amendments shifted toward an acquisition. Regeneron initially offered $5.00/share in April 2023. Decibel, advised by Centerview Partners, conducted outreach to four other pharmaceutical companies (Parties A, D, E, and F), but none resulted in a proposal. Negotiations between Decibel CEO Laurence Reid and Regeneron's Nouhad Husseini and CEO Leonard Schleifer saw the offer rise to $6.50 all-cash by early July. However, during late-stage diligence, Regeneron identified significant risks in the enrollment timeline for the DB-OTO Phase 1/2 trial. Consequently, Regeneron slashed its cash offer to $3.25 plus a CVR on July 24. Decibel's Transaction Committee (Finger, Kapusta, McLaughlin) pushed for better terms, leading to a final agreement on August 8 for $4.00 cash and a $3.50 CVR with an extended milestone sunset date of December 31, 2024, for the first payment.

## Rationale

The Decibel Board determined the transaction was superior to remaining independent due to the company's substantial capital needs, the high dilution risk of future equity raises, and the clinical risks associated with its early-stage pipeline. Regeneron sought to consolidate the collaboration assets and mitigate its financial exposure to clinical enrollment delays through the CVR structure.

## Evidence

- On September 25, 2023, the Merger was completed pursuant to Section 251(h) of the DGCL, with no vote of the Company’s stockholders required. (0001193125-23-240971)
- The Decibel Board unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the transactions... are fair to and in the best interest of Decibel and its stockholders. (0001193125-23-221145)
