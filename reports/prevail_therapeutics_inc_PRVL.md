## Summary

Prevail Therapeutics Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Eli Lilly and Company for $22.50 per share in cash plus a contingent value right of up to $4.00 per share based on the first regulatory approval of a gene therapy product. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, valuing the company at approximately $880 million upfront and up to approximately $1.04 billion including the CVR.

## Timeline

- 2019-06-28: Initial meeting between Prevail's Dr. Franz Hefti and Lilly's Dr. Daniel Skovronsky regarding CNS gene therapy interest.
- 2019-08-13: Prevail and Lilly enter into a mutual confidentiality agreement.
- 2020-01-13: Lilly provides a non-binding proposal for a co-development and co-commercialization collaboration at the J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference.
- 2020-03-06: Prevail opens a virtual data room for Lilly and Party A to conduct diligence for a potential partnership.
- 2020-11-13: Lilly shifts from partnership interest to an acquisition proposal, offering $21.00 per share in cash.
- 2020-11-20: Party B provides an oral indication of interest in an acquisition at a price in the mid-$20s.
- 2020-11-25: Lilly increases its cash offer to $23.50 per share.
- 2020-12-03: Party B withdraws from the acquisition process, citing a preference for a structured transaction.
- 2020-12-05: Lilly reduces its cash offer to $22.50 per share, citing updated development timelines.
- 2020-12-07: Lilly proposes adding a $4.00 CVR to the $22.50 cash offer.
- 2020-12-14: Prevail Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement.
- 2020-12-15: Transaction is publicly announced.
- 2020-12-22: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2021-01-21: Tender offer expires with approximately 79.8% of shares tendered.
- 2021-01-22: Merger completed; Prevail becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of Eli Lilly.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in mid-2019 as a search for a strategic partner. Prevail engaged with 20 potential parties, 17 of whom signed NDAs. By early 2020, Lilly and Party A were the primary contenders for a collaboration. In November 2020, Lilly pivoted to an acquisition offer of $21.00/share. Prevail's board, advised by Centerview Partners, conducted a targeted market check, contacting Party B, Party C, and Party E. Party B expressed interest in the mid-$20s, prompting Lilly to raise its bid to $23.50. However, Party B withdrew on December 3, 2020. Lilly subsequently lowered its cash bid to $22.50 on December 5, citing revised internal models. Prevail countered by requesting a CVR to bridge the valuation gap. Lilly agreed to a $4.00 CVR tied to the first regulatory approval of any gene therapy product in the portfolio. Final negotiations focused on the 'Commercially Reasonable Efforts' definition in the CVR agreement and the termination fee, which was set at $30 million.

## Rationale

The Prevail Board determined the offer was superior to remaining independent, citing the substantial premium (115% over the Nov 13 price), the risks and capital requirements of independent drug development, and the liquidity provided by an all-cash offer. Lilly sought to expand its CNS portfolio with Prevail's gene therapy platform and clinical-stage assets.

- On January 22, 2021... Purchaser accepted for payment all Shares validly tendered... Purchaser acquired a sufficient number of Shares to complete the merger... without a vote of the stockholders (0001193125-21-014276).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a tender offer under DGCL 251(h). The CVR milestone is tied to the first regulatory approval for commercial sale of any of the Company's gene therapy products (PR001 through PR014) in the US, UK, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, or Japan. Shares outstanding (34,248,033) were sourced from the SC 14D9 as of Dec 14, 2020.
