## Summary

Covis Group S.à r.l., through its indirect wholly-owned subsidiary Covis Mergerco Inc., acquired AMAG Pharmaceuticals, Inc. for $13.75 per share in cash, totaling approximately $499.8 million. The acquisition was structured as a tender offer followed by a merger, and was completed on November 16, 2020.

## Timeline

- 2019-12-01: Covis approached AMAG expressing interest in acquiring the global rights to Feraheme. AMAG communicated that it would not be considering a sale of Feraheme at that time due to an anticipated exploration of a potential sale of its women’s health assets.
- 2020-01-01: AMAG announced a review of its product portfolio and strategy, engaging Goldman Sachs as financial advisor to drive profitability and enhance stockholder value. Discussions began with numerous third-parties interested in acquiring all or some of AMAG's women's health assets.
- 2020-02-06: The Apollo Funds announced an agreement to acquire Covis from an affiliate of Cerberus Capital Management, L.P.
- 2020-04-06: Alexander Mironov, Covis’ chief business officer, contacted Mr. Santini to express Covis’ interest in AMAG and indicated an offer letter for acquisition would be sent the following week.
- 2020-04-07: Covis sent a non-binding letter of interest (the "April Proposal") to acquire all of AMAG’s outstanding shares for $11.00 per share in cash, requesting a limited period of exclusivity.
- 2020-04-08: The AMAG Board held a web meeting, with Goodwin Procter LLP present, to discuss the April Proposal. The Board determined it was in stockholders' best interests to focus on ongoing activities (divestiture of women's health assets, CEO search, business development, cost reduction) and instructed management to update AMAG’s long-range plan for Goldman Sachs' preliminary financial analyses. Mr. Santini was authorized to communicate to Mr. Mironov that AMAG would consider the proposal after focusing on the women's health divestiture.
- 2020-04-09: Mr. Santini communicated the Board's message to Mr. Mironov.
- 2020-04-24: The AMAG Board held a web meeting, with senior management and representatives of Goldman Sachs and Goodwin present, to update on ongoing activities and approve the appointment of Scott Myers as CEO and Board member. Management discussed preliminary financial forecasts, and the Board authorized Mr. Santini to communicate that it would consider the April Proposal after focusing on the women's health divestiture and onboarding Mr. Myers.
- 2020-04-25: Mr. Santini communicated the Board's message to Mr. Mironov.
- 2020-04-28: AMAG announced the appointment of Scott Myers as CEO and Board member.
- 2020-05-19: The AMAG Board held a regularly scheduled web meeting, with senior management and representatives of Goldman Sachs and Goodwin present. The Board discussed the April Proposal and a potential sale, reviewed preliminary financial forecasts, and authorized Mr. Santini to continue discussions with Covis, noting the April Proposal was inadequate but willing to share limited confidential information to help Covis improve its offer.
- 2020-05-21: Mr. Santini participated in a call with Mr. Mironov and Michael Porter (Covis CEO), stating the $11.00 per share offer was inadequate but the Board was willing to engage in preliminary discussions and share limited confidential information. AMAG also announced the sale of its rights to Intrarosa to Millicent Pharma Limited.
- 2020-05-29: AMAG and Covis executed a confidentiality agreement, including a year-long standstill provision. AMAG provided Covis with limited business and financial information.
- 2020-06-04: Goldman Sachs delivered a new relationship disclosure letter to the AMAG Board regarding its relationships with the Apollo Funds. The Board did not view these as a conflict of interest.
- 2020-06-09: The AMAG Board held a web meeting, with senior management and representatives of Goldman Sachs and Goodwin present. Goodwin reviewed fiduciary duties. Management discussed and the Board approved the Management Projections for use by Goldman Sachs. The Board discussed risks, confidentiality, and authorized management and advisors to continue preliminary discussions and due diligence with Covis, and requested Goldman Sachs propose a list of potential third parties for acquisition.
- 2020-06-19: Covis was granted access to AMAG's virtual data room.
- 2020-07-13: Covis sent a non-binding letter of interest (the "July 13 Proposal") to Mr. Myers to acquire all of AMAG’s outstanding shares for $11.50 per share in cash at closing, plus potential CVRs of up to $1.50 per share, and requested a limited period of exclusivity. Mr. Myers informed the Board.
- 2020-07-14: The AMAG Board held a web meeting, with senior management and representatives of Goldman Sachs and Goodwin present. They discussed the July 13 Proposal, CVR risks, preliminary financial analyses, and standalone prospects. The Board determined the July 13 Proposal was insufficient for full due diligence but allowed management and Goldman Sachs to provide limited due diligence to Covis to increase its offer. Goldman Sachs was authorized to contact eight global life sciences companies. The Board formed a Transaction Committee (Paul Fonteyne, David Johnson, Mr. Santini, Davey Scoon, and James Sulat) to oversee negotiations.
- 2020-07-23: AMAG announced an exclusive licensing agreement with Norgine B.V. for ciraparantag. Covis sent Mr. Myers a revised non-binding letter of interest (the "July 23 Proposal") to acquire all of AMAG’s outstanding shares for $13.00 per share in cash, requesting a six-week exclusivity period. Mr. Myers informed the Board and Transaction Committee.
- 2020-07-27: The Transaction Committee held a web meeting, with senior management and representatives of Goldman Sachs and Goodwin present. They discussed the July 23 Proposal, noting the elimination of CVRs and increased offer price. The Committee determined the proposal was insufficient for exclusive negotiations but allowed more in-depth due diligence for Covis to increase its offer. Goldman Sachs was authorized to continue discussions with two remaining interested parties. AMAG also announced the termination of its license agreement with Palatin Technologies, Inc. and the return of Vyleesi rights.
- 2020-08-04: The Transaction Committee held a web meeting to receive an update on discussions with Covis.
- 2020-08-20: The Transaction Committee held a web meeting and authorized Goldman Sachs to deliver a draft merger agreement to Covis and request a refreshed offer letter by month-end.
- 2020-08-24: Goldman Sachs communicated the Transaction Committee's message to Covis and provided the draft merger agreement, which included customary terms, exceptions to "Company Material Adverse Effect," buyer's obligation for antitrust approvals, AMAG's ability to consider superior proposals, and a 2.5% termination fee.
- 2020-09-01: Covis sent Mr. Myers a revised non-binding letter of interest (the "September 1 Proposal") to acquire all of AMAG’s outstanding shares for $13.00 per share in cash, requesting exclusivity until September 30, 2020.
- 2020-09-02: The AMAG Board held a web meeting, with senior management and representatives of Goldman Sachs and Goodwin present. They discussed the September 1 Proposal and Goldman Sachs reported that all 16 contacted parties declined to pursue an acquisition. The Board determined not to grant exclusivity at $13.00 but authorized senior management and advisors to permit Covis to complete due diligence to increase its offer. Mr. Myers and Goldman Sachs were instructed to communicate that $13.00 was inadequate and to emphasize the importance of a quick, increased offer.
- 2020-09-09: Covis sent Mr. Myers a revised non-binding letter of interest (the "September 9 Proposal") to acquire all of AMAG’s outstanding shares for $13.75 per share in cash, requesting exclusivity until September 30, 2020, and stating this was Covis’ best and final offer. Mr. Myers informed the Board and Transaction Committee.
- 2020-09-11: The AMAG Board held a regularly scheduled web meeting, with senior management and representatives of Goldman Sachs and Goodwin present. They discussed the September 9 Proposal, Goldman Sachs' financial analyses, and the lack of other interested parties. Given the risks and the increased offer, the Board authorized management and advisors to negotiate an exclusivity agreement with Covis, expiring September 30, 2020, and to continue discussions and confirmatory due diligence on an exclusive basis. AMAG and Covis entered into an exclusivity agreement.
- 2020-09-16: Paul Weiss (Covis' counsel) provided Goodwin with a revised draft of the merger agreement, including proposed changes to the definition of "Company Material Adverse Effect" and a termination fee of 3.5% of equity value.
- 2020-09-20: Goodwin provided Paul Weiss with a revised draft of the merger agreement, including further revisions to the definition of "Company Material Adverse Effect" and a termination fee of 3.25% of equity value.
- 2020-09-24: Paul Weiss provided Goodwin with a revised draft of the merger agreement, including provisions related to Covis’ debt financing and a reverse termination fee of 5.5% of equity value.
- 2020-09-29: The AMAG Board held a web meeting, with representatives of Goldman Sachs and Goodwin Procter present. Advisors reported agreement on material terms of the merger agreement and other transaction documents, including a reverse termination fee of 6.0% of equity value, but noted Covis’ debt financing sources needed more time.
- 2020-09-30: Bloomberg published an article indicating discussions between Covis and AMAG. The parties finalized definitive documentation, including the debt commitment letter, and completed due diligence.
- 2020-10-01: The Transaction Committee and the AMAG Board held a joint web meeting. Goldman Sachs reviewed its final analyses of the $13.75 cash per share, and Goodwin provided a review of fiduciary duties and an update on final negotiations. Goldman Sachs delivered its oral opinion, later confirmed in writing, that the $13.75 per share was fair from a financial point of view. The Board unanimously adopted resolutions approving the Merger Agreement and recommending stockholders tender their shares. AMAG and Covis executed the Merger Agreement and issued a joint press release.
- 2020-10-05: AMAG received a proposal from the FDA to withdraw marketing approval of Makena and a notice of opportunity for a hearing.
- 2020-10-15: Merger Sub commenced the Offer, and AMAG filed its Schedule 14D-9.
- 2020-11-12: The Offer expired at midnight (New York City time). 34,485,870 Shares, representing approximately 99.3% of outstanding Shares, were validly tendered and not withdrawn. All conditions to the Offer were satisfied or waived, and Covis and Merger Sub accepted all validly tendered Shares.
- 2020-11-16: Merger Sub merged with and into AMAG pursuant to Section 251(h) of the DGCL. AMAG became the surviving corporation and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Covis. Each remaining Share was converted into the right to receive $13.75 in cash. AMAG and Wilmington Trust entered into a Second Supplemental Indenture, and AMAG, Covis Finco, and Wilmington Trust entered into a Third Supplemental Indenture. AMAG and certain subsidiaries entered into guarantees for Covis Finco's obligations under an Amended and Restated Credit Agreement. AMAG notified NASDAQ of the consummation of the Merger and requested delisting.

## Interaction History

In December 2019, Covis approached AMAG regarding an acquisition of Feraheme, but AMAG declined to consider it at that time due to an anticipated divestiture of its women's health assets. In January 2020, AMAG engaged Goldman Sachs to assist with a strategic review. On April 6, 2020, Alexander Mironov, Covis' chief business officer, contacted Mr. Santini, a director of AMAG, to express interest in acquiring AMAG. On April 7, 2020, Covis submitted a non-binding offer of $11.00 per share, requesting exclusivity. The AMAG Board, after reviewing its fiduciary duties with Goodwin Procter LLP, deemed the offer inadequate and decided to focus on ongoing strategic initiatives, including the divestiture of women's health assets and the search for a new CEO. Mr. Santini communicated this to Mr. Mironov. On April 24, 2020, the Board approved Scott Myers as the new CEO and continued to consider the Covis offer, again communicating that it would revisit discussions after other priorities. On May 19, 2020, the Board, with Goldman Sachs and Goodwin present, reiterated the inadequacy of the $11.00 offer but expressed willingness to engage in preliminary discussions and share limited confidential information to help Covis improve its offer. On May 21, 2020, Mr. Santini conveyed this to Mr. Mironov and Michael Porter, Covis' CEO. A confidentiality agreement with a standstill provision was executed on May 29, 2020, and AMAG provided limited due diligence information. On June 4, 2020, Goldman Sachs disclosed its relationships with the Apollo Funds, which the Board did not view as a conflict. On June 9, 2020, the Board approved management's financial projections for Goldman Sachs' use and authorized continued preliminary discussions with Covis, while also instructing Goldman Sachs to identify other potential acquirers. Covis gained access to a virtual data room around June 19, 2020. On July 13, 2020, Covis submitted a revised non-binding offer of $11.50 per share plus potential CVRs of up to $1.50 per share, again requesting exclusivity. The Board, after discussion with its advisors, found this offer insufficient for full due diligence but allowed further limited due diligence. Goldman Sachs contacted eight identified potential parties, all but two of whom declined interest. On July 23, 2020, Covis submitted another revised non-binding offer of $13.00 per share, without CVRs, and requested a six-week exclusivity period. The Transaction Committee, formed on July 14, 2020, found this offer insufficient for exclusive negotiations but allowed more in-depth due diligence. Goldman Sachs subsequently contacted eight additional parties, all of whom declined. On August 20, 2020, the Transaction Committee authorized Goldman Sachs to deliver a draft merger agreement to Covis and request a refreshed offer. The draft included a 2.5% termination fee. On September 1, 2020, Covis submitted a non-binding offer of $13.00 per share, requesting exclusivity until September 30, 2020. The Board again deemed $13.00 inadequate and declined exclusivity but allowed Covis to complete due diligence to increase its offer. On September 9, 2020, Covis submitted a revised non-binding offer of $13.75 per share, stating it was their best and final offer, and requested exclusivity until September 30, 2020. On September 11, 2020, the Board, considering the risks and the lack of other interested parties, authorized an exclusivity agreement with Covis. A revised draft merger agreement from Paul Weiss (Covis' counsel) on September 16, 2020, proposed a 3.5% termination fee, which Goodwin countered with 3.25% on September 20, 2020. On September 24, 2020, Paul Weiss proposed a 5.5% reverse termination fee. Negotiations continued, and on September 29, 2020, the Board was informed that material terms were agreed, including a 6.0% reverse termination fee, but debt financing sources needed more time. On October 1, 2020, after final negotiations and Goldman Sachs' fairness opinion, the Board unanimously approved the Merger Agreement and recommended the offer. The Merger Agreement was executed the same day.

## Rationale

The AMAG Board recommended the offer due to the significant premium over recent trading prices, the certainty of immediate cash value for stockholders, and the avoidance of regulatory, commercialization, and other business risks associated with AMAG's products and product candidates. The Board also considered the inherent uncertainty of achieving standalone financial forecasts, the need for additional capital, and the lack of interest from other potential acquirers. The robust, arm's-length negotiation process, resulting in an increased offer price and favorable terms, further supported the Board's decision. The business reputation and financial resources of Covis and the Apollo Funds also contributed to the belief that the transaction could be completed quickly and orderly.

## Decision Notes

The deal is marked as 'completed' based on the 8-K filing on 2020-11-17, which states the merger was consummated on November 16, 2020. The per-share consideration is a straightforward cash payment of $13.75, with no CVRs. The shares outstanding figure of 34,502,648 is taken from the SC 14D9 filing as of September 25, 2020. The implied upfront equity value is calculated by multiplying the per-share consideration by the shares outstanding (13.75 * 34,502,648 = 474,411,410).

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, and reported strategic reviews for AMAG Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (AMAG) prior to its confirmed acquisition by Covis Group S.à r.l. on October 1, 2020.

*   **July 6, 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *TheStreet*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer(s):** Genzyme, Amgen, Johnson & Johnson, and DaVita.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported.
    *   **Details:** Following the FDA approval of Feraheme, speculation emerged that AMAG was "ripe for takeover." Analysts suggested Genzyme as a logical buyer due to its existing presence in the dialysis market, while Amgen and J&J were viewed as potential suitors looking to protect their nephrology franchises from upcoming Medicare reimbursement changes.

*   **August 3, 2011**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *FierceBiotech* / *Reuters*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** MSMB Capital Management (led by Martin Shkreli).
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** $378 million (approximately $18.00 per share).
    *   **Details:** MSMB Capital Management, an activist investor, launched an unsolicited $378 million hostile bid to acquire AMAG. The offer was intended to block AMAG’s proposed merger with Allos Therapeutics, which MSMB argued did not protect shareholder interests.

*   **January 22, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Reuters* / SEC Filings (Schedule 14D-9)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Unnamed "Party A" (later revealed in filings).
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported.
    *   **Details:** Media outlets reported that AMAG was in the early stages of exploring a potential sale. According to later SEC disclosures, an interested party ("Party A") withdrew its acquisition proposal the same day the rumors surfaced, citing concerns over the media leak.

*   **January 28, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *24/7 Wall St.* / Jefferies
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Not specified (General "takeover target").
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported (Jefferies price target was $70.00).
    *   **Details:** Following a significant drop in share price and weak sales for the drug Makena, Jefferies analysts issued a note stating that at its current valuation, AMAG had become a likely "takeover target" for larger pharmaceutical companies.

*   **January 9, 2020**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *GlobeNewswire* (Company Announcement) / *BioPharma Dive*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Not specified (Strategic Review).
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported.
    *   **Details:** AMAG officially announced a "strategic review" of its product portfolio and a leadership transition (CEO William Heiden stepping down). While not a specific rumor of a buyer, this announcement triggered widespread market speculation regarding a potential sale of the entire company or its individual assets (specifically Intrarosa and Vyleesi).

*   **September 30, 2020**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Seeking Alpha* / Market Rumors
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Not specified.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not reported.
    *   **Details:** On the final trading day before the official deal announcement, unusual trading activity and "takeover rumors" were noted by market observers. The stock closed at $9.42; the following morning, the definitive agreement with Covis Group was announced at $13.75 per share.
