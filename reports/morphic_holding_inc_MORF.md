## Summary

Morphic Holding, Inc. was acquired by Eli Lilly and Company for $57.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $3.2 billion. The transaction focused on Morphic's lead asset, MORF-057, an oral integrin inhibitor for inflammatory bowel disease, and was completed via a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL.

## Timeline

- 2024-08-16: Merger completed; Morphic becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Lilly.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in April 2024 after years of periodic business development contact. Lilly's initial offer of $46.00 (April 29) was rejected as insufficient. Morphic's board, advised by Centerview Partners and Fenwick & West, reached out to two other large pharmaceutical firms (Company A and Company B); both declined to pursue an acquisition. Lilly raised its bid to $50.00 (May 28) and then $53.00 (June 21). Morphic consistently pushed for $60.00. On June 24, Lilly's advisor (Citi) communicated a final price of $57.00 per share, explicitly rejecting the inclusion of a Contingent Value Right (CVR). Morphic agreed to exclusivity on June 25. One director, Dr. Nisha Nanda, recused herself from all deliberations due to her employment at Lilly. The board approved the $57.00 offer on July 7, 2024, noting it represented a 79% premium to the prior closing price.

## Rationale

The Morphic Board determined the deal was superior to remaining independent due to the risks of clinical trial failure for MORF-057, the need for substantial future capital raises (estimated at $800M through 2029), and the competitive landscape of the IBD market. Lilly sought to bolster its immunology portfolio with an oral integrin inhibitor.

## Evidence

- On August 16, 2024, Parent and Purchaser accepted for payment the Shares that were validly tendered... representing approximately 92.8% of the issued and outstanding Shares (0001193125-24-202123).
- The Board unanimously... determined that the Offer, the Merger and the other Transactions are fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company and its stockholders (0001193125-24-183114).

## Decision Notes

The deal value is approximately $3.2 billion on a fully diluted basis. Shares outstanding (50,221,010) were sourced from the SC 14D9 as of July 12, 2024. The transaction included no CVRs despite Morphic's request during negotiations.
