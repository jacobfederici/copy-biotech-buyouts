## Summary

Bioverativ Inc. was acquired by Sanofi for $105.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $11.6 billion. The transaction followed a series of unsolicited proposals from Sanofi starting in May 2017, eventually leading to a negotiated agreement that provided a significant premium to Bioverativ's standalone trading price and utilized Section 251(h) of the DGCL to effect a second-step merger without a stockholder vote.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in May 2017 with unsolicited outreach from Sanofi's Chairman Serge Weinberg to Bioverativ's Chairman Brian Posner. Bioverativ initially rebuffed Sanofi, stating they were focused on their standalone plan following the spin-off from Biogen. In November 2017, Sanofi submitted a written proposal at $98.50 per share. Bioverativ's board, advised by J.P. Morgan, Guggenheim Securities, and Paul Weiss, determined the price was too low but agreed to a management presentation on December 18, 2017, to highlight pipeline value. Following the presentation and subsequent diligence, Sanofi raised its offer to $101.50 on January 3, 2018. Bioverativ countered at $105.00 on January 4, 2018, which Sanofi accepted subject to exclusivity. A key negotiation point involved the Tax Matters Agreement with Biogen; the parties entered into a Letter Agreement on January 21, 2018, to ensure the merger would not jeopardize the tax-free status of Bioverativ's prior spin-off. Regulatory clearances were obtained from the JFTC (Japan) on January 31, 2018, and the HSR waiting period expired prior to the offer's conclusion.

## Rationale

The Bioverativ Board determined the $105.00 cash price provided immediate and certain value at a significant premium (63.8% over the last trading day) compared to the risks of remaining independent. Key risks considered included revenue concentration in ELOCTATE and ALPROLIX, competition from new entrants like Roche's Hemlibra, and clinical development uncertainties. Sanofi sought the acquisition to establish a leading position in the rare blood disorders market and leverage Bioverativ's specialized clinical and commercial expertise.
