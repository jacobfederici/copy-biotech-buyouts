## Summary

Sigilon Therapeutics, Inc. was acquired by Eli Lilly and Company via a tender offer for $14.92 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $111.64 per share. The transaction followed a strategic review process initiated after clinical holds and reprioritization of Sigilon's pipeline, ultimately resulting in Lilly acquiring its long-term collaboration partner to streamline the development of encapsulated cell therapies for Type 1 Diabetes.

## Timeline

- 2018-04-02: Sigilon and Lilly enter into a research collaboration and exclusive license agreement for T1D.
- 2021-07-09: Sigilon announces FDA clinical hold on SIG-001 for Hemophilia A; stock price drops 25%.
- 2021-12-13: Sigilon announces strategic reprioritization focusing on T1D and MPS-1, reducing workforce by 38%.
- 2022-04-30: Sigilon Board approves engagement of Lazard to explore strategic alternatives.
- 2022-09-13: Lilly representative contacts Sigilon CEO to discuss the strategic process.
- 2022-10-17: Special Committee of the Board formally authorized to evaluate potential transactions.
- 2022-12-12: Sigilon pursues a potential reverse merger with 'Party E' and associated PIPE financing.
- 2023-04-20: Special Committee determines to stop pursuing Party E transaction due to lack of investor interest in the PIPE.
- 2023-05-04: Lilly expresses potential interest in a whole-company acquisition rather than just an asset purchase.
- 2023-05-07: Lilly submits initial indication of interest for ~$33M upfront plus CVRs.
- 2023-05-30: Lilly submits revised offer with higher CVR milestones ($10M, $65M, and $200M aggregate levels).
- 2023-06-28: Sigilon Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement with Lilly.
- 2023-06-29: Public announcement of the transaction.
- 2023-07-13: Lilly commences the tender offer.
- 2023-08-09: Tender offer expires with 68.23% of shares tendered.
- 2023-08-11: Merger completed; Sigilon becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Lilly.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest in late 2022 after Sigilon's clinical setbacks. Sigilon, advised by Lazard and Canaccord, contacted 141 potential counterparties. Several parties (A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I) submitted various reverse merger proposals. A potential deal with Party E (a reverse merger with a $100M-$150M PIPE) was the lead option until April 2023, when it collapsed due to insufficient investor interest. Lilly initially expressed interest only in an asset purchase of the T1D program in April 2023. However, by May 4, 2023, Lilly shifted to a whole-company acquisition proposal. Initial bids from Lilly were rejected by the Sigilon Board as too low. On May 30, 2023, Lilly increased the CVR milestone values significantly. Final negotiations in June 2023 adjusted the upfront cash from $15.13 to $14.92 per share based on updated net cash and expense estimates. The Sigilon Board approved the final terms on June 28, 2023.

## Rationale

The Sigilon Board recommended the deal because it provided immediate liquidity at a significant premium (281.6% over the June 22, 2023 closing price) and allowed stockholders to retain upside through CVRs. The board noted Sigilon's need for substantial additional capital, the risks of clinical trial failure, and the lack of other executable superior proposals after an exhaustive search. Lilly's rationale was to consolidate its leadership in diabetes by acquiring full control of the encapsulation technology and islet cell program it had previously collaborated on with Sigilon.

## Evidence

- The Company Board unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions are advisable, fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company and its stockholders... and (iv) resolved to recommend that Company stockholders accept the Offer and tender their Shares (0001193125-23-186539).
- On August 11, 2023, following consummation of the Offer, Purchaser merged with and into the Company (the 'Merger'), with the Company as the surviving corporation (0001193125-23-210739).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a Section 251(h) merger following a successful tender offer. Shares outstanding (2,502,084) were sourced from the Item 1(b) of the SC 14D9. CVR milestones are time-bound: First Dosing by July 2027, Pivotal Trial by Dec 2028, and Marketing Authorization by Dec 2031.
