## Summary

Thorne HealthTech, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by L Catterton via a cash tender offer for $10.20 per share. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, resulting in Thorne becoming a privately held company and a wholly owned subsidiary of Healthspan Buyer, LLC.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began with multiple unsolicited inquiries from financial sponsors between July 2022 and April 2023. Paul Jacobson (CEO) managed initial contacts with Sponsor A, Catterton Growth, and Catterton Asia. After formalizing a Special Committee in May 2023, Thorne conducted a broad outreach to 38 potential acquirers (19 sponsors, 19 strategics). Catterton Flagship emerged as the primary bidder, initially offering $9.25 on July 21, 2023. A competitive bidding process ensued between Catterton Flagship and Sponsor C. On August 17, 2023, Sponsor C offered $10.15, prompting the Board to push Catterton Flagship to $10.20. Catterton Flagship agreed to $10.20 on the condition of exclusivity. Final negotiations focused on the 'no-shop' provisions and the termination fee, which was settled at $25.16 million (3.75% of equity value). Regulatory clearance was obtained when the HSR waiting period expired on September 26, 2023.

## Rationale

The Thorne Board determined that the cash offer provided certain value and liquidity to stockholders at a significant premium (112.7% over the unaffected 30-day VWAP). The Board considered the risks of remaining independent, including the need for substantial capital for international expansion, the volatility of being a small-cap public company, and the inherent uncertainty in achieving long-term projections for the Onegevity and OneDraw platforms.
