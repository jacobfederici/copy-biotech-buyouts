## Summary

Assertio Holdings, Inc. was acquired by Zydus Worldwide DMCC for $23.50 per share in cash, representing a total equity value of approximately $166.4 million. The transaction followed a competitive bidding process where Zydus emerged as a superior bidder over Garda Therapeutics, which had previously entered into two successive merger agreements with Assertio. The deal was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL.

- 2026-06-16: Merger completed; Assertio becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Zydus.

## Interaction History

The transaction was the result of a 17-month strategic review involving over 30 counterparties. Assertio initially signed a deal with Garda Therapeutics on April 8, 2026, for $18.00/share + CVR. During a 'window-shop' period, Zydus (Parent) and Party L emerged. Zydus initially offered $21.60. Garda matched this with a $21.80 cash offer on May 1, 2026. Zydus then raised to $23.10 and finally $23.50. Although Garda made a final bid of $23.60 + CVR on May 12, the Assertio Board, advised by Moelis and Gibson Dunn, determined the Zydus offer was superior. The Board cited Zydus's status as a creditworthy entity with a full guarantee and no financing contingencies, whereas Garda's bid relied on third-party debt/equity commitments that the Board found insufficient to cover all closing costs (including convertible note repurchases and transaction expenses).

## Rationale

The Board determined that a sale was preferable to a standalone path because Assertio was heavily dependent on Rolvedon, which faced a limited commercial lifecycle and projected revenue declines starting in 2028. The company lacked an internal R&D pipeline and faced difficulties identifying attractive acquisition targets. The Zydus offer provided immediate liquidity at a significant premium (75.8% over the unaffected price) and eliminated the risks associated with the company's projected EBITDA compression toward breakeven by 2029.

## Evidence

- Parent and Purchaser irrevocably accepted for payment, on June 16, 2026, all Shares that were validly tendered (0001104659-26-074619).

## Decision Notes

The deal value of $166.4M is the stated fully diluted equity value in the SC 14D9. Shares outstanding (6,462,180) are as of May 14, 2026, per the filing. The CVR from the Garda offer was excluded from the final Zydus deal. The $5.81M termination fee paid to Garda was a key closing condition funded by Zydus.
