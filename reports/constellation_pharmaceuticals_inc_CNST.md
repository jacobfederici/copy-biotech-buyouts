## Summary

Constellation Pharmaceuticals was acquired by MorphoSys AG for $34.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $1.7 billion. The transaction was supported by a strategic financing collaboration between MorphoSys and Royalty Pharma plc, which provided funding through royalty and revenue participation agreements. The deal aimed to bolster MorphoSys's oncology pipeline, specifically adding pelabresib (CPI-0610) for myelofibrosis.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in mid-2020 with partnership discussions. MorphoSys initially offered $35.00/share in October 2020, which Constellation rejected as insufficient. After a period of dormancy, discussions resumed in April 2021. Constellation conducted a broad outreach involving eight potential counterparties (Parties A-H). MorphoSys returned with a mixed cash/stock offer of $34.00 ($29 cash/$5 stock) on April 21, 2021. Competitive bidding ensued with Party F offering $36.00 and Party E offering $34.00. However, by late May 2021, all other bidders withdrew citing various internal reasons. MorphoSys then pivoted to an all-cash $34.00 offer on May 30, 2021, facilitated by a financing deal with Royalty Pharma. Constellation's board, advised by Centerview Partners and Wachtell Lipton, negotiated the final terms, including a $60 million termination fee, and approved the deal on June 1, 2021.

## Rationale

The Constellation board determined the $34.00 cash price provided immediate and certain value at a 68% premium to the market price. They considered the risks of remaining standalone, including the capital-intensive nature of Phase 3 trials and commercial launch for pelabresib. MorphoSys sought to acquire Constellation to transform its pipeline with late-stage oncology assets and utilized a novel financing structure with Royalty Pharma to fund the acquisition without a traditional financing condition.
