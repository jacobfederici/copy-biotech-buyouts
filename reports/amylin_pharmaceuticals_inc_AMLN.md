## Summary

Amylin Pharmaceuticals, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Bristol-Myers Squibb (BMS) on June 29, 2012, following a competitive auction process. BMS, through its subsidiary B&R Acquisition Company, launched a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares of Amylin for $31.00 per share in cash. The transaction was valued at approximately $5.3 billion in aggregate consideration, including the settlement of equity awards. Concurrently, BMS entered into an agreement to collaborate with AstraZeneca on the development and commercialization of Amylin's exenatide portfolio following the acquisition. The deal closed on August 8, 2012, via a short-form merger after BMS successfully acquired approximately 85.55% of shares through the tender offer and exercised a top-up option.

## Interaction History

The transaction followed a period of significant corporate transition for Amylin after terminating its partnership with Eli Lilly in late 2011. In February 2012, BMS CEO Lamberto Andreotti approached Amylin CEO Daniel Bradbury with an unsolicited $22.00 per share offer, which the board rejected. Following public leaks of the offer in March, Amylin faced pressure from activist investor Carl Icahn, who sued to open a window for board nominations. Amylin initiated a formal auction process in April 2012, contacting 12 large pharmaceutical companies. BMS initially offered $25.00-$27.00 in May. Competitive tension was maintained with three other parties (A, B, and C). Party B initially bid $32.00 but later withdrew, citing valuation concerns. By late June, BMS was the sole remaining bidder at $31.00. Amylin's board attempted to negotiate the price up to $32.00 on June 28, but BMS CSO Elliott Sigal informed Bradbury that $31.00 was the final price. The board accepted the $31.00 offer on June 29, 2012, noting it was a 101% premium to the 'undisturbed' price on March 27.

## Rationale

The Amylin Board recommended the deal because the $31.00 cash price provided immediate and certain value at a 101% premium to the undisturbed market price. The board considered the risks of remaining independent, including the commercial execution of the BYDUREON launch and the complexities of finding a new ex-U.S. partner. BMS sought the acquisition to expand its diabetes franchise and leverage its existing collaboration with AstraZeneca, which agreed to share in the costs and profits of the Amylin portfolio.

## Evidence

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, and reported bids for Amylin Pharmaceuticals Inc. (AMLN) prior to the official deal announcement on June 29, 2012.

*   **March 28, 2012**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Bloomberg News
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Bristol-Myers Squibb (BMS)
    *   **Rumored Price:** $22.00 per share (approximately $3.5 billion)
    *   **Details:** Reported that Amylin had rejected an unsolicited $3.5 billion takeover bid from Bristol-Myers Squibb in February 2012. The report stated that BMS had sent a letter proposing the acquisition at $22 per share, which the Amylin board turned down. Following this report, Amylin shares rose as much as 51% in a single day.

*   **March 28, 2012**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reuters / Sanford C. Bernstein (Analyst Speculation)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** AstraZeneca
    *   **Rumored Price:** 30% premium to stock price plus $1.5 billion for debt (approx. $4.5 billion total)
    *   **Details:** Following the Bloomberg report of the rejected BMS bid, analysts speculated that AstraZeneca was a more logical buyer. Sanford Bernstein analyst Tim Anderson suggested an acquisition would fit AstraZeneca's portfolio well, valuing the company at roughly $4.5 billion.

*   **May 10, 2012**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Bloomberg News
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Multiple (Bidding War)
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified (implied higher than $22.00)
    *   **Details:** Reported that Amylin had begun a formal auction process and was sharing confidential financial data with several potential pharmaceutical buyers after the initial rejection of Bristol-Myers Squibb.

*   **May 29, 2012**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Bloomberg News / FierceBiotech
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi and Merck & Co.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $25.00 per share (approximately $4 billion)
    *   **Details:** Reported that Sanofi and Merck & Co. had each submitted separate bids of approximately $25 per share. The report also noted that Takeda Pharmaceutical and Bristol-Myers Squibb were preparing new offers to stay in the bidding process.

*   **June 26, 2012**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Bloomberg News
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Bristol-Myers Squibb
    *   **Rumored Price:** $31.00 per share
    *   **Details:** Reported that Bristol-Myers Squibb was in advanced talks to acquire Amylin for about $31 per share. The report indicated that a deal could be announced within the week and that AstraZeneca was expected to partner with BMS to help fund the transaction.
