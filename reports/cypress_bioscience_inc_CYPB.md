## Summary

Cypress Bioscience, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Ramius Value and Opportunity Advisors LLC and Royalty Pharma to be acquired for $6.50 per share in cash. This followed a multi-month hostile pursuit by Ramius, which initially offered $4.00 per share in July 2010. The final agreed price of $6.50 represented a significant premium over the initial unsolicited bids and was reached after Cypress explored strategic alternatives and adopted a stockholder rights plan.

## Timeline

- 2010-05-27: Ramius and affiliates make first investment in Cypress common stock.
- 2010-06-20: Cypress enters license agreement with BioLineRx for CYP-1020.

- 2010-08-05: Cypress Board unanimously rejects the $4.00 per share proposal as grossly undervaluing the company.

- 2010-09-27: Cypress Board recommends stockholders reject the $4.25 offer and adopts a stockholder rights plan.
- 2010-12-14: Cypress enters into a definitive Merger Agreement with Ramius and Royalty Pharma at $6.50 per share.

## Interaction History

The interaction began in May 2010 when Ramius started accumulating shares. Following Cypress's acquisition of CYP-1020 from BioLineRx in June, Ramius issued an unsolicited $4.00 per share bid on July 19, 2010. The Cypress Board, led by CEO Jay Kranzler and lead independent director Daniel Petree, rejected this and a subsequent August proposal, characterizing them as opportunistic attempts to buy the company at a discount to its liquidation value. Cypress implemented a restructuring plan, cutting 86% of its workforce to focus on CNS drug development. On September 15, 2010, Ramius launched a hostile tender offer at $4.25 per share. Cypress responded by adopting a 'poison pill' rights plan on September 27, 2010, and engaging Jefferies & Co. and Perella Weinberg Partners to explore strategic alternatives. Negotiations eventually led to a white-knight-style partnership between Ramius and Royalty Pharma, resulting in a negotiated merger agreement on December 14, 2010, at a significantly higher price of $6.50 per share.

## Rationale

The Cypress Board initially rejected Ramius's hostile bids because they believed the offers grossly undervalued the company's Savella royalty stream and CNS pipeline. However, after a strategic review process, the Board determined that the $6.50 per share price provided by the Ramius/Royalty Pharma partnership represented fair value and was in the best interests of stockholders. Ramius sought to acquire the company to monetize the Savella royalty and potentially restructure the CNS development programs.
