## Summary

LAVA Therapeutics N.V. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by XOMA Royalty Corporation via a tender offer. Initially valued at $1.16 per share plus a CVR, the deal was amended on October 17, 2025, to $1.04 per share plus a CVR due to a reduction in the minimum closing net cash condition. The transaction provides LAVA shareholders with immediate liquidity and the potential for future payments through CVRs tied to LAVA's partnered programs with Pfizer and Johnson & Johnson, as well as its unpartnered LAVA-1266 program.

## Interaction History

Following the failure of LAVA-1207 in late 2024, LAVA's board engaged Leerink Partners to conduct a broad strategic review, reaching out to 99 potential counterparties. While LAVA initially prioritized in-licensing oncology assets from China (e.g., Party K), market conditions and fundraising challenges led the board to pivot toward a sale or merger. XOMA emerged as a preferred bidder due to its cash-and-CVR structure, which aligned with the board's goal of returning capital while retaining upside in partnered programs. Negotiations intensified in June 2025, focusing on the 'Net Cash' definition and CVR terms. A definitive agreement was signed on August 3, 2025, at $1.16/share. However, on October 17, 2025, the parties amended the deal to $1.04/share and reduced the minimum net cash condition from $31.5 million to $24.5 million, reflecting updated cash burn and liability estimates. Shareholders approved the reorganization on November 7, 2025, and the offer successfully closed in November.

## Rationale

The LAVA Board determined the transaction was in the best interest of the company and stakeholders after a failed clinical program and a broad strategic review. The board cited the certainty of cash value, the lack of a financing condition, and the potential upside from CVRs as superior to a standalone liquidation, which would have incurred higher wind-down costs and delayed distributions.
