## Summary

Pacira BioSciences acquired Flexion Therapeutics via a tender offer for $8.50 per share in cash plus a Contingent Value Right (CVR) worth up to $8.00 per share based on commercial and regulatory milestones. The transaction, valued at approximately $734.3 million excluding CVR payments, was completed on November 19, 2021, following a competitive auction process involving multiple strategic parties.

## Timeline

- 2021-05-21: Pacira CEO David Stack contacts Flexion Chairman Patrick Mahaffy to express interest in ZILRETTA.
- 2021-06-01: Flexion and Pacira execute a mutual confidentiality agreement with a standstill provision.
- 2021-06-22: Party A submits an unsolicited proposal to acquire ZILRETTA-only for $450 million.
- 2021-07-06: Lazard begins contacting 21 potential strategic counterparties.
- 2021-07-30: Party A submits a revised proposal for the whole company at $7.75 cash + $3.00 CVR.
- 2021-08-30: First round bids received from Party B, Party C, Party D, and Pacira ($7.50 cash).
- 2021-09-10: Updated bids received; Pacira offers $8.00 cash + $1.50 CVR.
- 2021-09-25: Pacira submits best and final offer of $8.50 cash + $8.00 CVR and demands exclusivity.
- 2021-10-11: Merger Agreement executed and transaction publicly announced.
- 2021-10-22: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2021-11-18: Tender offer expires with 51.7% of shares validly tendered.
- 2021-11-19: Merger completed; Flexion becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Pacira.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in May 2021 when Pacira CEO David Stack approached Flexion Chairman Patrick Mahaffy. Flexion engaged Lazard and Goldman Sachs to run a formal process, contacting 21 parties. Party A initially sought only the ZILRETTA asset for $450M but later bid for the whole company. By September 2021, four parties (A, C, D, and Pacira) remained. Party A's bid of $8.00 + $6.00 CVR was hampered by financing contingencies. Pacira initially bid $7.50 cash, then $8.00, and finally $8.50 cash plus an $8.00 CVR after Lazard pushed for higher upfront value and better CVR terms. The Board chose Pacira due to the higher upfront cash, lack of financing conditions, and Pacira's established commercial infrastructure in pain management. Exclusivity was granted to Pacira on September 25, 2021, after Party A failed to meet a 'best and final' deadline with a firm, non-contingent offer.

## Rationale

The Flexion Board determined the deal was superior to standalone prospects due to execution risks in scaling ZILRETTA sales, the need for dilutive future funding, and the immediate 47% premium offered by the cash component. Pacira's existing sales force targeting the same healthcare providers was seen as a way to maximize ZILRETTA's commercial potential.

## Evidence

- On October 10, 2021, our Board unanimously (a) determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions, including the Offer and the Merger, are fair to, and in the best interest of, Flexion and its stockholders (SC 14D9).
- On November 19, 2021, Pacira announced that the offering period of the Offer had expired... Purchaser subsequently accepted for payment all Shares validly tendered (8-K, 2021-11-19).

## Decision Notes

The deal value of $734.3 million is explicitly stated in the 8-K as the total consideration including debt repayment. CVR milestones are split between ZILRETTA net sales ($250M, $375M, $500M) and FDA approvals for FX-201 and FX-301.
