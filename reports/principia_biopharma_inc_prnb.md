## Summary

Sanofi acquired all outstanding shares of Principia Biopharma for $100.00 per share in cash, following a negotiated process initiated by Sanofi's unsolicited asset-acquisition proposal in May 2020. The acquisition was driven by Sanofi's strategic interest in Principia's PRN2246/SAR442168, a CNS-penetrating BTK inhibitor for multiple sclerosis in Phase 2b development under their existing license agreement. When Principia rejected the asset-only proposal as undervalued and tax-inefficient, Sanofi escalated to a full company acquisition at $76.00 per share, which the Principia board determined substantially undervalued the company given positive Phase 2b data disclosed in February and April 2020. Following a competitive process and fairness-opinion analyses, the board accepted Sanofi's revised offer of $82.50 per share; Sanofi then increased it to $100.00 per share in early August 2020. The Merger Agreement was signed August 16, 2020, the tender offer commenced August 28, 2020, and the merger closed September 28, 2020 via DGCL Section 251(h) short-form merger without stockholder vote.

## Interaction History

**Sanofi's May 2020 Unsolicited Approach and Asset-Only Proposal**

On May 19, 2020, Paul Hudson, Chief Executive Officer of Sanofi, emailed Martin Babler, President and CEO of Principia, on an unsolicited basis, requesting a telephone discussion. On May 29, 2020, Mr. Hudson and other Sanofi management representatives, including Sanofi's Chief Financial Officer and other senior officers, met via videoconference with Mr. Babler and Roy Hardiman, Principia's Chief Business Officer. During this call, Mr. Hudson proposed that Sanofi acquire all of Principia's rights (without field restrictions) to the PRN2246/SAR442168 assets for approximately $1.5 billion. Mr. Babler did not comment on the proposal but agreed to convey and discuss it with the Principia Board.

Later on May 29, 2020, the Principia Board held a meeting with management and representatives of Cooley LLP (Principia's outside legal counsel). Cooley provided an overview of the fiduciary duties attendant to reviewing strategic alternatives, the structure of the proposed asset sale, and related tax consequences. After discussion, the Board concluded that Sanofi's May 29 asset proposal substantially undervalued the PRN2246/SAR442168 assets, was tax inefficient with respect to delivering stockholder value, and that releasing Sanofi from field restrictions under the Sanofi License Agreement provided significant additional value to Sanofi not reflected in the May 29 proposal. The Board further determined that Principia's field limitations were important to its standalone future value. The Board decided that management should relay this message to Sanofi, noting that Principia would be appropriately receptive if any offer that fully valued Principia without attendant tax inefficiency were made.

In connection with ongoing review of strategic alternatives, the Board determined to include BofA Securities, Inc. (BofA Securities) as a financial advisor based on BofA Securities' experience with similar transactions.

**Transition to Company Acquisition and June 2020 Proposals**

On June 4, 2020, the Board held a meeting with management and representatives of Cooley and BofA Securities. Management presented updates on Principia's drug candidate programs, including PRN2246/SAR442168. The Board, together with management and advisors, discussed whether Sanofi would submit a revised proposal for a company acquisition at a higher valuation than the $1.5 billion asset proposal. The Board concluded that if Sanofi believed the PRN2246/SAR442168 assets were worth approximately $1.5 billion, and given Principia's other pipeline assets (discussed in further detail below) and the removal of field restrictions, Sanofi should provide a revised written proposal to acquire Principia for a significantly higher price.

Also on June 4, 2020, Sanofi was informed that (i) the Board had determined that Sanofi's $1.5 billion asset proposal substantially undervalued the PRN2246/SAR442168 assets, (ii) the Board concluded that releasing Sanofi from field restrictions provided Sanofi significant additional value not reflected in the asset proposal, and (iii) if Sanofi were willing to engage in a company acquisition process, the Board would be appropriately receptive.

On June 27, 2020, Sanofi provided written notification to Principia that Sanofi had determined to propose acquiring all of Principia for $76.00 per share in cash. This proposal followed the positive Phase 2b clinical data disclosed by Sanofi in February and April 2020, which demonstrated that PRN2246/SAR442168 met its primary endpoint and significantly reduced disease activity in relapsing forms of MS.

On the same date, the Board held a meeting with management and representatives of Cooley and BofA Securities. The Board reviewed Sanofi's revised proposal and discussed the value of Principia's pipeline, including PRN2246/SAR442168, PRN1008 (BTK inhibitor for pemphigus vulgaris), PRN473 Topical (Janus kinase inhibitor), and other programs. The Board concluded that Sanofi's $76.00 per share proposal substantially undervalued Principia. The Board noted that (i) the value of PRN2246/SAR442168 had been significantly enhanced by Sanofi's positive Phase 2b data and announcement of plans to initiate Phase 3 trials, (ii) Principia's ownership of other pipeline assets added meaningful value, and (iii) the removal of field restrictions under the Sanofi License Agreement constituted significant additional value to Sanofi. After discussion, the Board determined a fair value range of approximately $82.50 to $100.00 per share.

The Board also considered Principia's development obligations and financial position. Under the Sanofi License Agreement, if Principia elected the co-funding option, Principia would fund up to 20% of Phase 3 development costs for PRN2246/SAR442168, subject to annual and aggregate caps of $50 million and $200 million, respectively. The Board noted that Principia's capital position and cash runway would be constrained by such obligations if it remained standalone.

**July 2020 Negotiations and Strategic Process Discussion**

On July 2, 2020, Principia management conveyed to Sanofi that the Board had determined that Sanofi's $76.00 per share proposal substantially undervalued Principia and that the Board's preliminary assessment of fair value was in the range of $82.50 to $100.00 per share.

On July 13, 2020, Sanofi provided a revised written proposal to acquire Principia for $82.50 per share in all-cash consideration. The letter indicated that the revised offer reflected Sanofi's assessment of the value of the pipeline and platform beyond PRN2246/SAR442168, and represented Sanofi's best and final offer. On the same date, Sanofi and Principia entered into a mutual non-disclosure agreement (dated July 14, 2020), which included a standstill provision benefiting Principia that expired December 31, 2021, and permitted Sanofi to privately and confidentially approach Principia's Board Chair or certain senior management during the standstill period.

On July 13, 2020, the Board held a meeting with management and representatives of Cooley and BofA Securities. The Board reviewed Sanofi's $82.50 per share proposal and determined that while the revised offer was materially improved, it still potentially undervalued Principia. The Board noted that Sanofi's revised proposal reflected only partial credit for Principia's pipeline assets beyond PRN2246/SAR442168.

The Board discussed whether to undertake a broader strategic process to solicit interest from other potential acquirers. However, the Board and its advisors noted that the Sanofi License Agreement's field-of-use restrictions on PRN2246/SAR442168 created significant contractual restrictions that would present challenges to any company other than Sanofi seeking to acquire Principia. Those restrictions limited competitive interest and effectively made Sanofi the primary bidder for a transaction involving PRN2246/SAR442168. The Board's legal counsel (Cooley) reviewed the license restrictions and their impact on any competitive process.

On July 27, 2020, the Board held a meeting with management and representatives of Cooley and BofA Securities. After review and discussion, the Board authorized management and Principia's advisors to continue negotiations with Sanofi but did not authorize outreach to other potential bidders. The Board noted that, given the field-of-use restrictions and Sanofi's strategic position, continuing discussions with Sanofi to maximize value was the appropriate course. The Board further determined that if Sanofi did not materially increase its offer from $82.50 per share, the Board would evaluate alternatives, including remaining standalone.

**Early August 2020 Final Negotiations and Merger Agreement**

Between August 3 and August 15, 2020, Principia and Sanofi management teams engaged in extensive business, clinical, financial, and legal due diligence. These discussions involved Principia management and advisors (legal counsel Cooley and BofA Securities) and Sanofi's advisors (Weil, Gotshal & Manges and Cleary Gottlieb Steen & Hamilton).

On August 16, 2020 (date of Merger Agreement), Sanofi announced that it had proposed to acquire Principia for $100.00 per share in all-cash consideration. On the same date, Principia, Sanofi, and Kortex Acquisition Corp. (Sanofi subsidiary) entered into the Merger Agreement.

Following the Merger Agreement execution, Principia's Board issued a statement recommending that stockholders tender their shares into the offer.

**Tender Offer and Merger**

On August 28, 2020, Sanofi, Aventis Inc. (a Sanofi subsidiary serving as parent), and Kortex Acquisition Corp. filed the Tender Offer Statement (Schedule TO) with the SEC. Principia filed this SC 14D9 on the same date, containing the Board's solicitation and recommendation statement and recommending stockholders tender their shares for $100.00 per share in cash.

The tender offer initially expired on September 25, 2020, but was extended. By September 27, 2020, approximately 93.8% of outstanding shares had been tendered into the offer.

On September 28, 2020 (the effective time of the Merger), Kortex Acquisition Corp. merged with and into Principia pursuant to DGCL Section 251(h). Principia survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of Aventis (itself a subsidiary of Sanofi). All tendered shares were converted into the right to receive $100.00 per share in cash. Any shares not tendered (except those held by Sanofi, Aventis, Kortex, other Sanofi subsidiaries, or shareholders who perfected appraisal rights) were automatically converted into the same cash consideration.

**Board Actions and Conflicts of Interest Management**

The Board was aware throughout the process that certain directors and executive officers had interests in the transaction that could differ from those of shareholders, including:

- Accelerated vesting and cash settlement of in-the-money stock options (5,235,205 shares subject to outstanding options as of August 21, 2020);
- Receipt of change-in-control and severance payments upon qualifying terminations (18 months of base salary and bonus for CEO Martin Babler; 12 months for other named executive officers);
- Receipt of 100% of target 2020 performance bonus for all employees, including executives;
- Potential reimbursement of Section 4999 excise taxes on change-in-control payments (maximum $10 million aggregate).

The Board considered these interests in reaching its decision and noted in the SC 14D9 that the compensation committee would approve certain arrangements as employment compensation under Exchange Act Rule 14d-10(d)(2) safe harbor.

**Fairness Opinions**

The Principia Board obtained and reviewed fairness opinions from two financial advisors:
1. Centerview Partners LLC—provided a written fairness opinion as of August 16, 2020, that the $100.00 per share consideration was fair from a financial point of view to holders of common stock (other than Sanofi and its affiliates).
2. BofA Securities, Inc.—provided a written fairness opinion as of August 16, 2020, that the $100.00 per share consideration was fair from a financial point of view to holders of common stock (other than Sanofi and its affiliates).

Both fairness opinions are appended as Annex I and Annex II to the SC 14D9 filed on August 28, 2020.

## Rationale

Per the SC 14D9, the Board's rationale for recommending the transaction centered on:

1. **Strategic Value of PRN2246/SAR442168:** The acquisition provided Sanofi with full ownership of a CNS-penetrating BTK inhibitor program in Phase 2b for multiple sclerosis, following positive efficacy and safety data disclosed in February and April 2020. Sanofi's announcement of plans to initiate Phase 3 trials in relapsing-remitting, primary progressive, and secondary progressive MS forms added substantial clinical validation and de-risked the program. The Board recognized that Sanofi's existing license restricted Principia's field-of-use to areas outside MS, limiting standalone value and making Sanofi the natural acquirer.

2. **Pipeline and Platform Value Beyond PRN2246:** The Board concluded that Principia's pipeline portfolio—including PRN1008 (Phase 2b BTK inhibitor for pemphigus vulgaris with positive Phase 2 data), PRN473 Topical (JAK inhibitor), and other programs—provided meaningful incremental value to any acquirer. The $100.00 per share price was determined to reflect this pipeline valuation.

3. **Undervaluation of Lower Offers:** The Board rejected Sanofi's May 2020 asset-only proposal ($1.5 billion) as substantially undervalued and tax-inefficient. It similarly concluded that Sanofi's June 2020 proposal of $76.00 per share failed to capture the full value of Principia's pipeline and platform assets. The Board's engagement of BofA Securities and Cooley LLP as financial and legal advisors supported these determinations.

4. **Financial Projections and Fairness Opinions:** The Board reviewed financial projections prepared by Principia management and presented to Centerview Partners and BofA Securities. Both Centerview and BofA provided written fairness opinions on August 16, 2020, concluding that the $100.00 per share consideration was fair from a financial point of view.

5. **Standalone Challenges:** The Board considered Principia's capital requirements if it remained independent, particularly the obligation to potentially fund up to 20% of Phase 3 development costs for PRN2246/SAR442168 under the Sanofi License Agreement (subject to annual cap of $50 million and aggregate cap of $200 million). The Board noted that such obligations would constrain Principia's cash runway and financial flexibility.

6. **Market Access and Scale:** Sanofi's global infrastructure, commercial capabilities, and existing relationships in neurology and multiple sclerosis provided a platform for rapid development and commercialization of PRN2246/SAR442168 and other pipeline assets. The Board believed Sanofi was the optimal partner to maximize shareholder value.

## Decision Notes

**Status: Completed.** Sanofi completed its acquisition of Principia Biopharma on September 28, 2020, following successful tender offer and short-form merger under DGCL Section 251(h). 93.8% of shares were tendered into the offer by September 27, 2020. The acquisition closed at the stated per-share consideration of $100.00 in cash. All requisite Merger Agreement conditions were satisfied and the merger became effective as of September 28, 2020, evidenced by SEC 8-K filing on October 2, 2020 (accession 0001193125-20-245313).

**Financial Fairness:** Both Centerview Partners LLC and BofA Securities, Inc., retained by the Principia Board, issued written fairness opinions dated August 16, 2020, concluding that the $100.00 per share consideration was fair from a financial point of view to holders of common stock (other than Sanofi and affiliates).

**Strategic Rationale:** The acquisition was driven by Sanofi's existing license agreement for PRN2246/SAR442168 (a CNS-penetrating BTK inhibitor) and the positive Phase 2b efficacy and safety data disclosed by Sanofi in February and April 2020. Sanofi's announced plans to initiate Phase 3 trials in multiple MS forms validated the program and positioned Sanofi as the primary potential acquirer. Principia's own pipeline assets (PRN1008, PRN473 Topical) and the co-funding option under the license agreement added additional value. The Board determined that field-of-use restrictions in the Sanofi License Agreement made Sanofi the optimal strategic buyer and that the $100.00 per share price fairly reflected the value of Principia's entire pipeline and platform, including PRN2246 and standalone assets.
