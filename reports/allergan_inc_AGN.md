## Summary

Allergan, Inc. was acquired by Actavis plc in a white-knight merger following a prolonged hostile takeover attempt by Valeant Pharmaceuticals and Pershing Square Capital Management. Under the terms of the definitive merger agreement, Allergan stockholders received $129.22 in cash and 0.3683 Actavis ordinary shares per share. The transaction created a top-tier global pharmaceutical company and successfully fended off Valeant's unsolicited exchange offer, which the Allergan board had repeatedly rejected as grossly inadequate.

- 2015-03-17: Merger completed; Allergan becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Actavis.

## Interaction History

The interaction began with an unsolicited approach by Valeant Pharmaceuticals and Pershing Square Capital Management in April 2014. Allergan's CEO David Pyott and the Board, advised by Goldman Sachs and BofA Merrill Lynch, implemented a Rights Agreement (Poison Pill) to gain time. Over several months, Valeant increased its offer from an initial ~$153 value to a final unsolicited exchange offer of $72.00 cash and 0.83 Valeant shares. Allergan's board consistently rejected these bids, citing concerns over Valeant's 'slash-and-burn' R&D model and unsustainable organic growth. Pershing Square, led by Bill Ackman, initiated litigation and a proxy solicitation to call a special meeting to replace the board. In November 2014, Allergan announced a superior 'white knight' merger with Actavis plc for approximately $219 per share in cash and stock. Regulatory clearances were obtained from the FTC in January 2015, and the deal closed in March 2015 after receiving overwhelming stockholder support.

## Rationale

The Allergan Board recommended the Actavis merger as a superior alternative to the hostile Valeant bid. The Board believed the Actavis deal provided significantly higher value, a more sustainable business model focused on R&D and organic growth, and avoided the risks associated with Valeant's 'opaque' financial reporting and aggressive cost-cutting strategy.

## Evidence

- On March 17, 2015... Merger Sub merged with and into the Company, with the Company being the surviving entity... As a result of the Merger, the Company became a wholly owned indirect subsidiary of Actavis. (0001193125-15-096184)

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, and takeover reports for Allergan Inc. (AGN) that appeared before confirmed deal announcements.

*   **October 2012**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *The Wall Street Journal* (reported retrospectively in April 2014)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Valeant Pharmaceuticals International Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specified for this initial contact.
    *   **Details:** Valeant CEO J. Michael Pearson reportedly first approached Allergan CEO David Pyott regarding a potential merger. The proposition was rejected by Allergan a few weeks later.

*   **January 2014**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *FierceBiotech*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** N/A (Speculation on Allergan as an acquirer/target)
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** N/A
    *   **Details:** Allergan CEO David Pyott stated the company had a $10 billion "cache of cash" for its own acquisitions, leading to market speculation that Allergan was positioning itself to remain independent or grow through its own takeovers to ward off potential suitors.

*   **April 21, 2014**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *The Wall Street Journal* / *Reuters*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Valeant Pharmaceuticals & Pershing Square Capital Management (Bill Ackman)
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** ~$45 billion to $47 billion
    *   **Details:** Reports surfaced that activist investor Bill Ackman had amassed a nearly 10% stake in Allergan and was teaming up with Valeant Pharmaceuticals to launch a hostile takeover bid. This was reported just hours before the formal unsolicited offer was made public on April 22.

*   **May 12, 2014**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Reuters*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Shire Plc
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specified
    *   **Details:** Sources familiar with the matter reported that Allergan had been seeking "white knight" buyers to counter Valeant’s hostile bid, specifically reaching out to Shire Plc to discuss a potential alternative transaction.

*   **September 2014**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Bloomberg* / *The Wall Street Journal*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Actavis Plc
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** ~$60 billion
    *   **Details:** Reports emerged that Actavis had approached Allergan about a friendly takeover. Speculation intensified that Allergan preferred Actavis as a "white knight" to avoid the cost-cutting model proposed by Valeant.

*   **November 7, 2014**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Insider Monkey* / Market Rumors
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Actavis Plc
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** $200+ per share
    *   **Details:** Speculation grew that Actavis was preparing a formal bid to top Valeant’s latest offer, with reports suggesting Allergan was "trying to shun" the Valeant deal in favor of a higher Actavis bid.

*   **November 12–14, 2014**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Bloomberg*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Actavis Plc
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** At least $60 billion; rumored $210+ per share
    *   **Details:** Bloomberg reported that Actavis was in advanced talks to acquire Allergan. Reports indicated Allergan was demanding more than $210 per share to agree to a deal, which would place the valuation significantly above Valeant's $200 per share offer.

*   **November 17, 2014 (Pre-Market)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Zacks* / Market Rumors
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Actavis Plc
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** $215 to $220 per share (Total deal value ~$65.5 billion)
    *   **Details:** Rumors circulated in the early morning that Actavis was "closing in" on a deal and had agreed to a price between $215 and $220 per share, effectively ending the hostile pursuit by Valeant. (The deal was officially confirmed later that day at $219 per share).

*   **October 2015**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *The Wall Street Journal*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Pfizer Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** ~$150 billion
    *   **Details:** Reports surfaced that Pfizer was in preliminary talks to acquire Allergan in a massive "tax inversion" deal. Speculation suggested the deal would be the largest in pharmaceutical history. (A deal was announced in November 2015 but later terminated in April 2016).

*   **June 2019**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *The Wall Street Journal* / *Bloomberg*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** AbbVie Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** ~45% premium over current trading price
    *   **Details:** Just prior to the official announcement on June 25, reports emerged that AbbVie was in advanced negotiations to buy Allergan to diversify its portfolio away from its top-selling drug, Humira. Analysts had also floated rumors of a potential company split-up in the weeks leading up to this.
