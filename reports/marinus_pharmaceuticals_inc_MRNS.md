## Summary

Marinus Pharmaceuticals entered into a definitive merger agreement with Immedica Pharma AB for $0.55 per share in cash via a tender offer. The transaction followed a strategic review triggered by the failure of the Phase 3 TrustTSC trial, which left the company with a severely limited cash runway and significant debt obligations.

## Timeline

- 2024-04-12: DMC informs Marinus that the Phase 3 RAISE trial for RSE may continue but did not meet early stopping criteria.
- 2024-06-17: Company announces topline results from Phase 3 RAISE trial; one co-primary endpoint was not met.
- 2024-10-03: Marinus engages Barclays Capital Inc. as financial advisor.

## Interaction History

Following the failure of the TrustTSC trial in October 2024, Barclays contacted 77 potential counterparties. 44 expressed interest and 30 executed NDAs. Initial bids ranged from asset-only offers (Immedica initially bid $140M for assets) to low-value whole-company bids. The Board expressed a strong preference for a whole-company sale to address debt and liquidity. By December 23, six final bids were received. Immedica proposed $0.50 per share for the company. Marinus management, led by CEO Scott Braunstein and CFO Steven Pfanstiel, negotiated this up to $0.55 per share and secured a $1.275M employee bonus pool. Legal counsel Hogan Lovells (for Marinus) and Gibson Dunn (for Immedica) negotiated closing certainty terms, including the 'bring-down' standard for representations and the right to recover lost stockholder premium in certain damages claims. The deal was signed on December 29, 2024.

## Rationale

The Marinus Board determined the company could not continue as a going concern due to the failure of the TrustTSC trial and a cash runway expected to end by June 30, 2025. The transaction provided immediate liquidity and a significant premium over the depressed trading price, avoiding a likely bankruptcy filing. Immedica sought to acquire the commercial Ztalmy business and remaining ganaxolone assets.
