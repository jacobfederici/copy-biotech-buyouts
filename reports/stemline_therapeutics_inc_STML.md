## Summary

Stemline Therapeutics was acquired by Berlin-Chemie AG, a subsidiary of the Menarini Group, via a tender offer for $11.50 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $1.00. The transaction aimed to leverage Menarini's global infrastructure to commercialize Stemline's lead oncology product, ELZONRIS, particularly in European markets.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest in early 2020 following a meeting at the J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference between Stemline CEO Dr. Ivan Bergstein and Menarini CEO Elcin Barker Ergun. Menarini initially offered $10.00 per share on March 30, 2020. Stemline's board, advised by PJT Partners and BofA Securities, pushed for higher value, citing the impact of COVID-19 on the current stock price. A second offer of $11.00 plus a $0.50 CVR was made on April 7. Stemline countered with $12.50 plus a $1.00 CVR. On April 10, Menarini provided its final offer of $11.50 cash plus a $1.00 CVR tied to European approval and first sale of ELZONRIS. During this period, Stemline also held brief discussions with 'Party A' regarding a stock-for-stock merger, but the board determined the Menarini cash offer provided superior value and certainty. Exclusivity was granted on April 13, 2020, to allow for final due diligence and merger agreement drafting. The deal was finalized on May 3, 2020, with a termination fee set at $25.4 million.

## Rationale

The Stemline Board determined the offer was fair due to the significant premium (142% over the May 1, 2020 closing price), the immediate liquidity of cash, and the risks associated with remaining a standalone company, including the need for substantial capital to commercialize ELZONRIS in Europe and the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on market conditions. Menarini sought the acquisition to expand its oncology portfolio and utilize its established European infrastructure to launch ELZONRIS globally.
