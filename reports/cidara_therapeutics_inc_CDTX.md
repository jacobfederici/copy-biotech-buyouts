## Summary

Cidara Therapeutics, Inc. was acquired by Merck Sharp & Dohme LLC (a subsidiary of Merck & Co., Inc.) for $221.50 per common share in an all-cash tender offer followed by a merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The transaction focused on Cidara's lead drug-Fc conjugate candidate, CD388, a long-acting antiviral for the prevention of influenza.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2024 as Cidara sought partners for CD388. Following positive Phase 2b data in June 2025, Cidara engaged with multiple strategic parties (Companies A-F and Merck). A formal bidding process was initiated in October 2025 after Company D submitted an unsolicited $118/share bid. Merck initially offered $120/share on Oct 29. The process intensified in early November with multiple rounds of bidding. Company D withdrew after its $150.50/share bid was surpassed. In the final 'best and final' round on Nov 13, Merck significantly increased its offer from $156 to $221.50 per share, outbidding Company F ($185) and Company E ($173.50). The Board, advised by Evercore and Goldman Sachs, unanimously approved the Merck transaction due to the substantial premium and lack of financing conditions.
