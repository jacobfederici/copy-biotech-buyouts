## Summary

Adolor Corporation was acquired by Cubist Pharmaceuticals via a tender offer for $4.25 per share in cash plus a Contingent Payment Right (CPR) worth up to $4.50 based on regulatory and commercial milestones for ADL5945. The transaction was structured as a two-step merger, with Cubist acquiring approximately 83% of shares through the initial and subsequent tender periods and reaching the 90% threshold via a top-up option to effect a short-form merger.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began with informal meetings in early 2011 regarding specific assets like beloxepin. Following positive Phase 2 data for ADL5945 in August 2011, Cubist intensified its interest. Michael Bonney (Cubist CEO) and Michael Dougherty (Adolor CEO) led the discussions. Adolor's board formed a committee (Madden, Anido, Hager) to advise on negotiations. Initial bids from Cubist started at $4.00 per share. Adolor pushed for $5.50 upfront, but Cubist remained firm on $4.25, eventually improving the CPR terms to include EMA approval and sales milestones. Adolor conducted outreach to 10 other potential buyers but received no superior proposals. Legal advisors Dechert LLP (Adolor) and Ropes & Gray LLP (Cubist) finalized the merger agreement and CPR structure in October 2011.

## Rationale

The Adolor Board recommended the deal because the $4.25 upfront price represented a 121.4% premium over the previous closing price, and the CPRs allowed stockholders to participate in the potential success of ADL5945. The board also noted the company's need for significant financing to develop ADL5945 independently and the lack of other viable strategic partners after an extensive outreach process.
