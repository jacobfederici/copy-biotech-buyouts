## Summary

VectivBio Holding AG entered into a transaction agreement with Ironwood Pharmaceuticals, Inc. under which Ironwood commenced a tender offer to acquire all outstanding ordinary shares of VectivBio for $17.00 per share in cash. The transaction is structured as a tender offer followed by a merger with Ironwood Pharmaceuticals GmbH, a Swiss subsidiary of Ironwood. The deal aims to integrate VectivBio's lead asset, apraglutide, into Ironwood's gastrointestinal portfolio.

## Timeline

- 2023-03-21: Ironwood submits a non-binding proposal to acquire VectivBio for $14.00 per share in cash.
- 2023-03-23: VectivBio Board determines the $14.00 offer is inadequate and authorizes outreach to six other potential parties (Parties A-F).
- 2023-03-29: VectivBio and Ironwood enter into a confidentiality agreement with a standstill provision.
- 2023-04-25: Ironwood submits a revised proposal of $15.00 per share in cash plus a $2.00 CVR contingent on FDA approval of apraglutide.
- 2023-04-26: VectivBio counters with $16.00 per share plus a $3.00 CVR.
- 2023-04-28: Ironwood submits a 'best and final' all-cash proposal of $17.00 per share and requests exclusivity.
- 2023-04-29: VectivBio and Ironwood enter into an exclusivity agreement through May 21, 2023.
- 2023-05-21: VectivBio Board unanimously approves the Transaction Agreement and recommends the offer to shareholders.
- 2023-05-22: Joint press release announcing the execution of the Transaction Agreement.
- 2023-05-31: Ironwood formally commences the tender offer.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began on March 21, 2023, when Ironwood's management contacted VectivBio with an initial $14.00 per share cash offer. VectivBio, advised by Centerview Partners, Cooley (UK) LLP, and Homburger AG, rejected this as undervalued. Centerview contacted six other global pharmaceutical companies; only Party A showed interest but declined after a management presentation. Ironwood increased its offer on April 25 to $15.00 plus a $2.00 CVR. VectivBio countered on April 26 with $16.00 plus a $3.00 CVR. On April 28, Ironwood raised its bid to a 'best and final' $17.00 per share in cash, dropping the CVR structure in favor of a higher upfront price. Following confirmatory diligence and negotiations over the Minimum Condition (settled at 80%) and termination fees, the parties signed the agreement on May 21, 2023.

## Rationale

The VectivBio Board recommended the deal because the $17.00 cash price provided immediate and certain value at a significant premium (43% over the last closing price and 80% over the 90-day VWAP). The Board determined that the risks of remaining standalone—including the need for substantial additional capital, clinical trial execution risks for apraglutide, and regulatory uncertainties—outweighed the potential long-term value of remaining independent. Ironwood sought the acquisition to strengthen its gastrointestinal franchise with a late-stage orphan disease asset.

## Evidence

- On May 21, 2023, the Board unanimously (i) determined that the Transaction Agreement and the transactions contemplated therein are advisable and fair to, and to the benefit and in the best interest of, VectivBio and its shareholders... and (iii) resolved to recommend that holders of Shares accept the Offer and tender their Shares to Ironwood (SC 14D9).

## Decision Notes

The deal value is computed based on 62,806,038 shares outstanding as of May 17, 2023, multiplied by the $17.00 offer price. The Board's decision was supported by a fairness opinion from Centerview Partners. The transaction includes an 80% minimum tender condition and requires a shareholder vote at an EGM to amend articles of association and elect new board members.
