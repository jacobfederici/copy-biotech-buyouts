## Summary

NGM Biopharmaceuticals, Inc., a South San Francisco-based clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company with programs in liver and metabolic diseases, oncology, and rare diseases, was acquired by Atlas Neon Parent, Inc., an entity affiliated with The Column Group, LP (TCG), the company's largest stockholder holding approximately 26% of outstanding shares. TCG submitted an unsolicited expression of interest on December 28, 2023, the Special Committee of independent directors rejected an initial proposal of $1.45 per share, and negotiations led to a final price of $1.55 per share in cash. Four external strategic parties were contacted; all declined. The tender offer expired on April 4, 2024, with approximately 27% of outstanding shares tendered by unaffiliated stockholders, satisfying the minimum tender condition (majority of unaffiliated shares). The merger was effected on April 5, 2024 under DGCL Section 251(h) without a shareholder vote. TCG and other rollover stockholders (together approximately 48% of shares) contributed their shares to Parent in exchange for equity in Parent rather than cash.

## Interaction History

### Background and Initial TCG Overture (June – December 2023)

The SC 14D9 states that from June 2023, Peter Svennilson, a Managing Partner of TCG and a former member of the Company Board, began suggesting to CEO David Woodhouse that NGM would be better positioned as a private company. Svennilson sent an email on June 27, 2023, not proposing specific terms, but Woodhouse and the then-President and CFO began preparing materials for a potential August Board discussion. The General Counsel engaged Cooley LLP beginning in July 2023 for guidance on going-private transactions and special committee procedures.

At the August 9, 2023 Board meeting, directors discussed whether to explore strategic alternatives and whether to retain a financial advisor. On October 5, 2023, Woodhouse met with TCG representatives to discuss strategic direction, possible in-licensing opportunities within TCG's portfolio, and NGM's short- and long-term financing needs. On October 31, 2023, the Company entered into a non-disclosure agreement with The Column Group LLC, an affiliate of TCG.

At the November 8, 2023 Board meeting, Guggenheim Securities and Svennilson attended to review strategic alternatives, including status quo, rare-disease transformation (focusing on PSC), sale, merger, and partnering. After advisors departed, the Board discussed strategic alternatives in executive session and adopted the 2024 budget.

On December 6, 2023, Goeddel (a Managing Partner of TCG and Board member) called Woodhouse and Rieflin (Chairman) asking whether private company status would be beneficial. Both responded that merits would depend on circumstances. On December 22, 2023, Rieflin informed Woodhouse that Goeddel had indicated TCG was considering a going-private proposal and had asked whether Rieflin would be willing to roll his equity; Rieflin said he would. Rieflin also disclosed that TCG had retained Paul, Weiss as outside counsel.

### TCG's December 28, 2023 Expression of Interest and Special Committee Formation

On December 28, 2023, TCG submitted a written, unsolicited, non-binding expression of interest in a going-private transaction. TCG stated it would not move forward without a special committee recommendation and a majority approval of non-TCG stockholders. TCG stated it was not interested in selling its stake or supporting an alternative change-of-control transaction. On December 29, 2023, TCG filed an amendment to its Schedule 13D disclosing the expression.

Over December 28–30, 2023, Woodhouse called each director about the expression of interest and upcoming steps. On December 30, 2023, the Company Board met with Cooley and formed the Special Committee (Guyer, Ho, Hooper), excluding Goeddel (TCG Managing Partner), Perlmutter (Science Partner at TCG), Rieflin (committed to rollover and unable to serve on the Company side), and Woodhouse (management). The Special Committee was given full authority to evaluate, negotiate, and recommend or reject any strategic transaction. The Board resolved not to recommend any transaction without a prior favorable Special Committee recommendation. Hooper was designated Chairperson. Hogan Lovells was engaged as independent Special Committee counsel.

### Special Committee's Evaluation and Negotiations (January 2024)

On January 2, 2024, the Company filed an 8-K disclosing the expression of interest and Special Committee formation. On January 3, 2024, Woodhouse briefed Svennilson and informed him that any proposals should go through the financial advisor. Svennilson reported that TCG and long-standing stockholder relationships together represented approximately 50% of outstanding shares.

On January 4, 2024, TCG submitted a formal non-binding proposal at $1.45 per share in cash, claiming a 69% premium to the unaffected price. The proposal assumed a cash balance of at least $135 million and demanded a response by January 10, 2024. The Special Committee's January 12, 2024 meeting formally approved Guggenheim Securities as financial advisor (engagement letter dated January 12, 2024).

On January 19, 2024, the Special Committee reviewed Guggenheim Securities' DCF analysis, including probability-adjusted and financing-adjusted scenarios for NGM's programs. On January 23, 2024, Guggenheim Securities presented why the Company was not directly comparable to other biotechs, discussed sum-of-parts analyses, and reviewed the $1.45 proposal. The Special Committee directed Guggenheim Securities to inform TCG that $1.45 was inadequate and that the Special Committee remained open to discussions at a higher valuation.

On January 24, 2024, Guggenheim Securities conveyed the rejection to Svennilson by videoconference. Svennilson stated TCG was unwilling to pay beyond the substantial premium already implied, characterized the Company as needing near-term financing, and said TCG preferred to fund it privately. He declined a query on whether TCG might participate in a financing if the Company were restructured to reduce programs. Subsequent discussions on January 25, 2024 between Hogan Lovells and Paul, Weiss clarified TCG's contemplated rollover structure: certain TCG entities and other long-standing stockholders would contribute shares to a new buyer entity in exchange for buyer equity, while all-cash consideration would be paid to remaining public stockholders.

On January 26, 2024, the Special Committee met and discussed valuation scenarios and financing risks. The Special Committee authorized Guggenheim Securities to advise TCG that the Special Committee would be willing to recommend an offer exceeding $2.00 per share. Woodhouse, Rieflin, and Goeddel held a call at Goeddel's request; Goeddel confirmed TCG's offer remained at $1.45 and expressed concern about the timeline.

On January 28, 2024, Guggenheim Securities conveyed the >$2.00 position to Svennilson. Svennilson responded that TCG would not raise its offer. Goeddel sent an email to Woodhouse and Rieflin calling the Special Committee's counter "completely unreasonable" and asserting TCG's offer already represented approximately 90% premium and was "very fair."

On January 29, 2024, Svennilson first said $1.50 was TCG's "absolute last and final offer," but after Guggenheim Securities pressed for a further increase, delivered a revised written proposal at $1.55 per share, representing an 80% premium to the December 29, 2023 unaffected closing price. The new proposal had a January 31, 2024 response deadline. On the evening of January 29, the Special Committee met, reviewed the $1.55 proposal, and directed Hogan Lovells to clarify the cash-condition terms and to begin drafting definitive documents, while also authorizing Guggenheim Securities to commence outreach to third-party acquirors.

### Third-Party Outreach (January 30 – February 10, 2024)

Following Paul, Weiss's confirmation on January 30, 2024 that there would be no cash-at-close condition and that the Company's cash was expected to be approximately $110 million at March-end, Guggenheim Securities contacted three large pharmaceutical companies (Strategic Party A, B, C) and one mid-size pharmaceutical company (Strategic Party D). By February 7, 2024, Strategic Parties A, B, and C had each declined to pursue an acquisition. Strategic Party D entered a confidentiality agreement and held a diligence call with NGM management on February 9, 2024, but notified Guggenheim Securities on February 10, 2024 that it was not interested, citing uncertainty in the Company's programs.

### Merger Agreement Negotiation (February 3 – February 25, 2024)

Paul, Weiss delivered an initial Merger Agreement draft on February 3, 2024, providing for the two-step structure, rollover arrangement, no-shop with fiduciary exception, and a 3.5% termination fee. Hogan Lovells's February 9 revised draft reduced the termination fee to 1% of equity value and added restrictions on TCG transferring shares prior to close. Paul, Weiss revised again on February 16 to a 1.5% termination fee, which Hogan Lovells accepted on February 19. Between February 20 and February 24, parties finalized remaining open terms in the Merger Agreement and Company Disclosure Letter.

On February 22, 2024, the Special Committee met and noted that the Company's stock price had risen above $1.55 (closing at $1.91 on February 23). Guggenheim Securities noted that the current stock price may reflect investor interest in the transaction announcement and that current investors may not have a full appreciation for the Company's cash position decline since September 30, 2023. The Special Committee asked Guggenheim Securities to monitor the stock price and report back.

On February 24, 2024, Guggenheim Securities contacted Svennilson about whether TCG would increase its price given the elevated stock price. Svennilson declined, stating nothing had changed regarding the Company's fundamental value.

### Board Recommendation and Execution (February 25, 2024)

On February 25, 2024, the Special Committee held its final meeting. Guggenheim Securities reviewed its financial analysis and rendered an oral fairness opinion (confirmed in writing dated February 25, 2024) that the $1.55 per share consideration was fair, from a financial point of view, to the unaffiliated stockholders. The Special Committee unanimously recommended approval. The Company Board then met; Goeddel, Perlmutter, and Rieflin recused themselves. The remaining members unanimously authorized and approved the Merger Agreement. The Merger Agreement was executed on the evening of February 25, 2024.

### Closing (March – April 2024)

On March 8, 2024, the Purchaser commenced the tender offer. Rieflin had entered the Stockholder Rollover Agreement on the date of the Merger Agreement; Woodhouse signed a joinder on March 6, 2024. The rollover stockholders (TCG entities at approximately 26% and other rollover stockholders at approximately 22%) agreed not to tender and instead to contribute their shares to Parent.

The offer expired at one minute after 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on April 4, 2024. According to Equiniti Trust Company LLC (depositary), 22,323,295 shares were validly tendered and not withdrawn, representing approximately 27% of outstanding shares and a majority of the unaffiliated stockholder shares. All offer conditions were satisfied. An additional 39,516,567 shares were contributed by the rollover stockholders. On April 5, 2024, the Merger was effected under DGCL Section 251(h). Directors Guyer, Ho, and Hooper resigned at the Effective Time. NGM's common stock was delisted from Nasdaq.

## Rationale

The SC 14D9 states that the Special Committee and non-recused Company Board members considered the following factors in recommending and approving the transaction:

The Company's financial condition was a central concern. The filing describes the Company's deteriorating cash position, ongoing financing needs, and the significant time required to generate meaningful clinical data in the PSC and hyperemesis gravidarum programs. The Special Committee considered risks associated with continued operation as a standalone public company, including financing-related risks, execution risks, the risk that the market may not fully reflect the value of the Company's programs, and the Company's relationship with manufacturers and vendors.

The consideration of $1.55 per share represented, according to the filing, approximately 80% over the Company's closing stock price of $0.86 on December 29, 2023, approximately 90% over the 30-day volume-weighted average price of $0.82, and approximately 23.5% over the closing price of $1.255 on January 9, 2024 (the date of the Company's strategy press release).

The Special Committee noted the cash consideration provided certainty and immediate liquidity, eliminating long-term business and execution risk. The Special Committee considered the results of its third-party outreach (four companies contacted, all declined) and concluded no alternative acquiror was likely available on comparable or superior terms given TCG's refusal to vote for any alternative transaction. The Special Committee also considered the ability under the Merger Agreement to respond to superior proposals before closing.

The Special Committee relied on Guggenheim Securities' fairness opinion (delivered February 25, 2024) that the $1.55 per share consideration was fair, from a financial point of view, to the unaffiliated stockholders. Guggenheim Securities had conducted DCF analyses, probability-adjusted and financing-adjusted scenarios, and referenced premia in recent biotech minority squeeze-out transactions and recent biotech M&A transactions generally.

The SC 14D9 also notes that the Special Committee considered that the Company's stock price had risen above the offer price of $1.55 (reaching $1.91 on February 23, 2024) during the period between agreement in principle and execution. The Special Committee considered whether this should affect the process and concluded, after Guggenheim Securities' analysis and after TCG's refusal to raise its offer on February 24, 2024, to proceed at $1.55.
