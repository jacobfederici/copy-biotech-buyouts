## Summary

Longboard Pharmaceuticals, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by H. Lundbeck A/S for $60.00 per share in cash, representing a total equity value of approximately $2.6 billion. The transaction was executed via a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, aimed at strengthening Lundbeck's neuro-rare disease pipeline with the addition of bexicaserin.

## Timeline

- 2024-12-02: Merger completed; Longboard becomes an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Lundbeck.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2024 following positive Phase 1b/2a data for bexicaserin. Lundbeck initially offered $29.00 in April 2024, which the Board rejected as inadequate. Over six months, Lundbeck raised its bid through multiple rounds ($34.50, $54.00, and a $57+$2 CVR structure). Longboard's Board, advised by Evercore and Centerview, also engaged with Party A, which reached a $60.00 bid before withdrawing on October 2, 2024, due to risk concerns. Following Party A's exit, Lundbeck moved to an all-cash $59.00 offer. CEO Kevin Lind countered at $62.00, leading to Lundbeck's final $60.00 per share offer on October 11, 2024. The Board approved the deal on October 13, 2024, noting the 54% premium over the last unaffected closing price.

## Rationale

The Longboard Board determined the cash offer provided immediate and certain value at a significant premium (80% over the unaffected price). They considered the risks of remaining independent, including the need for substantial future capital raises (estimated at $400M) and the challenges of commercializing bexicaserin alone. Lundbeck sought the acquisition to enhance its neuro-rare disease portfolio and leverage its global commercial infrastructure.

## Evidence

- On October 13, 2024, the Board unanimously: (i) determined that the entry into the Merger Agreement and the consummation of the Transactions are fair and advisable, and in the best interest of, Longboard and its stockholders (0001193125-24-246339).
- On December 2, 2024, H. Lundbeck A/S... completed the previously announced acquisition of Longboard Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (0001140361-24-048133).

## Decision Notes

The deal value is frequently cited in media as ~$2.6B on a fully diluted basis, though the basic equity value based on 39M shares is ~$2.34B. Shares outstanding include 34,436,203 voting and 4,609,500 non-voting common stock as of Oct 25, 2024. A CVR was discussed during negotiations but replaced by a higher upfront cash payment in the final agreement.
