## Summary

Spark Therapeutics, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Roche Holdings, Inc. on February 22, 2019, under which Roche launched a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares of Spark for $114.50 per share in cash. The transaction, valued at approximately $4.8 billion on a fully diluted basis, was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. After an extended regulatory review period by the FTC and UK CMA, the deal closed on December 17, 2019.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in the context of a potential Hemophilia A collaboration. In December 2018, Roche CEO Severin Schwan pivoted to an acquisition interest, initially offering $70.00 per share. Spark's Board, advised by Centerview Partners and Goodwin Procter, rejected this and subsequent offers ($73.00) as inadequate. Spark conducted a targeted 'market check' by contacting Party A and Party C. Party C emerged as a competitive bidder, offering $75.00 and eventually $105.00. Roche, seeking to preempt the process, increased its bid to $91.00 and finally to $114.50 on February 22, 2019, after Spark's financial advisor indicated Party C had 'additional value' in the $90s. The Board unanimously approved the $114.50 offer, representing a 122% premium to the unaffected closing price. Regulatory review by the FTC and the UK Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) resulted in several extensions of the tender offer throughout 2019, but no divestitures were required.

## Rationale

The Spark Board determined that the all-cash offer provided immediate and certain value at a significant premium (122%), avoiding the inherent risks of standalone development, including clinical trial uncertainty, regulatory hurdles for gene therapies, and the lack of global commercial infrastructure. Roche sought to acquire a leading gene therapy platform to complement its existing hemophilia franchise (Hemlibra) and establish a center of excellence in Philadelphia.

## Evidence

- On February 22, 2019, the Board unanimously (1) approved, adopted and declared advisable the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereby, including the Offer and the Merger (0001193125-19-067381).
- On December 17, 2019, following consummation of the Offer, Merger Sub merged with and into the Company (0001193125-19-315712).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed following a lengthy regulatory review. Shares outstanding (38,053,350) and fully diluted value (~$4.8B) are sourced from the Item 1 and Item 2.01 of the 14D9 and 8-K respectively. No CVRs were included in this transaction.
