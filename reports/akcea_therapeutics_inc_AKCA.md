## Summary

Ionis Pharmaceuticals, Inc., which already owned approximately 76% of Akcea Therapeutics, Inc., acquired the remaining outstanding shares of Akcea for $18.15 per share in an all-cash tender offer followed by a second-step merger. The transaction aimed to simplify the corporate structure and integrate Akcea's commercial and development capabilities back into Ionis to maximize the value of their shared antisense technology pipeline.

## Timeline

- 2020-07-10: Ionis sends an initial letter to the Akcea Board expressing interest in acquiring the remaining shares of Akcea.
- 2020-07-11: Akcea Board refers the potential transaction to the Affiliate Transactions Committee (Special Committee).
- 2020-07-17: Akcea and Ionis enter into a Non-Disclosure Agreement.
- 2020-08-19: Ionis submits an initial non-binding proposal of $16.00 per share in cash.

- 2020-08-27: Ionis submits a second revised proposal of $17.75 per share.
- 2020-08-28: Special Committee counters at $18.25 per share; parties agree to proceed at $18.15 per share.
- 2020-08-30: Special Committee and Board approve the Merger Agreement.

- 2020-09-14: Commencement of the tender offer.
- 2020-10-09: Tender offer expires at 11:59 p.m. ET.
- 2020-10-12: Merger completed; Akcea becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Ionis.

## Interaction History

Negotiations were conducted by an Affiliate Transactions Committee (Special Committee) of independent directors (Barbara Yanni, Amber Salzman, and initially Sandford Smith). Ionis, represented by CEO Brett Monia and advisors Goldman Sachs and Stifel, initially proposed $16.00 per share on August 19, 2020. The Special Committee, advised by Cowen and Ropes & Gray, pushed for higher valuation, citing a prior 2019 proposal from 'Party A' at $23.50. Sandford Smith recused himself on August 21 due to potential conflicts regarding derivative litigation (the 'New Delaware Litigation') that would be extinguished by an all-cash merger. A sub-committee (Yanni and Salzman) evaluated the litigation value with Young Conaway and Analysis Group, concluding it was not material to the offer price. Ionis raised its bid to $17.00, then $17.75. The Special Committee countered at $18.25, and the parties ultimately compromised at $18.15 per share on August 28, 2020. The Board (excluding Ionis-affiliated directors Parshall, Klein, and McDevitt) approved the deal on August 30, 2020.

## Rationale

The Special Committee and Board considered the immediate liquidity and significant premium (59.5% over the prior close) provided to minority stockholders. They noted the risks of remaining a standalone company, including substantial future financing needs that could be highly dilutive. Ionis sought to consolidate Akcea to streamline operations and capture the full value of the antisense pipeline, particularly as Ionis already controlled 76% of the company and had stated it was not a seller of its stake.

## Evidence

- The Board (other than Ms. Parshall, Joseph Klein, III and Dr. McDevitt, all of whom recused themselves... and Sandford D. Smith and Elaine Hochberg, both of whom abstained from voting)... recommended that stockholders accept the Offer and tender their Shares (0001193125-20-244919).
- On October 12, 2020, following consummation of the Offer, Purchaser merged with and into the Company (the 'Merger'), with the Company as the surviving corporation (0001193125-20-268019).

## Decision Notes

The deal was a 'going private' transaction under Rule 13e-3 because Ionis was a controlling affiliate. Shares outstanding (101,759,772) were sourced from the SC 14D9 Item 1. The $1.9B deal value reflects the implied equity value including the cash-out of options and RSUs.
