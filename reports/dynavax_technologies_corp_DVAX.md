## Summary

Dynavax Technologies Corporation entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Sanofi for $15.50 per share in cash via a tender offer and subsequent merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The transaction valued Dynavax at approximately $1.78 billion based on outstanding shares, representing a 39% premium to its undisturbed closing price. The deal was completed on February 10, 2026, following the successful tender of approximately 73.92% of outstanding shares.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest in December 2025 after nearly two years of periodic contact. Sanofi's initial bid of $14.40 (Dec 2) was rejected. A subsequent bid of $15.00 (Dec 9) was also deemed insufficient. Dynavax counter-proposed $16.00 with exclusivity. Sanofi responded with a 'final' offer of $15.50 on Dec 10. During the exclusivity period, 'Party A' (a biotech company previously in merger talks with Dynavax) submitted an unsolicited $15.00 bid on Dec 23. The Dynavax Board, advised by Centerview and Goldman Sachs, concluded that Sanofi's $15.50 offer was superior in value and certainty, particularly as Party A's bid required debt financing and further diligence. Regulatory clearances were obtained under the HSR Act and German FCO/BMWK authorities.
