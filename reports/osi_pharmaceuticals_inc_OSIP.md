## Summary

Astellas Pharma Inc. acquired OSI Pharmaceuticals for $57.50 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $4 billion. The transaction followed a multi-month unsolicited pursuit by Astellas that began with a $52.00 per share hostile offer, which the OSI Board initially rejected as inadequate. After exploring strategic alternatives and contacting third parties, OSI entered into a definitive merger agreement with Astellas on May 16, 2010, at an increased price. The deal closed via a short-form merger on June 8, 2010.

## Interaction History

The interaction began in early 2009 when Astellas' Dr. Masaki Doi met with OSI's Dr. Colin Goddard. Astellas initially proposed $55.00–$57.00 per share in February 2009, which the OSI Board rejected due to the 'momentum of global Tarceva sales' and pipeline potential. After a period of no contact, discussions resumed in early 2010. On February 12, 2010, Astellas CEO Masafumi Nogimori proposed $52.00 per share, a price lower than the 2009 range. OSI's Board, advised by Centerview Partners and Lazard, rejected this on February 19, 2010, as significantly undervaluing the company. Astellas bypassed further negotiation and launched a hostile tender offer on March 2, 2010. OSI responded by initiating a strategic review process, contacting other pharmaceutical companies, and adopting a Change in Control Severance Plan. Following the FDA approval of a new Tarceva indication in April 2010 and continued negotiations, Astellas raised its offer to $57.50. The OSI Board subsequently approved the Merger Agreement on May 16, 2010.

## Rationale

The OSI Board initially rejected Astellas' offers as inadequate, citing the intrinsic value of the Tarceva franchise and the IGF-1R/IR inhibitor pipeline. However, after a strategic review and an increased offer to $57.50 per share, the Board determined the merger was in the best interests of stockholders. Astellas sought to acquire OSI to gain control of its profitable oncology and diabetes franchises and the long-term cash flows from Tarceva.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, and unsolicited offers for OSI Pharmaceuticals Inc. (OSIP) prior to the confirmed deal announcement with Astellas Pharma on May 16, 2010.

*   **January 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reported retrospectively by *Astellas Pharma* and *Reuters* (March 2010).
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Astellas Pharma Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not publicly disclosed at the time.
    *   **Details:** Astellas first privately approached OSI’s CEO to express interest in an acquisition. This remained a private "rumor" within the companies until disclosed during the 2010 hostile bid.

*   **February 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reported retrospectively by *Astellas Pharma* (March 2010).
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Astellas Pharma Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not publicly disclosed at the time.
    *   **Details:** Astellas made its first formal written proposal to acquire OSI. The offer was rejected by the OSI board as significantly undervaluing the company.

*   **March – June 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reported retrospectively by *Astellas Pharma* (March 2010).
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Astellas Pharma Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not publicly disclosed at the time.
    *   **Details:** Astellas sent subsequent letters in March and June 2009 reiterating its interest in a transaction, all of which were rebuffed by OSI management.

*   **February 12, 2010**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Reported retrospectively by *Astellas Pharma* (March 2010).
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Astellas Pharma Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $52.00 per share.
    *   **Details:** The CEOs of Astellas and OSI met in person. Astellas presented a formal proposal at $52.00 per share (a 50% premium at the time). OSI's board rejected the offer, leading Astellas to prepare a hostile tender offer.

*   **March 1, 2010**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *PR Newswire*, *Reuters*, *The Wall Street Journal*.
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Astellas Pharma Inc. (Hostile Bidder).
    *   **Rumored Price:** $52.00 per share ($3.5 billion total).
    *   **Details:** Astellas went public with its unsolicited $52.00 per share all-cash tender offer after more than a year of private rejections. This moved the situation from private speculation to a public hostile takeover attempt.

*   **March – May 2010**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *BioWorld Today*, *Drug Discovery News*, *MarketWatch*.
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Roche AG (as a "White Knight") or a "Dark Horse" bidder (e.g., Pfizer or Sanofi).
    *   **Rumored Price:** $55.00 – $60.00 per share.
    *   **Details:** Following the hostile bid by Astellas, market analysts and media outlets speculated that Roche (OSI's partner on the drug Tarceva) would launch a counter-bid to protect its interests. Other large pharmaceutical companies were frequently cited as potential "white knights" that might emerge during the 10-week standoff.

*   **May 14, 2010**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Reuters*, *Bloomberg*.
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Astellas Pharma Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $57.00 – $58.00 per share.
    *   **Details:** Speculation intensified that a deal was imminent as OSI's stock price rose to $59.80, trading above the original $52.00 offer. Reports suggested Astellas was preparing to "sweeten" its bid to approximately $4 billion to secure a friendly agreement before a scheduled shareholder meeting.
