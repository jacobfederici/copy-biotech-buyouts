## Summary

Perrigo Company plc was the target of an unsolicited, hostile tender offer by Mylan N.V. to acquire all outstanding ordinary shares for a combination of cash and stock. The Perrigo Board of Directors unanimously recommended that shareholders reject the offer, arguing it substantially undervalued the company's standalone growth prospects and posed significant risks due to Mylan's corporate governance and business model. The offer ultimately failed to meet the minimum acceptance condition and was terminated, followed by the resignation of Perrigo's CEO Joseph Papa in April 2016.

- 2015-08-28: Mylan shareholders approve the issuance of shares for the Perrigo transaction.
- 2015-09-14: Mylan formally commences the tender offer.
- 2015-09-16: Perrigo Board receives inadequacy opinion from Morgan Stanley and formally recommends shareholders not tender.
- 2015-09-17: Perrigo files litigation against Mylan in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York.
- 2016-04-24: Joseph C. Papa resigns as CEO of Perrigo to join Valeant Pharmaceuticals.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began with an unsolicited phone call from Mylan's Executive Chairman Robert J. Coury to Perrigo's CEO Joseph C. Papa on April 6, 2015. Perrigo's board, advised by Morgan Stanley and Wachtell Lipton, rejected multiple iterations of the proposal, characterizing them as 'value destructive.' The interaction was marked by a 'hostile' environment, including Mylan's use of a Dutch 'stichting' to block a counter-takeover by Teva Pharmaceutical Industries, which had proposed to buy Mylan. Perrigo management refused to meet with Mylan executives, stating the offer did not provide a basis for discussion. Mylan eventually bypassed the board by launching a formal exchange offer directly to shareholders on September 14, 2015. Perrigo responded with a 'Just Say No' campaign, highlighting Mylan's 'corporate governance prison' and the dilutive nature of the deal. The offer failed to garner sufficient support from Perrigo shareholders by the expiration date.

## Rationale

Mylan sought to acquire Perrigo to create a global pharmaceutical leader with a diversified portfolio across generics, OTC, and specialty medicines, claiming $800 million in annual synergies. Perrigo's board rejected the offer, stating it 'substantially undervalues' the company, denied shareholders the benefits of the Omega Pharma acquisition, and exposed them to Mylan's 'inferior' growth profile and 'shareholder-unfriendly' corporate governance.

## Evidence

- The Perrigo Board unanimously, and without reservation, recommends that you JUST SAY NO by taking no action. (0001193125-15-322658)
- On April 24, 2016, Joseph C. Papa resigned as Chairman of the Board, Director and Chief Executive Officer of Perrigo Company plc. (0001585364-16-000284)

    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Reuters* (Exclusive)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Mylan N.V.
