## Summary

Akouos, Inc. was acquired by Eli Lilly and Company via a tender offer for $12.50 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to an additional $3.00 per share. The transaction focused on Akouos's portfolio of adeno-associated viral (AAV) gene therapies for inner ear conditions, specifically its lead program AK-OTOF. The deal was structured as a two-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, following a successful tender of approximately 81.1% of outstanding shares.

## Timeline

- 2022-02-23: Akouos and Eli Lilly enter into a confidentiality agreement for exploratory collaboration discussions.
- 2022-05-23: Eli Lilly submits a non-binding proposal to acquire Akouos for $9.00 per share in cash.
- 2022-05-26: Akouos Board rejects the $9.00 offer as insufficient but authorizes due diligence and outreach to three other parties.
- 2022-06-06: Eli Lilly submits a revised proposal of $11.50 per share in cash.
- 2022-07-14: Eli Lilly and Akouos mutually agree to pause acquisition talks and pursue a partnership instead.
- 2022-09-09: Akouos receives FDA clearance for its IND application for AK-OTOF.
- 2022-09-15: Eli Lilly resumes acquisition interest with a proposal of $12.00 cash plus $2.50 CVR.
- 2022-09-20: Eli Lilly submits 'best and final' offer of $12.50 cash plus $3.00 CVR.
- 2022-10-17: Akouos Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement; Centerview renders fairness opinion.
- 2022-10-18: Public announcement of the transaction.
- 2022-10-31: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2022-11-29: Tender offer expires with 81.1% of shares tendered.
- 2022-12-01: Merger completed; Akouos becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of Eli Lilly.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2022 as collaboration talks between Akouos (led by CEO Emmanuel Simons) and Eli Lilly (led by Andrew Adams and Kenneth Custer). Lilly's initial May 2022 bid of $9.00 was rejected. A June bid of $11.50 led to deeper diligence, but talks stalled in July as Lilly pivoted toward a partnership model. During this time, Akouos contacted three other pharmaceutical companies (including 'Party B' and 'Party C'), all of whom declined to bid. The momentum shifted on September 9, 2022, when Akouos secured FDA IND clearance for AK-OTOF. Lilly returned with a $12.00 cash + $2.50 CVR offer on September 15. Akouos counter-proposed $14.00 cash + $3.00 CVR. Lilly responded on September 20 with a 'best and final' offer of $12.50 cash + $3.00 CVR. The Board accepted this final offer after negotiating extensions to the CVR milestone timelines to better align with development projections.

## Rationale

The Akouos Board determined the deal was superior to remaining independent due to the high cost and risk of developing gene therapies, the need for significant dilutive capital raises to reach future value inflection points, and the attractive premium offered by Lilly. Lilly sought to expand its genetic medicine portfolio and leverage Akouos's AAV platform for hearing loss.

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed following the satisfaction of the Minimum Tender Condition. CVR milestones include: $1.00 for 5th participant in AK-OTOF trial (by Jan 2025), $1.00 for 5th participant in a Second Program trial (by Jan 2027), and $1.00 for Phase 3 start or BLA approval of a Registration Study Product (by Dec 2026, with a monthly decay thereafter until Dec 2028).
