## Summary

Kite Pharma, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Gilead Sciences, Inc. for $180.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $11.9 billion. The transaction established Gilead as a leader in cellular therapy, specifically CAR-T, following the anticipated commercial launch of Kite's lead candidate, axicabtagene ciloleucel.

## Timeline

- 2017-01-01: Informal meeting between Gilead SVP Andrew Dickinson and Kite EVP Helen Kim at an industry conference.
- 2017-02-10: Gilead and Kite enter into a mutual confidentiality agreement.
- 2017-07-16: Gilead submits an unsolicited proposal to acquire Kite for $127.00 per share in cash.
- 2017-07-17: Kite Board determines the $127.00 offer is insufficient and establishes a Strategic Transaction Committee.
- 2017-07-28: Gilead increases its offer to $160.00 per share in cash.
- 2017-08-18: Gilead increases offer to $176.00; Kite CEO Arie Belldegrun counters at $180.00, which Gilead CEO John Milligan agrees to recommend to his board.
- 2017-08-27: Kite Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement at $180.00 per share.
- 2017-08-28: Public announcement of the transaction.
- 2017-09-05: Commencement of the tender offer.
- 2017-10-02: Tender offer expires with approximately 62.74% of shares tendered.
- 2017-10-03: Merger completed; Kite becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of Gilead.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began informally in January 2017 between Andrew Dickinson (Gilead) and Helen Kim (Kite). After several management meetings regarding Kite's CAR-T pipeline, Gilead CEO John Milligan issued a $127.00/share bid on July 16, 2017. Kite's board, advised by Centerview Partners and Sullivan & Cromwell, rejected this as 'disappointing.' On July 28, Milligan and Gilead Chairman John Martin met with Kite CEO Arie Belldegrun and raised the offer to $160.00. Kite management provided detailed data on axicabtagene ciloleucel (axi-cel) to justify a higher valuation. On August 18, Gilead proposed $176.00. Belldegrun countered at $180.00, citing the value of the early-stage pipeline. Gilead accepted the $180.00 price point on the condition of an expedited 10-day diligence and signing period. The parties negotiated the merger agreement through August 26, focusing on termination fees ($356 million) and employee retention. The deal was signed August 27, 2017.

## Rationale

The Kite Board considered the $180.00 cash price a compelling value that eliminated clinical and commercial execution risks. Gilead sought to acquire Kite to establish a leading position in cell therapy, leveraging its global scale and manufacturing expertise to commercialize axi-cel and the broader CAR-T/TCR platform.

## Evidence

- At a meeting held on August 27, 2017, the Company Board unanimously: approved the Merger Agreement... resolved to recommend that the stockholders of the Company accept the Offer (SC 14D9).
- On October 3, 2017, following consummation of the Offer, Purchaser merged with and into the Company (8-K, 2017-10-03).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a Section 251(h) merger following a successful tender offer. Shares outstanding (57,410,242) were sourced from the SC 14D9 as of August 31, 2017. The $11.9B deal value includes the assumption of equity awards and warrants.

    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *StreetInsider* / *Benzinga*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Gilead Sciences
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specified.
    *   **Details:** Market chatter increased regarding Gilead's "desperate" need for an acquisition to offset declining Hepatitis C revenues. Kite Pharma was frequently cited by analysts as a primary target due to its leadership in CAR-T therapy.

*   **June 12, 2017**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Endpoints News* / *BioSpace* (post-deal analysis of pre-deal activity)
