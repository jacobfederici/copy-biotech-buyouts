## Summary

Chimerix, a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing imipridone cancer therapies with lead candidate dordaviprone (ONC201) in Phase 3 development for H3 K27M-mutant diffuse glioma, agreed to be acquired by Jazz Pharmaceuticals for $8.55 per share in all-cash consideration. Following a structured process that ran from December 2024 through early March 2025, the Board selected Jazz's all-cash offer over Party A's CVR-based proposal after Jazz improved its initial $5.50 bid to $8.55. The tender offer commenced on 2025-03-21, achieved 90.45% tender by the 2025-04-17 expiration, and closed on 2025-04-21 with the surviving company becoming an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Jazz. The transaction valued the company at approximately $802 million on a fully diluted basis.

## Interaction History

**Early Strategic Conversations (August 2023 - November 2024):** Chimerix conducted ongoing exploratory discussions with multiple pharmaceutical and biopharmaceutical companies regarding potential partnerships, licensing arrangements, and strategic transactions. Party A, a global pharmaceutical company, engaged with Chimerix beginning in August 2023 regarding potential ONC201 licensing in ex-U.S. jurisdictions. Party C, a European pharmaceutical company, entered discussions in July 2024 and submitted a licensing proposal on September 30, 2024. Party B, a global biopharmaceutical company, began discussions in September 2024 with inbound interest in a potential strategic transaction. Party D, a specialty pharmaceutical company, entered discussions in November 2024 regarding ONC201 ex-U.S. licensing. Party A upgraded its confidentiality agreement in November 2024 to include a standstill provision as discussions shifted toward potential company-wide acquisition.

**December 2024 NDA Announcement and Buyer Interest:** On December 9, 2024, Chimerix publicly announced its plan to submit a complete NDA for accelerated approval of ONC201 before year-end. This announcement triggered accelerated interest from multiple parties. Jazz's Head of Corporate Development contacted Andriole and Riga on December 12 to express interest in a strategic transaction involving ONC201. Party B similarly expressed acquisition interest in a December 12 call with Riga and Franzinger. Party G also entered discussions on December 11 following inbound acquisition interest. The public announcement of the NDA submission created a catalyst that shifted discussions among several parties from licensing to potential full company acquisition.

**Initial Proposals (December 2024 - January 2025):** Party A submitted its first written acquisition proposal on December 19, 2024, proposing to acquire ONC201 and related assets for $350M upfront plus $100M contingent on FDA approval. Chimerix management informed Party A this was insufficient. Party A revised to an acquisition proposal on January 11, 2025, at $6.50 per share upfront plus one CVR per share with $1.50 payable upon FDA approval (by June 30, 2027) and $1.50 upon $700M+ U.S. net sales (by 2030). Jazz submitted its first acquisition proposal on February 6 at $5.50 per share upfront plus CVRs of $1.00 (FDA approval), $0.25 (European reimbursement in three of five markets), and $1.50 (global $500M+ sales).

**Board Strategic Review (December 2024 - January 2025):** The Board held meetings on December 18-19 and January 22 to evaluate strategic alternatives. The Board determined to pursue multiple options in parallel: potential ex-U.S. licensing for ONC201, potential equity financing, and potential acquisition. The Board reviewed with Centerview the competitive landscape and potential counterparties. Centerview and the Board assessed that Party A's January 11 proposal ($6.50 + CVR), while higher than Jazz's February 6 proposal ($5.50 + CVR), warranted further negotiation to determine if either party would improve terms or if alternatives existed.

**J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference Interactions (January 13-16, 2025):** Chimerix management conducted multiple conversations at the J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference. Andriole and Riga discussed with Jazz's CEO and CFO, with Jazz confirming strong interest in Chimerix. Party B indicated strong acquisition interest through its Chief Business Officer. Party A's head of M&A discussed acquisition interest. Party D and Party C discussed continued interest in ONC201 licensing. Party F, G, and E discussed potential acquisition interest. These conversations indicated substantial bidder interest but also revealed constraints: Party B raised concerns about justifying a premium; Party F noted challenges in assessing addressable market and approval likelihood; Party E later cited neuro-oncology risk assessment challenges.

**Process Letter and Best-and-Final Round (February 2025):** On February 10, the Board authorized Centerview to issue a process letter to Party A and Jazz establishing a structured timeline for best and final offers by March 3, 2025, and revised merger agreement drafts by February 26. Centerview delivered the process letter on February 11. Between February 26 and March 3, the three parties' counsel (Skadden/Cooley for Chimerix, Jazz counsel, and Party A counsel) negotiated key issues in merger and transaction documents. The key negotiation points included regulatory approval obligations, conditions to closing, non-solicitation provisions, termination rights, termination fee structure, employee equity award treatment, and CVR milestone formulation.

**FDA NDA Acceptance (February 18, 2025):** On February 18, Chimerix publicly announced that the FDA had accepted its NDA for accelerated approval of ONC201 with priority review status and a target action date of August 18, 2025. Andriole had separately notified Jazz's CEO and a senior executive of Party A on February 14 of this development in advance of public announcement. This milestone was significant as it moved the regulatory process forward from application acceptance to formal review, strengthening the competitive position of ONC201 and potentially the valuation case for Chimerix.

**Board Fiduciary Review and Fairness Opinion Preparation (February 19 and 25, 2025):** The Board held two meetings in late February to discuss fiduciary duties (with Skadden counsel present) and to review preliminary financial analyses. The Board verified that Centerview had no material relationships that would impair its independence. On February 25, the Board reviewed preliminary financial analyses and risk-adjusted projections for ONC201 reflecting management's expectations regarding ACTION Study timing, patent exclusivity assumptions, and cash flow projections through 2046.

**Final Proposals and Board Selection (March 3-4, 2025):** On March 3, Jazz improved its proposal to $8.55 per share in all cash (removing the CVR component). Party A submitted its final proposal of $7.50 per share upfront plus $2.00 CVR payable if ONC201 received full FDA approval by August 31, 2032 (with CVR void if the Phase 3 ACTION Study primary endpoint was not achieved or if the FDA issued a complete response letter). On the evening of March 3, the Board met to compare the two proposals. Centerview calculated an illustrative risk-adjusted net present value of $0.51 per share for Party A's CVR, implying a total per-share value of approximately $8.01 versus Jazz's certain $8.55 all-cash consideration. The Board evaluated the certainty of cash payment and risk-adjusted value analysis and determined Jazz's offer represented the highest price reasonably obtainable. The Board directed Centerview to inform Jazz it was the winning bidder and Party A that it was not selected. Party A did not submit a revised proposal after receiving this feedback.

**Document Finalization and Exclusive Negotiations (March 4, 2025):** Following Board direction, Chimerix entered into a one-day exclusivity agreement with Jazz, with the exclusivity period ending at 11:59 pm ET on March 5. Skadden, Cooley, and Jazz counsel worked to finalize transaction documents. Key items negotiated and finalized included the $2.1 million aggregate cap on excise tax gross-ups for senior executive officers. On the afternoon of March 4, the Board held a second meeting where Skadden and Cooley summarized the final negotiated terms and Centerview delivered its oral fairness opinion. The Board unanimously approved the Merger Agreement and authorized Andriole, Alrutz, and other authorized officers to execute the agreement. The Merger Agreement was executed in the evening of March 4.

**Regulatory and Bid Timeline:** The parties did not disclose any regulatory approval requirement as a condition to the Offer, indicating either that antitrust clearance was not anticipated to be material or that the parties assessed no material antitrust concerns. Jazz commenced the tender offer on March 21, 2025, with an initial expiration date of April 17, 2025. The parties did not extend the Offer expiration, indicating successful satisfaction of tender conditions and no material intervening events requiring extension. The Offer was oversubscribed, with 84,845,016 shares tendered (90.45% of outstanding), exceeding the majority-of-outstanding-shares condition and providing high certainty of consummation.

**Conflict Review and Governance:** The Board conducted a careful review of potential conflicts of interest. Michael Andriole (CEO) and certain other executives and directors held significant equity interests and would receive option/RSU acceleration cash payments. Director Fred Middleton held interests through affiliated Sanderling venture capital funds. The Compensation Committee reviewed and approved executive severance and equity treatment arrangements under Rule 14d-10 safe harbor. The Board determined that while conflicts existed, they were appropriately disclosed and considered in the Board's deliberation.

## Rationale

According to the Board and Centerview as stated in the SC 14D-9 filing, the rationale for the transaction centered on:

**Valuation and Certainty of Value:** The Board considered the $8.55 per share all-cash Offer Price in the context of Chimerix's historical and contemporaneous market performance. The Offer Price represented a 70% premium over the March 3, 2025 closing price, an 89% premium over the 30-day volume-weighted average price, and a 63% premium over the prior 52-week high. Jazz's all-cash consideration provided immediate and certain value to shareholders as opposed to contingent payments (such as Party A's CVR proposal) that carried regulatory and commercial risk.

**Risk-Adjusted Valuation vs. Standalone Company:** Centerview calculated the risk-adjusted net present value of Party A's CVR at $0.51 per share (implying total value of ~$8.01), compared to Jazz's certain $8.55 cash bid. The Board assessed that Chimerix's standalone strategy carried substantial risk: the FDA NDA for ONC201 had been accepted but not yet approved (target action date August 18, 2025); the Phase 3 ACTION Study was ongoing with inherent clinical risk; the addressable market size for H3 K27M-mutant diffuse glioma was limited; and commercialization of a rare pediatric oncology product on a standalone basis would require significant capital deployment and operational execution. The Board concluded that the probability-weighted value of a standalone company strategy did not reasonably exceed the Offer Price.

**Limited Alternative Paths and Bidder Competition:** The Board ran a structured process from December 2024 through early March 2025, engaging multiple parties (Party A, Party B, Party C, Party D, Party E, Party F, Party G) regarding potential transactions or partnerships. By March 3, only Party A and Jazz had submitted final offers. Party B declined to bid due to inability to justify a premium. Party C, D, F, and E either declined acquisition interest or dropped out during diligence (citing neuro-oncology risk, Phase 3 trial risk, or market size concerns). The Board concluded that Jazz's offer represented the result of a competitive process and the highest price reasonably obtainable.

**Immediate Certain Value Over Regulatory and Commercial Uncertainty:** The Board noted that even with the FDA NDA accepted and the ACTION Study ongoing, substantial uncertainty remained around approvalability, label scope, commercialization feasibility, and timeline. The filing stated that Chimerix would need to "incur significant losses for the foreseeable future" and might not achieve profitability. Shareholders faced regulatory execution risk (FDA approval might be delayed or denial issued), clinical execution risk (ACTION Study primary endpoint might not be achieved), and commercial execution risk (even if approved, sales might not justify the valuation). Jazz's all-cash offer eliminated those downstream risks for shareholders.

**Financial Strength and Likelihood of Consummation:** The Board considered Jazz's financial strength, cash on hand to fund the transaction, and track record of completing transactions. The Merger Agreement contained no financing condition and a broad definition of Material Adverse Effect that excluded industry-wide and general economic conditions. These terms provided high confidence that the transaction would close once tender conditions were satisfied.

**Strategic Rationale for Jazz:** While the filing did not detail Jazz's stated strategic rationale, the context indicates that Jazz viewed Chimerix's ONC201 program in H3 K27M-mutant diffuse glioma as a fit with Jazz's oncology franchise, the regulatory milestone (FDA acceptance of NDA with priority review) as de-risking the asset, and the fully diluted enterprise value of ~$803M as reasonable given the rare disease market opportunity and the program's development stage.

The Board's determination was that, in light of the totality of risks—clinical, regulatory, commercial, and financial—the certain, immediate, all-cash Offer Price best served shareholder interests compared to remaining independent or pursuing the contingent Party A proposal.
