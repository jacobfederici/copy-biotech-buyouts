## Summary

Neurocrine Biosciences, Inc. acquired Soleno Therapeutics, Inc. through a cash tender offer at $53.00 per share pursuant to a Merger Agreement dated April 5, 2026. Soleno was a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company that had received FDA approval in late March 2025 for VYKAT XR (diazoxide choline extended-release tablets) to treat hyperphagia in Prader-Willi Syndrome (PWS). The Soleno Board unanimously recommended stockholder acceptance of the offer on April 4, 2026. The offer expired on May 15, 2026 with 88.9% of outstanding shares validly tendered, and the merger was completed on May 18, 2026 without a stockholder vote, pursuant to Section 251(h) of the DGCL.

## Interaction History

**Early Strategic Review (April 2025)**

In early April 2025, following the FDA approval of VYKAT XR, the Soleno Board received three nonbinding term sheets from biopharmaceutical companies (the "Partnership Prospects") concerning licensing rights to market VYKAT XR outside the United States. After review, the Board determined that none presented a viable path for achieving Soleno's licensing objectives. The filing does not identify these companies by name.

**Party A Process (April–June 2025)**

On April 16, 2025, Soleno entered into a confidentiality agreement with a biopharmaceutical company identified as Party A, which included a customary standstill but no "don't ask, don't waive" restriction. On May 22, 2025, Party A submitted a nonbinding proposal to acquire Soleno for $86.00 per share in cash. The closing price of the shares on May 22, 2025 was $73.89, making the proposal a premium of approximately 16% to the then-current price.

On May 23, 2025, the Soleno Board met and authorized Centerview and Guggenheim Securities to contact six biopharmaceutical companies identified as potential acquirers with the financial capability to complete a transaction. Neurocrine was not among the six contacted. The Board elected not to contact the Partnership Prospects because they were perceived as focused outside the United States and unlikely to have the capability or interest to acquire Soleno.

Of the six contacted, two responded with interest: Party B indicated interest in a licensing transaction in the future after gaining more clarity on Soleno's European prospects, and Party C expressed immediate acquisition interest and initiated diligence (Soleno had entered into a confidentiality agreement with Party C on April 13, 2025). After conducting diligence, Party C declined to make a proposal, citing an inability to make an all-cash offer or compete on value. Party B conducted diligence beginning in the second half of 2025, facilitated by a confidentiality agreement signed July 31, 2025 (which did not include a standstill).

In early June 2025, the Board met to review the status of discussions and the responses from Party B and Party C. Later in June 2025, Party A withdrew its $86.00 proposal, citing concerns about the longevity of Soleno's intellectual property portfolio and the size of the European market opportunity.

**Parallel Exploration and Entry of Neurocrine (Fall 2025–February 2026)**

Following the withdrawal of Party A, the Soleno Board continued to explore licensing rights outside the United States, with substantive discussions occurring with Party B. In the fall of 2025, Dr. Bhatnagar and Dr. Gano met briefly at a social event; the filing states that an acquisition of Soleno by Neurocrine was not discussed at that meeting.

In early February 2026, Party B terminated its discussions with Soleno, citing concerns about European business prospects and a preference to continue monitoring the U.S. commercial launch. At approximately the same time, Party D's representative expressed acquisition interest to Centerview and Guggenheim Securities and requested an introduction to Dr. Bhatnagar. Separately, in early February 2026, representatives of Guggenheim Securities met with Samir Siddhanti, Neurocrine's Vice President of Business Development, during regular industry meetings; the parties discussed general industry dynamics including Soleno. Siddhanti accepted Guggenheim Securities' offer to facilitate an introduction to Dr. Bhatnagar. Both introductions were made on February 9, 2026, and Dr. Bhatnagar informed the Board, including Lead Independent Director Matthew Pauls, of both.

**Neurocrine Diligence and Proposal (February–March 2026)**

On February 17, 2026, Dr. Bhatnagar and Siddhanti spoke about VYKAT XR's efficacy and safety profile. Siddhanti informed Bhatnagar that Neurocrine management was interested in evaluating an acquisition of Soleno and that the Neurocrine Board would discuss the matter at an upcoming meeting.

On February 19, 2026, Soleno provided a confidentiality agreement to Party D; it was signed March 2, 2026, and Party D thereafter conducted diligence. On February 20, 2026, Siddhanti reported that the Neurocrine Board had authorized management to undertake diligence on Soleno. Later that day, Soleno provided Neurocrine with a confidentiality agreement, which was signed February 24, 2026 and included a customary standstill (no "don't ask, don't waive" restriction). Following signing, Soleno provided Neurocrine and its advisors with access to a virtual data room and responded to diligence inquiries, with Soleno management participating in diligence sessions.

On February 27, 2026, Soleno received information from the EMA that, per the filing, "caused Soleno concern that it may not receive approval from the EMA" for VIOKAT prolonged-release tablets in the EU. On March 12, 2026, Bhatnagar and Siddhanti discussed diligence matters, including the EMA communication.

On March 18, 2026, Drs. Bhatnagar and Gano spoke; Gano stated that Neurocrine would submit an acquisition proposal for $50.00 per share in cash, later provided in writing that same day (the "Initial Neurocrine Proposal"). The proposal stated Neurocrine wished to sign and announce the acquisition by April 6, 2026. It did not address retention, employment, or compensation of any Soleno officer or director post-closing. Closing price on March 18, 2026 was $32.80.

**March 19, 2026 Board Meeting and Party D Parallel Track**

On March 19, 2026, the Soleno Board met with management and representatives of Wilson Sonsini, Centerview, and Guggenheim Securities. Dr. Bhatnagar summarized the Initial Neurocrine Proposal and his discussions. The Board heard from the financial advisors on the financial perspectives of the proposal and the outcome of the 2025 strategic process. The Board considered: (1) the favorable terms of the Initial Neurocrine Proposal and Neurocrine's expressed urgency; (2) the risk that Neurocrine would terminate discussions if Soleno did not move rapidly; (3) the possibility that the EMA would not approve VIOKAT in the EU, which appeared more acute after the February 27, 2026 EMA communication; (4) the risk of leaks if Soleno engaged many additional bidders; (5) the fact that every participant in the 2025 process ultimately terminated discussions; (6) concerns about long-term business prospects as a standalone company, including dependence on VYKAT XR and challenges expanding outside the U.S.; and (7) general acquirer familiarity with Soleno as a public company.

The Board instructed advisors to focus on improving the terms of the Initial Neurocrine Proposal and to continue supporting Party D's diligence while informing Party D it needed to move quickly.

Later on March 19, 2026, Dr. Bhatnagar met with Party D's Chief Business Officer for dinner and communicated the need for Party D to significantly accelerate its diligence. Party D subsequently requested additional diligence information, which was provided. Still later that evening, Dr. Bhatnagar informed Dr. Gano that Soleno should be valued higher than $50.00 per share and would provide additional diligence to allow Neurocrine to improve its offer. Gano stated that any revision would only come after receipt of additional diligence.

Drs. Bhatnagar and Gano spoke on March 20, 2026, with Gano outlining key diligence areas and reiterating that further diligence was required before any revision to the Initial Neurocrine Proposal. They spoke again on March 25, 2026 about diligence progress.

On March 30, 2026, Party D informed Centerview and Guggenheim Securities that it could not make a proposal until after Soleno's Q1 earnings release and the EMA oral hearing. That same day, Drs. Bhatnagar and Gano spoke; Gano stated Neurocrine would submit a revised proposal for $53.00 per share as its "best and final" offer, delivered in writing later that day (the "Final Neurocrine Proposal"). Closing price on March 30, 2026 was $30.44.

**March 30, 2026 Board Meeting and Engagement of Counsel**

The Soleno Board met later on March 30, 2026 with management and advisors. Dr. Bhatnagar summarized the Final Neurocrine Proposal and its "best and final" characterization. Wilson Sonsini reviewed fiduciary duties. Management reviewed financial projections (the "Projections") and the Board approved them for use by the financial advisors. Centerview and Guggenheim Securities presented preliminary financial analyses based on the Projections. The Board considered the same factors as the March 19 meeting, including: the increased offer of $53.00, Neurocrine's largely completed diligence, the absence of competitive overlap between Soleno and Neurocrine (supporting antitrust approval confidence), the risk of losing Neurocrine's offer if Soleno sought further negotiations, the EMA risk, the leak risk, the outcome of all prior processes, standalone business concerns, and general acquirer familiarity.

The Board instructed advisors to focus on finalizing documentation within Neurocrine's proposed April 6, 2026 timeframe, and to quickly contact Party A to determine if it would restart discussions. The Board also authorized management and Wilson Sonsini to negotiate engagement letters with the financial advisors. Dr. Bhatnagar informed Gano that the $53.00 terms were potentially acceptable if agreement could be announced before market open on April 6, 2026, and the parties agreed to proceed with confirmatory diligence and merger agreement negotiation.

Late on March 30, 2026, Cooley LLP (Neurocrine's outside counsel) delivered a draft merger agreement to Wilson Sonsini.

**Final Negotiation (March 31–April 5, 2026)**

Between March 31 and April 4, 2026, Cooley and Wilson Sonsini negotiated the merger agreement terms, including: (1) the Soleno-to-Neurocrine termination fee and triggering circumstances; (2) the Neurocrine-to-Soleno termination fee in the event of antitrust failure; (3) exceptions to the "material adverse effect" definition; (4) the scope of Neurocrine's obligation to obtain antitrust approvals; and (5) Offer closing conditions.

On March 31, 2026, Centerview and Guggenheim Securities contacted Party A at the direction of the Soleno Board; Party A stated it was not interested in acquisition discussions. On April 1, 2026, Soleno entered an engagement letter with Centerview. On April 2, 2026, Cooley delivered a draft tender and support agreement; Cooley and Wilson Sonsini negotiated its terms over the following days.

On April 3, 2026, the Soleno Board met and received Wilson Sonsini's review of the draft agreements. On April 4, 2026, each financial advisor delivered relationship disclosure letters; the Board identified no disqualifying matters. Soleno entered an engagement letter with Guggenheim Securities the same day.

At the April 4, 2026 Board meeting, Wilson Sonsini presented the final merger agreement and tender agreement. Centerview delivered its oral fairness opinion (confirmed by written opinion dated April 4, 2026) that the $53.00 Offer Price was fair from a financial point of view. Guggenheim Securities delivered its oral opinion (confirmed in writing dated April 5, 2026) to the same effect. The Soleno Board unanimously: (1) determined the Merger Agreement and Transactions are advisable and fair to and in the best interests of Soleno and its stockholders; (2) declared it advisable to enter into the Merger Agreement; (3) approved execution, delivery, and performance; (4) resolved the Merger would be effected under Section 251(h) of the DGCL; and (5) resolved to recommend that stockholders accept the Offer and tender their shares.

On April 5, 2026, the parties finalized and executed the Merger Agreement and Tender Agreements. Anish Bhatnagar (CEO and Chairman) and James Mackaness (former CFO) each signed Tender and Support Agreements, collectively covering approximately 1.01% of outstanding shares.

**Completion**

On April 6, 2026, Soleno and Neurocrine issued a joint press release announcing the Transactions. On April 7, 2026, Soleno filed an 8-K announcing the voluntary withdrawal of its EMA Marketing Authorization Application for VIOKAT prolonged-release tablets in the EU, citing business and strategic considerations. The tender offer was commenced April 20, 2026. The offer expired on May 15, 2026 with 46,356,114 shares (approximately 88.9%) tendered. On May 18, 2026, the Merger was completed under Section 251(h) DGCL without a stockholder vote. The Company was delisted from Nasdaq and all Soleno officers and directors resigned effective as of the Effective Time. Matthew C. Abernethy and Darin M. Lippoldt (both affiliated with Neurocrine) became the directors of the surviving corporation.

## Rationale

The SC 14D9 (accession 0001193125-26-162853) sets forth the factors the Soleno Board considered in recommending acceptance of the Offer.

**Financial Terms.** The filing states the Board considered that the $53.00 per share Offer Price represents: (1) an approximately 34% premium to the April 2, 2026 closing price of $39.49; (2) an approximately 51% premium to the 30-day VWAP ending April 2, 2026; (3) an approximately 36% premium to the 90-day VWAP ending April 2, 2026; and (4) an approximately 74% premium to the March 30, 2026 closing price of $30.44 (the day Neurocrine submitted the "best and final" offer). The Board also noted the $3.00 per share increase from the Initial Neurocrine Proposal, and characterized the all-cash structure as providing certainty of value.

**Certainty of Completion.** The Board cited the absence of a financing condition, Neurocrine's financial strength and ability to fund the transaction from cash on hand, the absence of competitive overlap (indicating antitrust approval confidence), and the structure as a two-step tender offer under Section 251(h).

**Results of Strategic Process.** The Board noted that Party A withdrew a higher $86.00 per share proposal in June 2025 following concerns about IP longevity and the European market; that Party A declined to restart discussions in March 2026; that Party B terminated discussions in early February 2026 citing European prospects and a desire to monitor the commercial launch; that Party C declined to make any proposal; and that Party D stated it could not submit a proposal until after Q1 earnings and the EMA oral hearing.

**EMA Risk.** The filing states that the Soleno Board viewed the risk of EMA non-approval as "acute" based on the February 27, 2026 EMA communication, and cited the challenges of obtaining regulatory approvals outside the United States as a risk factor for standalone operations.

**Standalone Risks.** The Board cited Soleno's substantial dependence on VYKAT XR as its sole source of revenue, uncertainty about long-term commercial prospects for VYKAT XR (including prescriber confidence, MFN pricing legislation, and competitive risk), and the costs and regulatory requirements of international expansion.

**Fairness Opinions.** Centerview's DCF analysis yielded an implied per share equity value range of $44.30 to $49.40 at discount rates of 11%-14%. Centerview's selected public company analysis yielded a range of $27.95 to $43.35. Centerview's selected precedent transactions analysis yielded a range of $44.10 to $63.00. Centerview's precedent premia analysis yielded a range of $51.35 to $75.05. Centerview concluded the $53.00 Offer Price was fair from a financial point of view. Guggenheim Securities reached the same conclusion using similar methodologies.
