## Summary

Ambit Biosciences Corporation was acquired by Daiichi Sankyo Company, Limited for $15.00 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $4.50 per share based on commercialization milestones for quizartinib. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, valuing the upfront equity at approximately $315.3 million.

## Timeline

- 2013-12-16: Ambit business development team contacts Daiichi Sankyo to set up an introductory meeting.
- 2014-01-27: Ambit receives an unsolicited non-binding proposal from Company A to acquire Ambit for $16.25 per share.
- 2014-02-12: Ambit Board authorizes Centerview to inform Company A that the $16.25 price is inadequate.
- 2014-03-04: Company A increases its proposal to $17.25 per share plus $6.00 in sales-based CVRs.
- 2014-07-18: Daiichi Sankyo outlines a non-binding indication of interest for $200 million upfront plus $100 million in CVRs.
- 2014-07-21: Company A submits a revised proposal of $12.00 upfront and $8.00 in CVRs.
- 2014-07-26: Daiichi Sankyo increases offer to $15.00 per share upfront plus $100 million in CVRs.
- 2014-07-31: Ambit and Daiichi Sankyo enter into a confidentiality agreement with a standstill provision.
- 2014-09-08: Company A submits a revised proposal of $15.50 upfront and $8.00 in CVRs.
- 2014-09-13: Company A withdraws its proposal after completing commercial diligence.
- 2014-09-28: Ambit Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement with Daiichi Sankyo.
- 2014-10-10: Purchaser commences the tender offer.
- 2014-11-10: Tender offer expires; approximately 85% of shares tendered; merger completed.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2014 as a partnering discussion for quizartinib. Ambit simultaneously managed an unsolicited acquisition interest from Company A. In February 2014, the Board rejected Company A's $16.25 offer as inadequate. By July 2014, following a decline in Ambit's stock price, Daiichi Sankyo shifted from partnering to acquisition interest, initially proposing a $200M upfront deal. Michael Martino (CEO) led negotiations, pushing for mid-teens upfront value. Daiichi Sankyo moved to $15.00 per share plus $100M in CVRs on July 26. Company A re-entered with a $15.50 offer in September but withdrew on September 13 after internal modeling failed to support the valuation. Final negotiations with Daiichi Sankyo focused on the CVR milestones and the termination fee (settled at 3.5%). The Ambit Board formed a transaction committee (Foletta, Hatfield, Parkinson) on September 16 to oversee the final stages. The deal was signed on September 28, 2014.

## Rationale

The Ambit Board determined the deal was superior to remaining standalone, citing the immediate liquidity of the cash offer and the risks associated with funding a Phase 3 trial and commercial launch independently. Management estimated a need for $35M in equity and $15M in debt to reach 2017 without a partner. Daiichi Sankyo sought to strengthen its oncology pipeline with quizartinib.
