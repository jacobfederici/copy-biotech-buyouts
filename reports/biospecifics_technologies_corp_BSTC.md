## Summary

BioSpecifics Technologies Corp. was acquired by Endo International plc for $88.50 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $650 million. The transaction consolidates Endo's ownership of the collagenase clostridium histolyticum (CCH) intellectual property, which Endo already marketed under the brand names XIAFLEX and Qwo through a long-standing license agreement. The deal was completed via a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL on December 2, 2020.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in August 2020 when Endo CEO Blaise Coleman approached BioSpecifics CEO Joseph Truitt. Endo's initial bid of $85.00 was rejected by the Board as undervaluing the company's royalty stream and pipeline potential. Following a value-demonstration presentation by BioSpecifics and its consultant ClearView, Endo raised its bid to $88.50. However, after conducting 'first round' due diligence, Endo lowered its offer to $87.00 on October 5, 2020, citing concerns over third-party royalty obligations. BioSpecifics rejected the $87.00 price, leading Endo to return to $88.50 on October 7, 2020, characterizing it as a 'best and final' offer. Centerview Partners contacted one other potential suitor (Company A), which declined to proceed. The Board ultimately accepted the $88.50 offer on October 16, 2020, noting it represented a significant premium and provided immediate liquidity.

## Rationale

The BioSpecifics Board determined the deal was the best strategic alternative to maximize stockholder value, providing immediate cash liquidity at a substantial premium. They considered the risks of remaining standalone, including dependence on Endo's commercial execution of Qwo and XIAFLEX, and the finite term of royalty payments. Endo sought the acquisition to capture the full economics of the CCH franchise and eliminate royalty/milestone obligations to BioSpecifics.
