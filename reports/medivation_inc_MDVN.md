## Summary

Medivation, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Pfizer Inc. for $81.50 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $14 billion. The transaction followed a highly publicized unsolicited bid from Sanofi and a subsequent competitive bidding process involving multiple large pharmaceutical companies. The deal closed on September 28, 2016, via a merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, making Medivation a wholly-owned subsidiary of Pfizer.

## Interaction History

The transaction originated from an unsolicited approach by Sanofi in March 2016. After Medivation rejected Sanofi's initial $52.50 proposal, Sanofi launched a hostile consent solicitation to replace the board. Medivation, advised by J.P. Morgan and Evercore, initiated a formal strategic process in June 2016, contacting 15 parties and entering NDAs with five (Pfizer, Sanofi, and three others referred to as Companies 1-4). Pfizer, led by Douglas Giordano and CEO Ian Read, engaged in extensive due diligence throughout July. On August 8, Pfizer bid $65.00, while Company 1 bid $71.00. Medivation pushed for higher offers, leading to a second round. On August 19, Pfizer increased its bid to $77.00. On August 20, Medivation requested 'best and final' offers by noon. Pfizer submitted $81.50, surpassing Company 1's $80.25 and Company 2's $80.00. The Medivation board, determining Pfizer's offer was the highest and provided the best value, unanimously approved the merger agreement on August 20, 2016.

## Rationale

The Medivation board recommended the deal because the $81.50 cash price provided a significant premium (56.6% over the unaffected price) and immediate liquidity. The board determined, after a thorough competitive process, that Pfizer's offer was the highest value achievable and superior to remaining standalone. Pfizer sought the acquisition to strengthen its Innovative Health business and oncology portfolio, specifically adding the blockbuster XTANDI and a promising PARP inhibitor (talazoparib).

## Evidence

## Decision Notes

The deal value is frequently cited in media as ~$14B; the calculated equity value based on basic shares is ~$13.5B, but the total transaction value includes the acceleration of over 13 million options and other equity awards. Shares outstanding source: SC 14D9 Item 1(b) as of August 18, 2016.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, and hostile takeover bids for Medivation, Inc. (MDVN) prior to the confirmed acquisition by Pfizer on August 22, 2016.

*   **March 30, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Bloomberg* / *Reuters*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified (speculation of a premium over the then-current ~$37 share price)
    *   **Details:** Reports surfaced that Medivation had hired advisors to defend against a potential takeover after receiving unsolicited interest from Sanofi.

*   **April 13, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Bloomberg*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi (hostile), Amgen, and Bayer
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified
    *   **Details:** Sources reported that Medivation had rejected an initial private approach from Sanofi. Speculation grew that Sanofi might launch a hostile bid, while Amgen and Bayer were also mentioned as potential suitors.

*   **April 15, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Pharmaphorum* / *Reuters*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi, Johnson & Johnson (J&J), Amgen, Roche, and AstraZeneca
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified
    *   **Details:** Rumors intensified that Sanofi was preparing a hostile bid. Johnson & Johnson was reported to be "leading the pack" alongside Sanofi in terms of interest.

*   **April 18, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *The Sunday Times* (UK) / *FierceBiotech*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** AstraZeneca
    *   **Rumored Price:** £7 billion (~$9.9 billion to $10 billion)
    *   **Details:** Reports indicated AstraZeneca was considering a "white knight" bid of approximately $10 billion to counter Sanofi’s interest.

*   **April 28, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Public Disclosure / Press Release*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi (Unsolicited Public Bid)
    *   **Rumored Price:** $52.50 per share (approx. $9.3 billion)
    *   **Details:** Sanofi publicly disclosed its unsolicited all-cash proposal after Medivation refused to engage in private talks. Medivation officially rejected the offer the following day, calling it "substantially inadequate."

*   **May 25, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Bloomberg* / *BioSpace*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Gilead Sciences and Celgene
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified
    *   **Details:** Sources "familiar with the matter" reported that Gilead and Celgene were discussing the possibility of making an offer with their respective advisors, though neither had moved to a formal bid yet.

*   **June 2016 (Ongoing)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Reuters* / *CNBC*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Pfizer and Amgen
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified
    *   **Details:** Reports indicated that Pfizer and Amgen had entered the fray, with Pfizer reportedly considering a bid that would top Sanofi's offer.

*   **July 5, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Public Disclosure*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Sanofi (Sweetened Bid)
    *   **Rumored Price:** $58.00 per share cash + $3.00 CVR (approx. $10 billion+)
    *   **Details:** Sanofi raised its offer to $58 per share plus a Contingent Value Right (CVR) tied to the performance of the drug talazoparib. In exchange, Medivation agreed to open its books to Sanofi and other potential bidders under confidentiality agreements.

*   **July 25, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *FiercePharma* / *Guggenheim Securities*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Gilead Sciences
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified
    *   **Details:** Analysts noted Gilead was "in the hunt" for Medivation as it looked to offset flagging Hepatitis C sales, with speculation increasing after Gilead signaled a reduction in share buybacks to preserve cash for M&A.

*   **August 19, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *Reuters*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Pfizer, Merck & Co., Celgene, and Gilead Sciences
    *   **Rumored Price:** Speculation of bids exceeding $11 billion
    *   **Details:** Sources reported that at least five major companies (Pfizer, Merck, Celgene, Gilead, and Sanofi) had submitted formal expressions of interest or initial bids in a confidential sale process.

*   **August 21, 2016 (Evening before deal)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *The Wall Street Journal*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Pfizer
    *   **Rumored Price:** Over $80.00 per share (approx. $14 billion)
    *   **Details:** Reports emerged late Sunday that Pfizer was in advanced talks to acquire Medivation for a price in the low $80s per share, effectively winning the months-long bidding war.
