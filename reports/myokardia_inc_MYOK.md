## Summary

Bristol-Myers Squibb Company, through its wholly owned subsidiary Gotham Merger Sub Inc., acquired MyoKardia, Inc. for $225.00 per share in cash, totaling approximately $13.1 billion. The acquisition was structured as a cash tender offer followed by a merger, which was completed on November 17, 2020.

## Interaction History

The acquisition of MyoKardia by Bristol Myers Squibb was the culmination of a series of discussions and negotiations that began in March 2019 with initial talks about a potential strategic partnership for danicamtiv. These discussions continued intermittently through early 2020, broadening to include MyoKardia's early stage pipeline and a potential gene therapy collaboration. On April 23, 2020, Bristol Myers Squibb expressed interest in a broader collaboration, leading to a new confidentiality agreement on July 2, 2020. MyoKardia presented its pipeline, including mavacamten, to Bristol Myers Squibb, which in turn presented its capabilities for a global strategic partnership.

On September 2, 2020, Giovanni Caforio, M.D., Chairman and CEO of Bristol Myers Squibb, verbally proposed to Tassos Gianakakos, President and CEO of MyoKardia, an acquisition of MyoKardia for $185.00 per share in cash, followed by a formal non-binding letter. Mr. Gianakakos informed the MyoKardia Board of Directors, which, after reviewing the proposal with financial advisors Centerview Partners LLC and Guggenheim Securities, LLC, and legal counsel Goodwin Procter LLP, deemed the offer insufficient. Mr. Gianakakos conveyed this to Dr. Caforio on September 7, 2020, highlighting additional value in MyoKardia's non-public data and events.

On September 13, 2020, Dr. Caforio made a verbal, non-binding proposal of $210.00 per share. MyoKardia's Board directed its financial advisors to contact another global pharmaceutical company, Party A, to gauge interest in a potential sale. Party A subsequently declined to engage. The MyoKardia Board, after further deliberation and review of its own long-range plan and financial projections, decided to provide limited due diligence information to Bristol Myers Squibb to encourage an improved offer, subject to a new confidentiality agreement with a standstill provision, which was executed on September 17, 2020.

Bristol Myers Squibb conducted priority due diligence, and on September 24, 2020, Dr. Caforio verbally proposed $220.00 per share, conditioned on full confirmatory due diligence, a target signing date, and exclusive negotiations, including a 3.5% termination fee. The MyoKardia Board authorized Mr. Gianakakos to negotiate for a higher price. On September 25, 2020, Mr. Gianakakos negotiated with Dr. Caforio, who then proposed $225.00 per share, subject to exclusive negotiations. The MyoKardia Board approved this proposal and authorized exclusive negotiations. The parties then finalized the merger agreement and a tender and support agreement with Mr. Gianakakos.

On October 3, 2020, the MyoKardia Board, with advice from its financial and legal advisors, unanimously approved the Merger Agreement and the transactions, determining them to be advisable, fair, and in the best interests of the Company and its stockholders. Centerview and Guggenheim Securities rendered fairness opinions. The Merger Agreement was executed on October 3, 2020, and a joint press release was issued on October 5, 2020. The tender offer commenced on October 19, 2020, and was successfully completed on November 17, 2020, followed by the merger.

## Rationale

The MyoKardia Board of Directors' rationale for recommending the acquisition included: the significant premium offered over recent trading prices (61% over the closing price on October 2, 2020, and 98% over the closing price on September 1, 2020); the aggregate consideration being the highest paid in comparable biotech transactions; the inherent uncertainties and risks associated with developing, obtaining regulatory approval for, and commercializing drug products (especially with no approved products yet); the robust arm's-length negotiation process that led to an increased offer price and favorable deal terms; the limited interest from other potential acquirers in the cardiovascular space; the certainty of value and liquidity provided by the all-cash consideration; and the anticipated short timeframe for closing due to the tender offer structure. The Board also considered the opinions of its financial advisors, Centerview Partners LLC and Guggenheim Securities, LLC, who both found the consideration to be fair from a financial point of view.
