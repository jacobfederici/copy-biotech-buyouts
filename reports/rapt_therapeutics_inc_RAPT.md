## Summary

RAPT Therapeutics, Inc. was acquired by GSK plc via a cash tender offer for $58.00 per share, representing a total equity value of approximately $1.79 billion. The transaction focused on RAPT's lead asset, ozureprubart, an oral CCR4 antagonist being developed for food allergy and chronic spontaneous urticaria. The deal was completed on March 3, 2026, following a successful tender offer in which approximately 93.36% of shares were tendered.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2025 following RAPT's release of Phase 2 data for ozureprubart. GSK initially approached RAPT in October 2025. After a management presentation in November, GSK initiated outreach to a significant RAPT stockholder, prompting the RAPT Board to formalize a Transaction Committee (Lori Lyons-Williams, Scott Braunstein, Ashley Dombkowski). GSK's Chris Sheldon and David Redfern led the buyer's side, while Brian Wong and Lori Lyons-Williams led for RAPT. GSK made four distinct offers: $50.00 (Dec 22), $53.00 + $4.00 CVR (Dec 23), $55.50 + $4.00 CVR (Dec 30), and finally $58.00 all-cash (Jan 1). RAPT conducted a market check by contacting eight other potential strategic counterparties; only one (Party A) requested a presentation but ultimately declined. RAPT pushed for all-cash consideration to increase value certainty. The final $58.00 offer was accepted after J.P. Morgan provided a fairness analysis showing the range of $49.45 to $62.05 per share.

## Rationale

The RAPT Board determined the deal was the best alternative for maximizing stockholder value, providing immediate liquidity and a significant premium (65% over the prior closing price). The Board considered the high capital requirements and clinical/regulatory risks of developing ozureprubart as a standalone company, as well as the challenging financing environment for biotech. GSK sought to bolster its immunology and allergy pipeline with a late-stage oral candidate.
