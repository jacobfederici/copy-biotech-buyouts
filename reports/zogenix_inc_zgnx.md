## Summary

UCB S.A. acquired Zogenix, Inc. through a tender offer that commenced February 1, 2022 and expired March 4, 2022. UCB's merger subsidiary Zinc Merger Sub accepted 37,646,050 shares representing approximately 66.9% of outstanding shares on March 7, 2022, satisfying the minimum tender condition. The merger was effected the same day under Section 251(h) of the DGCL without a stockholder vote. The transaction originated from UCB's interest in a European partnership for Zogenix's commercial product Fintepla (fenfluramine), which had been approved in the U.S. for Dravet syndrome and was pending EU expansion for Lennox-Gastaut syndrome. The board approved the transaction unanimously on January 18, 2022 following a competitive process involving UCB (Parent) and one other unnamed pharmaceutical company (Party A), with a third party (Party B) that withdrew prior to final bidding. Total upfront consideration was $26.00 per share in cash plus one non-transferable CVR carrying a potential $2.00 additional payment contingent on Fintepla retaining orphan drug designation at the time of its EU approval for treatment of LGS on or prior to December 31, 2023.

## Interaction History

The strategic process that led to UCB's acquisition of Zogenix originated from commercial partnership discussions and progressed over approximately seven months before culminating in a definitive agreement on January 18, 2022.

**Initial Partnership Context (June–August 2021)**

A representative of Party A's financial advisor introduced Michael P. Smith, Zogenix's CFO, to a Party A executive on June 4, 2021 to explore a potential EU and ex-U.S. partnership for Fintepla. Separately, on July 19, 2021, an SVB Leerink representative introduced Mr. Smith to David Flint, Head of M&A at UCB (Parent), to discuss a potential EU partnership for Fintepla. Both discussions originated as partnership rather than acquisition conversations. Zogenix entered confidentiality agreements with Party A (July 21, 2021) and UCB (August 25, 2021); neither CDA included a standstill provision. Virtual data rooms were opened for both parties.

On August 3, 2021, a financial advisor to Party B introduced Dr. Farr to a Party B executive regarding potential U.S. partnering or acquisition. On August 11, 2021, Party B executives called Dr. Farr and Mr. Smith expressing interest in U.S. partnership or full company acquisition and stated Party B was beginning an assessment. Dr. Farr responded that the Company was not interested in U.S. partnership opportunities given its own commercialization capabilities and was also not interested in a sale.

**Financial Advisor Engagements and Board Committee Formation (July–September 2021)**

Zogenix engaged BofA Securities on July 27, 2021 pursuant to an engagement letter for a potential strategic transaction. SVB Leerink was engaged on September 9, 2021 as an additional financial advisor. At its September 15, 2021 regularly scheduled board meeting, the board formed a Transactions Committee (initially called Pricing Committee) consisting of Louis C. Bock, Cam L. Garner, Caroline M. Loewy, Erle T. Mast, Renee P. Tannenbaum, and Mark Wiggins, to oversee financing and strategic opportunities. BofA Securities presented an overview of the biotech capital markets and recent M&A activity.

**UCB's Initial and Revised Proposals (September–October 2021)**

On September 23, 2021, Mr. Flint, Anna Lisa Picciolo-Lehrke (Head of Global Business Development Neurology, UCB), and other UCB representatives called Mr. Smith to announce that UCB would be making a non-binding proposal. Later the same day, Charl van Zyl (Executive Vice President Neurology & Head of Europe/International Markets, UCB) called Dr. Farr to orally convey a non-binding offer at $20.50 per share in upfront cash. Dr. Farr indicated the Company was not pursuing a sale and was focused on an EU partnership. UCB submitted this offer in writing the same day. The Transactions Committee met on September 28, 2021 and directed management to communicate the price was materially inadequate. On September 29, 2021, Dr. Farr informed Mr. van Zyl the offer was materially inadequate and not an attractive enough starting point; the Company would only consider granting further diligence access with a materially higher offer.

On October 6, 2021, Mr. van Zyl and Mr. Flint called Dr. Farr and Mr. Smith and UCB submitted a revised offer at $23.00 per share in upfront cash. Mr. Smith simultaneously called Party A to inform them of another party's acquisition interest. On October 11, 2021, a Party B executive emailed Dr. Farr to state Party B's assessment was still actively underway. The Transactions Committee met on October 12, 2021; BofA Securities and SVB Leerink provided preliminary financial analysis; the committee directed management to communicate $23.00 was still inadequate. On October 13, 2021, Dr. Farr and Mr. Smith called Mr. van Zyl and Mr. Flint to convey the rejection.

On October 25, 2021, Mr. van Zyl called Dr. Farr to note UCB remained interested and that Dr. Jean-Christophe Tellier, UCB's CEO, would be reaching out to Cam Garner (Board Chair) to discuss an offer structure involving upfront cash and CVRs and to indicate the importance of additional diligence access. On October 26, 2021, Dr. Tellier called Mr. Garner, expressed the view that there was a strong strategic fit, and requested limited additional diligence to prepare a new offer. Mr. Garner agreed further diligence was acceptable if necessary for an improved offer.

**Diligence and Party A's Entry into Acquisition Discussions (October–November 2021)**

UCB provided high-priority diligence questions to the Company on October 28, 2021. On November 2, 2021, Mr. Flint and Mr. Smith organized a deeper virtual data room and upcoming calls. Throughout November 2021, BofA Securities and SVB Leerink held update calls with UCB's financial advisors Lazard and Barclays on the overall process and timeline. The Company conducted due diligence calls with UCB to discuss high-priority questions.

On November 5, 2021, a Party B executive emailed Dr. Farr to state that Party B had paused its assessment due to prioritization of other company initiatives. Party B withdrew from the process entirely at this point.

On November 18, 2021, Party A's CEO called Dr. Farr and stated Party A and its board viewed the companies as having a good strategic fit and Party A was actively conducting diligence in preparation of submitting a non-binding offer to acquire the Company.

**Competing Offers and Intensified Negotiations (December 2021)**

On December 3, 2021, UCB submitted an updated non-binding offer with a maximum value of $27.00 per share: $23.00 upfront cash, a $2.00 CVR if Fintepla retains orphan drug designation at EU approval for LGS by April 30, 2023, and an additional $2.00 CVR if aggregate global annual net Fintepla sales excluding Japan were at least $700 million in calendar year 2026. UCB also proposed a draft exclusivity letter through December 31, 2021. A call followed among Mr. Smith, Mr. Flint, Loïc Hameon (Head of Business Development, M&A and Alliance Management, UCB), and representatives of BofA Securities, SVB Leerink, Lazard, and Barclays.

On December 7, 2021, Party A submitted a non-binding offer at $21.50 per share in all upfront cash. The Transactions Committee met December 8, 2021 to review both offers; Latham reviewed board fiduciary duties and directed management to seek improved offers from both parties. The full board met December 9, 2021 and Mr. Garner updated the board on the committee's deliberations. Dr. Farr separately called Party A's CEO that day to inform him Party A's terms were materially inferior to the other bid.

On December 10, 2021, at the direction of the board, BofA Securities and SVB Leerink informed UCB's advisors that the Company sought $25.00 per share upfront plus a $2.00 CVR with no time limitation on EU approval for LGS. On December 13, 2021, Party A submitted a revised offer at a maximum value of $25.00 per share: $23.00 upfront cash, a $1.00 CVR on 2022 Fintepla net sales ≥ $180 million, and a $1.00 CVR on 2023 Fintepla net sales ≥ $310 million. That same day, UCB previewed and then submitted on December 14 a revised offer providing two options: Option 1 at $25.00 per share all upfront cash; Option 2 at $24.00 upfront with a $2.00 CVR on Fintepla orphan drug designation at EU approval for LGS by December 31, 2023. UCB proposed four-week exclusivity.

The Transactions Committee met December 14, 2021 and directed management to continue seeking improved offers. On December 15, 2021, Dr. Farr called Dr. Tellier and Mr. van Zyl requesting UCB increase the offer to $26.00 upfront. Lazard and Barclays called BofA Securities and SVB Leerink; UCB's advisors acknowledged the request but expressed disagreement on valuation. The Transactions Committee met December 16 and 17 to discuss ongoing negotiations, financing capabilities of both parties, antitrust considerations, and potential signing timelines. On December 17, Party A provided draft commitment papers from two financing sources indicating the ability to finance the acquisition.

On December 18, 2021, Party A submitted a further revised offer: maximum value $27.00 per share ($25.00 upfront + $1.00 CVR on 2022 Fintepla net sales ≥ $166 million + $1.00 CVR on 2023 Fintepla net sales ≥ $300 million). On December 19, Party A provided a draft exclusivity letter through January 19, 2022. That same day, BofA Securities and SVB Leerink informed UCB's advisors that the Company had received a more attractive offer and was moving forward with another party.

On December 20, 2021, UCB submitted its final non-binding offer: maximum value $28.00 per share ($26.00 upfront cash + $2.00 CVR if Fintepla retains orphan drug designation at EU approval for LGS by December 31, 2023), proposing four-week exclusivity with expiration by close of business December 21 if not accepted. The Transactions Committee met December 20 and determined UCB's offer was superior to Party A's. UCB's outside counsel Covington & Burling provided draft Merger Agreement and CVR Agreement to Latham and a BofA Securities representative the same day. An introductory call among UCB and Company representatives addressed definitive agreements and antitrust approvals. Party A was informed the Company had received a superior offer. On December 21, Party A and its financial advisor informed the Company that Party A would not be making another improved offer.

**Exclusivity and Definitive Agreement Finalization (December 22, 2021 – January 18, 2022)**

On December 22, 2021, Dr. Farr called Dr. Tellier and confirmed confirmatory diligence was the remaining item for UCB. Dr. Farr stated the Company planned to add an obligation for UCB to use commercially reasonable efforts to achieve the CVR milestone, and proposed exclusivity ending January 9; Dr. Tellier indicated the need for up to four weeks given the holiday period. The Transactions Committee met the same day, determined a broader market check was unlikely to improve terms and could jeopardize existing offers, and authorized execution of the exclusivity agreement with UCB (ending January 19, 2022). The exclusivity agreement was executed on December 22, 2021.

During the exclusivity period, UCB submitted numerous confirmatory diligence requests. The Company opened a more comprehensive virtual data room and organized multiple diligence calls. Latham provided a revised Merger Agreement on December 30, 2021 proposing UCB's obligation to litigate or accept antitrust remedies, a 5.0% reverse termination fee, and a 2.5% company termination fee; also provided a revised CVR Agreement requiring UCB's commercially reasonable efforts toward the milestone. Covington responded on January 6, 2022 with a further revision removing UCB's antitrust obligation to litigate and the reverse termination fee, and proposing reversion to a 3.5% company termination fee; the CVR revision accepted commercially reasonable efforts subject to specified exceptions.

On January 10, 2022, Covington and Latham discussed remaining open issues including the company termination fee. On January 11, 2022, Mr. van Zyl, Mr. Flint, Dr. Farr, and Mr. Smith held a conference call; key terms were agreed: company termination fee of 3.25% of upfront equity value (approximately $59 million), UCB not obligated to litigate or accept antitrust remedies, and UCB to reimburse certain external Company advisor fees up to $5.0 million aggregate if the transaction did not close due to antitrust failure. On January 12, 2022, Latham provided revised drafts reflecting those terms; Covington and Latham finalized remaining issues over the ensuing days.

The board met January 14, 2022 (financial analyses and Merger Agreement presentation) and January 18, 2022 (final deliberations). On January 18, BofA Securities delivered its oral fairness opinion (confirmed in writing the same date), and SVB Leerink delivered its oral fairness opinion (confirmed in writing the same date). Both opined the consideration was fair from a financial point of view to holders of Zogenix common stock (other than holders of excluded shares). The board unanimously approved the transaction on January 18, 2022. The Merger Agreement was executed on the evening of January 18, and a joint press release was issued before market open on January 19, 2022.

**Completion**

The tender offer, which commenced February 1, 2022, expired March 4, 2022 with 37,646,050 shares (approximately 66.9%) validly tendered. Merger Sub accepted shares and the Section 251(h) merger was effected on March 7, 2022. The First Supplemental Indenture to the Company's 2.75% Convertible Senior Notes due 2027 was executed on March 7, 2022.

## Pipeline

Zogenix's pipeline described in the SC 14D9 filing included its commercial product Fintepla (fenfluramine oral solution), one clinical-stage product candidate (MT-1621), and a preclinical gene therapy collaboration with Tevard Biosciences. The filing notes financial projections were risk-adjusted based on probability of regulatory approvals of Fintepla in additional indications and MT-1621. The CVR milestone was specifically tied to Fintepla's EU orphan drug designation status at time of LGS approval.

## Rationale

The SC 14D9 states that the Company Board concluded that entering into the Merger Agreement with UCB and Zinc Merger Sub would yield the highest value reasonably available to the Company's stockholders and would be fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company's stockholders.

The Board cited the following material factors for its recommendation, as stated in the filing (accession 0001193125-22-024104):

**Premium to Market Price.** The upfront offer price of $26.00 per share represented a premium of approximately 66% over the January 18, 2022 close of $15.64 (the last trading day prior to announcement) and approximately 72% over the 30-day volume-weighted average closing price prior to signing. The Board reviewed historical trading range and volatility of the shares, noting the 52-week range of $11.25 to $23.43 as of January 18, 2022.

**Certainty of Value.** The consideration was almost entirely upfront cash, providing liquidity and certainty to stockholders. Taking into account risks of remaining independent and pursuing current business and financial plans, the Board believed this certainty was compelling relative to the expected long-term standalone value creation potential.

**Opportunity to Realize Additional Value.** Each stockholder would also receive one CVR per share providing an opportunity to receive an additional $2.00 per share cash payment if the specified milestone was achieved by December 31, 2023.

**The Company's Operating and Financial Condition; Prospects.** The Board evaluated the Company's current and historical financial condition, commercial product performance, and development pipeline. The Board considered: risks and uncertainties associated with Fintepla U.S. and European net sales levels; marketing approval of Fintepla for Lennox-Gastaut syndrome; continued clinical development of product candidates; and the significant capital required to support commercialization and product development efforts.

**Potential Strategic Alternatives.** The Board considered alternatives including continuing as an independent company and exploring additional licensing and collaboration opportunities. The Board determined that none of the possible alternatives was reasonably likely to present superior opportunities to create greater value for stockholders.

**Highest Value Reasonably Obtainable.** The Board believed that $26.00 per share plus one CVR represented the highest value reasonably obtainable, based on the progress and outcome of negotiations with UCB, favorable changes in the Merger Agreement relative to UCB's initial drafts, and the fact that Party A did not propose a higher valuation and withdrew from the process.

**Speed and Likelihood of Completion.** The Board noted the structure as a cash tender offer allowing stockholders to receive consideration in a relatively short time frame. UCB represented it would fund the purchase price from existing cash resources and other existing debt sources, without requiring additional financing.

**Business Reputation of Parent.** The Board considered UCB's business reputation, management, and financial resources.

**Financial Advisor Opinions.** BofA Securities and SVB Leerink each delivered written fairness opinions dated January 18, 2022 to the effect that the consideration was fair, from a financial point of view, to holders of Zogenix common stock (other than holders of excluded shares).

The Board also identified risks, including: stockholders would not participate in future earnings beyond any CVR payments; the possible failure to complete the Offer and Merger; interim business restrictions pending closing; no-solicitation provisions and the $59 million termination fee; the risk that the CVR milestone might not be achieved by December 31, 2023; effects of the transaction announcement on ongoing business relationships and key personnel; timing risks from regulatory approvals; and the taxable nature of the consideration.

The filing states that the transaction originated in UCB's interest in acquiring European rights to Fintepla, which was commercially launched in Germany in February 2021 and had received full EC approval for Dravet syndrome in December 2020. The pending LGS expansion (sNDA under FDA priority review; EMA variation application filed December 2021) was both a standalone value driver and the basis for the CVR milestone, which was tied specifically to Fintepla retaining EU orphan drug designation at the time of its EU approval for LGS.

## Decision Notes

Report accepted. This is a completed tender offer acquisition. All completion evidence (66.86% tender rate, March 7 merger effective date) sourced to 8-K filed 2022-03-07. The SC 14D9 filed 2022-02-01 (accession 0001193125-22-024104) contains a detailed background section with named parties, specific dates, and documented negotiation events. Two financial advisors (BofA Securities and SVB Leerink) provided fairness opinions; two outside counsel firms (Latham & Watkins for the Company; Covington & Burling for UCB) negotiated the definitive documents. Pipeline classification covers Fintepla (approved small molecule, primary driver), the LGS expansion (pending regulatory applications at time of filing), MT-1621 (TK2 deficiency; not further staged by the filing), and the Tevard preclinical gene therapy collaboration. The EODHD historical market cap API returned 403 for ZGNX.US; market cap in the stock price table was computed from close price × SEC-sourced shares outstanding using the most recent observation available as of each price week. Party A and Party B identities are not disclosed in the filing; both are referred to by letter only.
