## Summary

Theseus Pharmaceuticals entered into a definitive merger agreement with Concentra Biosciences, LLC, an affiliate of Tang Capital Partners, to be acquired for a base cash price of $3.90 to $4.05 per share (finalized at $4.05) plus a contingent value right. The deal followed Theseus's decision to discontinue its lead program THE-630 and a subsequent strategic review that explored reverse mergers before ultimately pursuing a cash-out transaction and liquidation-style distribution.

## Interaction History

Following the failure of its lead asset THE-630 in July 2023, Theseus management and Leerink Partners contacted 71 parties. Initial focus was on reverse mergers, but market conditions led the board to pivot to a cash-out or liquidation strategy in November 2023. Concentra (led by Kevin Tang) emerged as a primary bidder alongside several private biopharma companies (Parties C, E, F) and existing major stockholders (Parties A and B). Negotiations were handled by a Special Committee to manage conflicts with affiliated stockholders. Parent's offer was favored due to their experience in rapid wind-downs and higher probability of execution. Final price negotiations between Theseus CFO Bradford Dahms and Kevin Tang increased the base price from $3.80 to $3.90, with a mechanism to pay up to $4.05 based on closing net cash. The final cash amount was confirmed at $4.05 per share upon the offer's expiration.

## Rationale

The Theseus Board determined that the transaction provided a substantial premium (82% over the pre-announcement price) and certain value compared to the risks of remaining independent or pursuing a liquidation. Management's dissolution analysis estimated a present value of $3.76 per share in a liquidation scenario, which was lower than the $3.90-$4.05 cash offer. The CVR provided additional upside for stockholders from any future asset sales.

    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Concentra Biosciences, LLC (controlled by Tang Capital Partners)

    *   **Details:** Theseus received an unsolicited proposal from Concentra Biosciences to acquire all outstanding shares. The CVR was specified as representing 80% of net proceeds from the license or disposition of Theseus’ programs.

    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Foresite Capital, LLC and OrbiMed Advisors LLC (Joint Proposal)
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified (Expression of Interest)
    *   **Details:** Theseus confirmed receipt of an unsolicited "expression of interest" from its two largest shareholders, OrbiMed (40.1% owner) and Foresite Capital (12.6% owner), to explore a transaction to take the company private.
