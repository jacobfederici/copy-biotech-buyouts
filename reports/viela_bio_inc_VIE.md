## Summary

Viela Bio, Inc. was acquired by Horizon Therapeutics plc for $53.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $3.05 billion. The transaction followed a strategic review process (Project Zenith) where Viela explored partnering and sale opportunities. The deal closed via a Section 251(h) merger on March 15, 2021, following the successful tender of approximately 94% of Viela's outstanding shares.

## Timeline

- 2020-07-02: Introductory call between Viela CEO Bing Yao and Horizon CEO Tim Walbert regarding potential collaboration.
- 2020-07-15: Viela and Horizon enter into a Confidentiality Agreement.
- 2020-09-18: Viela Board meets with Goldman Sachs to discuss the biotech landscape and partnering strategies.

- 2021-01-31: Viela Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement at $53.00 per share.
- 2021-02-01: Public announcement of the transaction.
- 2021-02-12: Commencement of the tender offer.
- 2021-03-12: Tender offer expires at midnight.
- 2021-03-15: Merger completed; Viela becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Horizon.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in July 2020 as collaboration talks but shifted to acquisition interest by October 2020. Horizon's initial bid of $44.00 (Oct 29) was rejected by the Viela Board as substantially undervaluing the company. Viela's financial advisor, Goldman Sachs, conducted 'Project Zenith,' reaching out to eight global pharmaceutical companies. While five companies engaged in management presentations, none submitted acquisition proposals. Horizon increased its bid to $49.50 (Nov 12) and finally to $53.00 (Nov 16) after Viela signaled a requirement for a price closer to $55.00. Discussions were briefly halted in December 2020 due to Horizon's TEPEZZA supply issues but resumed in mid-January 2021. The Viela Board reviewed the final terms on January 31, 2021, receiving a fairness opinion from Goldman Sachs and unanimously recommending the offer to stockholders.

## Rationale

The Viela Board determined the merger was the best alternative for maximizing stockholder value, considering the risks of remaining independent, including the need for substantial additional capital to fund commercialization and clinical trials, the uncertainty of drug pricing, and the speculative nature of clinical development. Horizon sought to expand its autoimmune and severe inflammatory disease portfolio with a commercial-stage asset (UPLIZNA) and a deep mid-to-late-stage pipeline.

## Evidence

- At a meeting of the Board held on January 31, 2021, the Board unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions are advisable and fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company and its stockholders (0001302628-21-000004).
- On March 15, 2021, Purchaser completed its acquisition of the Company pursuant to the terms of the Merger Agreement (0001193125-21-080639).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed as a cash tender offer followed by a 251(h) merger. Shares outstanding (54,904,033) were sourced from the SC 14D9 as of February 9, 2021. The $3.05 billion deal value includes the cash-out of vested options and the assumption of unvested options by Horizon.
