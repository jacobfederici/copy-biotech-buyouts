## Summary

Ignyta, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Roche Holdings, Inc. via a cash tender offer for $27.00 per share, representing a total equity value of approximately $1.9 billion. The transaction focused on Ignyta's lead asset, entrectinib, a CNS-active tyrosine kinase inhibitor for NTRK and ROS1 fusion-positive tumors. The deal was completed on February 8, 2018, following the successful tender of approximately 84.71% of outstanding shares.

## Timeline

- 2016-01-01: Ignyta initiates broad licensing process for entrectinib, contacting 25 pharmaceutical companies including Roche.
- 2016-05-17: Ignyta and Roche enter into a confidentiality agreement for licensing discussions.
- 2016-09-01: Roche informs Ignyta it will no longer seek to license entrectinib rights.
- 2017-11-13: Ignyta Board meets to discuss strategic options and decides to engage BofA Merrill Lynch to explore a potential sale.
- 2017-11-20: Roche contacts Ignyta on an unsolicited basis expressing renewed interest in entrectinib.
- 2017-12-06: Party A submits an initial non-binding proposal to acquire Ignyta for $23.00 per share.
- 2017-12-12: Party A increases its proposal to $23.75 per share.
- 2017-12-13: Party A submits a 'final' proposal of $25.00 per share with a December 21 signing deadline.
- 2017-12-17: Roche submits a verbal indication of interest at $27.00 per share.
- 2017-12-21: Ignyta Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement with Roche at $27.00 per share.
- 2017-12-22: Public announcement of the Merger Agreement.
- 2018-01-10: Commencement of the tender offer.
- 2018-02-07: Tender offer expires at midnight.
- 2018-02-08: Merger completed; Ignyta becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Roche.

## Interaction History

Negotiations were characterized by a competitive process between Roche and an unnamed 'Party A'. From 2016 to late 2017, Ignyta focused on licensing entrectinib but shifted to a sale process in November 2017. Party A made three successive bids ($23.00, $23.75, and $25.00). Roche, which had previously passed on a license in 2016, re-entered the process in November 2017. On December 17, Roche's Head of Partnering proposed $27.00 per share. Ignyta's CEO, Jonathan Lim, pushed Roche to accelerate their timeline to match Party A's December 21 deadline. Roche's CEO confirmed board support on December 20. Final negotiations on December 21 resolved open issues regarding representations, warranties, and a $58 million termination fee. The Board approved the Roche offer as superior to Party A's in both price and contractual certainty.
