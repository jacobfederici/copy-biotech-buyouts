## Summary

Elevation Oncology entered into a definitive merger agreement with Concentra Biosciences, LLC, an affiliate of Tang Capital Management, to be acquired for $0.36 per share in cash plus a contingent value right. The transaction followed Elevation's decision to discontinue its lead program, EO-3021, and a subsequent strategic review process that evaluated 23 potential parties. The deal was structured as a tender offer under Section 251(h) of the DGCL and closed on July 23, 2025, after approximately 67.09% of shares were tendered.

## Interaction History

Following the discontinuation of lead asset EO-3021 in March 2025, Elevation's Board formed a Transaction Committee (Carruthers, Clackson, Elms) to evaluate strategic alternatives. Management engaged with 23 parties, leading to 9 active discussions. Party A initially proposed a $23M cash deal on April 7. On April 23, Kevin Tang of Concentra (an existing investor) proposed $0.37/share. Throughout May, Elevation received competing bids from Party B (net cash minus $3.75M) and Party C (96.5% of net cash). The Transaction Committee favored Concentra due to their extensive history of closing similar 'cash-shell' acquisitions and their advanced due diligence. Negotiations focused on the CVR duration and the 'Closing Net Cash' floor. On June 8, Redwood Valuation Partners provided a fairness opinion, and the Board approved the $0.36/share + CVR offer, which included a $26.4M minimum net cash condition.

## Rationale

The Elevation Board determined the merger was the best path to maximize value given the discontinuation of its lead clinical program and the risks associated with a standalone liquidation. The Board noted that the offer provided immediate liquidity at a premium to the market price, while the CVR offered potential upside from asset monetization and excess cash. Management's dissolution analysis suggested a liquidation might only yield $0.34 per share (present value), making the $0.36 upfront offer more favorable.

## Evidence
