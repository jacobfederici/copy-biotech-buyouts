## Summary

Allakos Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Concentra Biosciences, LLC on April 1, 2025, following a strategic review process triggered by the failure of its lead clinical program. Concentra acquired Allakos through a cash tender offer of $0.33 per share, representing a 50% premium to the closing price prior to the announcement. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a back-end merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, with a closing condition requiring a minimum net cash balance of $35.5 million.

## Interaction History

Following the failure of AK006 in January 2025, Allakos management, led by CEO Robert Alexander and the Transaction Committee, contacted 42 potential parties. 40 parties expressed interest in reverse mergers (including Party B, C, and D), while Parent (Concentra) and Party A pursued cash acquisitions. Negotiations focused on the 'Net Cash' closing condition and the speed of execution. Party C and Party B's reverse merger proposals were deemed risky due to their need for PIPE financing and lack of public company readiness. Party A offered $0.33 per share but required 30 days of exclusivity for diligence. Parent (Concentra), led by Kevin Tang, offered $0.33 per share with substantially completed diligence. The Board prioritized Parent's offer to maximize certainty and minimize cash burn during the closing period. Final negotiations between Wilson Sonsini (Allakos counsel) and Gibson Dunn (Parent counsel) resolved the 'Closing Net Cash' definition and termination fees ($1.2M).

## Rationale

The Allakos Board determined that a cash sale to Concentra provided the best value for stockholders compared to a standalone liquidation or a risky reverse merger. A liquidation analysis estimated a present value of $0.22 to $0.27 per share, significantly lower than the $0.33 offer. Reverse merger options were rejected due to the high uncertainty of PIPE financing and the speculative nature of the counterparties' clinical pipelines.

## Evidence

- On May 15, 2025, Merger Sub completed a tender offer to purchase all of the Company’s outstanding shares... in exchange for $0.33 in cash per Share (0001140361-25-019156).
- The Allakos Board recommends that the Company’s stockholders accept the Offer and tender their Shares pursuant to the Offer (0001193125-25-088441).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a Section 251(h) merger on May 15, 2025. Shares outstanding (90,376,972) were sourced from the SC 14D9 Item 1 'Securities' section as of April 1, 2025. No CVRs were included in the final Parent agreement, despite being discussed in the Party A proposal.
