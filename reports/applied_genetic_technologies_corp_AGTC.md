## Summary

Applied Genetic Technologies Corporation (AGTC) was acquired by Syncona Limited through a tender offer for $0.34 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $50 million in the aggregate (approximately $0.73 per share). The deal was driven by AGTC's critical liquidity shortfall, which threatened a bankruptcy filing by the end of 2022, and followed an extensive 24-month strategic review process involving 32 potential partners.

## Interaction History

Negotiations were led by AGTC CEO Susan Washer and Syncona CIO Chris Hollowood. AGTC conducted an exhaustive search for capital, contacting 32 companies between March and June 2022. Syncona initially proposed an asset purchase of the ocular programs for $20 million in July 2022. The AGTC Board rejected the asset-only structure, pushing for a whole-company acquisition to maximize value. Syncona revised its offer multiple times, eventually proposing a structure with $32.5 million upfront (later reduced to account for warrant liabilities) and $50 million in CVRs. A critical late-stage negotiation involved the 'Black Scholes Condition,' where Syncona required a termination right if the liability from AGTC's July 2022 warrants exceeded $9.5 million. AGTC's board accepted this, noting the company had engaged a restructuring expert and faced bankruptcy without the deal.

## Rationale

The AGTC Board recommended the deal because the company lacked sufficient cash to fund operations past the end of 2022. Despite positive clinical data, AGTC faced extreme difficulty raising equity capital in a deteriorating biotech market. The Board determined that the offer provided a 41.6% premium over the closing price and was superior to the likely alternative of a Chapter 11 bankruptcy liquidation, which would have resulted in little to no recovery for stockholders.
