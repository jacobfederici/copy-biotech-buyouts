## Summary

Hyperion Therapeutics entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Horizon Pharma plc for $46.00 per share in cash, representing a total fully diluted equity value of approximately $1.1 billion. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The acquisition significantly expanded Horizon's orphan disease business by adding RAVICTI and BUPHENYL, treatments for urea cycle disorders, to its portfolio.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2014 with unsolicited interest from Party A and Party B, both of which were rejected due to low valuation or unfavorable stock-for-stock structures. Horizon Pharma entered the process in February 2015. Following the settlement of Hyperion's Andromeda litigation on February 17, 2015, Horizon provided a formal range of $42.00-$45.00 on March 5. Hyperion's board, advised by Centerview Partners and Shearman & Sterling, conducted a targeted market check, contacting four additional strategic parties (Parties C and D). Party C withdrew due to valuation, and Party D withdrew after diligence. On March 27, Horizon offered $44.00. The Hyperion board countered, leading Horizon to submit a 'best and final' offer of $46.00 on March 28. The board met on March 29, receiving fairness opinions from Centerview and Houlihan Lokey, and subsequently approved the deal. The transaction closed on May 7, 2015, following the successful tender of 86% of outstanding shares.

## Rationale

The Hyperion board determined the cash offer provided immediate certainty of value and a significant premium over historical trading prices, mitigating risks associated with remaining independent, such as generic competition and Paragraph IV challenges. For Horizon, the deal expanded its orphan disease portfolio, leveraged existing infrastructure, and was expected to be immediately accretive to adjusted EPS and contribute $100 million in adjusted EBITDA by 2016.

## Evidence

- At a meeting of the Company Board held on March 29, 2015, the Company Board unanimously: (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated by the Merger Agreement, including the Offer and the Merger, are advisable, fair to and in the best interests of the Company and its stockholders (SC 14D9).

## Decision Notes

The deal value is stated as $1.1 billion on a fully diluted basis in the announcement. Shares outstanding (20,874,966) and equity awards (3,108,274 options and 386,570 RSUs) as of April 1, 2015, were used for valuation context. The merger was completed under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, obviating the need for a stockholder vote.
