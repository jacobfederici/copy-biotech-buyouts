## Summary

AstraZeneca acquired CinCor Pharma for $26.00 per share in cash plus a contingent value right of $10.00 per share, totaling approximately $1.8 billion in transaction value. The deal focused on CinCor's lead asset, baxdrostat, a highly selective aldosterone synthase inhibitor for cardio-renal diseases, following mixed Phase 2 results in the BrigHtn and HALO trials.

## Timeline

- 2021-05-10: CinCor and AstraZeneca enter into a confidentiality agreement for licensing discussions.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2021 with licensing talks between CinCor CEO Marc de Garidel and AstraZeneca CEO Pascal Soriot. AstraZeneca made a pre-IPO bid in late 2021 that was rejected. Following positive BrigHtn data in August 2022, AstraZeneca bid $54.00/share, eventually rising to $60.00 plus a CVR. However, AstraZeneca insisted on seeing HALO trial data before signing. After the HALO trial failed to meet its primary endpoint in November 2022, the stock price collapsed. AstraZeneca returned with a significantly lower bid of $22.00 upfront. CinCor's board, advised by Centerview Partners and Cooley LLP, pushed for $30.00 upfront but eventually settled for $26.00 cash plus a $10.00 CVR after AstraZeneca indicated it would not go higher. Centerview contacted other potential buyers (Company A and E), but none showed interest post-HALO failure. The final agreement was signed on January 8, 2023.

## Rationale

The CinCor board determined the deal was superior to standalone prospects given the risks of future clinical trials after the HALO failure and the difficulty of raising capital in a challenging macroeconomic environment. AstraZeneca sought to strengthen its cardiorenal pipeline and leverage baxdrostat's potential synergy with its existing drug, Farxiga.

## Evidence

- On January 8, 2023, the Board unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions are advisable and fair to, and in the best interest of, CinCor and its stockholders (0001193125-23-012959).
- Purchaser irrevocably accepted for payment, on February 24, 2023, all Shares that were validly tendered (0001193125-23-047746).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a Section 251(h) merger following a successful tender offer. The CVR milestone is tied to the submission of an NDA to the FDA or an MAA to the EMA for baxdrostat by December 31, 2033. Shares outstanding based on the SC 14D9 filing dated January 17, 2023.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The acquisition of CinCor Pharma, Inc. (CINC) by AstraZeneca was announced on **January 9, 2023**. Based on a review of financial news and subsequent regulatory filings, there were no widely reported public rumors or specific media leaks regarding a buyout prior to the official announcement.

While the deal was a surprise to the public market, subsequent SEC filings (Schedule 14D-9) revealed a long history of private negotiations that did not leak to the press at the time.

### **Chronology of Private Speculation and Negotiations (Revealed Post-Deal)**
The following events occurred before the deal was announced but were only made public through regulatory disclosures after the January 9, 2023, announcement:

*   **May 2021**
    *   **Reporting Source:** SEC Schedule 14D-9 (filed Jan 2023).
    *   **Involved Party:** AstraZeneca.
    *   **Details:** AstraZeneca initially approached CinCor to discuss a potential licensing deal for its lead asset, baxdrostat. These discussions did not lead to a deal at that time.
*   **December 21, 2021**
    *   **Reporting Source:** SEC Schedule 14D-9 (filed Jan 2023).
    *   **Involved Party:** AstraZeneca.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $700 million upfront plus $400 million in milestones.
    *   **Details:** AstraZeneca submitted a private non-binding proposal to acquire CinCor. The offer was rejected by CinCor’s board as undervaluing the company.
*   **August 2022**
    *   **Reporting Source:** SEC Schedule 14D-9 (filed Jan 2023).
    *   **Involved Party:** AstraZeneca.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $54.00 per share.
    *   **Details:** AstraZeneca CEO Pascal Soriot privately expressed strong interest in an acquisition and eventually proposed a price of $54.00 per share (a 53% premium at the time). Negotiations continued through October 2022 but were paused pending clinical trial data.
*   **November 29, 2022**
    *   **Reporting Source:** SEC Schedule 14D-9 (filed Jan 2023).
    *   **Involved Party:** AstraZeneca.
    *   **Details:** Following a significant drop in CinCor's stock price due to mixed Phase 2 data (HALO trial), AstraZeneca re-engaged privately to discuss a lower acquisition price.

### **Public Rumors**
**No pre-deal public rumors exist.** 
The acquisition was considered a "stealth" deal by market analysts. There were no reports from major outlets such as Bloomberg, Reuters, or specialized M&A rumor sites like Betaville prior to the January 9, 2023, press release. The stock's 121% surge occurred only after the official announcement was made.
