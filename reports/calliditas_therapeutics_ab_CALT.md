## Summary

Calliditas Therapeutics AB entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Asahi Kasei Corporation via a public tender offer for SEK 208 per common share and SEK 416 per ADS in cash. The transaction, which was not actively sought by Calliditas, followed multiple unsolicited proposals from Asahi Kasei and a competitive bidding process involving three other parties. The offer represents a significant premium to Calliditas' historical trading prices and is supported by irrevocable undertakings from shareholders representing approximately 44.65% of the company's outstanding shares.

## Interaction History

The transaction originated from an unsolicited approach by Asahi Kasei CEO Mark Hensley in April 2023. After several rejections and a subsequent approach in early 2024, Calliditas CEO Renée Aguiar-Lucander and the Board engaged Lazard to conduct a targeted outreach to ten potential strategic bidders. This process resulted in non-disclosure agreements with Party B and Party C, while Party A re-engaged independently. Bidding progressed through May 2024, with Asahi Kasei consistently offering the highest cash consideration without CVR contingencies. Negotiations culminated in Asahi Kasei's 'best and final' offer of SEK 208 per share on May 10, 2024. To secure the deal, Asahi Kasei and Lazard obtained irrevocable undertakings from major shareholders, including BVF Partners and Linc AB. Regulatory clearances were pursued concurrently, with Swedish FDI approval received on July 2, 2024, and the U.S. HSR waiting period expiring on June 28, 2024.
