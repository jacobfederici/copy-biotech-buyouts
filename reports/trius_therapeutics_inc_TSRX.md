## Summary

Trius Therapeutics entered into a definitive merger agreement with Cubist Pharmaceuticals for an upfront cash payment of $13.50 per share plus a non-transferable contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $2.00 per share based on 2016 sales milestones for tedizolid phosphate. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a short-form merger, valuing the upfront portion of the deal at approximately $658 million.

## Timeline

- 2012-07-24: Cubist and Trius enter into a Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement for partnering discussions.
- 2013-03-07: Trius Board forms an informal strategic transaction committee to oversee European collaboration and US co-promotion efforts.
- 2013-06-06: Cubist President Robert Perez contacts Trius CEO Jeffrey Stein with an unsolicited interest to acquire the company for $12.25 per share in cash.
- 2013-06-07: Cubist submits formal non-binding proposal at $12.25 per share.
- 2013-06-08: Company D expresses unsolicited interest in an all-stock combination.
- 2013-06-24: Trius Board formally authorizes a sale process and engages Centerview and Citi as financial advisors.
- 2013-07-18: Company C submits a non-binding proposal for $13.00-$14.50 per share in cash.
- 2013-07-23: Cubist attempts to preempt the final bid process, requesting to execute a merger agreement before the August 9 deadline.
- 2013-07-26: Cubist increases offer to $13.25 cash plus $2.00 CVR; Trius counter-proposes $13.50 cash and lower CVR thresholds.
- 2013-07-30: Trius Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement at $13.50 cash plus $2.00 CVR.
- 2013-08-13: Purchaser commences the tender offer.
- 2013-09-11: Tender offer expires; Purchaser exercises Top-Up Option and completes short-form merger.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began after Cubist submitted an unsolicited $12.25 per share bid in June 2013. Trius, led by CEO Jeffrey Stein and advised by Centerview and Citi, contacted 12 other pharmaceutical companies. Company C proposed $13.00-$14.50 but later indicated it could not meet the timeline. Company D proposed a stock deal but failed to provide specific valuation updates. Cubist, represented by Robert Perez and Michael Bonney, aggressively sought to preempt the process in late July. After several rounds of price and CVR threshold negotiations (moving from $12.25 to $13.25 and finally $13.50 upfront), the parties agreed on a CVR structure paying $1.00 at $125M in 2016 sales plus $0.10 per $1M above that, capped at $2.00. Legal counsel Cooley (for Trius) and Ropes & Gray (for Cubist) finalized the merger agreement on July 30, 2013.

## Rationale

The Trius Board determined the deal provided immediate liquidity and certain value at a significant premium (30% over the prior day's close). They noted the risks of remaining independent, including the need to raise $80M in capital and the execution risks of building a commercial infrastructure for tedizolid phosphate. Cubist's existing hospital-based sales force was viewed as an ideal platform to maximize the value of the lead asset.

## Evidence

- On July 30, 2013, our Board of Directors unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions, including the Offer and the Merger, are advisable to, and in the best interest of, Trius and its stockholders (0001193125-13-332369).
- On September 11, 2013, Parent announced the completion of the Offer... Purchaser effected a short-form merger of Purchaser with and into Trius (0001104659-13-069285).
