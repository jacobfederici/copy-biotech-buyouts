## Summary

Chembio Diagnostics entered into a definitive merger agreement with Biosynex SA for an all-cash tender offer at $0.45 per share. The transaction followed a period of significant financial distress for Chembio, including a revoked EUA for its COVID-19 test, ongoing securities litigation, and a lack of alternative financing options. The deal was structured as a two-step transaction under Nevada law (NRS 92A.133), allowing for a merger without a stockholder vote following the successful completion of the tender offer.

## Interaction History

Following the FDA revocation of its COVID-19 EUA in 2020, Chembio faced severe liquidity issues. In April 2022, the board engaged Craig-Hallum to explore a sale (Project Cheetah), contacting 80 potential acquirers. Biosynex initially expressed interest only in the German subsidiary but later re-engaged for the whole company. Negotiations with 'Party A' occurred in late 2022 but stalled over 'Party A's' demand for complex commercial arrangements and price reductions for litigation settlements. Biosynex proposed a cleaner two-step tender offer structure in December 2022. On January 27, 2023, the parties agreed on a $0.45 per share price. The Chembio board, led by CEO Richard Eberly and CFO Lawrence Steenvoorden, unanimously approved the deal on January 31, 2023, after receiving a fairness opinion from Craig-Hallum.

The Chembio board recommended the deal due to the company's precarious financial position, including substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern. The board noted that the company's lender, Perceptive, was unwilling to restructure debt maturing in September 2023, and alternative equity financing attempts had failed. The $0.45 cash price provided immediate liquidity and a premium over recent trading prices, which were depressed by delisting risks and litigation.
