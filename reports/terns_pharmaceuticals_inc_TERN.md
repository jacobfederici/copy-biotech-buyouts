## Summary

Terns Pharmaceuticals, Inc. was acquired by Merck Sharp & Dohme LLC, a subsidiary of Merck & Co., Inc., for $53.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer followed by a second-step merger. The transaction, valued at approximately $6.12 billion, was primarily driven by Merck's interest in Terns' lead oncology asset, TERN-701, an allosteric BCR::ABL1 inhibitor for chronic myeloid leukemia.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began following a $58.00 unsolicited bid from Party C in December 2025. Terns formed a Transaction Committee (David Fellows, Jeffrey Kindler, Heather Turner) and engaged Centerview and Jefferies. Party C later increased its bid to $61.00 plus a CVR but withdrew on February 16, 2026, citing concerns over TERN-701 data nuances. Merck entered the process in late January 2026, initially proposing $61.00 on February 5. However, after extensive due diligence and review of updated CARDINAL trial data, Merck lowered its offer to $50.00 on March 18, 2026. Terns countered at $56.00. Merck moved to $52.00 and finally to $53.00, which it characterized as its 'best and final' offer. The Terns Board accepted the $53.00 price on March 19, 2026, noting it represented a significant premium over unaffected trading levels and addressed the risks of remaining standalone.

## Rationale

The Terns Board recommended the deal because it provided immediate and certain cash value at a substantial premium (47% over the price prior to Merck's first proposal). The Board considered the significant risks and long timeline (3+ years) associated with standalone clinical development and commercialization of TERN-701. Merck sought the acquisition to bolster its oncology pipeline with a potentially best-in-disease CML therapy.

- On March 24, 2026, the Board unanimously... resolved to recommend that the stockholders of Terns accept the Offer and tender their Shares to Purchaser (SC 14D9).
- On May 5, 2026, following the Offer Acceptance Time, Purchaser merged with and into Terns... with Terns continuing as the surviving corporation (8-K, 2026-05-05).
