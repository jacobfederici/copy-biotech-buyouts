## Summary

Gilead Sciences acquired Pharmasset for $137.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $11.2 billion. The acquisition focused on Pharmasset's industry-leading hepatitis C virus (HCV) pipeline, specifically the nucleotide analog PSI-7977, to bolster Gilead's antiviral portfolio.

## Timeline

- 2011-06-20: Pharmasset CEO P. Schaefer Price meets Gilead CEO John C. Martin to discuss HCV landscape.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in June 2011 with informal meetings between CEOs P. Schaefer Price (Pharmasset) and John Martin (Gilead). Gilead issued a formal $100/share bid on Sept 2, 2011. Pharmasset's board, advised by Morgan Stanley and Sullivan & Cromwell, rejected this as insufficient but allowed due diligence. On Oct 7, Gilead raised its bid to $125/share. Pharmasset then initiated a competitive process, contacting Party A, B, C, and D. Party D declined immediately; Party B refused the confidentiality terms. Following the AASLD conference in early November, where Pharmasset presented strong data for PSI-7977, Gilead raised its bid to $135 on Nov 17. Parties A and C dropped out. On Nov 20, after intense negotiations over the termination fee (reduced from 4% to 3%) and financing certainty, Gilead provided a final offer of $137/share. The board approved the deal that evening.

## Rationale

The Pharmasset Board recommended the deal due to the significant 89% premium over the unaffected share price, the certainty of all-cash consideration, and the risks associated with remaining an independent clinical-stage company. Gilead sought the acquisition to secure a market-leading position in the HCV space, specifically acquiring PSI-7977 which had the potential to become the backbone of future all-oral, interferon-free treatment regimens.

## Evidence

- At a meeting held on November 20, 2011, the Board unanimously: (i) determined that the terms of the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereby... are fair to and in the best interests of the Company... (iii) recommended that the holders of Shares accept the Offer (0001193125-11-332648)
- On January 17, 2012, Purchaser merged with and into Pharmasset, with Pharmasset continuing as the surviving corporation (0001193125-12-013972)

## Decision Notes

The deal value is often cited as $11.2B in media, though the equity value based on basic shares outstanding is ~$10.4B; the difference accounts for the cash-out of significant unvested options. Shares outstanding sourced from the SC 14D9 Item 1. No CVRs were included in this transaction.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

No pre-deal rumors exist. 

While Pharmasset Inc. (VRUS) was frequently cited by analysts as a logical acquisition target due to its promising Hepatitis C pipeline, there were no specific reports of an imminent buyout, rumored price, or identified suitor in the media prior to the official announcement on November 21, 2011. The $11 billion acquisition by Gilead Sciences was widely described by financial news outlets, including *Reuters*, *FierceBiotech*, and *Bloomberg*, as a "surprise" to the market. 

Subsequent regulatory filings and post-deal reporting revealed that Gilead had been in private discussions with Pharmasset for approximately two years, but these negotiations did not leak to the press as actionable rumors before the definitive agreement was signed.
