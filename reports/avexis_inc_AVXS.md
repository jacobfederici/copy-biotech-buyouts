## Summary

AveXis, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Novartis AG for $218.00 per share in cash, representing a total equity value of approximately $8.7 billion. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, aimed at accelerating Novartis's entry into the gene therapy space through AveXis's lead candidate AVXS-101 for spinal muscular atrophy.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest in early 2018 following AveXis's alignment with the FDA on BLA submission for AVXS-101. Novartis CEO Vasant Narasimhan and M&A Head Nigel Sheail contacted AveXis CEO Sean Nolan in February 2018, initially proposing $190.00 per share. AveXis also engaged with 'Company C', which offered $175.00 and later $185.00 per share but withdrew on April 3, 2018, citing discomfort with the valuation relative to the clinical stage. AveXis pushed Novartis for higher certainty on regulatory and clinical risks, leading to a 'reverse termination fee' structure. On March 30, Novartis raised its bid to $200.00. Following a competitive process where AveXis indicated another bidder was present, Novartis submitted a final bid of $218.00 on April 6, 2018. The deal included a provision to increase the price to $225.00 if the outside date was extended, though the deal closed before this was triggered.

## Rationale

The AveXis Board determined the cash offer provided immediate liquidity and certainty of value, mitigating the significant risks associated with FDA approval, commercial launch, and competition in the SMA market. For Novartis, the acquisition provided a leading gene therapy platform and a potential blockbuster treatment for SMA, aligning with their strategic focus on transformative medicines.

- On May 15, 2018... Merger Sub merged with and into the Company, with the Company surviving as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (0000950157-18-000538).

The acquisition of AveXis, Inc. (AVXS) by Novartis for $8.7 billion was announced on **April 9, 2018**. While the deal itself was a significant event in the biotech sector, there were relatively few specific, named-source rumors with price targets published in major outlets immediately preceding the announcement. Most speculation was generalized within the context of a "gene therapy M&A wave" following the 2017 approval of Spark Therapeutics' Luxturna.
