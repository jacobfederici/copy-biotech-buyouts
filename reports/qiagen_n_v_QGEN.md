## Summary

QIAGEN N.V. entered into a Business Combination Agreement with Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc., under which Thermo Fisher's subsidiary, Quebec B.V., launched a tender offer to acquire all issued ordinary shares of QIAGEN for €39.00 per share in cash. The transaction is structured as a tender offer followed by potential integration steps under Dutch and German law.

## Timeline

- 2019-11-13: Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc. and QIAGEN N.V. enter into a Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement.
- 2020-03-02: Thermo Fisher enters into letter agreements with key QIAGEN executives including Thierry Bernard and Roland Sackers.
- 2020-03-03: QIAGEN and Thermo Fisher execute the Business Combination Agreement; joint press release and ad hoc announcement issued.
- 2020-04-27: QIAGEN and Quebec B.V. enter into a Non-Tender Agreement.
- 2020-05-18: Thermo Fisher formally commences the tender offer; QIAGEN Managing and Supervisory Boards issue a Joint Reasoned Position Statement recommending the offer.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2019, evidenced by a Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement signed on November 13, 2019. Throughout early 2020, the parties negotiated the terms of a Business Combination Agreement. On March 2, 2020, Thermo Fisher secured letter agreements with QIAGEN's executive leadership (Thierry Bernard, Roland Sackers, Barthold Piening, Thomas Schweins, Jonathan Sheldon, and Jean-Pascal Viola) regarding their roles and compensation post-transaction. The Business Combination Agreement was signed on March 3, 2020, proposing a cash tender offer at €39.00 per share. Following the signing, the parties engaged in regulatory planning and the preparation of the Offer Document. On April 27, 2020, a Non-Tender Agreement was reached between QIAGEN and the purchaser. On May 18, 2020, the offer was formally launched with the filing of the Schedule TO by Thermo Fisher and the Schedule 14D-9 by QIAGEN, containing the boards' recommendation to shareholders.
