## Summary

Alder BioPharmaceuticals entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by H. Lundbeck A/S for $18.00 per share in cash upfront, plus a $2.00 CVR tied to the European approval of Eptinezumab. The transaction was structured as a tender offer under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, aimed at strengthening Lundbeck's neuro-biologic pipeline with a late-stage migraine prevention asset.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2018 focused on an Ex-U.S. partnership for Eptinezumab. In March 2019, Lundbeck CEO Deborah Dunsire signaled interest in a full acquisition. Following due diligence on the partnership, Lundbeck issued its first formal acquisition bid on July 21, 2019, at $14.00 cash plus a $4.50 CVR. Alder's CEO Robert Azelby and the Board, advised by Centerview Partners, rejected this as inadequate. Lundbeck raised the cash component to $17.00 on August 10. Alder countered on August 12 for $18.00 cash and a $5.00 CVR. Lundbeck eventually agreed to $18.00 cash but capped the CVR at $2.00 on August 16. Confirmatory diligence and contract negotiations (handled by Skadden for Alder and Baker McKenzie for Lundbeck) occurred through early September. Regulatory clearances were obtained via HSR filing on September 20, 2019, and Austrian FCA filing on September 23, 2019.

## Rationale

The Alder Board determined the offer was superior to remaining a standalone company, citing the significant upfront cash premium (79% over the last closing price), the immediate liquidity for stockholders, and the risks associated with Alder's lack of commercial infrastructure for a global launch. Lundbeck sought to acquire Alder to bolster its position in brain diseases and secure a potential blockbuster migraine treatment to complement its existing portfolio.

## Evidence

- On October 22, 2019, Lundbeck completed its acquisition of Alder by consummating the Merger, without a meeting of stockholders of Alder in accordance with Section 251(h) (0001193125-19-271319).
- The Alder Board unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement, the Offer and the Merger are fair to, and in the best interest of, Alder and its stockholders (0001193125-19-252413).

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a tender offer. The CVR milestone requires European Commission approval of Eptinezumab by December 31, 2024. Shares outstanding are based on the SC 14D9 filing as of September 20, 2019.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The acquisition of Alder BioPharmaceuticals (ALDR) by Lundbeck was announced on September 16, 2019. While Alder was frequently cited by analysts as a logical takeover target due to its late-stage migraine drug eptinezumab, there were no specific, widely reported "leaks" or rumors of an imminent deal with a named buyer in the days immediately preceding the announcement.

The following timeline details the general speculation and the private negotiation history that was revealed in regulatory filings after the deal was finalized:

*   **Early 2019**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Filings (reported post-deal by *FiercePharma*)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** 10 unnamed companies
    *   **Rumored Price:** N/A
    *   **Details:** Alder began seeking a partner for the commercialization of eptinezumab outside the United States. Ten companies initially expressed interest in a potential partnership, which eventually evolved into buyout discussions for some.

*   **March 6, 2019**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** SEC Filings (reported post-deal by *FiercePharma*)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Lundbeck
    *   **Rumored Price:** N/A
    *   **Details:** Lundbeck CEO Deborah Dunsire met with Alder board member Clay Siegall. Dunsire expressed interest in a "broader scope transaction" rather than just a regional partnership.

*   **July 2019**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** SEC Filings (reported post-deal by *FiercePharma*)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Lundbeck
    *   **Rumored Price:** $14.00 per share upfront + $4.50 CVR
    *   **Details:** Lundbeck made its first formal private proposal to acquire Alder. The offer included $14.00 per share in cash plus a Contingent Value Right (CVR) of up to $4.50 based on regulatory approvals and sales milestones. Alder’s board rejected the offer as inadequate.

*   **August 2019**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** SEC Filings (reported post-deal by *FiercePharma*)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Lundbeck
    *   **Rumored Price:** $17.00 per share upfront + $2.00 CVR
    *   **Details:** Lundbeck increased its private offer to $19.00 total ($17.00 upfront). Alder’s board rejected this second offer but continued parallel negotiations for both a partnership and a potential sale.

*   **September 6, 2019**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *MarketScreener* (referencing the Letter of Intent date)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Lundbeck
    *   **Rumored Price:** ~$1.5 billion
    *   **Details:** Lundbeck signed a non-binding Letter of Intent to acquire Alder. This remained private until the definitive agreement was reached.

*   **September 16, 2019 (Pre-Market)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** *BioPharma Dive*, *GeekWire*, *FierceBiotech*
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Lundbeck (Confirmed Deal)
    *   **Rumored Price:** $18.00 per share upfront + $2.00 CVR
    *   **Details:** The definitive agreement was officially announced. Media reports noted that while Alder had been a "perennial" name on analyst lists of takeover targets (alongside peers like Biohaven), there were no specific public rumors of the Lundbeck deal in the 48 hours prior to the announcement.
