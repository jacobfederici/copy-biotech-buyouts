## Summary

CTI BioPharma Corp. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Swedish Orphan Biovitrum AB (Sobi) for $9.10 per share in cash, representing a total equity value of approximately $1.7 billion. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The deal aimed to integrate CTI's lead product, VONJO, into Sobi's leading rare disease and hematology portfolio.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2022 with Company A, followed by an introduction to Sobi in February 2023 via MTS Health Partners. Sobi initially proposed $8.00 per share on April 5, 2023. CTI engaged in a competitive process involving Sobi, Company B, and Company C. Company B provided a range of $8.50-$9.00 but later submitted a final bid of $8.75 on May 8. Sobi increased its offer to $8.80 on May 2 and finally to $9.10 on May 9. The CTI Board, advised by Centerview Partners and Skadden Arps, determined Sobi's $9.10 offer was superior in value and certainty compared to Company B's lower final bid and Company A's lack of a formal offer. Regulatory clearance was obtained via HSR Act expiration on June 9, 2023.

## Rationale

The CTI Board recommended the deal to provide stockholders with immediate liquidity and a significant premium (89% over the closing price) while mitigating the risks of commercializing VONJO as a standalone company, including the need for substantial additional capital for label expansion and ex-U.S. commercialization. Sobi sought to acquire CTI to strengthen its hematology portfolio and leverage its global infrastructure to expand VONJO's reach.

## Evidence

- At a meeting held on May 9, 2023, the CTI Board unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions, including the Offer and the Merger, are fair to, and in the best interest of, CTI and its stockholders (0001193125-23-154170).
- On June 26, 2023, Sobi completed its acquisition of CTI by consummating the Merger without a meeting of stockholders of CTI in accordance with Section 251(h) (0001193125-23-174551).
