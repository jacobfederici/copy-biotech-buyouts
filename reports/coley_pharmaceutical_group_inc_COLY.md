## Summary

Coley Pharmaceutical Group, Inc. entered into a definitive merger agreement with Pfizer Inc. on November 15, 2007, under which Pfizer launched a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares of Coley for $8.00 per share in cash. The transaction followed a multi-year collaboration between the companies and a competitive bidding process in late 2007. The deal closed on January 4, 2008, after approximately 91.3% of shares were tendered, followed by a short-form merger.

## Timeline

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest in July 2007 when Coley CEO Robert Bratzler approached Pfizer's Senior VP for Worldwide Business Development about a strategic transaction beyond their existing VaxImmune license talks. In September 2007, Coley engaged J.P. Morgan and formed a Strategic Transaction Committee. Pfizer conducted extensive due diligence throughout October 2007. On October 29, J.P. Morgan sent process letters to Pfizer and other third parties. On November 6, a third party indicated interest but withdrew on November 10 after failing to get executive committee approval. Pfizer bid $6.25 on November 7. Coley's advisors (J.P. Morgan and Mintz Levin) countered with guidance of $8.00 on November 9. Pfizer raised its offer to $8.00 on November 12, contingent on a tender agreement from major stockholders (Venrock, Thomas McNerney, and management). The parties negotiated the final merger agreement and disclosure schedules from November 12-15, leading to the unanimous board approval on November 15.

## Rationale

The Coley Board recommended the deal because the $8.00 cash price represented a 167% premium over the $3.00 closing price on the day before announcement. The Board cited the risks of remaining independent, including the need for substantial additional capital and the early-stage nature of the pipeline. Pfizer sought to secure full control of the TLR9 agonist platform and vaccine adjuvant technology it had previously licensed.
