## Summary

TESARO, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by GlaxoSmithKline plc for $75.00 per share in cash, representing a total equity value of approximately $5.1 billion. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, aimed at strengthening GSK's oncology pipeline with the addition of the PARP inhibitor ZEJULA and a portfolio of immuno-oncology assets.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2018 as TESARO sought capital to fund its long-range plan following setbacks with VARUBI. Management, led by CEO Leon Moulder and President Mary Lynne Hedley, initially focused on co-development/co-promotion deals for ZEJULA. Party A emerged as a primary collaboration suitor, while Party C proposed synthetic royalty financing. GSK entered discussions in June 2018, initially regarding a collaboration, but shifted to an acquisition interest in September. GSK's first formal bid was $66.00 on Oct 24, which the Board rejected as too low. After a market check of seven other companies resulted in no other acquisition bids, and Party A's collaboration offer was deemed to undervalue ZEJULA, TESARO pushed GSK for more value. GSK raised its bid to $69.00 on Nov 18 and finally to $75.00 on Nov 21, labeling it 'best and final.' TESARO granted exclusivity on Nov 23 to finalize diligence and the merger agreement. The Board approved the deal on Dec 2 after receiving fairness opinions from Citi and Centerview.

## Rationale

The TESARO Board determined the transaction was the best alternative to maximize stockholder value, citing near-term capital needs, the risks of remaining independent (including clinical trial and regulatory risks), and the substantial 110% premium over the 30-day VWAP. For GSK, the acquisition provides a major shift into oncology, adding a commercial-stage PARP inhibitor and a robust pipeline of immuno-oncology assets to its R&D portfolio.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, and reported sale processes for TESARO, Inc. (TSRO) prior to the official announcement of its acquisition by GlaxoSmithKline (GSK) on December 3, 2018.

*   **July 1, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** BioSpace / Bidness ETC
    *   **Rumored Acquirer(s):** Gilead Sciences, Amgen, Celgene, Pfizer, or Medivation.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified.
    *   **Details:** Following positive Phase III data for niraparib (Zejula), speculation intensified that Tesaro was a prime acquisition target. Analysts suggested it would be a "perfect fit" for Medivation to thwart a hostile takeover bid from Sanofi.

*   **June 12, 2017**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** StreetInsider / Fierce Pharma
    *   **Rumored Acquirer(s):** Sanofi and Gilead Sciences.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Approximately $10 billion (roughly $185 per share).
    *   **Details:** Reports indicated that Tesaro had officially put itself "on the block" for auction. Sanofi was identified as a lead bidder looking to recover after losing Medivation to Pfizer, with Gilead also reportedly "kicking the tires."

*   **July 14, 2017**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** CNBC / Fierce Pharma
    *   **Rumored Acquirer(s):** Sanofi and Gilead Sciences.
    *   **Rumored Price:** $9 billion to $10 billion.
    *   **Details:** Reports emerged that the sale process was stalling because potential buyers (Sanofi and Gilead) were unwilling to meet Tesaro’s high price expectations, which were influenced by the $14 billion Pfizer had paid for rival Medivation.

*   **June 20, 2018**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Intereconomia.com / Investing.com
    *   **Rumored Acquirer(s):** Roche.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified.
    *   **Details:** Shares of Tesaro rallied over 16% following rumors that Roche was preparing a potential acquisition offer to strengthen its oncology portfolio.

*   **November 16–19, 2018**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Bloomberg / Fierce Pharma
    *   **Rumored Acquirer(s):** Unnamed "potential suitor" (later identified as interest that sparked a broader search); Gilead Sciences was again cited by analysts as a logical buyer.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified (though the stock was trading around $36–$40 at the time).
    *   **Details:** Bloomberg reported that Tesaro was working with financial advisers to explore a sale after receiving takeover interest from at least one party. The advisers were reportedly reaching out to other big pharma companies to gauge interest.

*   **November 22, 2018**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** StreetInsider
    *   **Rumored Acquirer(s):** Unnamed "Large Pharma" company.
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified.
    *   **Details:** Speculation increased regarding active M&A talks with a major pharmaceutical company, causing a significant spike in share price during the week of Thanksgiving.
