## Summary

Concentra Biosciences, LLC acquired Jounce Therapeutics, Inc. via a tender offer for $1.85 per share in cash plus one contingent value right (CVR) per share. The transaction was finalized on May 3, 2023, following Jounce's decision to terminate a previously announced reverse merger with Redx Pharma plc in favor of Concentra's superior all-cash proposal.

## Interaction History

In November 2022, following disappointing clinical trial results and challenging financing markets, the Jounce Board initiated a review of strategic alternatives and formed a transaction committee. TD Cowen was engaged as the financial advisor. In late 2022, Jounce engaged with Gilead Sciences regarding the GS-1811 program, culminating in a December 27, 2022 agreement where Gilead bought out its remaining financial obligations for $67.0 million.

In early 2023, Jounce continued its strategic outreach, contacting numerous potential counterparties. On January 17, 2023, Redx Pharma submitted a proposal for a business combination. After several weeks of negotiations and due diligence, Jounce and Redx announced a definitive agreement on February 23, 2023, for a reverse merger.

On March 2, 2023, Kevin Tang, affiliated with Tang Capital Partners (TCP), which had accumulated a nearly 10% stake in Jounce, expressed opposition to the Redx merger. On March 14, 2023, Concentra Biosciences (controlled by TCP) submitted an unsolicited proposal to acquire Jounce for $1.80 per share in cash plus a CVR representing 80% of net proceeds from certain legacy programs. The Jounce Board granted Concentra limited due diligence access to encourage a better offer.

On March 24, 2023, Concentra increased its offer to $1.85 per share plus the CVR. After comparing the offer to the Redx transaction and a potential liquidation scenario, the Jounce Board determined on March 26, 2023, that the Concentra offer was a superior proposal. The Board withdrew its recommendation for the Redx transaction, terminated the Redx agreement, and approved the merger agreement with Concentra. The transaction was publicly announced on March 27, 2023, and the tender offer successfully closed on May 3, 2023.

## Rationale

The Jounce Board considered the substantial premium to the historical market prices of the Shares prior to the March 14 Proposal. The all-cash Offer Price and the Merger Consideration provided certain and immediate value and liquidity to Jounce's stockholders, compared to the risks associated with the Redx Business Combination or a standalone liquidation. Furthermore, the transaction offered stockholders an opportunity to participate in up to 80% of any monetization event from the disposition of legacy programs through the CVR. The Board determined that the Concentra offer represented the highest price reasonably obtainable by the Company under the circumstances.
