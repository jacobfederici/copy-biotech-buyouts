## Summary

Shire plc acquired NPS Pharmaceuticals, Inc. for $46.00 per share in cash, representing an equity value of approximately $5.2 billion. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a short-form merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. Shire assumed the regulatory risk associated with the impending FDA PDUFA date for NPS's key product candidate, Natpara.

## Interaction History

On October 9, 2014, an executive at a biopharmaceutical company ("Party A") contacted Francois Nader, NPS's President and CEO, to explore a possible combination without proposing a price. The Board met on November 3, 2014, and determined that such a combination was not strategically attractive. The Board also formed a Strategic Committee excluding a director ("Director A") who served on Party A's board.

On November 14, 2014, Flemming Ornskov, MD, Shire's CEO, left a message for Dr. Nader. The following day, Dr. Ornskov proposed an all-cash acquisition at $41.00 per share, noting confidence in announcing a deal before the January 24, 2015 PDUFA action date for Natpara. On November 30, the Strategic Committee reviewed the proposal with financial advisors Goldman Sachs and Leerink and determined to seek a higher price. Dr. Nader informed Dr. Ornskov that the $41.00 offer was inadequate.

On December 5, 2014, Dr. Ornskov revised the offer to $45.00 per share. The Strategic Committee allowed Shire to conduct limited due diligence. A confidentiality agreement was signed on December 16, and management presentations were held on December 19.

On December 18, "Party B" reached out to Leerink to explore a stock-for-stock combination.

On December 31, Dr. Ornskov reaffirmed the $45.00 offer. On January 1, 2015, Dr. Nader encouraged a higher bid. Dr. Ornskov increased the offer to $46.00 per share, describing it as Shire's best and final offer. On January 2, the Strategic Committee concluded the offer was compelling, particularly because Shire agreed to assume the full risk of any FDA actions regarding Natpara. The Committee directed Goldman Sachs and Leerink to conduct a market check of nine strategic parties (excluding Party A).

Between January 3 and January 9, 2015, all nine strategic parties contacted during the market check declined to pursue a transaction, including "Party C" which signed a confidentiality agreement on January 7 but ultimately withdrew.

On January 11, 2015, Goldman Sachs and Leerink rendered fairness opinions. The Board unanimously approved the Merger Agreement, which included a termination fee of approximately $155.9 million. The parties executed the Merger Agreement and announced the transaction. The transaction closed on February 21, 2015.

## Rationale

The Board of Directors concluded that the $46.00 per share in cash provided certainty of value and liquidity for the company's stockholders, eliminating the business risks associated with remaining an independent company. Those risks included the impending FDA action date for Natpara, the complexities of launching Natpara while also commercializing Gattex/Revestive, international commercialization challenges, and the capital requirements to build out global infrastructure and fund clinical development of other pipeline candidates. The Board also noted that Shire's offer represented a strong premium and that Shire agreed to assume the regulatory risk associated with the FDA's decision regarding Natpara.
