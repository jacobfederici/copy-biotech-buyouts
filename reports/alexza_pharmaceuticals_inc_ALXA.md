## Summary

Alexza Pharmaceuticals entered into a definitive merger agreement with Grupo Ferrer Internacional, S.A. (Parent) and its subsidiary Ferrer Pharma, Inc. (Purchaser) on May 9, 2016. The transaction was structured as a cash tender offer for all outstanding shares at $0.90 per share plus a Contingent Value Right (CVR) representing a pro-rata share of up to $32.8 million in milestone payments. The deal followed a period of severe financial distress for Alexza, including a default on its Atlas Notes and the termination of its U.S. marketing partnership with Teva. The merger was completed on June 21, 2016, via a short-form merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL.

## Interaction History

Negotiations were led by Alexza's CEO Thomas B. King and the Alexza Board, advised by Guggenheim Securities and Cooley LLP. The process involved contacting 83 potential parties. Party A (a Japanese firm) and Party B (private) expressed interest but failed to secure financing. Parent, already a major shareholder and commercial partner, emerged as the only actionable bidder. Negotiations were complicated by Alexza's default on $45 million in Atlas Notes and a spike in stock volatility that increased the Black-Scholes value of outstanding warrants, leading Parent to reduce the upfront cash offer from an initial $1.04 to $0.90. Final terms were reached after Parent agreed to a two-way adjustment on CVR payments and limited deductions for Guggenheim's fees. The Noteholders of the Atlas Notes agreed to a forbearance and eventual cancellation of the notes in exchange for participation interests in future ADASUVE revenue.

## Rationale

The Alexza Board recommended the deal because the company was facing immediate insolvency, recurring operating losses, and potential bankruptcy. Liquidity resources were nearly depleted, and the company had defaulted on interest payments for its Atlas Notes. The board believed a liquidation would result in zero recovery for stockholders, whereas the Ferrer offer provided immediate cash and potential future upside through CVRs. Parent was the only bidder with a funded, actionable offer.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of buyout rumors, acquisition speculation, and reported non-binding negotiations for Alexza Pharmaceuticals Inc. (ALXA) prior to the definitive merger agreement announced on May 10, 2016.

*   **February 29, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Alexza Pharmaceuticals SEC Filing (Form 8-K) / Press Release
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Grupo Ferrer Internacional, S.A. (Ferrer)
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified in the initial disclosure.
    *   **Details:** Alexza officially disclosed that it had entered into a non-binding letter of intent with its commercial partner, Ferrer, regarding a potential acquisition of all outstanding shares of Alexza common stock. This followed a period of "strategic alternative" exploration initiated in late 2015.

*   **March 29, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** FierceBiotech / BioSpace
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Grupo Ferrer Internacional, S.A. (Ferrer)
    *   **Rumored Price:** Not specified.
    *   **Details:** Media outlets reported that Alexza was in "emergency talks" with Ferrer for a total acquisition. Reports highlighted that Alexza only had sufficient capital to fund operations through the end of April 2016, making the acquisition a likely "rescue" deal to prevent the company from shutting down.

*   **May 9, 2016**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Market Speculation / Trading Activity
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Grupo Ferrer Internacional, S.A. (Ferrer)
    *   **Rumored Price:** Speculation centered on a significant premium over the then-trading price of approximately $0.54.
    *   **Details:** On the day prior to the official announcement, trading volume and price action suggested heightened investor anticipation of a finalized deal with Ferrer, following the expiration of the previously disclosed funding window.

*   **May 10, 2016 (Pre-Market)**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** PR Newswire / FirstWord Pharma
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Grupo Ferrer Internacional, S.A. (Ferrer)
    *   **Rumored Price:** $0.90 per share in cash plus Contingent Value Rights (CVRs).
    *   **Details:** Final reports immediately preceding the market open confirmed the definitive agreement. These reports noted that the $0.90 price represented a 210% premium to the share price on February 26, 2016 (the last trading day before the non-binding letter of intent was first made public).
