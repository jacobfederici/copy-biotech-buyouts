## Summary

Imago BioSciences, Inc. entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Merck Sharp & Dohme LLC, a subsidiary of Merck & Co., Inc., for $36.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $1.35 billion. The transaction focused on Imago's lead asset, bomedemstat, a lysine-specific demethylase 1 (LSD1) inhibitor for the treatment of myeloproliferative neoplasms. The deal was completed on January 11, 2023, following a successful tender offer in which approximately 89.2% of outstanding shares were tendered.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in earnest in September 2022 after Parent (Merck) expressed renewed interest following routine updates. Imago management, led by CEO Hugh Y. Rienhoff, Jr., contacted 10 other potential strategic counterparties. Party A and Party C showed initial interest but ultimately declined to bid. On November 13, 2022, Parent offered $28.00 per share. The Imago Board, advised by Centerview Partners and Latham & Watkins, pushed for higher value. On November 14, Parent raised the offer to $32.00 and then $35.00. Centerview informed Parent the Board was looking for $36.00. Parent agreed to $36.00 on November 15 as its 'best and final.' Negotiations then shifted to deal protection terms; the Board successfully negotiated the termination fee down from Parent's requested 4.5% to 3.5% ($47.1 million) and extended the 'outside date' from 6 months to 12 months. Regulatory clearance was obtained via HSR filing on December 5, 2022.

## Rationale

The Imago Board determined the $36.00 cash price provided immediate and certain value at a significant premium (107% over the prior closing price). They considered the risks of remaining standalone, including the need for significant future capital raises (estimated $250M) and the inherent clinical/regulatory risks of bomedemstat. Merck sought to bolster its hematology pipeline with a mid-stage asset targeting myeloproliferative neoplasms.

## Evidence

- At a meeting held on November 19, 2022, the Imago Board unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereby, including the Offer and the Merger, are fair to, and in the best interest of, Imago and its stockholders (0001193125-22-303114).
- On January 11, 2023, Merck completed its acquisition of Imago by consummating the Merger without a meeting of stockholders of Imago in accordance with Section 251(h) (0001193125-23-006137).
