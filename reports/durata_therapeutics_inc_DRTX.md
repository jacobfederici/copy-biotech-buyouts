## Summary

Durata Therapeutics was acquired by Actavis plc via a tender offer for $23.00 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $5.00 per share. The transaction focused on Durata's lead antibiotic, dalbavancin (Dalvance), and utilized Section 251(h) of the DGCL to effect a merger without a stockholder vote following the successful tender of approximately 79.7% of outstanding shares.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in July 2014 when 'Company A' approached Durata CEO Paul Edick for co-promotion talks, which pivoted to acquisition interest. Company A offered $20.00/share in August, which the Board rejected as too low. In September, Company A added a $7.00 CVR. Durata then conducted a targeted market check, contacting five other firms. Actavis emerged with a superior bid of $23.00 cash plus a $5.00 CVR. Meanwhile, Company A reduced its cash bid to $18.00 on October 1, citing diligence findings. Despite Durata management's attempts to get Company A to improve its bid, Company A declined to match Actavis's $23.00 upfront price. On October 5, 2014, the Durata Board met, received a fairness opinion from BofA Merrill Lynch, and approved the Actavis deal. Regulatory clearance was obtained following HSR filings on October 20, 2014.

## Rationale

The Durata Board determined that the Actavis offer provided higher and more certain value than remaining independent or pursuing other strategic alternatives. The Board noted the significant premium (66% over the unaffected closing price) and the opportunity for stockholders to participate in future upside via the CVR. Actavis sought to strengthen its infectious disease portfolio with the recently approved Dalvance.

## Evidence

- On November 17, 2014, Parent completed its acquisition of the Company by consummating the merger of Purchaser with and into the Company... in accordance with Section 251(h) (0001193125-14-415059)

## Decision Notes

The deal value is calculated based on 26.79M shares outstanding as of Oct 14, 2014. CVR milestones include: $1.00 for EMA approval of dalbavancin for ABSSSI by 12/31/2018; $1.00 for single-dose infusion labeling by 12/31/2018; and $3.00 for cumulative worldwide net revenues exceeding $600M between 1/1/2016 and 12/31/2017.
