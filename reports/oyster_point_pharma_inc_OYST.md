## Summary

Viatris Inc. acquired Oyster Point Pharma for $11.00 per share in cash plus a contingent value right of up to $2.00 per share based on 2022 performance milestones for TYRVAYA Nasal Spray. The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, aimed at establishing Viatris' ophthalmic franchise.

## Timeline

- 2021-11-02: Oyster Point and Viatris enter into a mutual confidentiality agreement to discuss a potential ex-U.S. license for TYRVAYA.
- 2022-06-02: Oyster Point Board authorizes management to explore strategic alternatives, including a sale of the company, due to financing environment challenges.
- 2022-06-23: Viatris executives meet with Oyster Point CEO Jeffrey Nau and express interest in a strategic acquisition rather than just a license.
- 2022-08-05: Viatris verbally indicates a valuation range of $11.00 to $13.00 per share contingent on exclusivity.
- 2022-08-12: Oyster Point counters at $16.00 per share; Viatris refuses to negotiate price until due diligence is complete.
- 2022-08-15: Oyster Point enters into a 30-day exclusivity period with Viatris.
- 2022-09-13: Viatris submits a revised proposal of $10.00 upfront plus CVRs totaling up to $13.00.
- 2022-09-15: Viatris increases upfront cash to $11.00 per share, stating it is their best and final offer.
- 2022-11-04: Oyster Point Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement.
- 2022-11-07: Merger Agreement is executed and publicly announced.
- 2022-12-31: Tender offer expires with 85.3% of shares tendered.
- 2023-01-03: Merger completed; Oyster Point becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Viatris.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2021 as a licensing discussion for ex-U.S. rights to TYRVAYA. By June 2022, Oyster Point engaged Piper Sandler and later Centerview Partners to run a broader strategic process. Piper Sandler contacted 8 parties; Party C proposed an all-stock merger, while others (Parties A, B, D, E) declined due to fit or timing. Viatris, led by Executive Chairman Robert Coury and CEO Michael Goettler, shifted focus to a whole-company acquisition in June 2022. Viatris insisted on exclusivity and expressed concern over TYRVAYA prescription trends during diligence. This led to the introduction of a CVR structure to bridge the valuation gap between Oyster Point's $16.00 counter and Viatris's $11.00-$13.00 range. Final negotiations centered on the upfront cash component (raised from $10 to $11) and the specific net revenue/prescription thresholds for the CVR milestones.

## Rationale

The Oyster Point Board determined the deal was superior to a standalone path, which would require substantial dilutive capital raises in a difficult financing environment. Viatris sought the acquisition to acquire a commercial-stage ophthalmic asset (TYRVAYA) and a specialized commercial infrastructure to serve as the foundation for its new ophthalmology therapeutic segment.

## Evidence

- On November 4, 2022, the Oyster Point Board unanimously (i) determined that the Merger Agreement and the Transactions, including the Offer and the Merger, are fair to, and in the best interest of, Oyster Point and its stockholders (0001193125-22-296161).
- On January 3, 2023, Purchaser was merged with and into Oyster Point without a vote of the stockholders of Oyster Point in accordance with Section 251(h) of the DGCL (0001193125-23-000324).

## Decision Notes

The CVR milestones are based on 2022 performance: Milestone 1 ($1.00) requires ≥$21.6M net revenue and ≥131,822 prescriptions; Milestone 2 ($2.00) requires ≥$24.0M net revenue and ≥146,469 prescriptions. Shares outstanding taken from Item 1(b) of the SC 14D9.

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

No pre-deal rumors exist for Oyster Point Pharma, Inc. (OYST). 

The acquisition of Oyster Point Pharma by Viatris Inc. was announced on November 7, 2022, without any prior public reporting of buyout rumors, specific acquisition speculation, or leaked takeover discussions in major financial or pharmaceutical news outlets. Market analysts and reporting from publications such as *BioPharma Dive*, *Fierce Pharma*, and *BioCentury* noted that the deal was unveiled simultaneously with Viatris' third-quarter earnings and a separate acquisition of Famy Life Sciences, catching the market largely by surprise. 

While the company had been a subject of general "M&A candidate" lists in the biotech sector due to its FDA-approved product *Tyrvaya*, there were no specific reports of a buyer, price, or active negotiations before the official definitive agreement was made public.
