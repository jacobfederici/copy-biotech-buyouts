## Summary

Kronos Bio, Inc. was acquired by Concentra Biosciences, LLC via a tender offer for $0.57 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR). The deal followed a strategic review process initiated after the discontinuation of Kronos's lead clinical program, istisociclib, and was structured to provide immediate liquidity and potential future value from the company's remaining KAT inhibitor assets and cost savings.

## Interaction History

Following the failure of a lead clinical asset in late 2024, Kronos engaged Leerink Partners to run a broad strategic process contacting 25 potential counterparties. After a planned reverse merger with 'Party D' collapsed in March 2025 due to PIPE financing difficulties, the board pivoted to financial buyers. Concentra (Parent), led by Kevin Tang, emerged as a lead bidder alongside 'Party C' and 'Party E'. Negotiations focused heavily on the 'Closing Net Cash' condition and the structure of the CVR. Parent initially offered $0.95 per share but reduced the upfront cash to $0.57 as the net cash target was lowered to $40M to ensure deal certainty. The board, advised by Goodwin Procter and Leerink, determined that Parent's offer provided the highest probability of execution and superior value compared to a standalone liquidation, which management estimated at $0.54 per share. The final agreement included a CVR covering asset dispositions and cost savings, and a limited guaranty from Tang Capital Partners, LP.

## Rationale

The Kronos Board determined the deal was superior to a standalone liquidation. Motives included the certainty of immediate cash value, the potential for additional upside via CVRs, and the high execution risk of remaining independent following the failure of its lead clinical program. The board also noted that Parent's ability to rapidly wind down operations would likely result in higher value for stockholders than a company-led dissolution.

## Decision Notes

The deal was accepted as a completed buyout. The CVR milestones include 50% of proceeds from KAT inhibitor sales within 2 years, 100% of legacy product proceeds within 6 years, and tiered percentages of cost savings versus the $40M closing net cash target.
