## Summary

Novartis AG acquired The Medicines Company for $85.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $9.7 billion. The transaction focused on the acquisition of inclisiran, a Phase 3 siRNA therapy for lowering LDL cholesterol, and was completed via a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL on January 6, 2020.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in mid-2018 with various parties (Parties A-G) exploring partnerships. In early 2019, CEO Mark Timney engaged Novartis, who initially declined interest but re-engaged after positive ORION-3 data in May 2019. A formal auction process involving 12 potential buyers was launched in October 2019. Novartis emerged as the primary bidder, initially offering $74.03 in September, then $85.00 in early November. On November 6, Novartis verbally increased the offer to $90.00 contingent on exclusivity and a fast-track announcement during the AHA Scientific Sessions. However, following intensive due diligence on the Alnylam license and patent portfolio, Novartis executive Nigel Sheail informed Timney on November 18 that they were lowering the price back to $85.00, characterizing it as their 'best and final' offer. The Board deliberated on standalone risks versus the $85.00 cash certainty and approved the deal on November 22, 2019. Regulatory clearance was obtained via HSR filing, and the deal closed following the tender of 75% of shares.

## Rationale

The Board determined the $85.00 cash price provided immediate and certain value that outweighed the risks of remaining standalone. Key risks included the significant capital required for a global commercial launch of inclisiran, the inherent uncertainty of FDA approval, and the need to service existing convertible debt. Novartis sought the acquisition to bolster its cardiovascular portfolio with a potentially transformative siRNA therapy.

## Decision Notes

The deal value of $9.7 billion includes the cost of settling the company's significant convertible notes (2022, 2023, and 2024 series). Shares outstanding (80,183,716) were sourced from the Item 1(b) of the SC 14D9 filed Dec 5, 2019. The transaction did not include a CVR.
