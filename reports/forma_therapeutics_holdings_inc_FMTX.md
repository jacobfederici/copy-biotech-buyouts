## Summary

Forma Therapeutics entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Novo Nordisk for $20.00 per share in an all-cash tender offer valued at approximately $1.1 billion. The transaction focused on Forma's lead candidate, etavopivat, a potentially disease-modifying therapy for sickle cell disease. The deal followed a strategic shift by Forma to focus on etavopivat while out-licensing its oncology assets, including olutasidenib, to Rigel Pharmaceuticals.

## Timeline

- 2021-01-01: Novo Nordisk initially contacts Forma to discuss interest in etavopivat collaboration.
- 2021-12-16: Novo initiates discussions regarding a potential global license or asset acquisition of etavopivat following ASH Annual Meeting data.
- 2022-01-12: Novo EVP Ludovic Helfgott contacts Forma CEO Frank Lee to discuss acquiring global licensing rights.
- 2022-03-17: Novo presents a high-level framework for a global strategic partnership without economic terms.
- 2022-05-02: Novo proposes a global profit share with $225M upfront and milestones (May 2 Framework).
- 2022-05-12: Forma rejects the May 2 Framework; Novo indicates interest in a potential acquisition of the whole company.
- 2022-06-10: Novo submits a non-binding proposal for $410M upfront plus a $120M CVR on a cash-free, debt-free basis.
- 2022-06-15: Novo submits a revised proposal of $8.20 per share plus net cash and a $2.40 CVR.
- 2022-06-27: Forma and Novo enter into a Confidentiality Agreement with a 12-month standstill.
- 2022-07-27: Forma enters into a license agreement with Rigel Pharmaceuticals for olutasidenib.
- 2022-08-03: Novo submits a proposal for $15.25 per share plus a $2.40 CVR.
- 2022-08-14: Novo increases offer to $19.00 per share plus a $2.40 CVR.
- 2022-08-16: Novo increases offer to $20.00 per share in cash, eliminating the CVR, in exchange for exclusivity.
- 2022-08-17: Forma and Novo enter into an Exclusivity Agreement through August 31, 2022.
- 2022-08-31: Forma Board unanimously approves the Merger Agreement at $20.00 per share.
- 2022-09-01: Public announcement of the transaction.
- 2022-09-15: Tender offer formally commences.
- 2022-10-13: Tender offer expires with 91.5% of shares tendered.
- 2022-10-14: Merger completed; Forma becomes a wholly owned indirect subsidiary of Novo Nordisk.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in early 2021 as collaboration talks but shifted toward an acquisition in mid-2022. Ludovic Helfgott (Novo EVP) and Frank Lee (Forma CEO) were the primary points of contact. Novo initially sought only etavopivat rights but eventually proposed a whole-company acquisition because they had no interest in Forma's oncology programs, which Forma subsequently out-licensed to Rigel. Forma's board, advised by Centerview Partners and Goodwin Procter, conducted a targeted market check with three other global pharmaceutical companies (Parties A, B, and C), all of whom declined to pursue a transaction. Novo's bids progressed from an initial cash-free/debt-free aggregate value to a per-share model. The final price of $20.00 was reached after Forma's board pushed for the conversion of a proposed Contingent Value Right (CVR) into upfront cash to provide certainty to stockholders. Regulatory clearance was obtained via HSR filing on September 12, 2022, with the waiting period expiring prior to the offer's conclusion.

## Rationale

The Forma Board determined the deal was superior to a standalone path due to the inherent risks of clinical development, the need for significant future capital raises which would be dilutive, and the immediate liquidity provided by the 52% premium over the prior day's closing price. Novo Nordisk sought the acquisition to bolster its rare disease pipeline, specifically in hemoglobinopathies.

## Decision Notes

The deal was completed via a Section 251(h) merger following a successful tender offer where 91.5% of shares were tendered. Shares outstanding (47,866,342) were sourced from the SC 14D9 'Securities' section as of Sept 13, 2022. The final offer eliminated a previously discussed CVR in favor of a higher upfront cash payment.
