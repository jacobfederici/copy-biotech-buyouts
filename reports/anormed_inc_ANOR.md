## Summary

AnorMED Inc. was acquired by Genzyme Corporation for $13.50 per share in cash — approximately $580 million — following a multi-bidder contest. Genzyme opened with an unsolicited $8.55-per-share hostile tender offer (via its subsidiary Dematal Corporation), which the AnorMED Board rejected as inadequate. AnorMED then ran a sale process and, on September 26, 2006, signed a support agreement with Millennium Pharmaceuticals at $12.00 per share. Genzyme responded on October 10, 2006 by raising its proposal to $13.50 per share; after Millennium declined to match within its contractual window, AnorMED and Genzyme executed a support agreement at $13.50 on October 17, 2006. Genzyme's offer expired on November 7, 2006 with approximately 96% of shares tendered, and Genzyme took up and paid for the shares and completed a compulsory acquisition of the remainder. (An earlier version of this report incorrectly recorded Millennium as the acquirer at $12.00; Genzyme was the winning bidder at $13.50.)

## Interaction History

In August 2006, Genzyme Corporation made an unsolicited proposal to acquire AnorMED for $8.55 per share. The AnorMED Board, led by Interim CEO Kenneth Galbraith and a Strategic Initiatives Committee (chaired by Dr. Joseph Dougherty), reviewed the proposal with financial advisor Goldman Sachs and legal counsel, determined it was inadequate, and rejected it. Genzyme took the $8.55 offer directly to shareholders on September 1, 2006 through its subsidiary Dematal Corporation. AnorMED adopted a Shareholders' Rights Plan and solicited competing bidders. On September 26, 2006, AnorMED announced a friendly support agreement with Millennium Pharmaceuticals at $12.00 per share — roughly a 40% premium to Genzyme's bid — backed by lock-up agreements; Millennium commenced its tender offer on October 5, 2006.

On October 10, 2006, Genzyme announced it would amend its tender offer to increase the price from $8.55 to $13.50 per share in cash (approximately $580 million) and asked the AnorMED Board to determine that the proposal constituted a "superior proposal" under the Millennium support agreement. That agreement gave Millennium a right to be notified and at least three business days to match any superior proposal. Millennium did not match at $13.50. On October 17, 2006, AnorMED and Genzyme executed a mutually agreed support agreement at $13.50 per share, and the AnorMED Board changed its recommendation in favor of the Genzyme offer. Following expiry of the HSR waiting period on November 6, 2006, the Genzyme offer expired on November 7, 2006 with approximately 96% of shares tendered; Genzyme took up and paid for the tendered shares at $13.50 and completed a compulsory acquisition of the remaining shares, after which AnorMED was delisted and deregistered.

## Rationale

The AnorMED Board ran a competitive process to maximize value for its lead asset, MOZOBIL (plerixafor), a late-stage CXCR4 antagonist for stem-cell transplantation. The $12.00 Millennium support agreement set a floor that drew Genzyme back with a higher $13.50 offer; once Millennium declined to match, the Board concluded the Genzyme offer delivered superior value and recommended it. Genzyme acquired AnorMED to add MOZOBIL to its oncology and transplant franchise.

## Evidence

- Amendment No. 14 to the Schedule 14D-9 records that "On October 10, 2006, AnorMED Inc. announced that it had received a proposal from Genzyme Corporation, acting through Dematal Corp., to amend the previously commenced tender offer to acquire all of the outstanding common shares of AnorMED from US$8.55 in cash per share to US$13.50 in cash per share" (0000945234-06-000843).
- Amendment No. 16 records that "On October 17, 2006, AnorMED Inc. and Genzyme announced that they have executed a mutually beneficial support agreement under which Genzyme will acquire AnorMED in an all cash transaction valued at US$13.50 per outstanding share, or approximately US$580 million" (0000945234-06-000862).
- The final Genzyme Schedule TO-T/A reports that the offer "expired at 8:00 a.m. (Vancouver time) on November 7, 2006," that "approximately 41,553,884 Shares had been validly tendered and not withdrawn ... representing approximately 96.0% of the outstanding Shares," and that the Offeror took up and paid for the shares at $13.50 and "completed a compulsory acquisition of the remaining outstanding Shares" (0001047469-06-013652).

## Decision Notes

The transaction was a competitive bidding situation. Millennium acted as a "white knight" against Genzyme's initial $8.55 hostile bid and signed a $12.00 support agreement on September 26, 2006, but Genzyme ultimately prevailed by raising to $13.50 (Millennium did not exercise its three-business-day match right). The completed acquirer was Genzyme Corporation (through Dematal Corporation) at $13.50 per share, ~$580 million; the deal closed via tender offer and compulsory acquisition on November 7, 2006. Shares outstanding were taken from the October 5, 2006 Schedule 14D-9 filing. NOTE: a prior version of this report erroneously listed Millennium as the acquirer at $12.00 — corrected here against the SC 14D-9 amendments and the final Genzyme SC TO-T/A.
