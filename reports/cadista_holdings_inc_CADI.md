## Summary

Jubilant Generics Inc., a subsidiary of Jubilant Pharma Limited and an affiliate of Jubilant Life Sciences Ltd., launched a tender offer to acquire all outstanding shares of Cadista Holdings Inc. not already owned by the Jubilant group for $1.60 per share in cash. The Jubilant group already owned approximately 82.38% of the company's common stock prior to the offer. The transaction is structured as a tender offer followed by a short-form merger to provide liquidity to minority shareholders and consolidate ownership.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began following pressure from the Pillai Shareholder Group for liquidity in late 2013. Jubilant Life Sciences initially rejected a $2.75/share demand. In August 2014, Jubilant proposed $1.60/share. A Special Committee (Messrs. Seth and Becker) was formed and hired Seyfarth Shaw LLP and Cassel Salpeter. The Committee initially pushed for $1.76/share on October 1, 2014, citing accumulated cash. Jubilant's representative, R. Sankaraiah, argued that the higher price did not account for $17 million in required capital expenditures and the loss of a major retail customer for methylprednisolone. After reviewing revised 'October Projections' reflecting these headwinds, the Committee's advisor, Cassel Salpeter, issued a fairness opinion for $1.60. The Pillai group also signaled acceptance of the $1.60 price. The Board approved the recommendation on October 24, 2014, with non-independent directors (Delaney, Sankaraiah, Mandan) recusing or abstaining.

## Rationale

The Special Committee and Board recommended the deal because it provides immediate cash liquidity for minority shareholders in a stock that is otherwise illiquid with no active trading market. They also noted the declining market share of key products, the loss of a major customer, and the fact that Jubilant, as the majority owner, would not support any alternative third-party sale.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

No pre-deal rumors exist for Cadista Holdings Inc. (CADI).

The company was already a majority-owned subsidiary of **Jubilant Life Sciences Limited** (which held approximately 82.4% of the shares) for several years prior to the final buyout. The acquisition of the remaining minority stake was conducted through a formal tender offer process initiated by the parent company rather than being preceded by speculative reports or third-party buyout rumors.

The key milestones for the confirmed deal were:
*   **September 30, 2010:** Jubilant Life Sciences made an initial formal attempt to buy out the remaining 17.6% stake at $0.30 per share, which did not succeed.
*   **November 13, 2014:** Jubilant Life Sciences officially announced and commenced the successful tender offer to acquire the remaining minority stake at $1.60 per share.
*   **December 23, 2014:** The deal was officially completed, making Cadista a wholly-owned subsidiary.
