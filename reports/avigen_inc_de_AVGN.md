## Summary

Avigen, Inc. was acquired by MediciNova, Inc. following a period of strategic review and a hostile tender offer attempt by Biotechnology Value Fund (BVF). Under the final merger agreement, Avigen stockholders received approximately $1.24 per share, payable in either cash or secured convertible notes of MediciNova, plus a Contingent Payment Right (CVR) linked to milestones from a Genzyme assignment agreement. The deal followed Avigen's board rejecting a $1.00 per share cash tender offer from BVF as inadequate.

## Interaction History

Following the failure of AV650 in October 2008, Biotechnology Value Fund (BVF), led by Mark Lampert, pressured Avigen to liquidate. Avigen's CEO Dr. Kenneth Chahine and Chairman Dr. Zola Horovitz resisted, seeking to monetize assets like AV411. MediciNova first proposed an acquisition on December 8, 2008. BVF launched a hostile $1.00/share tender offer on January 23, 2009. The Avigen Board, advised by RBC Capital Markets and Pacific Growth Equities, rejected BVF's offer on January 29, 2009, citing it undervalued the company's cash (>$1.00/share) and pipeline. After a brief period in March 2009 where Avigen considered liquidation, negotiations with MediciNova resumed in June 2009, leading to a definitive agreement in August 2009 at a higher valuation (~$1.24/share) than the BVF offer.

## Rationale

The Avigen Board recommended the MediciNova merger as it provided superior value to the BVF tender offer and a potential liquidation. The Board noted that Avigen held more than $1.00 per share in net cash, and the MediciNova deal allowed stockholders to retain upside through CVRs and convertible notes while valuing the AV411 program and the company's public listing.

## Evidence

- On August 20, 2009, MediciNova, Inc. ... and Avigen, Inc. entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (8-K, 2009-08-25)
- The Board, at a meeting held on January 29, 2009, determined that the [BVF] Offer is inadequate and not in the best interests of Avigen’s stockholders. (SC 14D9, 2009-02-06)

## Decision Notes

The deal value is computed based on the estimated $1.24 per share consideration multiplied by the 26,769,115 shares outstanding as of Jan 31, 2009. CVR milestones relate to an assignment agreement with Genzyme Corporation. Shares outstanding source: SC 14D9 Item 1.

## Buyout Rumors

Source: OpenRouter web search (Gemini 3 Flash Preview). Pre-deal rumors only.

The following is a chronological timeline of acquisition rumors, unsolicited bids, and takeover speculation involving Avigen, Inc. (AVGN) prior to the final definitive merger agreement with MediciNova, Inc. in August 2009.

*   **December 24, 2008**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Greenbackd / PR Newswire
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** MediciNova, Inc. (MNOV)
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** 1.75 million shares of MediciNova common stock (valued at approximately $7 million in cash consideration to be received by MediciNova from Avigen's reserves).
    *   **Details:** Reports surfaced that MediciNova had submitted a merger proposal to Avigen. Under the terms, Avigen would wind down its business activities and satisfy obligations, while shareholders would receive a pro rata portion of MediciNova stock. Avigen confirmed it was evaluating the proposal but had not yet reached an agreement.

*   **January 9, 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Biotechnology Value Fund (BVF) Press Release / Reuters
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** MediciNova, Inc. (via BVF engineering)
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Estimated liquidation value of approximately $1.20 per share.
    *   **Details:** Avigen’s largest shareholder, Biotechnology Value Fund (BVF), publicly called for a special meeting to replace Avigen’s board with nominees who would support the MediciNova offer. BVF argued the deal offered "downside protection" near the cash value of $1.20/share with "tremendous upside potential" through equity in the combined company.

*   **January 26, 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** FierceBiotech / San Francisco Business Times
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Biotechnology Value Fund (BVF) / MediciNova
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** $20.9 million (approximately $1.00 per share).
    *   **Details:** Reports indicated that BVF had formally offered to acquire Avigen for $20.9 million, contingent on board replacement and the removal of a "poison pill" shareholder rights plan. An Avigen spokesperson clarified to the press that the company had not rejected the bid but was considering it among other strategic options.

*   **February 6, 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** FierceBiotech
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Multiple Unnamed Parties
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specified.
    *   **Details:** Following Avigen's formal rejection of BVF’s $1.00 per share offer, CEO Kenneth Chahine stated the company had received "multiple proposals" from other parties in the preceding weeks that placed significant value on the company's cash, IP, and NASDAQ listing.

*   **March 12, 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** BVF Press Release / FierceBiotech
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** MediciNova, Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Not specified (focused on "downside protection" vs "upside potential").
    *   **Details:** In a letter to stockholders, BVF reiterated the benefits of the MediciNova merger, claiming the current Avigen board had failed to engage in substantive discussions with MediciNova despite the offer's potential to protect remaining assets.

*   **March 20, 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** Greenbackd (reporting on SEC filings)
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** Biotechnology Value Fund (BVF)
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** $1.20 per share (increased from $1.00).
    *   **Details:** Reports emerged that RBC (Avigen's advisor) contacted BVF to negotiate an increase in their tender offer price to compensate for the value of Avigen's AV411 assets and Genzyme milestones. BVF subsequently raised its offer to $1.20 per share.

*   **June 25, 2009**
    *   **Reporting Outlet:** GlobalNewswire / MediciNova Press Release
    *   **Rumored Acquirer:** MediciNova, Inc.
    *   **Rumored Price/Premium:** Net cash liquidation value plus $3 million.
    *   **Details:** The companies announced they had reached an "understanding of certain key terms" for a merger. While this was a formal announcement of intent, it was not yet a definitive, legally binding merger agreement, which remained subject to further negotiation and due diligence.
