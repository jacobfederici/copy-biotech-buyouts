## Summary

Biogen Inc. acquired Apellis Pharmaceuticals, Inc. for $41.00 per share in cash plus a contingent value right (CVR) worth up to $4.00 based on sales milestones for SYFOVRE. The transaction was executed via a tender offer followed by a second-step merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL, valuing the upfront equity at approximately $5.3 billion.

## Timeline

- 2024-09-20: Biogen CEO Christopher Viehbacher contacts Apellis Chairman Gerald Chan to discuss industry trends.
- 2024-11-01: Apellis and Biogen enter into a confidentiality agreement with a 12-month standstill.
- 2025-06-03: Biogen submits non-binding proposal for $26.00 upfront plus $5.00 CVR; Apellis Board rejects as undervaluing the company.
- 2025-10-02: Biogen increases proposal to $37.50 upfront plus $3.00 CVR; Apellis Board rejects.
- 2025-10-17: Biogen proposes $41.00 per share all-cash upfront; Apellis Board rejects but authorizes limited engagement.
- 2025-11-18: Biogen proposes $37.50 upfront plus $10.00 in tiered CVR milestones; Apellis Board rejects.
- 2026-02-27: Biogen re-submits $41.00 per share all-cash upfront proposal.
- 2026-03-09: Apellis conducts targeted market check with seven other potential counterparties; all decline interest.
- 2026-03-28: Negotiations focus on CVR structure to bridge valuation gap; Biogen proposes a holdback for contingent liabilities which Apellis rejects.
- 2026-03-30: Apellis Board unanimously approves the merger agreement at $41.00 upfront plus $4.00 CVR.

## Interaction History

Negotiations began in late 2024 with industry discussions between Biogen CEO Christopher Viehbacher and Apellis Chairman Gerald Chan. Biogen made several non-binding offers throughout 2025, starting at $31.00 (including CVR) and moving to $41.00 cash. The Apellis Board, advised by Evercore and WilmerHale, consistently rejected these as undervaluing the company's prospects, particularly the launches of SYFOVRE and EMPAVELI. In March 2026, after a market check of seven other large biopharma companies yielded no other bidders, Apellis focused on Biogen. Final negotiations involved the CVR structure; Biogen initially sought a CVR tied to contingent liabilities, but Apellis successfully negotiated for a CVR tied purely to SYFOVRE sales milestones ($1.5B and $2.0B annual net sales). The deal was finalized at $41.00 cash plus the $4.00 CVR.

## Rationale

The Apellis Board determined the offer provided immediate liquidity and certain value at a significant premium (140% over the prior day's close), while the CVR allowed shareholders to retain upside in SYFOVRE's commercial success. The board also considered the risks of remaining standalone, including the need for substantial additional capital and execution risks in a volatile market.

The deal was completed via a 251(h) merger on May 14, 2026. CVR milestones are: $2.00 for $1.5B annual net sales of SYFOVRE by 2030, and $2.00 for $2.0B annual net sales by 2031. Shares outstanding (128,007,815) sourced from the SC 14D9 as of April 9, 2026.
